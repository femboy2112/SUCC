# Observational SUCC

**What is SUCC?** A freestanding mathematical theory of how a finite, embodied observer
constructs knowledge from lawful causal interactions with a *source* — modelled as
source-labelled observations, successor/FUCC dynamics, and the categorical laws governing
faithful transport between representations. RH and π-normality are downstream *applications*,
rigorously separated from the core theory.

**Working moonshot:** base-16 normality of π. Category theory is the scaffolding we use to
reason toward it — it predicts obligations and obstructions; it is not a substitute for the
missing arithmetic estimate, and the actual source decides whether its lines describe π.

### Carrier reconstruction · Source-indexed transitions · Observer-relative proof access

**Observational SUCC (ObsSUCC)** is an independent mathematical research framework for
**constructing, relating, completing, and observing mathematical carriers through lawful
source-indexed transitions**, with explicit accounting of which structure, information, proof
authority, and causal history survive each transformation.

Its underlying research question is **how a finite embodied observer builds, relates, and justifies
mathematical knowledge from causal interactions** — and, concretely, how one constructs an
*equivalent interface to a larger carrier* out of smaller source carriers and their admissible
categorical edges. The physical-observer intuition is the historical motivation; it is **not** a
prerequisite for the mathematics, and the general theory is **independent of the Riemann
hypothesis**. RH is a demanding downstream application, not SUCC's definition.

> **Status.** Early-stage formal and computational research. Some results are classical mathematics
> realized as exact finite source charts; some are exact new-to-project derivations or tested finite
> models; others are open conjectures and preserved counterexamples. **The Riemann hypothesis
> remains OPEN. RH PROVED: NO.** No carrier equivalence in this repository produces Weil positivity,
> and no positive Hilbert covariance is identified with the signed completed Weil form. See the
> [claim index](docs/CLAIM_INDEX.md) and [claim ledger](docs/CLAIM_LEDGER.md).

## The central mathematical objects

SUCC works with actual carriers — `N, Z, Q, R, C, Ẑ, Q_p, A_f, A_Q` — and groups, rings, fields,
topological spaces, categories and their structure-preserving morphisms. The crucial idea is not
growing bigger sets; it is building a lawful interface to a larger carrier from smaller source
carriers via explicitly justified operations: **group completion, localization, quotients and
gluing, Cauchy and topological completion, inverse and direct limits, Yoneda reconstruction,
Pontryagin duality, and Fourier/Mellin/Poisson transport.**

The discipline is to distinguish rigorously between the original smaller carrier, its completed
carrier, a proper embedding, a quotient, a categorical equivalence, an isomorphism of a particular
structure, and a finite executable representation of one point. **An equivalence between completed
mathematical objects does not mean the original smaller carriers were already equivalent.**

- **[Carrier Atlas](docs/CARRIER_ATLAS.md)** — the backbone: every reconstruction with its
  construction, comparison map, strength (iso / equivalence / embedding / quotient / dense), what is
  preserved, what is lost, status, and evidence.
- **[Carrier Foundations Synthesis](research/2026-10-15/SUCC_CARRIER_FOUNDATIONS_SYNTHESIS.md)** —
  the paper-style account: notation, the *carrier reconstruction certificate* schema, propositions,
  counterexamples, verification, and a bounded originality statement.

### The π / source / observer machinery (2026-10-24)

The [source path and infinite observer continuation](research/2026-10-24/PI_PATH_SELECTION_SYNTHESIS.md)
now makes the interference proposal exact: finite source projectors, a uniform
BBP selection-depth mixture theorem, and a quantitative history-preservation
bridge with exact circle transport. The surviving arithmetic obligations are
real all-scale collision saving and preservation of the same selected source
law. Source uniqueness and ensemble mixing alone do not settle them.

This stack studies how a finite observer reconstructs knowledge from a *selected source*; the π
work is a downstream testbed, not the definition of the theory. In plain terms:

**Proved (paper or classical), with stated scope.**
- *Source fidelity.* The authentic BBP source (initial state `y₀=0`, forcing `r(n)`, bounded
  backward tail `t₀=π/16`) is characterized exactly; the earlier "four-pole lane closes CYC-03"
  claim is **withdrawn** and replaced by the corrected audit. A `1/3` orbit and an index-pole
  pushforward are *generic negative controls*, not members of the source.
- *Finite observers / proof access.* The FieldClass taxonomy (Simple / IdealHard / Fucked /
  Unresolved) and the fidelity criterion are exact; "toolkit-relative dark" is explicitly **not**
  an impossibility theorem.
- *Circle geometry and prime clocks.* Rational polygon enclosures of π, exact odd-prime first-birth
  counts `B(N)~6N/log N`, `p^k` valuation power laws, and minimal rational recurrences are exact
  finite mathematics.
- *Carry transport.* Exact carry repair, minimal future memory, and the regret/normality
  correspondence are proved at the stated finite/asymptotic scope.
- *Galois averaging and entropy.* The Poisson-kernel energy minimum, Haar convergence, and the
  subquadratic positive-regret / minimum-relative-regret conditions are proved **equivalent
  (asymptotically)** to normality for a base-`b` orbit. The *sufficient* direction was already
  valid; the equivalence is **not a new proof step**.

**Only finite-tested (exact at finite horizons, not a theorem for all `n`).**
Prime-window spectral projections, Galois second-moment interval bounds (e.g. selected `p=97`
amplitude `>1/20`), cyclotomic selected-source cancellation, and the collision/entropy receipts are
verified on explicit finite ranges with exact rational / interval arithmetic. Finite numerical
agreement is **not** a proof certificate.

**The exact open problem.** A *selected-source* collision/Fourier estimate: whether
`K_p(1)=p^{o(1)}` holds on all sufficiently large good primes at the prescribed scale `c=2/5`
(SCE-07) — a strict quantitative sufficient condition that would imply base-16 (hence base-2)
normality of π. **This is open and unverified.**

**Not proved, and not claimed.** π normality in any base, the *independence* of π-normality from
PA/ZFC, RH, and absolute/decimal normality. Observer reachability and geometric minimality supply
**no** elapsed-time, cost, probability, visit-frequency, or universal-liveness theorem — they detect
invented endpoints, they do not count digit frequencies. Lean kernel authority covers only the
explicitly formalized finite lemmas and does **not** propagate to a neighbouring unformalized theorem.

git clone https://github.com/femboy2112/SUCC.git

further probes (carrier, Tate, Pontryagin, field-trichotomy, Suzuki); each dedicated receipt is
re-checked in its own CI workflow.
