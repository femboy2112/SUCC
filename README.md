# Observational SUCC

**A research program on finite observation, causal history, semantic understanding, and mathematical knowledge.**

Observational SUCC studies how an observer embedded in the physical world builds, revises, and verifies mathematical structures from an indefinitely extensible history of finite observations. Its central distinctions are **observation vs. inference**, **record vs. semantic representation**, **finite proof vs. ordinal completion**, and **local correlations vs. global mathematical reports**.

This is an independent mathematical/physical research program. Quantum/Hilbert-space descriptions of the physical carrier are a motivating, empirically grounded modeling framework—not a claim that quantum theory proves arithmetic propositions. Theorems, executable models, physical hypotheses, and conjectures are tracked separately.

**Research status:** Foundational development. No claim of an implemented human-cognition theory, physical transfinite computation, proof of Gödel incompleteness within a formalized PA implementation, or proof of the Riemann hypothesis.

The original source work was developed in [femboy2112/FuckingRH](https://github.com/femboy2112/FuckingRH) and is being migrated into this repository with exact commit and file provenance. RH remains a downstream application, not the definition of Observational SUCC.

The complete standalone documentation, independently testable code, and explicit RH handoff protocol are under construction on a dedicated bootstrap branch. This seed README establishes the new repository without importing the original RH repository's main-branch history.

## Principles

- Preserve the original finite observation and its source journal even if a later connected/logarithmic representation has zero residual.
- Distinguish mathematical limit ordinals from physical elapsed time and new axioms from new observations.
- Treat the observer's physical Hilbert-space carrier as **modeling context**, not a proof of the positivity of an unrelated arithmetic form.
- Require source-derived mathematics, falsification controls, and explicit epistemic statuses for every substantial claim.
- Develop broadly useful observational/computational foundations here; send independently established RH-relevant results back to the originating research repository with proofs and controls.
