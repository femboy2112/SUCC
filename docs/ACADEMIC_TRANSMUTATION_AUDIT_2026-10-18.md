## Minimal object and strongest results

The mathematical object is a partial deterministic transition system on
complete source/history states, with declared reports, proof-task outputs and
cost receipts. Immutable provenance differs from changing configuration.
Encoding history or residual resources does not imply finite computability or
physical implementation. Verifier-output equality does not prove verifier
soundness; hypothetical reachability does not make an event actual.
The [paper](../research/2026-10-18/ACADEMIC_TRANSMUTATION_FOUNDATIONS.tex)
([PDF](../research/2026-10-18/ACADEMIC_TRANSMUTATION_FOUNDATIONS.pdf)) gives this
minimal specification and five full proofs.

## Rejected routes and additive corrections

Agreement only on commonly lawful words is nontransitive. Observed disabledness
needs a lawful refusal/menu instrument. Local reports can glue while private
midpoints disagree; fractional contextual laws need not have a discrete global
assignment. Uniform existential lifting does not furnish a causal resolver.

Bounded arithmetic authentication, even with the new family's global counting
and zero summaries, does not force `V_p S = S^p V_p`. Its explicit two-dilation
defect is `-1`. This does not refute genuine all-place/carry/source laws, nor
does the near-model preserve canonical completion or the full Weil distribution.
Global uniform dilation counting remains equivalent rigidity, not a weaker
genesis axiom. No genuinely weaker sufficient genesis system was established.

Two historical operator formulas require correction, recorded with originating
commits/blobs in the arithmetic dossier. MOG08's two-coordinate compression
has eigenvalues `1+1/p±1/sqrt(p)`, not full-operator endpoints
`(1±1/sqrt(p))^2`; positivity survives. MII06's finite-section functionals
are states, not traces on `B(l²)`: their values on `V_2*V_2` and `V_2V_2*`
are `1` and `floor(N/2)/N`, and cluster states remain nontracial. The separate
nonnormality conclusion survives. Historical rows are preserved.

A tighter Gamma remainder is insufficient for a better signed Schur lower
certificate: the retained action moves its residual center. At identical
parent-fitted coefficients, `J=24`, `eta=1/4`, `M=184`, all four tail error
bounds shrink but the worst lower diagonal worsens from approximately
`-0.0012647738` to `-0.0014750548`. Re-fitting also remains ambiguous. A
negative conservative bound is not a negative original-source witness.
Original `S_4` positivity remains **OPEN**. RH is **NOT PROVED**; first-window
positivity alone would not satisfy the every-radius criterion.

rational valuations/counting, operator compression/trace controls, integration
by parts and an independent original-source moment oracle, and Lean plus a
separate standard-library checker. Cross-lane reviews challenged domains and
quantifiers. These are different methods, not independent human referees or
independently authenticated arithmetic sources.

10 arithmetic, 8 Gamma and 13 independent-checker tests. Six current theorems

for every pair of blocks. Dossiers record external exhaustive and fresh

## Novelty and the next decisive theorem

The [theorem-by-theorem comparison](../research/2026-10-18/ACADEMIC_NOVELTY_COMPARISON.md)
credits Myhill–Nerode/coalgebra, predictive-state and MDP abstraction,
subset construction, descent/contextuality, Beurling and the classical
operator/trace and Weil–Tate–Connes–Suzuki interfaces. No strict extension of
general coalgebra or gluing theory has been established. The specialized
resource threshold, simultaneous arithmetic nonidentification family and
source-specific Gamma certificate are candidates for research contribution;
scholarly priority remains **UNVERIFIED**.

The highest-value next theorem bounds the original high inverse in the four
actual low-coupling directions: rigorously enclose `B* D^-1 B` with the complete
authentic source and valid domains tightly enough for a strictly positive
lower bound on `A_4-B*D^-1B`. Such a directional estimate could remove much
of the scalar `kappa^-1` penalty. It is an **OPEN TARGET**, not a result.
