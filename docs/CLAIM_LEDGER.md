# Research claim ledger

**Policy:** A theorem, an exact computation, a finite experiment, an interpretation, and an RH-equivalent conjecture are different evidence types. This ledger governs current project claims; immutable founding documents retain their historically correct original wording and provenance.

Use these labels:

- **CLASSICAL** — previously established mathematical theorem, attributed and cited.
- **EXTERNAL THEOREM** — a published result used as an explicit dependency; the stated local audit does not imply an independently rebuilt formal proof.
- **EXTERNAL CLAIM** — an unrefereed external result (possibly machine-generated) used as a dependency: cited and source-audited for hashes and statement faithfulness, but neither peer-reviewed nor independently re-verified (e.g. its Lean formalization was not rebuilt here).
- **PROVED LOCAL** — mathematically justified finite-scope or elementary claim with assumptions and reproduction.
- **TESTED MODEL** — a specific executable example behaves as implemented; not a theorem of the physical universe.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — proposed general structural or empirical claim, unproved.
- **UNVERIFIED** — required identification, noncircular global sign, or comprehensive physical validation not supplied.
- **REFUTED** — specified candidate inference fails against a proved or explicit counterexample.
