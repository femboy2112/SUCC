# Research claim ledger

**Policy:** A theorem, an exact computation, a finite experiment, an interpretation, and an RH-equivalent conjecture are different evidence types. This ledger governs current project claims; immutable founding documents retain their historically correct original wording and provenance.

Use these labels:

- **CLASSICAL** — previously established mathematical theorem, attributed and cited.
- **PROVED LOCAL** — mathematically justified finite-scope or elementary claim with assumptions and reproduction.
- **TESTED MODEL** — a specific executable example behaves as implemented; not a theorem of the physical universe.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — proposed general structural or empirical claim, unproved.
- **UNVERIFIED** — required identification, noncircular global sign, or comprehensive physical validation not supplied.
- **REFUTED** — specified candidate inference fails against a proved or explicit counterexample.

New knowledge-ladder controls: three 2-frame local discovery segments alternating between observers, each caught up by one remote informed step; correct source/origin replay; stale and forged packets; missing future hardware capability despite shared semantics; snapshot-only compression that omits a redundant observed fact and changes the next physical frame; third-party evidence laundering rejection; all 32 small binary source table transfers.

New absorbing/transmission controls: valid positive probe genesis with no contradiction; epistemically available but physically blocked probes; Kripke nonforcing of constructive excluded middle while classical LEM remains true; non-omega-continuous closure at omega+1; a self-enabling observation rejected by lawful replay; a shorter target-only witness that cannot certify causal acquisition; false bit-cardinality claims; and exact receiver-side-information channel coloring.

Additional observer-trap controls: truth-labeled source B versus false preferred A under an exhaustive but closed echo-only menu; an available-but-never-scheduled separator; a periodic schedule that finds the separator; two sources yielding identical endogenous histories; an externally imposed replay-checkable intervention; a valid revision A→B that traps again while C is true; and forged causal histories. The toy finite source and probe tables are mathematical counterexamples, not human-neuroscience experiments.

Additional ideal-frame controls: zero versus a nonzero flat smooth germ with identical all-order origin jets; off-origin evaluation that defeats that darkness; compatible finite analytic polynomial jets with divergent factorial omega series; premature zero-polynomial closure refuted by a delayed fourth derivative.

Some controls are *historical* and live in the archived research documents, while others are directly covered by current tests. A new RH hypothesis must not be described as source-specific unless it has faced the controls applicable to its exact domain.

An update to a theorem's status must link to an actual proof or regression evidence. Never promote a claim merely because more numerical observations agree.
