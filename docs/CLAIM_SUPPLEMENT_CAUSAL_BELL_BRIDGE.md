# Causal Bell bridge — entanglement shapes, local interaction supplies information

**Draft SUCC PR #604 (2026-11-12) continuation; original BBP source and `U_0=0` unchanged.**

The same two observers can be represented in two ways on a **restricted boundary language**:

1. **Internal/causal:** prepared Bell pair, distinct future-directed local paths, chosen local source-coupling phases `α`, `−β`, and present joint readout at rendezvous `M`.
2. **External/bond:** Bell tensor-network internal edge satisfying `(M⊗I)|Φ_d>=(I⊗Mᵀ)|Φ_d>`. This is a diagrammatic operator mirror and equality of boundary amplitudes, not a traversable wormhole or an instantaneous message.

The rational U(1) path increments + source-pin transport give a gauge-invariant relative phase at `M`. Local marginals are always `I/2`, but `<XX>=cos(α−β)` and `<XY>=sin(α−β)` are accessible at the joint meeting across separately prepared ensembles. A classical dephased state has the same marginals and no quadrature signal. Two identical wrong source pins can still agree, so Bell correlation is not individual path authentication.

The original four signed poles and `U_0=0` are retained in a separate exact source-label adapter; authentic `U_1 mod1=2/15` is represented in `Q(i)[Q/Z]` without claiming any new signed cancellation. **R0, π normality, Goldbach, completed Weil positivity, RH, a physical wormhole, and a source-derived Bell interaction remain OPEN.**
