## Additional source and scope repairs

**#554 averaged seed bound.** Its earlier uniform bound contains the fixed term 2^-80,
so that displayed bound alone does not tend to zero. The actual average over A<=n<2A
obeys the repaired bound

```math
\frac1A\sum_{n=A}^{2A-1}|U_n(s_A)|
\le\frac{2^{-80}}{15A}
+\frac{16^{-10}}{225A(2A+11)^2}
+\frac1{15(A+1)^2}\longrightarrow0.
```

The geometric sum of the scaled seed error, rather than its uniform maximum, pays this
claim. This is a control estimate, not the zero-seed signed theorem.

**#572 Fourier boundary convention.** Its real parity expansion is an L2/a.e. identity.
A symmetric Fourier series takes midpoint value 0 at jumps, irrespective of the chosen
half-open step convention. Irrational completed pi phases avoid those rational jumps;
the exact CRT parity proof does not evaluate the series there.

**#544 prediction floors.** The fixed-alphabet approximation theorem retains both its
normality and infinitely-many-selected-clock hypotheses. Large L1 approximation error
and failed frozen tables do not bound the signed Weyl mean from below. Literal carry 16
is not silently excluded from the source.

**#549 / #551 / #555 / #561.** Fibonacci interpolation error is a representation bound;
two-jet rigidity is only within the fixed positive-offset template; pointed recovery of
forcing is not digit genericity; growing-word entropy is a sufficient unproved arithmetic
premise, while certified digits are finite. Preserve #567's NONREPLICATION outcome.

**Evidence independence.** Proof audits, adversarial count oracles, source replays and Lean
kernel execution use different methods but share mathematical provenance and a model
family. They are not independent external refereeing. Reused A512 data and reused certified
prefixes are marked spent; no fresh holdout is claimed for their replay.
