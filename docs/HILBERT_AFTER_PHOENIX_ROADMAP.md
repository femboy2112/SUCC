## 4. General SUCC value outside RH

A finite successor itinerary can index a dense/reconstructed continuum while preserving dyadic filtration, Lebesgue area measure and L² pairings. It does **not** preserve all mathematical operations: global inverse locality fails; Sobolev H¹ transport fails; \|\|I-E_k\|\|=1 on the whole L² unit ball despite fixed-source strong L² convergence. Source-specific continuity and regularity remain necessary. These are reusable interface lessons for observer cognition, scientific visualization, sparse spatial tasks and data processing, not assertions about literal physical fractal structure.
