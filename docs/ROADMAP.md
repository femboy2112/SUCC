## Absorbing Hard SUCC and finite communication track

**Implemented finite research model:** replace a privileged preferred hypothesis with the full compatible-source version space, sound named predictions and exact least Horn closure. Evaluate every as-yet-available instrument, distinguish new epistemic probe construction from physical feasibility, and certify report consistency and lawful causal access. This is a deliberately bounded finite implementation, not a physical supertask.

**Implemented rigorous mathematical boundaries:** finite Horn closure and classical omega-continuous fixed points; monotone non-omega-continuous omega+1 counterexample; constructive Kripke nonforcing of excluded middle versus classical LEM; digital countability obstruction for exact arbitrary real-valued completions; separate minimum target-witness cardinality and full causal-history certification.

**Implemented exact optimized channel:** one-shot zero-error sender→receiver messages with receiver-specific side information. Factorization feasibility and confusability graph chromatic number yield exact minimum fixed-width bits for the declared finite problem. This is classical information theory, not a new general coding theorem or physical capacity estimate.

**Next missing tests/theorems:** a source- and resource-dependent physical feasibility relation (including whether failed physical attempts themselves convey an outcome), coinductive/recursive theory descriptions with effective finite certificates of omega fixed points, and a logically typed notion of semantic model revision whose contradictions do not make classical LEM false. Extend coding to source-faithful proof certificates, noisy observations and real transmission cost.

## Knowledge-aided SUCC and inter-observer ladders

**Implemented:** a shared source/knowledge basis, separately feasible physical probes for each observer, source-faithful provenance-labeled observation batches, an exact finite **K-SUCC** decoder that replays multiple lawful A frames but requires only one B epistemic update, and a three-rung A/B alternating 2+2+2 discovery ladder. Matching current epistemic placement does not imply matching future physical transitions.

**Adversarial demonstration:** minimizing the subset of transmitted facts that reproduces the absorbing snapshot can drop an already observed event, causing receiver and sender to perform different *next* physical SUCCs despite identical present conclusions. Full causal relay and future-equivalence must stay distinct. The finite implementation checks honest ordered probe activation and refuses stale basis/receiver, impossible probe results and third-party source attribution.

**Mathematical omega boundary:** the ladder forms a compatible directed history when every finite rung agrees. Omega-continuous closure may preserve the directed union, but finite per-rung messages do not imply finite total transmission: arbitrary infinite bitstreams give an exact no-go. No physical supertask, communication complexity speedup or RH theorem follows.

**Next research:** define a categorical/coalgebraic observer-process bisimulation and **minimal future-sufficient information statistic**; optimize the resulting continuation equivalence classes using the existing zero-error channel toolkit; prove sound concurrent rebase/join for observers with independent histories; identify colimit-preserving and non-preserving transport functors, and add provenance/physical resource budgets.

**Sources:** [knowledge-aided SUCC ladder](../research/2026-10-10/KNOWLEDGE_AIDED_SUCC_LADDER.md), `knowledge_ladder.py`, `test_knowledge_ladder.py`, claim ledger O67–O73.

## Relative knowledge transport and proof-bearing probe shapes

**Implemented finite proof obligations:** same field placement requires equal compatible source knowledge, empirical probe feasibility and usable future frontier, previously observed probe values, and reasoning/inference capabilities. The strongest relative code now anchors on this **operational position**, not an identical historical event transcript: A and B can have independently acquired the same knowledge and retain their different causal event origins. Verified exact relative path directions and only non-deducible outcome codes bring the receiver to the sender's position in one informed K-SUCC.

**Verified conservative language update:** versioned, checked XOR/AND/NOT definitions can change the shared probe/concept basis while preserving its old source semantics and physical devices. A delta referencing the new basis is rejected by an epistemically frozen old receiver until a valid upgrade is installed.

**Proof-shaped probe criterion:** a named probe value is deductible without empirical execution exactly when all sources compatible with the current evidence return the same result. The implementation creates/checks a contextual proof-shaped value certificate and distinguishes it from actual source event, mere experimental option, or independently observed source-dependent probe availability.

**Frozen-base no-go:** for n arbitrary unknown source bits relative to a fixed context, 2^n alternatives force some exact one-shot binary message to be at least n bits. Finite transmissions at every finite ladder rung do not imply a finite universal digital message for every limit source. Finite descriptions/proofs of particular infinite structures remain possible.

**Next core work:** minimal **future-bisimulation-sufficient** path/value codes with all shared-basis and provenance costs counted; source-independent verified translators for genuine new mathematical and physical probes; nontrivial path/groupoid coherence; dynamic physical feasibility and quantum backaction; effective finite certificates for limits of moving bases.

**Research:** [Relative knowledge SUCC and proof-shaped probes](../research/2026-10-11/RELATIVE_DELTA_PROOF_SHAPES_AND_FROZEN_BASE.md). Code: relative_delta.py; tests: test_relative_delta.py; ledger: O76–O83.

## Ideal observational-frame completion track (independent of RH)

- **First result implemented:** classify source-identification times as finite, omega-only, or persistently probe-dark, **relative to a specified source class, probe access policy, and target report**. Study three exact cases under an unchanged derivative-probe tower: bounded polynomials, analytic germs, smooth flat germs.
- Require two distinct omega certificates: **uniqueness/faithfulness** (injective observation map for the desired target) and **realizability/regularity** (omega data actually come from the stated source class). Divergent factorial Taylor coefficients falsify the naive implication from finite analytic consistency to analytic omega realization.
- Define probe-access morphisms and prove adaptive indistinguishability, distinguishing latent field distinctions from observable report distinctions. A semantic fixed point must not be counted as source identification.
- **Implemented finite observer-trap foundation:** exact source-faithful model-dependent probe menus, stationary-pair iff theorem, four access/scheduling/source-blind diagnostics, adaptive identical-history no-go, compatibility-union one-step separation theorem, and rational-revision-then-trap counterexample. Source: [MODEL_DEPENDENT_OBSERVER_TRAPS.md](../research/2026-10-10/MODEL_DEPENDENT_OBSERVER_TRAPS.md), observer_traps.py and 13 native test methods (including exhaustive small-world mutation sweeps).
- **Next, not yet proved:** extend the finite ideal-frame laboratory to *truth-conditioned physical feasibility* and resource-sensitive endogenous probe invention. Determine whether every alternative-model probe is actually performable, and whether exploration repairs the true accessibility gap rather than assuming an omniscient external world.
- Extend finite exact jets to model-dependent probe menus and typed semantic closure, then derive source-relative reconstruction bounds or explicit impossibility witnesses. Compare static derivative probes against off-origin additions and change-of-chart access.
- Research record: [ideal frame completion trichotomy](../research/2026-10-10/IDEAL_FRAME_COMPLETION_TRICHOTOMY.md), with exact tests `tests/test_ideal_frames.py`. All-order and analytic limit facts remain **mathematical proofs**, not simulated omega test passes.

## Completion criteria

Every stage needs: a precise theorem/model statement; source and assumptions; independently checkable proof or certified numeric bounds; adversarial controls; executable regression tests; and a ledger entry that distinguishes demonstrated behavior from general claims. “Our finite probes were all positive” is not a global sign theorem.
