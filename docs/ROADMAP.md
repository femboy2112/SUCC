# Research roadmap

**ObsSUCC is an independent program.** Milestones should produce reusable observation/proof or information-theoretic results even if no RH theorem follows. An item is complete only when its mathematical scope, negative controls, documentation and reproducibility are adequate.

## Stage 0: historical and software foundation — implemented in bootstrap PR

- Independently installable Python package with no RH-repo runtime dependency.
- Source SHA-backed archival provenance for the 55 migrated Python files and 17 verbatim research sources.
- Structured theory dossier, claim ledger, contributor/agent policies and explicit RH handoff protocol.
- Existing SUCC/ordinal/Gödel source work preserved with 20 regression suites.
- New exact 2-dimensional quantum instrument model and causal, append-only measurement history.
- Python 3.10 / 3.13 CI, provenance verifier, core mathematical/hostile test suites.

## Stage 1: physical-to-semantic operational models (priority)

**Objective:** rigorously specify a category of finite quantum measurement instruments with classical causal outcomes and a separate semantic update functor.

- Define information-preserving versus information-losing maps using operational probe families.
- **Implemented:** prove a finite exact total-variation data-processing inequality for declared stochastic semantic readouts and real-qubit outcome controls; extend to state-dependent/quantum channels next.
- Model instruments whose reduced states agree while historical records distinguish different causal orderings.
- Test both faithful histories and lossy reductions; falsify proposed commuting-square claims with noncommuting or incompatible instruments.
- No claim of a general empirical cognitive model until quantitatively testable measurements exist.

**Output:** exact theorem(s), counterexample(s), reusable APIs, independent tests; paper-like report with physical scope.

## Stage 2: rigorous logic and ordinal realization

- Formalize a full proof-predicate/representability story for syntax and diagonal substitution in a proof assistant (Lean, Coq or Isabelle), clearly separating the project’s finite syntax engine from established Gödel theorems.
- Distinguish successor of observation from successor of deductive strength; extend the finite support theorem to typed theory chains and explicit rule signatures.
- Model \(\omega\) directed colimits and \(\omega+1\) report operations without claiming a physical supertask.
- Compare plain oracle search, a sound \(\omega\)-rule, externally supplied reflection, and an ordinary finite universal proof.
- Add Kleene's recursion theorem, Rice's theorem and computable-analysis probes as **separate** theorems with their proper hypotheses.

## Ideal observational-frame completion track (independent of RH)

- **First result implemented:** classify source-identification times as finite, omega-only, or persistently probe-dark, **relative to a specified source class, probe access policy, and target report**. Study three exact cases under an unchanged derivative-probe tower: bounded polynomials, analytic germs, smooth flat germs.
- Require two distinct omega certificates: **uniqueness/faithfulness** (injective observation map for the desired target) and **realizability/regularity** (omega data actually come from the stated source class). Divergent factorial Taylor coefficients falsify the naive implication from finite analytic consistency to analytic omega realization.
- Define probe-access morphisms and prove adaptive indistinguishability, distinguishing latent field distinctions from observable report distinctions. A semantic fixed point must not be counted as source identification.
- **Implemented finite observer-trap foundation:** exact source-faithful model-dependent probe menus, stationary-pair iff theorem, four access/scheduling/source-blind diagnostics, adaptive identical-history no-go, compatibility-union one-step separation theorem, and rational-revision-then-trap counterexample. Source: [MODEL_DEPENDENT_OBSERVER_TRAPS.md](../research/2026-10-10/MODEL_DEPENDENT_OBSERVER_TRAPS.md), observer_traps.py and 13 native test methods (including exhaustive small-world mutation sweeps).
- **Next, not yet proved:** extend the finite ideal-frame laboratory to *truth-conditioned physical feasibility* and resource-sensitive endogenous probe invention. Determine whether every alternative-model probe is actually performable, and whether exploration repairs the true accessibility gap rather than assuming an omniscient external world.
- Extend finite exact jets to model-dependent probe menus and typed semantic closure, then derive source-relative reconstruction bounds or explicit impossibility witnesses. Compare static derivative probes against off-origin additions and change-of-chart access.
- Research record: [ideal frame completion trichotomy](../research/2026-10-10/IDEAL_FRAME_COMPLETION_TRICHOTOMY.md), with exact tests `tests/test_ideal_frames.py`. All-order and analytic limit facts remain **mathematical proofs**, not simulated omega test passes.

## Stage 3: second-order information geometry and topology

- For a source-derived two-time kernel, implement mixed finite rectangle increments that cancel single-time contributions and track all boundary terms.
- Derive covariance positivity from bona fide positive states only on the correct observable algebra.
- Prove counterexamples to unjustified passage of positive finite states to regular strong/continuity limits; characterize sufficient tightness and topology hypotheses.
- Seek source-exclusive second-order observables that can distinguish fake composite arithmetic and non-Euler models.

## Stage 4: RH handoff targets (application only)

- Complete a fully rigorous **Gamma/pole** interval oracle to pair with the certified finite-prime Suzuki wavefront.
- Establish the actual domain, weights, normalization and continuity for the completed two-time kernel.
- Test genuine Euler products against matched non-Euler Davenport–Heilbronn and artificial composite/phase/half-density controls.
- Attempt a **noncircular** positive-definite distribution construction identified with the complete Weil functional. It remains RH-equivalent; no claim is made that the earlier stages force it.
- When a genuine lemma survives independent controls, prepare a versioned handoff in handoffs/rh/ and link its source commit and proof status in femboy2112/FuckingRH.

## Completion criteria

Every stage needs: a precise theorem/model statement; source and assumptions; independently checkable proof or certified numeric bounds; adversarial controls; executable regression tests; and a ledger entry that distinguishes demonstrated behavior from general claims. “Our finite probes were all positive” is not a global sign theorem.
