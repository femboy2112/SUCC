## Integration decisions

- The older #576 claim of uniform Fourier truncation on an atomic
  sample is withdrawn by #579. Symmetric fixed-epoch convergence
  survives with a paid boundary error.
- #579's contraction selects a unique bounded arithmetic root. Capture
  safety, source-labelled clock simulation and a typed compiler have
  separate proof obligations. #576's three Lean files are carried as
  scoped declarations. This audit grants no new kernel verification.
- The finite phase bound in #582 underflowed to zero at a long horizon.
  The repaired helper returns a conservative exact rational bound.
  The finite unitary map and gate truth tables leave OpenQASM compilation
  and physical implementation unverified.
- Exact moment aliases and the endpoint operator analysis are paper
  results with independent calculations and finite controls. A positive
  quadratic form or a zeta Gibbs normalizer supplies no signed completed
  explicit formula.
- Keep the new oracle, inverse aliases and infinite operator notes under
  research. The oracle specializes to a declared fixed horizon and
  consumes substantial retained space. Package promotion needs a stable
  API and a caller with a reusable contract. The Scott/Cayley tools in
  the maintained package are part of the reviewed donor, subject to
  #579's scope corrections.
