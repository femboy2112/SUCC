## Actual mathematical gains

The complete primitive abc moment remains proved at paper level for `0<=t<2/3`, with every effective convergence exponent `delta<(2-3t)/5`. The true obstruction at `t>=1` survives review. The unrestricted interval `2/3<=t<1` remains open.

The new small-selected-divisor theorem proves, for fixed `0<=t<1`, real `X>=2`, `Q>=X²`, `B>=1`,

\[
T_t(X,Q;B)\le K_t B(1+\log B)X^{1+t}(X^2/Q)^{(1-t)/2},
\]

where the sum retains actual primitive incidences with `def>Q` and `min(d,e,f)<=B`, including their multiplicities and arbitrary prime-power depths. For `alpha=(1+t)/2`,

\[
C_t=\prod_p\left(1+\frac{p^{-(3-t)/2}}{1-p^{-(1-t)/2}}\right)<\infty,
\qquad K_t=3C_t^3/(1-\alpha).
\]

Choosing `B=max(1,X^(1-t)/(1+log X))` gives a genuine quadratic dyadic saving on this growing family. The proof uses a square/cubic-tail convolution majorant and a source-preserving cofactor injection. An infinite primitive Pythagorean family independently disproves a bounded fixed-cofactor fiber shortcut. Neither result controls the remaining three-large-divisor sector.

The independent conic route proves a restricted full source moment `<=23 X^(691/350)` at `t=5/7`. Its cofactor catalog and root-radical-excess cutoff are explicit. A separate sparse Pythagorean corollary has no prime-depth cutoff and gives a quadratic bound through `t=7/10` for fixed short parameter width. Its growing-width example is `<=2 X^(2467/1250)` at `t=17/25`. These are declared thin families, not the unrestricted moment theorem.

The new geometric machinery computes the congruence index within the actual saturated section lattice and the resulting covolume after rank loss. It proves `a(X-beta)_+^r<=A(X)<=a(X+beta)^r`, with an explicit basis-dependent boundary budget and uniform regime `r beta/X->0`. It also gives simplex laws with residue-dependent logarithmic corrections beyond the square-root scale and rational entropy bounds for linear dimension. Ambient index cannot be inherited by a section without calculation; an exact positive primitive rank-two family demonstrates the failure. The entropy asymptotic now explicitly requires `N->infinity`.

The valuation package determines every finite truncated-valuation sum cell, including leading-unit cancellation, through both character and recurrence derivations; terminal depth k means zero modulo p^k. It supplies exact CRT composition and justified infinite-prime tails. The local factors for joint primitivity, summand-only pairwise coprimality and total-inclusive pairwise coprimality differ: for three summands at p=2 they are 7/8, 1/2 and 1/8. These are local factors, not global integer densities.

For positive integer mode costs and fixed beta>0, the infinite-carrier package separates local finiteness (`w_j->infinity`), finite Gibbs partition/heat trace (`sum exp(-beta w_j)<infinity`), and finite mean energy. It proves cylinder, support and total-variation statements with exact tail bounds for explicit spectra. Escaping dyadic costs can have infinite trace; another trace-class spectrum has infinite expected energy. These are constructed analytic carriers, not identifications with an arithmetic signed Weil source.

The new exterior Lean project checked 11 declarations, including the effective modulus `Pq/gcd(q,g)` and the globally primitive full-product corollary for arbitrary finite dimension. Three false mutations were rejected. The observation remains `KERNEL_CHECKED_NOT_AUTHORITY_ADMITTED`; it does not formalize the height/Hadamard bound, rational rank theorem, point count or abc moment.

The secondary Fourier oracle certifies a finite pi-orbit sample:

\[
1/25<\left|32^{-1}\sum_{j<32}e^{2\pi i 2^j\pi}\right|^2<1/20.
\]

Polygon/Taylor arithmetic is cross-checked by Machin-source and nested-radical evaluation. This is finite calibration, not pi normality or uniform-horizon cancellation.
