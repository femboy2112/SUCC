# Academic Main VIII research frontier

## Established and precisely bounded

The primitive ordered positive abc-triple moment and Euler-product mean have
paper proofs for `0<=t<2/3`. The effective theorem gives every fixed exponent
`0<delta<(2-3t)/5`, with constants depending on `t,delta`. At `t=1` the
normalized quadratic moment diverges at least logarithmically; at `t>1` it
has a power lower bound. The interval `2/3<=t<1` is open.

The exterior modulus is the exact congruence-lattice index
`E=(product d_i)q/gcd(q,gcd_i d_i)`. If `E>X^m`, the source vectors have rank
at most `m-1`. There are explicit primitive rank-two families with unbounded
multiplicity: cardinality does not follow from rank alone.

The original signed-source inverse has a certified full finite moment-Gram
improvement, with independent rational integration checks. The documented
fixed-budget ceiling proves that this particular correction is insufficient.
It does not determine the genuine signed S4 sign or disprove positivity.

Polygon geometry supplies rigorous rational pi intervals. Transport uses the
actual interval width and exact input types. Haar measure on the path carrier
does not make every path generic and does not prove pi normality.

## The next decisive arithmetic obligation

For fixed `2/3<=t<1`, the low divisor-product sector `D<=X^2` already has a
quadratic upper bound. The missing estimate is the weighted aggregate over
occupied `D>X^2` strata. Each divisor stratum has at most one primitive slope,
but there may be many strata. The cofactor identity gives `uvw<X/4`; it
does not count the weighted solutions of `du+ev=fw`. All gcd restrictions,
source heights, powerful-divisor coefficients and multiplicities must remain.

The [preregistered research contract](high_divisor_hole_contract.md) separates
a genuine bound, a restricted-family theorem, a noncircular conditional input,
and a strategy counterexample. A restatement of this missing sum is not a
new theorem. Independent constructor and hostile analyses are reconciled only
after their initial derivations.

Other concrete obligations are: actual saturated-section lattice counts after
rank drop; dimension-corrected simplex asymptotics; valuation cancellation
laws retaining the total coordinate; and analytic tail conditions for infinite
weighted carriers. Local finiteness alone supplies no Gibbs or trace estimate.
