# The remaining walls, tested shortcuts and the next distinguishing lamps

This is SUCC's *reality-expectations ledger*: precisely what existing mathematics, certified source data and hostile controls do **not** license us to conclude. Its purpose is to preserve a steelman after falsifying a shortcut, not to declare the wider program impossible.

The recurring classification is **pathway gap** (no route), **probe gap** (rivals remain observationally identical), **access gap** (instrument/source unavailable), or an **actual demonstrated boundary**. A finite test is never universal proof. A claim may be true but unreachable with the current toolkit, without thereby being false.

## 1. Reconstructing a carrier is not proving its typicality

**Steelman preserved:** an embedded observer can prove global truths from internal lawful observations if the probe family, source laws and asymptotic transport are strong enough.

**Wall:** Yoneda reconstruction/fully faithful observation gives *identity up to a declared category/equivalence*. It does not supply a signed sum bound over unbounded time. A normal and a nonnormal deterministic path can have the same finite collection of observations at a finite horizon. **Current verdict:** PROBE GAP. See [pinbed proof](../../research/2026-11-07/fourier_yoneda_pinbed/THEOREMS.md), [current frontier](../PI_NORMALITY_FRONTIER.md).

**Lamp:** source-selected inequality for an actual future-conditioned Fourier/Haar pin, plus quantified moving-boundary and sample-size errors; coherent wrong seed must fail it.

## 2. Seed-ensemble Haar is not literal U0=0 Haar

**Steelman preserved:** the virtual field/space of counterfactual paths can organize which source histories are possible.

**Wall:** a Haar-distributed seed ensemble generates random-looking digit laws independently of whether the one selected zero seed is normal; point evaluation is not continuous in the relevant averaging norm without a strong cost. A same-forcing coherent irrational seed \(-\pi/16\) has completed constant orbit. The original source is unchanged, the selected outcome is different. A **fixed rational seed shift** preserves normality status (Wall's rational-addition invariance); the epoch-dependent rational p-integral shadows are finite-window counterexamples to seed-blind *uniform* estimates, not examples of a fixed forever-nonnormal rational seed.

**Lamp:** demonstrate a genuinely zero-seed-selective source theorem; explicitly test irrational coherent, nearby periodic and epoch-dependent rational shadows.

## 3. Even infinite local odd-prime Haar need not give real parity

**Steelman preserved:** finite prime fibers, Frobenius phases and CRT arithmetic may constrain the real orbit.

**Wall:** the real representative is chosen by an **ordered global CRT winding**; finite prime-local marginals alone cannot reconstruct its parity. Artificial even-only canonical-representative laws can look nearly Haar on every fixed odd-modulus marginal while global parity is identically even. The genuine source also retains inherited prime powers with zero incoming new injection and the 2-adic integer-lift clock.

**Lamp:** an all-epoch signed source-conditioned bound on the *complete* prime/real complementary sign, not a product of independent local references. See [all-prime carry](../../research/2026-11-08/all_prime_carry_boundary/THEOREMS.md), [parity](../../research/2026-11-09/pi_parity_pressure/PROOFS.md).

## 4. The full Haar wavelet energy is not the one parity mode

**Steelman preserved:** Haar details are signed successor contrasts; detecting them can reveal nonuniformity.

**Wall:** the full word collision energy has a sharp finite-sample floor. At B=16^k cells and N starts, E_k≥B/N−1 even for a normal source sampled at overly fine resolution. The discrete digit-group h=8 parity mode is one projection and is not the continuous Weyl h=8 circle character. The latter does not alone control the parity step function's infinite real Fourier ladder.

**Lamp:** prove decay of the *selected signed parity* projection or its full source-conditioned two-fiber complement; retain paid sampling/boundary terms. For full normality require the other digit characters and all finite word contexts.

## 5. Time differences telescope for a maximally nonnormal source

**Steelman preserved:** positive-minus-negative unit successor generates a real signed contrast when applied to **digit-label** child fibers.

**Wall:** averaging the chronological operator \(I-S_{\rm time}\) over a finite path gives only an endpoint divided by N. It tends to zero for *every bounded path*, including a constant nonnormal one. Repeated higher signed finite differences suppress some spectral frequencies automatically with their own transfer multiplier—this does not change the underlying distribution.

**Lamp:** declare whether the successor acts on time, digit labels, prime-exponent coordinates or morphisms. Compute its Fourier symbol and show the intended real signed arithmetic signal survives the operator. See [Tenacious D](../../research/2026-11-09/tenacious_d_haar/PROOFS.md).

## 6. Uniform marginal, mixing and local matrix spectra are not normality

**Wall:** the periodic 16-digit cycle has uniform one-digit counts but only sixteen two-digit transitions. An order-2 de Bruijn cycle is pair-Haar but periodic with non-Haar triple statistics. A strictly positive stationary Markov chain can have uniform one-digit marginals but nonuniform pair law. Non-Haar matrices can have the same eigenvalues as Haar through nilpotent Jordan modes; a preregistered inverse-frequency anomaly in pi's 131,072-digit source sample failed to replicate.

**Lamp:** preserve full conditional future-tail characters at a declared context depth, rather than a finite scalar spectrum or unconditioned first-digit histogram. Any all-depth upgrade pays an actual growing-depth arithmetic estimate.

## 7. Better approximation, narrower error, and historical source completeness do not mean decay

**Wall:** exact BBP source → completed π phase transport can cost O(A^-2), and an observer reconstruction can cost O(log A/A), while the signed mean of either source may still be order one. Exact source identification and its four-pole Gamma/Pascal/Laplace kernels do not imply genericity.

**Lamp:** bound the actual signed observable, not only difference between two representations. Every completed source identity and error estimate is a transport contract, not a source-selected dynamical mixing theorem.

## 8. Gamma, golden ratio, Pascal and π have multiple different natural constants

**Wall:** classical γ, weighted BBP finite-part γ, grouped-log γ_g, local Mellin log Γ(2) jet and q-Gamma/digamma γ_q have different definitions, domains and normalizations. The old Oily–Macaroni \(\phi^{-1}-\gamma\) is a declared mathematical reference. Positive moment or Gamma(2) limits are shared by changed-source countermodels. A matching convergence exponent or suggestive ratio is not an identity of mechanisms.

**Lamp:** require a typed exact map connecting a source-preserving scalar to the **selected sign** or a new demonstrable arithmetic inequality. Consult [typed Gamma](../MAIN_XXII_GAMMA_OBSERVABLES.md) before calling two objects “the same constant.”

## 10. Relational equivalence is relative, not “more real”

**Steelman preserved:** there is no a priori upper bound on a useful observer's feature/basis dimension; an apparent impossibility may be a missing probe.

**Wall:** at a fixed declared probe category and source, equal outputs define a legitimate equivalence class. Adding a new probe can split that class. It does not retroactively prove that the old class was uniquely identifying, and a complete observational category still needs sound source transport to support external conclusions.

**Lamp:** produce the new separating probe and preregister pass/fail; don't rename an untested representation “carrier equivalent.”

## 11. Positivity for finite operators does not automatically prove RH

**Wall:** positive theta/Pascal moments and finite Schur forms are compatible with explicit fake sources having off-critical zeros. If positive quadratic forms genuinely converge pointwise on a common domain, their limits remain nonnegative; a valid countermodel must have a domain, convergence or hidden-sector defect. The completed Weil explicit-formula source, Gamma/poles and all test functions are the load-bearing requirements.

**Lamp:** source-authenticated, all-test-function completed signed positivity with explicit control of finite-middle blocks and tails; fake Euler and off-critical fake completed functions must be rejected. See [RH program](../RH_HANDOFF.md), [theta](../../research/2026-11-08/theta_extruded_interference/PROOFS.md).

## 12. Quantum/cosmological metaphors need empirical physical signatures

**Steelman preserved:** source-labelled potential histories, actualized observers and phase-rich internal relational models resemble language used in quantum, information and gravitational physics. These are *legitimate sources for hypotheses*.

**Wall:** a finite complex Hilbert model can be an ordinary classical computational representation. It does not demonstrate literal quantum superposition, ER=EPR, black-hole interior information transfer, measurable vacuum energy, Casimir mechanics or Lorentz-compatible geodesics. In particular, local measurement of one entangled qubit cannot communicate or read an independent event-horizon crossing performed on its distant partner without classical-side correlations (no-signalling).

**Lamp:** explicitly define the Hamiltonian/source state, quantum channel and permitted operations; recover the no-signalling bounds and physical conservation laws, then predict a discriminating outcome that differs from a classical toy model. Do not retrospectively label a convenient algebra as new physics.
