## Provenance labels

- **USER–DIRECT:** exact words present in the chat transcript available to this research round; quotes are never attributed to a textbook or claim ledger. Chat content is not a peer-reviewed math source.
- **USER–RECONSTRUCTED:** paraphrase from retrieved prior SUCC conversations or historical repo dossiers. Not a verbatim quote; never fabricated as a dated sentence.
- **ASSISTANT–STEELMAN:** a mathematical interpretation we proposed while developing the user's question. Faithful to the idea, but **not** something the user necessarily explicitly asserted.
- **REPO–PROVED-PAPER / REPO–LOCAL-EXACT:** an explicitly stated derivation or exact finite algebra, subject to its original source conditions, published import and test status. Not novel priority or formal Lean proof unless the original artifact says so.
- **REPO–OBSERVED-FINITE:** finite computations, certified arithmetic receipts, tested effects. Not an all-epoch estimate.
- **REFUTED-SHORTCUT:** a precise *implication* killed by a counterexample. A failed implication does not refute the author's broader creative intuition or the open conjecture.
- **OPEN / UNVERIFIED:** load-bearing global hypothesis remains unpaid, or the proposed instrument/source map was not certified.
- **EXTERNAL:** independently linked music metadata, published mathematical antecedents or documentation, not derived from SUCC.

## Ground rules for narrative truth

Treat five objects as distinct: (1) *what the author imagined*; (2) *what an assistant actually proposed*; (3) *what exact code or paper derivations demonstrate*; (4) *what finite holdouts show*; (5) *what the mathematical carrier is presently known to do for all sufficiently large indices*. To say "the intuition was right" requires the precise scope: a correct mathematical instance is not confirmation of every metaphysical or physical reading of the same metaphor.

The principal example remains the original zero-seeded four-pole BBP recurrence. Its exact rational/analytic transport to the completed hexadecimal pi orbit is known; source-selected signed all-epoch cancellation is not. Original-zero **hexadecimal digit parity balance remains OPEN**, full **base-16 normality of pi remains OPEN**, and **RH remains OPEN**.
