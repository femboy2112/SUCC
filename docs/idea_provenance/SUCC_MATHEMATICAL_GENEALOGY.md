Dates indicate the conversation or repository research *episode* visible in historical summaries/dossiers; older classical mathematics retains its independent provenance. ## The recurrent proof-obligation pattern

Across all episodes, a useful representation often *does* exist. The repeated logical debt is exactly the move from **representation or source compatibility** to **selected signed asymptotic behavior**. The authentic BBP zero seed is not a randomly sampled seed and the real CRT winding is not a product of independent prime-local signs. Likewise completed zeta's sign problem is not the positive finite moment problem.

These are distinctions worth retaining, not cynical reasons to abandon the program. The next promising claim is one that predicts different outcomes for the genuine zero seed and the coherent source-matched wrong seed **and proves an inequality on the original arithmetic trajectory**, rather than merely fitting either trajectory afterward.
