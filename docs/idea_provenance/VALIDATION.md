## Limits of the scope

This is a broad **twenty-episode/representative-theme intellectual atlas**, not one new theorem per item of the complete Main XXII claim catalog. The actual mathematical claims remain in the code/proof source linked in the atlas, and user conversations available only by summaries cannot be promoted into a fully authenticated public oral-history transcript. A future user-approved chat export could supply per-message hashes, timestamps and direct quotations, making the historical bearings stronger.

The present package deliberately does **not** attach a copyrighted original sleeve image, change the original BBP source/Lean/provenance, alter frozen research files, merge main or assert that the song's mathematical characters encode a known mass-learning physics law.
