This is not just interpolation of numerical reports. A continuation must preserve the original source and target, causal compatibility, sufficient memory and the permitted comparison interface.

Write an observer as O=(M,Γ,Ψ,R;σ), using Principia Genesis T15–T38: actual transcript M, original-world/current-state coupling Γ, source-replayed self-phase Ψ, reason/reorientation model R, and passport σ. Distinguish (i) a mathematical jointly feasible family, (ii) a single lawful execution, (iii) a policy guaranteed for all compatible original worlds, and (iv) agreement on a target. None implies omniscient access to hidden worlds or logs.

For an original-source target g(w), knowledge at O requires g constant on its nonempty source fiber, and common knowledge of that target requires the same value at each observer, not merely pairwise intersection of fibers. ## I1. Finite coherent continuation (CLASSICAL / PROVED)

Let finite layers X_0,...,X_N contain admissible marked observer states. Let E_i⊆X_i×X_{i+1} be legal edges, preserving original source and the declared shared target. The retained state must suffice for future admissibility (or include all history that matters). Endpoints a∈X_0,b∈X_N are fixed. Define L_0={a}, L_{i+1}=E_i[L_i], R_N={b}, R_i=E_i^{-1}[R_{i+1}]. Then

G_i=L_i∩R_i

is precisely the set of all states at cut i on some complete chain. A complete chain exists iff G_i is nonempty at one (equivalently every) cut.

Proof: a chain supplies both prefix and suffix. Conversely, at an intersection concatenate the certified prefix and suffix; the common marked state is sufficient for valid composition. Forward/backward recurrences give constructive witnesses. For a non-layered finite graph use ordinary reachability in the full retained-state graph. A seam of vertices or edges is mandatory iff deleting it disconnects the designated endpoints (directed reachability if directed). This is a proof, not a distance analogy.

Crucible: fibers {a,b},{b,c},{a,c} have all pairwise intersections but no joint world. Pairwise agreement cannot be glued by forgetting world identity. If legal actions have multiple possible outputs, existential reachability is not a winning policy; replace it by Principia Genesis T26's AND/OR attractor on beliefs, treating all possible successors of an action universally. A path of selected favorable reports is not guaranteed reorientation.

## I2. Granularity and completion (CLASSICAL / PROVED)

Let P_m be finite sets of admissible complete chains at resolution m, with total restriction maps r_m:P_{m+1}→P_m preserving source, endpoint and agreement witnesses. There is a compatible infinite system iff every P_m is nonempty. Surjectivity is not required. Proof: in the product of finite discrete sets impose the closed adjacent compatibility equations. Every finite collection is satisfiable by choosing a chain at a sufficiently fine level and restricting; compactness gives the intersection. Alternatively use König's lemma on the finitely branching tree of finite compatible prefixes. This is completion, not infinite elapsed execution.

For a real-continuous realization, require more. Let X be a complete metric space of admissible states and let compatible dyadic chains γ_m:D_m→X, D_m={j/2^m}, satisfy a single modulus d(γ_m(s),γ_m(t))≤ω(|s-t|) with ω(h)→0 as h→0. Their union on dyadics is uniformly continuous and extends uniquely to [0,1] by completeness. If X is a closed subset of a complete ambient space, the extension stays admissible. Any additional edge/path predicate must separately have the requisite closure; a pointwise continuous curve is not a proof of executable continuous dynamics. Compactness can extract compatible uniform-modulus dyadic chains from nonempty closed compact finite-resolution families by the same finite-intersection argument (not for arbitrary noncompact families).

Controls: (a) finite-depth feasibility of the decreasing sets (m,∞)⊂R does not give a common witness; (b) discrete step approximations to a discontinuous threshold can agree on refinements but lack a shared modulus; (c) base interval connectedness does not lift to arbitrary observer fibers: X=[0,1]×{−1,+1} with endpoints in different sheets has no continuous connecting path despite every base point having a fiber. The obstruction is in the admissible total space, not the carrier coordinate alone.

## I3. Scalar seam, discrete seam, and transported orientation (CLASSICAL / PROVED)

Let γ be an admissible continuous path and let D:X→R be a continuous signed relative comparison after declared common-basis transport. If D(γ(0))<0<D(γ(1)), IVT forces D(γ(t))=0 for some t. In a finite chain with opposite endpoint signs some adjacent values straddle zero (or a vertex already is zero); if each edge changes D by at most ε, some vertex has |D|≤ε/2. Exact continuous null follows from compatible compact seam witnesses and continuity if ε→0; without these hypotheses only the discrete statement survives. A separating seam is precisely an endpoint cut in the admissible graph.

A gauge-aware version: let L→B be a real line bundle with continuous section v, and let a loop in B have orientation-reversing line transport, i.e. the pullback line bundle on the loop has a continuous interval frame e(t) with e(1)=−c e(0), c>0. Since v returns to the same fiber value, its scalar coordinates obey D(1)=−D(0)/c. Either an endpoint is zero or IVT forces an interior zero. The seam is independent of positive rescaling of the frame. This is the familiar nontrivial real line-bundle obstruction, not a claim that SUCC's actual BBP or CM source has such a holonomy. For arbitrary vector-valued transport, complex phase or nontrivial higher-rank bundle a scalar sign need not exist; rotating a unit vector avoids zero. Declare the orientation transport rather than comparing unmatched signs.

A D-null is only a null of that signed comparison. It does not say M, Γ, Ψ, hidden causal derivatives, source mass, or available interventions vanish. ## I4. Simultaneous nulls and minimal seam complexity (CLASSICAL / PROVED)

Let λ be finite positive nonatomic Borel measure on [0,1] assigning positive mass to every open subinterval. A complementary observer s:[0,1]→{±1} a.e. has finitely many distinct interior sign switches. Its reports are A_j=∫_{s=+1}u^j dλ and B_j=∫_{s=−1}u^j dλ, so A_j+B_j=∫u^j dλ identically. Its retained signed source measure is sλ; M_j=A_j−B_j=∫u^j s dλ. The measure and coordinate are fixed; these are mathematical source-cut observers, not automatically realizable destructive-probe sequences.

**Theorem.** For each n≥1 there is a unique (up to global orientation and λ-null changes) n-switch s_n with M_0=...=M_{n−1}=0. Fewer than n switches cannot do it. With the orientation positive at the right endpoint, M_n(s_n)>0.

Proof of necessity: if k switches r_i, P=±∏(u−r_i), sign P=s a.e. Then ∫Ps dλ=∫|P|dλ>0, yet vanishing moments through k imply zero. Proof of existence: minimize ∫|P|dλ over monic polynomials of degree n. This is coercive since integration of absolute value is a norm on the finite-dimensional polynomial space, by full support; hence a minimizer exists. Its zero set is finite and λ-null; dominated differentiation in any lower-degree coefficient gives ∫u^j sign(P)dλ=0 for 0≤j<n. Necessity forces exactly n sign-changing roots, all distinct and interior. Convexity shows two minimizers have equality in the triangle inequality almost everywhere; signs agree a.e. (else strict on an open interval), forcing identical simple roots and monic polynomials. Conversely any n-switch null sign gives a monic sign polynomial and the convex subgradient inequality certifies minimality. Finally ∫u^n s_n dλ=∫P_n s_n dλ=∫|P_n|dλ>0 since lower moments vanish.

This is the monomial/Chebyshev-system case of classical Hobby–Rice signed balancing, here with a self-contained L1 extremal proof, uniqueness and a complexity certificate. For general integrable f_1,...,f_n and nonatomic λ, the classical Hobby–Rice theorem uses an antipodal family: x∈S^n determines consecutive interval lengths x_i² and signs sign x_i; the n-vector of integrals is continuous and odd (zero-length intervals cause no jump by absolute continuity of the integrals). Borsuk–Ulam gives a simultaneous null with at most n switches after coalescing adjacent signs. Minimality/uniqueness require additional structure and do not follow for arbitrary f_i. Reference: Allan Pinkus, "A simple proof of the Hobby–Rice theorem", Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 60 (1976), 82–84, https://pinkus.net.technion.ac.il/files/2021/02/hr.pdf .

Crucible: D(t)=(t,t²−1) on [−2,2] has separate zeros of both coordinates but no simultaneous zero. Scalar IVT cannot supply simultaneous nulls; the antipodal family or minimization and its independent dimensions are essential. A single scalar null of a source can be satisfied by infinitely many distinct underlying fields. Source-agnostic claims of normality from signed partitions fail for all positive full-support measures and nonnormal endpoint constructions.

## I5. Exact finite granularity, fractional seam cells and convergence (CLASSICAL / PROVED)

For distinct ordered nodes x_i, positive weights w_i, i=1,...,N, N>n, put A_{ji}=w_i x_i^j for j=0,...,n−1 and c_i=w_i x_i^n. Maximize c·s subject to As=0, |s_i|≤1. The feasible cube-section is compact, contains a ball relative to ker A around zero, and c is not in the row span by Vandermonde rank. Hence the maximum is strictly positive. At an extreme optimum at most n entries can be strictly inside [−1,1]: otherwise there is a nonzero null direction supported on those entries and one can perturb in both signs. Thus finite carriers admit exact n-null observers if partial allocation of at most n cells is legal. A binary-only atomic carrier need not admit even one null (one positive atom). For any nonzero s solving As=0, after ignoring zero entries there must be at least n sign changes; otherwise a sign polynomial of degree<n gives ∑w_i P(x_i)s_i>0, contradiction. The rank bound is on independent moments, not their number of labels.

An additional constructive realization uses a minimizer of ∑w_i|P(x_i)| over monic degree-n polynomials. With N>n this is coercive. At a minimizer, the convex subgradient condition supplies s_i=sign(P(x_i)) at all nonzero evaluations and s_i∈[−1,1] at roots, with As=0. To see this directly, directional derivatives in a lower-degree direction Q have nonnegative one-sided slope ∑_{P_i≠0}w_i sign(P_i)Q(x_i)+∑_{P_i=0}w_i|Q(x_i)|; if no assignment at the zeros balances the lower coefficients, finite-dimensional separation of the compact convex set of possible subgradients produces a direction of negative slope. There are at most n root cells.

If positive atomic λ_h converge weakly to the full-support λ on [0,1], for each fixed n the monic minimizers P_{n,h} converge coefficientwise to P_n of I4 (provided each grid has >n nodes eventually). Proof: integrals of |P| converge uniformly on the unit coefficient sphere, since the sphere is compact, dependence on coefficients is uniformly Lipschitz with bounded mass, and a finite net reduces to weak convergence. Thus the atomic norms are uniformly coercive for large h. Monic minimizers have uniformly bounded coefficients by comparison with u^n. Every subsequential limit minimizes the limiting norm and is P_n by uniqueness. The associated fractional assignments agree with sign P_n on nodes outside arbitrarily small neighborhoods of its roots for sufficiently fine grids. Weak convergence and atomlessness make those neighborhoods' limiting mass arbitrarily small. Consequently s_hλ_h→s_nλ weakly as signed measures; this is source-faithful convergence of the declared quadrature, not a guarantee of a nested execution journal. This is a precise carrier-granularity repair of the scalar theorem.

## I6. Infinite simultaneous nulls force loss of binary orientation (CLASSICAL / PROVED)

The unique minimal n-switch s_n of I4 satisfies ∫f s_n dλ→0 for every continuous f: approximate f uniformly by a polynomial, whose moment is zero for sufficiently large n, and bound the error by λ([0,1]) times the uniform approximation error. Thus s_nλ converges weakly to the zero signed measure, while ‖s_n‖_{L1}=λ([0,1]) is constant. If a measurable binary observer s satisfied every moment null, Weierstrass density and uniqueness of finite signed measures would force sλ=0, contradicting |s|=1 almost everywhere. In particular no strong L1-convergent subsequence of s_n exists. Infinite resolution is not the same as an admissible binary completion: a relaxed fractional observer s=0 exists, but has lost the orientation distinction.

For a compact admissible space X and closed nested Z_n={x:D_0(x)=...=D_n(x)=0}, nonempty Z_n for all n imply a common null, by compactness. Here the binary space is not compact in the needed topology; weak compactification adds a nonbinary object. This is the precise missing hypothesis in the tempting quantifier swap ∀n∃O_n→∃O∀n. Track the retained orientation and do not call the relaxed zero a new binary observer.

## I7. General quantitative obstruction (CLASSICAL / PROVED)

Suppose λ has density at least c>0 on an interval of length L. For k≥1 switch points r_i, remove intervals of radius L/(4k) about the r_i from that interval. At least L/2 length remains, so ∫|∏(u−r_i)| dλ≥c L/2·(L/(4k))^k. Its coefficient l1 norm is ∏(1+r_i)≤2^k. Therefore

max_{0≤j≤k}|M_j(s)| ≥ (c L/2)(L/(8k))^k.

For k=0, |M_0|=λ([0,1]). Thus approximate finite-resolution nulls too small for their switch budget force more complexity, not automatic exact nulls. These conservative bounds are uniform over switch locations. The actual BBP consequence and exact two-place obstruction are in the research dossier.

## I8. Multiple completions — topology is a source contract (CLASSICAL / PROVED)

The real interval and Q_p have incompatible notions of connected path: each p-adic open ball is also closed; two distinct elements can be separated by such a ball. Thus every continuous map from a connected interval into Q_p is constant. Any path into a product of real and p-adic coordinates with all projections continuous is constant at each finite place. A useful multi-place observer continuation therefore must be built from explicit discrete transitions, local topologies and source-preserving comparison maps, not from an assumed ordinary real path through all places.
