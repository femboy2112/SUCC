## Intended minimal implementable interfaces

- **CarrierProtocol**: source_id, epoch, lawful states/actions, reset/preparation policy, observation outcomes, state-update kernel and admitted history.
- **ObserverProtocol**: available toolkit, horizon/resource budget, legal adaptive policies, effective probe vocabulary and output map.
- **Interpretation/forgetting arrow**: original source/basis, target observation, preserved invariants, forgotten fibers, transported propositions, proof/witness for factorization.
- **AuthorityReport**: syntactic proof, model validity, observational equivalence, compatible-model consequence, source realization and probe availability as SEPARATE fields. No combined numeric truth score.
- **EvidenceProvenance**: trace identity, independence family, source-map calibration, state-backaction effect, assumptions and unfinished obligations.

Use source-typed immutable records, fail-closed on basis/epoch mismatch, and make witness availability distinct from existence. Philosophical “I am I” is a user-origin **motivation annotation**, not a runtime truth oracle or psychology API.
