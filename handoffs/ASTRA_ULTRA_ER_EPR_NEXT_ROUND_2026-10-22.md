## Preferred theorem target: covariant arithmetic-weighted coding

Fix p=2 initially. Let G=GL2(F2)≅S3 act on the three genuine BT root
directions. Existing W_f with strictly positive nonuniform f is invariant
only under the stabilizer of one direction. This is an exact obstruction,
not a numerical artifact. Seek the weakest enlargement that has:

1. A stated arithmetic logical algebra and a nontrivial proper recovery region.
2. An explicit orientation/frame register or alternative tensor family.
3. Maps U_g on boundary and u_g on logical/frame degrees such that
   U_g W = W u_g, including group multiplication and adjoints.
4. Compatible radius refinements and overlap cocycle/descent equations.
5. A transported prime-mirror fidelity certificate and source/epoch checks.

Preregister the smallest falsifier: all six G actions, all logical matrix
units, every radius2 erasure region, then radius3 held-out frames. Averaging
encoded density matrices must not be mistaken for a coherent encoding.
Direct-summing orientation sectors is allowed if its cost and accessible
algebra are explicit; determine whether it adds genuine geometric information
or simply stores an externally selected frame.

Do not ask for full invariance while quietly permitting an untracked
permutation of the arithmetic source. If this target fails, prove the exact
no-go and classify the maximal surviving subgroup/algebra.

## Independent analytic target: mixed Gibbs entanglement

The state ρ_g=e^(−βH_g)/Z_g is proved nonproduct, but this is weaker than
entangled. Construct a finite partial-transpose compression whose negative
expectation transfers to the infinite state with a **proved trace-norm error**.
Derive that error before reading signs. Compare g and −g, β>1 close to1 and
large β, and prime rectangles versus nonrectangular integer prefixes.
Finite free prefixes are correlated, so their product defect is not a valid
interaction witness. A negative finite eigenvalue without a certified thermal
tail is numerical evidence only. If a separable regime exists, prove it.
