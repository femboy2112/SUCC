## Proved foundations to reuse

1. A target P can be known without identifying the entire source: require P
   constant on the actual observation fiber, not that the fiber be singleton.
2. In a finite declared deterministic model, the algebra generated by readout
   indicators, transition pullbacks and pointwise products equals all rational
   functions constant on all-word behavioral classes. Target membership is an
   exact source-bound row-span certificate. Joint products are indispensable.
3. This algebra describes counterfactual experiments. Destructive measurements
   can make a target algebraically identifiable but impossible to learn on one
   unresettable source. The planner preserves original/current state pairs.
4. Compact closed source fibers with a continuous target permit shrinking target
   uncertainty while arbitrary hidden tags survive. An effective rate requires
   a separate error modulus. Finite digit prefixes do not decide normality on
   the unrestricted infinite sequence carrier.
5. The scalar finite-part target can be constrained by a contrast of two unequal
   common-source readouts: (4 F(2t)-F(t))/3. Its full rational error certificate
   keeps both source tails, unlike informal cancellation of infinities.

## Mathematical interface for the pi workstreams

Specify the exact admissible source class before applying quotient language.
The authentic zero-initial four-pole BBP recurrence already specifies one
sequence; computational difficulty is not ambiguity between different exact
solutions. A weakened finite probe family can have counterfeits; mark that
weakened family explicitly and retain which global BBP assumption each fails.

For matrix purity, global rotation is a harmless observer gauge: the target is
invariant. Do not reconstruct that phase unnecessarily. Instead seek bounds on
purity or the remaining conductor-flat signed innovation which hold over the
whole genuinely admissible fiber of the chosen arithmetic constraints.

A useful completion witness consists of (source class, permitted operations,
current fiber, target functional, algebra/policy authority, target-error bound,
proved limit modulus, source-map adequacy). If an upper envelope converges to
zero, the desired estimate transfers without recovering every hidden state.
Merely agreeing with one true representative is not enough when the target
varies inside the fiber. Do not turn toolkit-relative ambiguity into a claim of
absolute undecidability or mathematical falsehood.
