**The one target.** Base-16 normality of π is equivalent (Weyl) to
$S_N(h)=\sum_{t\le N}e^{2\pi i h x_t}=o(N)$ for every fixed $h\ne0$, with $x_t$ the
certified base-16 orbit point of the authentic four-pole source. Every priority
below is a different arithmetic handle on *that* cancellation. Do not replace
$x_t$ by a surrogate; do not swap prime-depth / source-time / completion limits
without a theorem controlling the exchange.

**Priorities (attack order is yours; A and B are the shortest paths):**

- **A — selected harmonic/cyclotomic saving (#284).** Prove
  $\frac1N\sum_{k<K_N}w_{K_N,k}|W_{k,N}(h)|\to0$ for each fixed $h\ne0$, with an
  arithmetic estimate **strictly stronger than the trivial bound**, from the
  original four-pole forcing, selected initial state, signed carries, and
  controlled time/prime correlations. Not a restatement of the criterion.
- **B — prescribed real collision / trimmed entropy (#278, SCE-07).** Prove
  $K_p(1)=p^{o(1)}$ at $c=2/5$, or the source-authenticated subexponential trimmed
  real-cell collision excess, **with real-circle prime-window coverage** and the
  needed global transport. Dyadic residue collisions are a different quantity.
- **C — source-selected four-bit Walsh jet (#271).** A nontrivial estimate for
  the signed highest-order interaction $M_{\{1,2,3,4\}}$. Half-bit marginal
  control does not substitute (even-parity countermodel).
- **D — missing prime-window interaction (#277/#278/#279).** Power-saving for
  $\sum_{t\in\mathcal W_p}e^{2\pi i\,4h16^t/p}e^{2\pi i\,h w_t}$ with $w_t$ the
  **actual** complementary BBP source; then prove **separately** whether the
  prime-family estimate implies A or B.
- **E — alternative bases (#270/#276/#280).** Base-3 rigidity and rational-base
  $11/10$ as **separate** tracks; no unproved cross-base transfer; primes below 16
  not assumed sufficient.
- **F — RH (separate, #285/#286/#287).** Reuse the Euler/Li/Weil boundary
  accounting as infrastructure only. No finite Γ-jet authenticates ζ; no finite Li
  bound proves all-degree Li positivity; no positive graph/Hilbert kernel
  identifies the signed Weil form.
