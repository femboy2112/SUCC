## 1. The universal proto-path and a free translated carrier

In an additive group/module \(M\), choose the origin and a direction \(d\in M\). Define
\[
\boxed{S_d(x)=x+d,\qquad
0\xrightarrow{S_d}d\xrightarrow{S_d}2d\xrightarrow{S_d}3d\cdots .}
\]
The path has *one primitive SUCC step per arrow*, measured in declared d-units; if the ambient metric is the usual real metric, the physical length of that step would instead be \(|d|\).

**Proof:** \(S_d^n(0)=nd\) by induction, and \(S_dS_e=S_{d+e}\), \(S_0=\mathrm{id}\). Choosing \(d=1\) in \((\mathbb Z,+)\) recovers \(S(n)=n+1\).

A genuinely FORMAL instance lives in \(\mathbb Z[D]\), with the origin 0 and primitive one-step path \(0\to D\). Its first n addresses are \(nD\). Specialization \(D\mapsto u\in R\) induces a representation of this universal path in a particular carrier. In the implementation we need only the *rank-two \(\mathbb Z\)-submodule* \(\mathbb Z1+\mathbb ZD\), not the full polynomial ring or a presumed inverse of D.

**Critical negative control:** Specialize \(D=0\). Every successor address collapses to zero. A symbolic coefficient of D can remain meaningful *in the universal model* even though it can no longer be recovered from the specialized zero-valued carrier. Thus parameter specialization can destroy distinctions. Conversely, a nonzero d in a field may be invertible while a nonzero d in a residue ring may not be; never silently divide by d in the wrong coefficient ring.

The starting BASIS fixes the meanings of \(D\), \(S_D\) and origin; it still takes a separate initial-state coordinate to specify an arbitrarily translated walk from \(x_0\ne0\).

## 2. Unit gauge versus nonunit change in \(p\)-adic clocks

Let the finite p-adic approximation be \(C_{p,r}=\mathbb Z/p^r\mathbb Z\), with
\(S_d(x)=x+d\mod p^r\). For an INTEGER d, the orbit of the origin is the additive subgroup generated by d, of cardinality
\[
\boxed{T_{p,r}(d)=\frac{p^r}{\gcd(d,p^r)}}
\]
(with \(\gcd(0,p^r)=p^r\), hence \(T_{p,r}(0)=1\)). For \(d\ne0\), equivalently,
\[
T_{p,r}(d)=p^{\max(0,r-v_p(d))}.
\]

**Proof:** \(S_d^n(0)=nd\bmod p^r\). The first positive n satisfying \(p^r\mid nd\) is \(p^r/\gcd(d,p^r)\). Every orbit element is a residue multiple of d, and the cycle has no repeats before that n.

If \(p\nmid d\), then d is an element of the unit group \((\mathbb Z/p^r\mathbb Z)^\times\), the clock orbit is full, and the invertible coordinate map \(C_d(x)=dx\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{C_d\circ S_1=S_d\circ C_d.}
\]
Thus the new SUCC unit is genuinely a **coordinate/gauge choice** on the same entire clock. In the limit, multiplication by a fixed \(p\)-adic unit is an automorphism of \(\mathbb Z_p\).

If \(p\mid d\), the orbit is PROPER and \(C_d\) is not invertible modulo p^r. Example:
\[
p=5,\ r=2:\quad
T_{5,2}(1)=25,\qquad T_{5,2}(5)=5,\qquad T_{5,2}(25)=1.
\]
This is a physically meaningful *representation-access distinction within the model*, but it does not prove a physical FuckedField or inaccessible RH certificate.

**Important refinement:** For nonzero \(d\in\mathbb Z_p\) divisible by p, multiplication \(x\mapsto dx\) remains injective as a map on \(\mathbb Z_p\), but is not surjective. Finite reductions mod p^r may have nontrivial kernel. The obstruction is to a **full frame isomorphism**, not the claim that every nonunit step loses all source information.

## 3. Universal SUCC/FUCC affine defect and what “normalization” requires

With \(V_p(n)=pn\) (true prime p on the authentic Euler place),
\[
\boxed{V_p S_d=S_{pd}V_p.}
\]
On integer destination addresses,
\[
\boxed{S_dV_p(n)-V_pS_d(n)=d(1-p).}
\]

**Proof:** The left address is \(pn+d\); the right address is \(p(n+d)=pn+pd\). Their difference is \((1-p)d\). On a Hilbert space of kets, the operator commutator is **the vector**
\([S_d,V_p]|n\rangle=|pn+d\rangle-|pn+pd\rangle\), NOT the scalar \(d(1-p)\) times the original ket.

When \(d\) is invertible in the local coefficient ring, the normalized *address* difference is
\[
\boxed{d^{-1}(S_dV_p(n)-V_pS_d(n))=1-p.}
\]
Hence the factor \(1-p\) remains invariant under genuine **unit rescalings** of primitive proto-SUCC. In \(\mathbb Z[D]\), one may also read the coefficient of the formal symbol D in \(D(1-p)\) without claiming D is invertible. **After specializing D=0**, the evaluated discrepancy becomes 0 and the coefficient cannot be inferred from the specialized reading alone.

At \(p=5,d=5\) the raw address difference is \(-20\equiv0\bmod5\), whereas the normalized unit-\(d=1\) defect is \(1-5\equiv1\bmod5\). Division by d is illegal inside this clock. This is an exact negative control for unqualified “normalize by a changing unit.”

recovering \((p-1)p^k\) as the normalized coefficient in d. **The identity is true for composite multipliers too**, so this affine algebra cannot pick authentic Euler primes without an independent prime-locality condition.

## 4. One level higher: the successor acts on its step

Let \(x_n\) be the current position, \(d_n=\Delta x_n\) its current one-step increment, and \(a=\Delta d_n=\Delta^2x_n\) a second-level unit (constant). Define
\[
\boxed{T(x,d,a)=(x+d,\ d+a,\ a).}

\[

\left(
x_0+nd_0+\binom n2a,\quad d_0+na,\quad a

**Proof:** Either induction using Pascal's identity

### 4b. A necessary-and-sufficient theorem for stability of the changing step unit

For a fixed prime p and a linearly evolving step
\[
d_n=d_0+n a,\quad n\ge0,\quad d_0,a\in\mathbb Z,
\]
all SUCC steps remain **unit changes of frame on the full \(p\)-adic clock**, at EVERY n and EVERY precision \(p^r\), **if and only if**
\[
\boxed{p\nmid d_0\quad\text{and}\quad p\mid a.}
\]

**Proof:** A number in \(\mathbb Z_p\) is a unit iff it is nonzero mod p. When \(p\mid a\), the residue \(d_n\bmod p=d_0\bmod p\) is constant; thus initial unit status persists at all n. Conversely, if \(p\mid d_0\), the starting frame is already invalid. If \(p\nmid d_0\) and \(p\nmid a\), the residue class \(a\bmod p\) is invertible and the congruence \(d_0+na\equiv0\bmod p\) has the unique solution
\[
\boxed{n_{\rm first}\equiv-d_0a^{-1}\pmod p,\quad
1\le n_{\rm first}\le p-1.}
\]
At that *finite* step the local unit becomes divisible by p; the claim of a full-clock gauge then fails at **all** levels \(p^r\). The underlying integer successor \(x_{n+1}=x_n+d_n\) remains perfectly well-defined. No physical unobservability is deduced.

This yields an exact classification:
- \((d_0,a)=(2,5)\), p=5: all d_n≡2 mod5, so every instantaneous S_{d_n} is invertibly conjugate to S_1 modulo every \(5^r\).
- \((d_0,a)=(1,4)\), p=5: \(n_{\rm first}=1\); the first update changes d from 1 to 5 and invalidates the full-clock conjugacy.
- \(p\mid d_0\): no unit-gauge interpretation holds even initially.

The implementation verifies the theorem by direct modular classification and brute-source tests for **1,125** combinations of primes p=2,3,5,7,11 and \(d_0,a\in[-7,7]\), including correct first invalid indices, but the universal all-n conclusion follows from the algebraic proof, NOT from finite enumeration.

**Scope restriction:** this iff applies to a first derivative that changes by a CONSTANT higher SUCC a. If a_n itself evolves, the unit-stability condition must be proved against that higher-order trajectory; one cannot automatically reuse the affine criterion.
