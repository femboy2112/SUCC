## 0. Steelman and two actual objects

The maintainer suggests: (a) with SUCC running along an additive path, FUCC should structurally recognize prime divisibility, so prime sieves are natural local source filters; (b) verification requires actual categorical commuting paths and loops, not just plausible endpoint agreement; and (c) replace the two-person alternating ladder with two **climbing lanes on every tier**, where observers discover new evidence cooperatively on their ascent, send certified histories **diagonally downward**, and a base observer E can issue one final **knowledge-aided macro-SUCC** when enough evidence is present.

We adopt *two* precise finite structures, not one invented universal functor:

1. **Arithmetic path groupoid:** actual Gaussian integer source, true split-prime FUCC factors, quotient SUCC graph whose arrows retain integer displacement, a voltage 1-cocycle and its zero-holonomy certificate. Prime refinement is a source-preserving functor between typed path categories.
2. **Evidence network:** a fixed finite source class, nondestructive source observations at physically distinct nodes, time-ordered authenticated-*by-hypothesis* records, authorized directed network edges, provenance-preserving record transport, and a base E that constructs a source-target certificate from the finite joint fiber.

The common interface is **typed path/provenance/certificate transport**, not a claim that the arithmetic sieve's escape dynamics is itself a physical observer. Making one operationally faithful functor between these worlds is the *next* theorem obligation.

## 1. Gaussian prime FUCC as actual arithmetic, not a guess from observations (H1)

For `R=Z[i]`, FUCC multiplication is `V_z(w)=zw`. For any nonzero nonunit z, the following are equivalent by the Euclidean-domain property of R:

- z is irreducible / Gaussian prime;
- z cannot be factored as two nonunit FUCC generators;
- the ideal `(z)` is prime;
- `R/(z)` is a field.

For off-axis `z=a+bi` with both a and b nonzero, this is equivalent to `a²+b²` being a rational prime. For an axial z, it is equivalent to the nonzero coordinate's absolute value being an ordinary prime `3 mod 4`. In particular 3 on the axis is Gaussian prime even though its norm is 9. Source values 3+4i and 5 on an axis are *not* Gaussian prime. The new `gaussian_prime` and `fucc_path_observations` independently exercise both classes and retained sieve exceptions.

The SUCC/FUCC covariance identity

\[
 V_\pi S_d=S_{\pi d}V_\pi
\]

is exact, including for **composite** multipliers: the identity alone does *not* certify prime authenticity. The norm/field test is a separate source gate. The sieve `A(P)=R\setminus\bigcup_{\pi\mid p\in P}\mathrm{im}(V_\pi)` can contain infinitely many composite Gaussian integers. "Every surviving lattice point is Gaussian prime" is FALSE; the finite Gaussian-prime exceptions when adding source sieves remain necessary.

## 2. Source-preserving loops and holonomy functor (H2)

Fix a finite product `Q` of distinct rational split primes and an integer **squared** SUCC step bound `K>=1`. Let `Γ(P,K)` be the finite directed multigraph of nonzero-sieve residues mod Q; for every legal actual step `d∈Z²` with `0<||d||²<=K` keep the dart `r --d--> r+d mod Q`. **Do not discard quotient self-loops or different dart labels with identical endpoints.**

The free path groupoid `Π₁Γ` is generated by directed darts with formal inverses and reduction of immediate inverse pairs. The voltage functor is

\[
 \Delta: \Pi_1\Gamma\longrightarrow B\mathbb Z^2,
 \quad \Delta(d_1\cdots d_n)=\sum_j d_j.
\]

For a closed observed walk γ the value Δγ can be **nonzero**, indicating that the lifted source path escaped to a translated copy of the same observed residue. Let `H(P,K)` be the subgroup of `Z²` generated by all closed voltages in **all** quotient connected components. Then

\[
 \boxed{H(P,K)=0 \iff \text{every lifted component of }A(P)\text{ is finite}.}\tag{H2}
\]

**Proof:** A spanning tree in each finite quotient component chooses an integer lift potential g. Every nontree edge contributes `(g(r)+d)-g(r')`, a closed-word voltage. If all vanish, every dart satisfies `g(r')-g(r)=d`; each lifted component is a finite translate of the finite quotient component. If any is nonzero, translating the corresponding closed path repeatedly yields an infinite connected component. This is classical voltage-covering-graph mathematics, *not* new mathematical priority (see Gross–Tucker, *Topological Graph Theory*, 1987, and the prior SUCC P1 proof).

**New exact instrument:** `gaussian_holonomy.py` computes *all* cycle voltages in a finite quotient, reduces their integer span to a rank-0/1/2 Hermite basis, and returns an explicit nonzero closed-word witness whenever the subgroup is nonzero. An independently implemented pairwise-determinant/gcd oracle checks the rank-two subgroup index on 280 fresh small random generating sets. The square-step arithmetic inputs and both true-prime factors are independently validated. This does not create a successful modulus for all K.

## 3. Prime refinement as a commuting square (H3)

If `P⊆P'`, then the period `Q|Q'`, every fine surviving residue reduces to a coarse surviving residue, and every lawful dart keeps the **same source displacement**. The true source diagram commutes:

\[
 \begin{array}{ccc}
 \Gamma_{P',K}&\xrightarrow{S_d}&\Gamma_{P',K}\\
 \downarrow\rho&&\downarrow\rho\\
 \Gamma_{P,K}&\xrightarrow{S_d}&\Gamma_{P,K}.
 \end{array}
\]

Thus coarse projection induces a functor on lawful graph paths, with `Δ_{P'}=Δ_P ∘ ρ` on arrows and loops. Hence

\[
 \boxed{H(P',K)\subseteq H(P,K).}\tag{H3}
\]

**Proof:** each fine closed path projects to a coarse closed path with exactly the same integer displacement. The subgroup of possible fine displacements is therefore contained in the coarse subgroup. For the zero-holonomy certificate this recovers monotone proof transport; for nonzero holonomy the earlier escape word need not survive refinement.

This is a true source/representation commuting structure, not yet a proof of the existence of an all-K fine sieve with zero holonomy.

## 4. A finite-template FUCC annihilation theorem (H4)

Let γ be a finite legal **closed quotient walk** in `Γ(P,K)` with integer displacement `h=Δγ ≠0`, and choose a new genuine Gaussian split-prime factor π of norm p such that `π∤h`. Identify `R/(π)` with `F_p`, and write its reduction `φπ`. Since `φπ(h)≠0`,

\[
 \{\phi_\pi(z+jh): j=0,\ldots,p-1\}=\mathbb F_p.
\]

There is exactly one `j0` in `[0,p-1]` with `φπ(z+j0h)=0`, computed as

\[
 \boxed{j_0\equiv-\phi_\pi(z)\,\phi_\pi(h)^{-1}\pmod p.}\tag{H4}
\]

Thus the `j0`-th translated source starting point is forbidden in the new sieve; **the identical period motif cannot repeat p times**. If `j0>=1` then its `j0`-th complete traversal already has a forbidden endpoint, so no more than `j0-1` full traversals finish without entering the new forbidden class. An even earlier intermediate vertex may fail. This is a *single-template* obstruction, not a bound on all paths.

For any **finite collection** of nonzero-holonomy quotient words, one can choose a rational split prime `p` larger than all their nonzero displacement norms. Neither Gaussian factor of p divides any displacement; the same p simultaneously eliminates infinite repetition of **each listed word**. Existence of arbitrarily large primes `1 mod 4` is classical, not supplied by FUCC formal syntax. Combining finite witnesses is not identical to eliminating all future refined paths.

**Actual control:** for the seven-unit-step old-sieve walk

`i -> 1+i -> 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 -> 4+i -> 5+i`,

we have `P={5}`, `h=5`. A new factor `2+3i` of norm 13 gives `j0=1`: `5+i` is already forbidden at the first translated endpoint. A factor `1+4i` of norm 17 gives `j0=6`: it kills the old repeated motif later. Yet the **new** full periodic graph for `P={5,17}` still has rank-two escape holonomy. That is a decisive negative control against the claim "killing the finite old list produces a globally confining sieve."

**Finite-to-limit counterexample:** on the unrestricted lattice, for period `Q_n=2^n`, the quotient holonomy groups are `2^n Z²`, each nonzero, while their mathematical intersection is zero. No finite stage has zero holonomy. Therefore `∩H_n=0 ⇒ ∃n H_n=0` is **REFUTED**; a zero inverse-limit shadow does not manufacture a finite proof certificate. ## 6. The diagonal two-lane many-observer climbing tower

Fix a finite source set `X`, a source ID and basis version, nondestructive probe tables `p_j:X→Y_j`, designated physical probe owners, and each probe's causally required prior probe names. Build two climbers A_l, B_l at every tier `l=0,1,...,L-1` plus a base receiver E. Each upper climber must receive both lower-tier observations before making its own licensed physical observation. From the top, A_l transmits evidence to B_(l-1) and B_l to A_(l-1); base-level A_0/B_0 send to E. Every packet carries original event IDs; no relay becomes the **origin** of a forwarded observation. Upward prerequisite messages and downward diagonal messages are different typed edges. Their network can contain directed cycles but actual send/observe events remain time-ordered and finite.

This is deliberately **not** a general protocol for destructive actions, shared changing carriers, physical source authentication, noisy communication, or asynchronous Byzantine consensus. The source tables are declared; the simulator alone uses the hidden source index to perform physical probes. The receiver checks delivered records against the declared source model but does not read the simulator's hidden source index.

## 7. Evidence joins as commuting categorical squares (T1)

For a fixed original source class X, every verified event e has the source fiber

\[
 K(e)=\{x\in X:p_e(x)=v_e\}.
\]

For a finite compatible evidence collection J put `K(J)=∩_{e∈J}K(e)`. For any two collections U,V,

\[
 \boxed{K(U\cup V)=K(U)\cap K(V).}\tag{T1}
\]

Set union and intersection are associative, commutative and idempotent. Thus **two independent deliveries of already licensed nondestructive original-source evidence** yield a commuting square from current evidence to the joined fiber, regardless of delivery order; including the same event twice adds no new independent fact. This is a contravariant map from the finite event-inclusion poset into source-version spaces. It is *not*, merely by naming it, a sheaf-descent theorem over arbitrary physically changing carriers.

A physical event can have prerequisites. The necessary stronger notion is a **causally down-closed evidence set**: for every delivered event, its original prereq event receipts must themselves be delivered. This condition is independent of its semantic usefulness for the target.

## 8. A precise one-macro-SUCC proof-descent theorem (T2)

Let D be a finite collection delivered to E over authenticated-*by-premise* nondestructive original-source events, all using the **same** source/basis table. Assume every original probe in D was physically executable at its recorded predecessor state and D contains all its recursively required ancestors. Let t:X→Y be the declared fixed original-source target. Then a sound E decision from D alone exists **iff**

\[
 \boxed{|t(K(D))|=1,}\tag{T2}
\]

with nonempty K(D). This is the exact factorization criterion.

**Necessity:** if two sources x,y give all the same delivered observation outcomes but different target values, any deterministic receiver gives the same answer on both and must be wrong on at least one. **Sufficiency:** enumerate the compatible fiber, check that t is constant, and return its unique value; all source/probe/causal receipts have been checked. The source-truth guarantee is conditional on the declared premises (the issuance log is not a physical signature).

The receiver may **stage and accumulate** any finite number of authenticated packets before calling this procedure exactly once. A single receiver SUCC is one *epistemic macro-update*, not one CPU instruction or a physically instantaneous transmission/reasoning process. The already performed physical measurements, multi-hop relay steps, proof verification, and source table do not disappear.

## 9. Semantic support and causal support diverge linearly (T3)

Let `X={0,1}^{2L}` with no cross-bit source restrictions, and at tier l put `A_l` on bit `b_(2l)` and `B_l` on bit `b_(2l+1)`, with both tier-l readings physically requiring both tier-(l-1) readings for `l>=1`. E's goal is the XOR of the two top bits

\[
 t(x)=b_{2L-2}\oplus b_{2L-1}.
\]

Then for every actual original x,

\[
 \boxed{\min\#\mathrm{semantic\ evidence}=2,
 \qquad \min\#\mathrm{causal\ event\ ancestors}=2L.}\tag{T3}
\]

**Semantic proof:** observing both top bits fixes XOR. Either bit alone is insufficient: change the missing independent bit while preserving every available top reading. **Causal proof:** physically executing either top-tier measurement requires the two observations at tier L-2, which recursively require both at every lower tier. Induct downward: both top receipts have exactly 2L distinct ancestral physical observations including themselves. The ratio of minimal causal support to semantic support is **L**, arbitrarily large in the finite family; this does **not** imply a physically completed infinite tower or a free universal proof.

**Exact positive control:** at L=3, actual source bits `101101`, six authenticated mathematical-model observations, fourteen ordinary transmissions and a single E epistemic commit certify target parity 1. The semantic-minimal source witnesses are `p4,p5` (two); their honest causal down-closure is `p0,...,p5` (six). A run which stages *only* p4 and p5 already has semantically determined target 1, but E explicitly REFUSES the final source-justified certificate until the missing ancestor receipts arrive. All 64 possible six-bit source states have been independently replayed and certified under the same policies.

## 10. Communication cut and future-indistinguishability (T4)

For the fixed two-column network, the top node A_(L-1) has exactly one downward outgoing edge, to B_(L-2), and B_(L-1) only to A_(L-2). Delete either top downward delivery in an execution and send every other licensed report. The corresponding top bit cannot reach E. Since that bit is independent in X and XOR depends on it, the delivered history is compatible with two sources having the same readings but opposite target values. By T2 **no sound E decision** is possible. Restoring the missing licensed edge and transmitting its authentic record repairs the proof without giving E the private source.

This is a finite *information cut*, not a zero-error bit-optimal channel theorem. The implementation reports physical observation counts, actual network sends, and forwarded event references. These are not all equal to payload bits, elapsed time or minimum bandwidth.

## 11. Why the evidence square fails for destructive source updates (T5)

On one original two-bit source `(a,b)` let A report the current a and reset b to zero, and B report current b and reset a. Starting from (1,1):

- Path AB yields A=1, B=0, final carrier (0,0).
- Path BA yields B=1, A=0, final carrier (0,0).

The endpoint agrees, but the original-source report tables do not. Merging AB and BA as if both were simultaneous nondestructive readings from one original carrier is *invalid*. The diagram cannot commute as an evidence-collection square without a separately proven same-source and action-commutation (or independent-reprepared-carrier) contract. This reproduces the repository's older noncommuting-probe obstruction in the new tower adapter, and it is a decisive hostile test of the extension beyond its current domain.

## 12. The infinity, arithmetic, and authentication firewalls (T6)

For any finite level L, the model can complete as above. A growing tower whose source has independent new bits at unbounded heights cannot have a single fixed-length message that determines all bits or all evolving top-tier targets: change an unobserved higher bit after the finite communication horizon. This is the classical finite information support argument. A formal omega union of compatible receipts is not a physically executed SUCC. The one-E-update claim concerns a fixed finite goal under fixed model assumptions.

The named source/basis IDs, replayable event receipts and mathematically complete finite model allow *internal* consistency verification, not external attestation of observations. A separate hostile control rewrites a declared trusted issuance record's value while preserving its labels, and E can be made to certify the wrong original source. **A commuting loop alone never manufactures source truth.** Independent physical attestation, proof-carrying source semantics, or sound mathematical axioms must establish the premises.

## 14. Actual arithmetic-to-tower bridge: six-shard voltage proof descent (T7)

This is the first **concrete adapter between the two constructions**, instead of leaving the connection metaphorical. For a **fixed declared Gaussian sieve**, the arithmetic source provides a finite residue graph `Γ(P,K)`. A separately computed candidate potential `g` assigns an actual integer lift to every surviving residue. Partition the **entire source vertex set** into disjoint pieces `V_i`, one for each climbing observer. Assign to each observer all its outgoing source darts `(r,d,r')` with `r∈V_i`. Its shard verifier checks both `g(r) ≡ r (mod Q)` and

\[
 \forall r\in V_i,\;\forall (r\xrightarrow d r'):\qquad g(r')-g(r)=d.
\tag{T7-local}
\]

**Distributed arithmetic gluing theorem:** if (1) the verified shards cover every surviving residue **once**, (2) they all refer to exactly the same true-prime sieve, period, step alphabet and candidate potential, (3) each verifies **all** outgoing darts of its assigned residue set, and (4) E receives all shards through lawful, causal, provenance-preserving relays, then E can independently recheck their union in one **epistemic acceptance/update operation**. By complete edge coverage, `δg=d` on every dart. H2 then implies `H(P,K)=0`, so **every lifted connected component is finite**. Conversely, a nonzero closed voltage implies no candidate g can pass every local predicate, and any complete sound shard-cover must reject. Proof: disjoint union of all local outgoing-dart obligations is exactly the set of all quotient darts; every equation is checked. The one-acceptance event does not erase the earlier distributed work or E's own finite verification.

The implementation `voltage_tower.py` reuses the same physically typed A/B climbing and downward diagonal link structure as `diagonal_tower.py`, but its actual local "observations" are **exact mathematical source-edge checks**, not simulated bit measurements. Each higher climber is enabled only after receiving both lower-tier proof shards; every forwarded shard retains its original checker. E independently verifies the complete issuance/relay event chronology, the literal source signature, all candidate integer potentials, all shards' full outgoing edge relations, disjoint/full vertex coverage, and an independent whole-quotient holonomy computation. Identity/hash strings are coherence checks, not physical source authentication.

**Calibrated run:** `P={5,13}`, `K=1`, Q=65: 2,304 surviving quotient vertices partitioned between six A/B climbers; **4,356 directed source-edge equations** checked; fourteen authorized network relay events; E accepts **one** global mathematical proof with exact **580-vertex maximum component size**, independently reconstructed. A purported proof for the escaping `P={5,17}` source is rejected before shard production because its potential constructor encounters nonzero holonomy. Rewriting one potential, falsifying a shard's edge count, omitting a source region, forging E's possession of transmitted shards or withholding a diagonal top relay causes **rejection**, and repairing a truthful dropped relay restores the complete proof. Both ascending and descending probe dependencies are explicitly replayed.

**Critical limitation:** the producer constructs the candidate global potential *before* distributing its checks; it already uses a finite global quotient search. Thus this is a proof-splitting/distributed verification theorem and a real SUCC cross-module instantiation, **not** discovery of a confining sieve for arbitrary step bound. It cannot lower the arithmetic all-K burden, independently authenticate an empirical source, or demonstrate wall-clock speedup. Source-derived certification is strongest here because Gaussian divisibility and edge equations are mathematical, reproducible primitives rather than merely named physical testimony. This construction belongs near classical distributed proof-labeling schemes (e.g. Feuilloley et al., *Redundancy in Distributed Proofs*, DISC 2018, DOI 10.4230/LIPIcs.DISC.2018.24); no original priority is asserted.

**Next diagnostic:** replace the globally constructed candidate potential by **locally proposed** partial potentials and gluing maps (shifts by `QZ²` on overlaps). Prove exactly which overlap cocycle obstructions prevent assembly, and distinguish recoverable gauge mismatch from genuine nonzero source holonomy. That is the plausible path to a mathematically substantive sheaf/categorical tower rather than another verifier wrapper.
