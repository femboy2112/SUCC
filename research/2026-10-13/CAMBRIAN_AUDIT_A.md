## B07 and B11: continuous eigenfunctions and advancing targets

**PASS, specified source and function space.** Independently use
psi_k(x)=T_k(1-2x). The logistic identity 1-2f(x)=T_2(1-2x) gives
U psi_k=psi_(2k). Its inverse pair x,1-x gives L psi_(2k)=psi_k and odd
mode annihilation. Eighty-five direct rational Chebyshev recurrence checks
verify these identities without importing the candidate's mode transfer
rule as the oracle.

For 0<lambda<1, the series g=sum_j lambda^j psi_(2^j) converges uniformly
because |psi_k|<=1. Thus g is continuous; bounded transfer permits termwise
application, Lg=lambda*g, and each nonconstant mode has arcsine mean zero.
At x=0 every mode is 1, so ||g||_infinity=1/(1-lambda). The triangle bound
attains equality there. This supports the exact residual, not a finite plot.
For 36 rational lambda/truncation cases, the finite truncation fails the
eigenrelation by exactly -lambda^J psi_(2^(J-1)). The omitted tail accounts
for it. No truncated series is mislabeled as an exact eigenfunction.

The candidate's universal statement is correctly scoped:

`for every fixed continuous g, ||L^n(t) U^m(t)g - mu(g)||_infinity -> 0`

iff n(t)-m(t)->+infinity. Cancellation of adjacent LU identities proves
the normal form. For sufficiency, approximate fixed g uniformly by a
polynomial, whose nonconstant modes eventually die; contraction and mean
continuity bound the residual by twice the approximation error. For
necessity, if the surplus fails to diverge, it has a subsequence bounded
above; a single fixed geometric g retains a strictly positive residual on
that subsequence. The candidate distinguishes this from each-individual-target
necessity and from uniform operator-norm decay.

**Fresh controls.** Alternating clocks n=10t+(t when t odd, else 0), m=10t
both diverge, and their surplus grows on one subsequence, but the even
subsequence leaves the lambda=2/3 target residual exactly 3. A fixed odd
polynomial can finish at bounded surplus; a constant requires mean subtraction
and otherwise never decays. At every fixed transfer depth d, the normalized
mean-zero target psi_(2^d) retains sup norm 1 after transfer, refuting a
uniform unit-ball decay claim. Candidate high-mode controls include depths
40 and 91 without expanding their astronomical power-polynomial degrees.

**CONDITIONAL analytic authority boundary.** These infinite claims are paper

## ST7: successful tightening of a valid bound

The initial target at f906bb1 stated TV(P(.|A),Q(.|A))<=epsilon/min(alpha,beta).
This is valid, and equal-mass rare receipts make it sharp as a global
statement. Unequal masses permit a stronger bound:

\[
\operatorname{TV}(P(\cdot\mid A),Q(\cdot\mid A))
\le\min\left(1,\frac{\epsilon}{\max(\alpha,\beta)}\right),
\quad \alpha=P(A)>0,\ \beta=Q(A)>0.
\]

Suppose alpha<=beta, write u=P_A/alpha, v=Q_A/beta. Then
beta(u-v)=(P_A-Q_A)+(beta-alpha)u. Its L1 norm is at most
||P_A-Q_A||_1+beta-alpha. The complementary slice L1 difference is at
least beta-alpha. Thus this numerator is at most the full joint L1
difference 2epsilon. Divide by 2beta and exchange the roles if needed.

**Sharp unequal-mass control.** In cell order (x,A),(y,A),(x,A^c),(y,A^c),
put P=(1/3,0,2/3,0), Q=(1/3,1/3,1/3,0). Their joint TV is 1/3,
event masses are 1/3 and 2/3, and conditional TV is 1/2. The original
bound is 1; the stronger bound is exactly 1/2. More generally
P_A=alpha*delta_x and Q_A=alpha*delta_x+(beta-alpha)*delta_y attain
(beta-alpha)/beta, with matching complementary masses adjusted.

The independent exact sweep includes 5,929 positive-event row pairs at
denominator six, 4,886 with unequal event masses, and 1,484 positive
unequal-mass saturations of the capped improved bound. All satisfy it.
These finite tests corroborate the displayed proof; they do not replace it.
After the auditor sent the derivation, the principal separately re-derived
it and updated ST7 at 484dc20. The exact patch and new unequal-event test
were inspected. **PASS sharpened statement, no falsehood attributed to
the earlier min version.**
