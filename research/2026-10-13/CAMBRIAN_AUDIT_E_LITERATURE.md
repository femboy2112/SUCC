## B08.3: precisely what the primary theorem supplies

The original [Misiurewicz1981 paper](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1981__53__17_0.pdf)
was read at §3, §6 and Theorem8.1, printed pp.24–25,38–41,45–46. The theorem
concerns a spectral component K preserved by f^k, its absolutely continuous
invariant component measure, and periodic points in K outside the exceptional
postcritical set B. If that measure has maximal entropy, the periodic
f^k-multiplier equals the exponential of period times component entropy.
It assumes conditions(i)–(vi), including regularity, nonvanishing derivative
off the exceptional partition, nonpositive Schwarzian, **strict expansion at
every periodic point**, nonrecurrence near that partition, and local derivative
control. It is not a theorem about arbitrary finite adjacency matrices.

The application currently establishes algebra for one two-cycle, its multiplier
M=-alpha²+2alpha+4 and |M|>2. Those local facts do not establish the following:

1. **Global condition(iv):** every periodic point is repelling; neutral cycles
   must also be excluded. A negative-Schwarzian citation is not that proof.
2. **Exceptional-set match:** the paper's one-sided endpoint/critical partition,
   its postcritical closure B, and the cycle must be matched on the actual chosen
   carrier or its iterate. A finite list of postcritical points alone does not
   establish all other hypotheses.
3. **Component and support:** construct the relevant acim component K and show
   that the chosen cycle belongs to K outside B. Whole-carrier topological
   entropy is not a substitute for h_top(f^k restricted to K).
4. **Symbolic lift:** justify the Parry law as a carrier measure with the needed
   entropy and support. The symbolic stationary vector is not a Lebesgue density.

If k=2 and the candidate period-two cycle becomes a fixed point for f² on K,
the required comparison is |(f²)'|=exp(h_top(f²|K)). This becomes2 only after
the component entropy has been identified as log2. Alternatively a valid
k=1 component proof would yield the equivalent period-two comparison using
h_top(f|K)=log(sqrt2). These are proof obligations, not additional measurements.

**Required integration handling:** retain CONDITIONAL for natural-acim versus
Parry inequality and null for the uncomputed natural branch weight. Keep the
separate PF proof refuting a cellwise-constant invariant density: it does not
refute accidental equality of a few aggregated masses. The next actual lamp is
a complete applicability proof or a certified weighted-density construction.
This audit has not supplied either and has not promoted the conditional claim.

## Strong lumpability: correct all-prior quantifier, incomplete original scan access

The coordinator's ST1 uses the full joint row J_s(p,z), not just either
marginal. It is the finite row-factorization condition through q. A labelled
instrument with changing charts is wider than a stationary Markov chain, but
the finite representative argument explicitly proves that extension.

The original Kemeny–Snell chapter scan endpoint failed during this auditor's
retrieval; its theorem was **not independently reread here**, even though the
coordinator reports an earlier successful read. The author paper
[Geiger–Temmel](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1212.4375), Definition7 and Theorem9, was
read: strong lumpability quantifies over every initial distribution, unlike
stationary weak lumpability. Its entropy characterizations carry irreducible,
aperiodic, stationary setting assumptions. Those extra entropy theorems are not
silently applied to ST's arbitrary finite one-step instruments. This source
supports the quantifier distinction; ST's elementary proof supplies its own
exact joint-row criterion.

The [Fritz paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.07021), Definition12.8 and
Proposition12.9, explicitly constructs the conditional product from compatible
marginals and identifies it as the unique joint law displaying the specified
conditional independence. This confirms that A's and EOS's conditional-product
machinery is established mathematics; the independence assumption cannot be
replaced by common source labels or matching supports.

## C, E and F: source matches that survive inspection

- **Compact history realization:** the [Stacks FIP statement](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0059),
  Lemma5.12.6, and [product/limit section](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/08ZS),
  Theorem5.14.4 and Lemma5.14.6, were read. They provide the compactness ingredients
  used by C16. Closed constraints and a complete declared original class remain
  necessary application gates; classical choice is not an algorithm.
- **Covers:** [Hatcher's author textbook](https://math.uchicago.edu/~farb/algtop2023/Hatcher.pdf),
  Propositions1.32 and1.39 plus Theorem1.38, were read. Sheet number is subgroup
  index; connected covers are classified by subgroups; deck actions are
  transitive on fibers precisely in the normal-cover case. Finite connected
  graphs satisfy the local connectivity assumptions. C15's stabilizer/orbit
  result is a classical application, and its nonregular deck-invariance
  countermodel is consistent with that distinction.
- **Bounded p-adic measures:** [deShalit](https://jtnb.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/jtnb.955.pdf),
  Theorem1.2 and §5.1/Proposition5.1, were read. Continuous-function coefficients
  tend to zero, while bounded measures belong to the Banach dual and have bounded
  transform coefficients. E26's compatible-cylinder extension additionally uses
  its explicit density/ultrametric proof. Compatibility without boundedness gives
  a distribution rather than the claimed bounded functional. Real Haar measure
  is not contradicted by a Q_p-valued boundedness obstruction.
- **Quantum instruments:** [Watrous chapter2](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/TQI.2.pdf),
  equations2.258–2.262, and [chapter3](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/TQI.3.pdf),
  Corollary3.40, were read. Instruments specify outcome probabilities AND posterior
  state; positive trace-preserving maps contract trace norm. A normalized selected
  branch is not generally trace preserving. F31's instruments and postselection
  distinction belong to this established framework.
- **Hidden memory:** [Parish–Duraisamy author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.03311),
  equations10–14 and nearby discussion, was read. The exact Mori–Zwanzig identity
  contains Markovian, memory and noise terms; projected/approximate equations need
  additional assumptions. F32's block-linear formula is an elementary exact
  specialization with an explicit hidden-initial-state term, not a new universal
  nonlinear closure. The paper's finite-memory LES approximations do not prove
  exact universal Navier–Stokes closure.
- **Open systems:** [Baez–Courser](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.04630), Theorem3.9,
  was read. Its structured-cospan double category requires finite colimits and a
  colimit-preserving functor. C17 correctly claims only finite `Rel` behavior
  semantics, leaving physical black-box functor laws to separate verification.
- **Restricted probes:** the [Stacks Yoneda lemma](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/001P)
  was read. Full representability does not itself establish faithfulness after
  restriction. C13's additional retract proof supplies a sufficient criterion;
  no new Yoneda theorem is claimed.
