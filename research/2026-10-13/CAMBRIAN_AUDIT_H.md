## Whole resource cost, rather than a toy round counter

The conditional learning and optimal-action statements survive. Their
scope does not supply a generally cheap physical implementation.

H ran a valid **65-state unary Moore source** with max_rounds=1.
The correct source-side teacher certified the reconstructed 65-state
model after:

| Resource | Actual count |
|---|---:|
| Outer equivalence calls | 1 |
| Distinct membership queries | 66 |
| Sequential source ticks across reset membership trials | 2,145 |
| Maximum queried word length | 65 |
| Reconstructed states | 65 |

Thus max_rounds bounds outer teacher iterations, not membership queries,
inner table repairs, time, memory or teacher work. This is an actual
finite-source control, not a nonregular source outside the theorem.
Reset preparation cost is additional and was not physically measured.
An arbitrary membership callback can also fail to terminate; the theorem
assumes valid finite-source, effective teacher interfaces.

The simulator's equivalence teacher performs product-state exploration
over source and hypothesis states. That privileged source access remains
on the teacher side; it is not acquired merely because the learner lacks
transition arrays. The reported 667 membership queries and 39
counterexamples are experiment counts, not total machine or physical
cost bounds.

The full-source minimality oracle enumerates set partitions under an
explicit at-most-eight-state budget. Its certification work grows with
the Bell-number partition count. Constructive refinement count at most
the finite residual-state count does not absorb that oracle cost, word
length, source evaluation or validation.

For a generic universe of p possible source/current pairs and horizon h,
a belief-state dynamic program can face up to (h+1)·2^p abstract
belief/horizon keys, with action/outcome processing and exact-number
cost in addition. Reachability may reduce this; no sharp universal
runtime bound is claimed here. The independent full strategy-tree
oracle has a separate depth≤3 budget. Finite toy action prices measure
the modeled intervention cost, rather than the verifier's runtime,
communication, reset preparation, energy or source-authentication cost.

The constructive finite-source witness count and one-way bit lower
bound remain different resources. No claim that classical choice
computes a witness or that one report performs a physical supertask
survives the report.
