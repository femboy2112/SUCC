\[
E_n=\{p\in T_n:\forall m\ge n\;\exists q\in T_m,

## 6. Complete proof and noncanonical Kraus no-go

Each Tₙ is finite by induction from finite preparation and finite branching.

surjectivity. For any surjective subsystem Y and p∈Yₙ, successively lifting
