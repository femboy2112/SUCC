## 1. Original question and faithful steelman

When do source-indexed proof supports commute with a change of source carrier or observational basis? More precisely: for a commuting finite square, which exact hypothesis is necessary and sufficient for existential evidence transport, and does the same hypothesis preserve the actual witnesses of that evidence?

The strongest faithful reading does not identify current carrier state with original source state. It asks for lawful transport of declared source-relative evidence, followed by a separate test that the transported evidence still means the original claim. The crucial distinction is between a truth-valued support and a family of distinct proof/history receipts.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects and exact types

**Base category.** Work first in `FinSet`; a carrier is a finite set X with a declared source identity/version. A mathematical action is a total map U:X→Y. A physically lawful action requires an additional independently authenticated certificate. The theorem does not manufacture such certificates.

**Truth-valued fibre.** Over X use the poset P(X), with arrows S→T precisely when S⊆T. For f:X→Y:

- f*:P(Y)→P(X) is inverse image;
- f!:P(X)→P(Y) is direct image;
- f∀:P(X)→P(Y) sends S to {y: every x with f(x)=y belongs to S}.

Thus f! ⊣ f* ⊣ f∀. The total predicate category has objects (X,S), and an arrow over f is allowed when f(S)⊆T. Its projection to FinSet is a split fibration, with cartesian lift (X,f*T)→(Y,T), and an opfibration, with cocartesian lift (X,S)→(Y,f!S).

**Witness-valued fibre.** Over X use `FinSet^X`: an object A is a family of finite sets A_x, and a morphism is a family of functions. An element a∈A_x can retain a distinct proof or causal-history receipt; nonemptiness alone means only that some declared witness exists. Reindexing is `(f*A)_x=A_{f(x)}`. Existential transport is the dependent sum

\[
(f!A)_y=\coprod_{x:f(x)=y} A_x.
\]

The total family category has arrows over f:X→Y given by functions A_x→B_{f(x)} for every x. It is equivalent to the codomain category of finite sets over finite sets. Cartesian lifts are pullbacks; cocartesian lifts are dependent sums. The choice of explicit fibre indexing is split on inverse image; sums compose by canonical reassociation, with the standard coherent isomorphisms rather than an undeclared literal equality.

**Square.** Let f:X→Z, g:Y→Z, p:W→X, q:W→Y satisfy fp=gq. W is an arbitrary finite witness set, not necessarily a relation. The comparison

\[
c:W\longrightarrow X\times_ZY,\qquad c(w)=(p(w),q(w))
\]

is well typed exactly because the square commutes. A weak pullback here means c is surjective. A categorical pullback in FinSet means c is bijective.

**Topology/resources.** All these objects are finite discrete sets; no infinite limit or compactness assumption enters. Maps and witness tables are supplied exactly. Exhaustive tests below have fixed small bounds and no heavy compiler process. Neither observer access to a simulated original state nor an unperformed counterfactual counts as an actual receipt.

## 4. Candidate theorem statements

### SWM-A01.1 — Truth and witness base-change classification (CLASSICAL)

For every finite commuting square as above:

1. `q!p*S = g*f!S` for **every** S⊆X iff c is surjective.
2. `q∀p*S = g*f∀S` for **every** S⊆X iff c is surjective.
3. The canonical comparisons for all finite families A over X,

\[
\beta_A:q!p*A\longrightarrow g*f!A,
\qquad (w,a)\longmapsto (p(w),a)
\]

are all isomorphisms iff c is bijective. Equivalently, `(∀ A, β_A is an isomorphism) ↔ c is bijective`.

In part 3, the quantifier is `∀A, β_A is an isomorphism`; it is not enough that one selected family has this property. In fact the constant singleton family detects bijectivity already.

Consequently Boolean existential truth can commute across a square that fails proof/history transport as an isomorphism. Existing CV6 remains correct: because its W is literally a subset of X×Y, c is automatically injective, so surjectivity is equivalent to W being the full pullback relation.

### SWM-A01.2 — Exact original-claim descent through an intervention (CLASSICAL)

For every finite U:X→Y and every original-source Boolean claim S⊆X, there exists T⊆Y with U*T=S iff

\[
S=U^*U_!S.
\]

Equivalently S is a union of U-fibres, or membership in S is constant on every fibre of U. T is unique on U(X), with T∩U(X)=U(S); it need not be unique outside the reached image.

The requirement is stronger than existence of a commuting observation/report square. U!S means that **some** original predecessor satisfies S; it does not mean that the actual predecessor did. Proof of the actual original claim from the current carrier requires the fibre criterion.

## 5. Complete direct proofs and coherence checks

These are direct derivations from the definitions; bounded enumeration is not used as the universal proof.

**Adjunctions and lifts.** For S⊆X and T⊆Y, f(S)⊆T iff S⊆f⁻¹(T). Also f⁻¹(T)⊆S iff every predecessor of every y∈T lies in S, namely T⊆f∀S. In the total predicate category an arrow (V,R)→(Y,T) over fh factors uniquely over h through (X,f*T), because its condition is fh(R)⊆T iff h(R)⊆f*T. Dually an arrow from (X,S) to (V,R) over hf factors uniquely through (Y,f!S), because hf(S)⊆R iff h(f!S)⊆R. Identities and composition follow from identity and composition of ordinary maps, and inverse images compose strictly.

For finite families, to give a map `(f!A)_y→B_y` for all y is to give A_x→B_{f(x)} for all x; restriction to and assembly from the disjoint-sum inclusions are mutually inverse. This proves the family adjunction. The cartesian universal property follows by the same componentwise restriction. The cocartesian property follows by uniquely assembling component maps on the dependent sum. For two composable maps f,g, the reassociation `(y,(x,a))↦(x,a)` at each z with g(y)=z and f(x)=y is a bijection. Threefold reassociation sends every nested tuple to the same (x,a) regardless of parenthesization, giving associativity coherence; the singleton identity-fibre maps give unit coherence.

**Part 1, sufficiency.** For y∈Y:

\[
y\in g^*f_!S\iff \exists x\in S\; f(x)=g(y).
\]

If c is surjective, each such pair (x,y) is c(w) for some w. Then p(w)∈S and q(w)=y, so y∈q!p*S. Conversely any such w gives f(p(w))=g(q(w))=g(y), by commutativity, so y∈g*f!S. This reverse inclusion holds for every commuting square even without surjectivity.

**Part 1, necessity.** If a compatible pair (x,y) is missing from c(W), choose S={x}. Then y∈g*f!S, whereas y∈q!p*S would furnish w with c(w)=(x,y), contradiction. Thus equality for all singleton predicates alone forces surjectivity.

**Part 2.** For a finite map f, `f∀S=Y\f!(X\S)`. Inverse image commutes with complement, so the universal base-change equality is equivalent, via complement, to the existential equality for X\S. As complements range over all predicates, part 1 gives the equivalence. Empty fibres are included: universal quantification there is true, while existential quantification is false; the complement identity handles both correctly.

**Part 3, sufficiency.** At y, the domain consists of (w,a) with q(w)=y and a∈A_{p(w)}; the codomain consists of (x,a) with f(x)=g(y) and a∈A_x. β sends the former to the latter, and is well typed by commutativity. If c is bijective, its unique preimage w of (x,y) defines the inverse `(x,a)↦(w,a)`. These functions are mutually inverse at every y. For a family map α:A→B, both composites around the naturality square send `(w,a)` to `(p(w),α_{p(w)}(a))`, proving naturality without identifying distinct w's.

**Part 3, necessity.** Choose A_x={*} for every x. Then at y the domain identifies with q⁻¹(y), and the codomain with {x:f(x)=g(y)}. β_y is precisely w↦p(w). If every β_y is bijective, every compatible pair (x,y) has exactly one preimage w, so c is bijective. Hence the constant singleton family already detects failure. A family vanishing at selected x may hide that failure, which is why the theorem quantifies over all families.

**SWM-A01.2: original-claim descent.** If S=U*T, then a fibre of U cannot contain one point inside S and another outside S. Hence U⁻¹(U(S))⊆S; the opposite inclusion always holds. Conversely if equality holds, choose T=U(S), yielding U*T=S. If T,T' both decode S, they agree on every y∈U(X) by selecting its actual predecessor and reading membership; unreachable y impose no constraints.

**Truncation map and its limits.** Define τ_X(A)={x:A_x≠∅}. Directly, τ_X(f*A)=f*τ_Y(A), and τ_Y(f!A)=f!τ_X(A). Thus the diagram comparing witness-valued transport to predicate-valued transport has actual commuting maps. For a surjective noninjective c, every β_A is surjective, hence nonemptiness is preserved; some β_A is noninjective, hence receipt identity is lost by truncation. This is not a license to count, weigh, or independently multiply the duplicated witnesses.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

**Existing causal-history decoder.** Let U:X→Y be the actual carrier update, and h:X→H the actual retained pre-update history report. Define

\[
\widetilde U:X\to Y\times H,\qquad x\mapsto(U(x),h(x)).
\]

For an original target t:X→T, the typed criterion is

\[
\exists d:\widetilde U(X)\to T\; d\widetilde U=t
\iff \ker\widetilde U\subseteq\ker t.
\]

It is the already kernel-typed factorization object in `EODCS_CausalDescent.lean`; its Boolean specialization is SWM-A01.2. In the erased-bit countermodel h(x)=x produces distinct records (current,0) and (current,1), so d(current,b)=b repairs original-bit decoding. This is lawful only if that history was actually obtained and remains available. Without h, no decoder exists.

**Witness-to-support comparison.** The τ_X maps above form an explicit natural comparison from the family fibration to the predicate fibration and commute with reindexing/dependent sums. Thus distinct history paths may become observationally identical truth supports. The duplicate-path countermodel proves that forgetting this map's loss of information is mathematically material. This supports a source-indexed architecture with separate witness transport and truth transport; it does not require a metaphorical identification with a sheaf or physical universe.
