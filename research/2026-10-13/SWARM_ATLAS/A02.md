## 4. Candidate theorem

**A02-T — exact free-history morphism adequacy.** For every finite acyclic directed multigraph G, its free path category C, and every full subcategory J on any selected vertices (including none),

\[
\boxed{R_J\text{ is faithful}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad M\subseteq\operatorname{Reach}(J).}
\]

Quantifiers are over all objects x,y and every pair f≠g in C(x,y). Therefore a single root probe is mathematically faithful whenever the root reaches every vertex of such a free category. This conclusion asserts injectivity on hom-sets, not fullness or object separation.

For clarity, the general exact conditions that must not be replaced by A02-T are:

1. **Morphism separation, any category:** for every f≠g:x→y, there exist j∈J and u:j→x with f∘u≠g∘u. This is equivalent to faithfulness.
2. **Object separation up to isomorphism, any category:** for every x≇y, there is no family of bijections θ_j:C(j,x)→C(j,y) satisfying `θ_j(v∘u)=θ_k(v)∘u` for every u:j→k in J and v:k→x. This is equivalent to `R_J(x)≇R_J(y)`. Equal hom cardinalities alone do not supply the coherent family. Strict label inequality is not the notion being certified.
3. **Fullness:** every component family η_j:C(j,x)→C(j,y) satisfying those naturality equations is induced by some f:x→y. Together with condition 1 this requires a unique f for every family; it is full faithfulness, the dense-inclusion condition.

These are separate universally quantified mathematical claims. The concrete probe function `restricted_yoneda` returns word profiles; its own docstring warns that profiles alone are not complete presheaf data in a nonthin category. A02 uses actual precomposition maps and naturality.

## 5. Proof attempt

**Complete paper derivation of A02-T.** First, for any category, two represented natural transformations are equal exactly when all their component functions are equal. Evaluating at every j and u gives

\[
R_J(f)=R_J(g)\iff
\forall j\in J\ \forall u\in C(j,x),\quad f\circ u=g\circ u.
\]

Suppose M⊆Reach(J). Given distinct parallel paths f,g:x→y, the source x lies in M. Choose j∈J and a path u:j→x. In the free category, the composite words are `word(u)·word(f)` and `word(u)·word(g)`. Left cancellation in finite words implies these are distinct. Thus u separates f,g, so the component maps differ. This proves faithfulness on every hom-set.

Conversely, if x∈M lies outside Reach(J), choose y and distinct f,g:x→y. Every C(j,x) is empty. Each component of R_J(f) and R_J(g) is the unique empty-domain function, so the transformations agree. The functor is nonfaithful. This proves both directions. Acyclicity ensures this is a finite SUCC instrument; free-path cancellation is the load-bearing mathematical premise.

The object criterion in §4 follows because an isomorphism of presheaves is a natural family of bijections. Conversely the component inverses of a natural bijection family are natural: compose its naturality square with θ_j^{-1} and θ_k^{-1}. A fully faithful R_J reflects object isomorphism: lift an isomorphism and its inverse by fullness, and lift their two identity equations by faithfulness. Faithfulness alone cannot lift those maps.

\[
R_J(r)(l)=\varnothing,\qquad R_J(l)(l)=\{1_l\}.

The two nonisomorphic objects are separated, but R_J(f)=R_J(g) is the unique map ∅→{1_l}. This witness also happens to be **full**: all four restricted hom maps are surjective. Fullness therefore does not repair its nonfaithfulness.

**Minimality, with domain stated.** In a free acyclic category one object has only an identity, so nonfaithfulness needs at least two objects. Distinct parallel arrows in this class are nonidentities between distinct objects, requiring at least two arrows beyond their identities. Our two-object/four-arrow example realizes the lower bound. If arbitrary finite categories with nonidentity endomorphisms are allowed, the absolute minimum under a nonempty proper J and two nonisomorphic objects is instead two objects/three arrows: isolated a with `{1_a,e}`, e²=e, plus isolated b with 1_b; J={b}. Empty versus singleton separates objects, while 1_a and e collapse. The 4-arrow claim is restricted to free acyclic histories.
