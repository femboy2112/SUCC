# SWM-A02 — Adequacy is generation; fullness needs density

## 1. Original question
When do permitted probe contexts distinguish source morphisms, and when do all compatible report transformations arise from source morphisms?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
`yoneda.py` computes both presheaf components and precomposition naturality; `operational_yoneda_descent.py` separates category-theoretic faithfulness from source-claim-preserving causal descent. [Riehl, Category Theory in Context](https://emilyriehl.github.io/files/context.pdf), §2.2 and Definition 6.5.11, treats Yoneda and density. Generation and density are classical, different requirements.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Let C be a locally small source/history category and J:P→C a declared probe subcategory. Y_P(X)=C(J−,X) is a presheaf on P. A generator family means: for all parallel f≠g:X→Y, some p and u:Jp→X satisfy fu≠gu. Composition and identities are mandatory, rather than labels or profile sizes alone.

## 4. Candidate theorem
Restricted Yoneda is faithful iff the declared probe family is generating. Full faithfulness additionally means density of J. A finite report family h_i:X→O_i separates **states** iff ⋂ker h_i is equality; this does not itself prove fullness for a history category.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete local derivation
Y_P(f)=Y_P(g) means every component sends every u:Jp→X to the same composite. By definition this is fu=gu for all allowed generalized elements. Thus generating probes force f=g, and failure of generation gives unequal arrows with identical restricted images. This establishes both directions. For states, equality of their tuple reports is exactly membership in every kernel, so injectivity is equivalent to the stated intersection criterion. Fullness requires every natural transformation of presheaves to be represented; the next counterexample disproves its inference from faithfulness.

## 6. Crucible
In the divisibility category on {1,2,3}, probe {1} makes every presheaf component a singleton. The category is thin, so faithfulness is automatic. However Hom(2,3)=∅ while Nat(Y_P2,Y_P3) contains one map: fullness fails exactly.

In the actual path category on {1,2,3,6} with generators ×2 and ×3, the two arrows 1→6 have words (2,3) and (3,2). With only probe 6 their components have empty input, so both restricted maps coincide. Adding context 1 splits them through its identity arrow. These examples call the repository's naturality checker, while A01's generated-word source oracle is separately implemented.

A01's three-state future-action countermodel also shows that current-state report separation cannot be substituted for all lawful continuations. Omitting a declared source action is rejected by the new finite-source contract.
