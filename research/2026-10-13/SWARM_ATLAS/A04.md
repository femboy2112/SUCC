# SWM-A04 — Section covers, nonabelian stabilizers, and actual descent

## 1. Original question

When does nonabelian source holonomy obstruct a global parallel section, and
what is the smallest connected cover that admits one? A lawful section upstairs
must not silently become a claim downstairs. Steelman: preserve every original
edge map while distinguishing **one section**, **all-fiber trivialization**, and
**descent of that section**.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Let Γ be a finite connected graph, retaining loops and parallel darts, and
F its path-groupoid functor to finite nonempty sets and bijections. Reverse
darts carry inverse maps. Fix root r, fiber X, and tree transports
t_v:X→F(v), with t_r=id. For e:u→v set
`g_e=t_v⁻¹ U_e t_u`. Let Π=π₁(Γ,r), ρ:Π→Sym(X), and H=imρ.
All products mean composition, rightmost map first.

A connected d-sheet cover p:C→Γ pulls F back without changing U_e.
A parallel section q obeys `q(head)=U_e(q(tail))` on every lifted dart.
For a chosen root sheet y₀, put K=p_*π₁(C,y₀). For x∈X write
H_x={h:hx=x}, O_x=Hx, and
`core_H(H_x)=⋂_{h∈H} h H_x h⁻¹`.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL, subject to independent review.** For every such F
and every connected finite cover:

1. `Sections(p*F) ≅ X^{ρ(K)}` by evaluation at y₀.
2. The least degree of a connected cover admitting at least one section is
   `min_{x∈X}|O_x|`.
3. Restricted to regular connected covers, the least degree is
   `min_{x∈X}[H:core_H(H_x)]`.
4. A lifted section descends exactly when q is constant on **every fiber
   pair**: p(z)=p(z′) ⇒ q(z)=q(z′). Deck-invariance is sufficient for
   regular covers; it need not be sufficient for nonregular covers.

This differs from the already established minimum |H| for trivializing the
entire faithful H-action. For a regular-torsor fiber the action is free, so
the new one-section minimum reduces to |H|.

## 5. Proof attempt

**Classification.** Let Y=p⁻¹(r). Lifting based loops makes Y a transitive
Π-set. Tree lifting identifies the other sheet fibers with Y. Root values
of a parallel section give a map f:Y→X satisfying
`f(γy)=ρ(γ)f(y)`. Conversely this equation defines q at every vertex by
`q(v,y)=t_v f(y)`, and gives the lifted edge equation. Because Y is
transitive, f is determined by x=f(y₀). It is well-defined precisely when
every γ fixing y₀ fixes x, i.e. x∈X^{ρ(K)}.

**Minimum without regularity.** The image of any such f is the whole orbit
O_x, so d=|Y|≥|O_x|. Equality is attained by the orbit cover with vertices
`(v,y), y∈O_x`, lifted darts
`(u,y)→(v,g_e y)`, and `q(v,y)=t_v y`. Invertibility gives the covering
property; tree edges preserve y and loop generators act transitively on O_x,
so it is connected. The actual map check is
`q(v,g_e y)=t_v g_e y=U_e t_u y=U_e q(u,y)`.

**Regular minimum.** Regularity means K⊲Π. Thus N=ρ(K)⊲H. A section with
root x requires N≤H_x, hence N≤core_H(H_x). The surjection
Π/K→H/N gives
`d=[Π:K]≥[H:N]≥[H:core_H(H_x)]`.
Taking K=ρ⁻¹(core_H(H_x)) attains equality and fixes x. Minimize over X.
This argument covers regular covers whose sheet actions do not factor
through H as well.

**Descent.** Necessity of fiber-pair constancy follows from q=s∘p.
Conversely constancy defines a unique s(v)=q(z) for any z over v;
lifting each dart shows s is parallel. A regular cover's deck group is
transitive on each fiber, making deck-invariance equivalent to constancy.
This is exactly the causal factorization theorem with q there replaced by
p, source update identity, and report the lifted section. Regularity is
needed only for replacing the full fiber pair by deck orbits.

## 6. Crucible

base section. Its orbit cover has three sheets with those same sheet
permutations. The section q(y)=y is lawful and unique. Its deck group
is the common centralizer of a,b, which the exact oracle finds to be
{id}. Consequently q is deck-invariant, but p(0)=p(1) and q(0)≠q(1):
**deck-invariance without regularity is REFUTED**.

The root-sheet stabilizer is {id,(12)}. Pulled-back holonomy is still

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

Under vertex frames A_v, define
`U′_e=A_v U_e A_u⁻¹`, `t′_v=A_v t_v A_r⁻¹`.
Then `g′_e=A_r g_e A_r⁻¹`, H′=A_rHA_r⁻¹, and
Φ(v,y)=(v,A_r y) identifies the orbit covers. Their sections obey
`q′(Φ(v,y))=A_v q(v,y)`. Thus lifted arrow equations, orbit minima,
cores and descent commute with gauge transport. Choosing another tree
likewise yields an isomorphic cover, not a changed original source law.

For causal observations, the same descent square is p:C→Γ followed by
s:Γ→X_v, with q=s∘p. A report constant on the smaller deck-orbit quotient
can still fail to factor through p. The exact S3 fiber pair displays the
missing information rather than merely drawing an analogy.
