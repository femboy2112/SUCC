## 4. Candidate theorem

**CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL, by the construction below.** For every such P,C,D,K,F, pointwise L=`Lan_K F` and R=`Ran_K F` exist and, for every G:D→E, have natural bijections

\[
\operatorname{Nat}(L,G)\cong\operatorname{Nat}(F,GK),\qquad
\operatorname{Nat}(G,R)\cong\operatorname{Nat}(GK,F).
\]

Thus `Lan_K ⊣ K* ⊣ Ran_K`. If K is fully faithful, their unit F→LK and counit RK→F are isomorphisms. No assertion about actualized source events follows.

## 5. Proof attempt — completed finite construction

Write J_d=(K↓d), with objects `(c,α:Kc→d)` and arrows k:c→c′ satisfying α′Kk=α. Write H_d=(d↓K), with objects `(c,β:d→Kc)` and arrows satisfying Kkβ=β′. For each p∈P define

\[
L(d)_p=\left(\coprod_{(c,\alpha)\in J_d}F(c)_p\right)/\sim,
\quad[(c,\alpha,x)]\sim[(c',\alpha',F(k)x)],
\]

\[
R(d)_p=\{(x_j)_{j\in H_d}:x_j\in F(c_j)_p,
\ F(k)x_j=x_{j'}\text{ for every }k:j\to j'\}.
\]

These are finite. The colimit relation never identifies different tags. The right construction uses a singleton empty product **per p**. An arrow u:d→e sends `[c,α,x]` to `[c,uα,x]` in L, and sends a right tuple to its coordinates `(c,γu)` for each γ:e→Kc. Quotient relations and compatibility equations make these maps well defined; substitution proves identity/composition laws. Tags persist.

The unit is η_c(x)=[c,id,x]; the counit ε_c selects `(c,id)`. Both are natural. Given θ:F→GK, define T_d[c,α,x]=G(α)θ_c(x). Naturality of θ respects the quotient, and postcomposition proves naturality of T. Conversely T maps to (TK)η. These operations are inverse: identity representatives recover θ; naturality of T determines its value on every representative, proving uniqueness.

Given θ:GK→F, define S_d(z)=(θ_cG(β)z)_(c,β). Naturality of θ gives every compatibility equation; precomposition gives naturality of S. Conversely S maps to ε(SK). Identity coordinates recover θ, and naturality of S determines every coordinate, proving uniqueness. The formulas also commute with natural transformations of F and G, giving the adjunctions.

If K is fully faithful, `(c,id)` is terminal in K↓Kc and initial in Kc↓K: every required arrow lifts uniquely through K. The colimit/limit therefore recovers F(c), compatibly with all C arrows. This proves the stated isomorphisms.

## 6. Crucible

**Smallest nonempty-old-language missing witness:** C={old}, D={old,new} discrete, K inclusion, F(old)={r} tagged p. Both comma categories at new are empty. L(new)=∅; R(new)={(p,())}. This singleton records no constraints. The event predicate can be false or true there without changing F,K or their extensions. Therefore `universal extension ⇒ original-source actualization` is **REFUTED** by these two interpretations.

**Contradiction:** C is a→y←b, with F(a)={r₀}, F(b)={r₁}, F(y)={0,1}; maps send r₀→0 and r₁→1, all tagged p. D adds d→a,b,y with commuting composites. Replacing r₁→1 by r₁→0 gives the positive control. Empty results exist mathematically but fail the repository’s realized `Capsule` admission.

**Lossy migration:** folding two discrete old objects into one new object is not fully faithful. Old cardinalities 1 and 2 yield left cardinality 3 and right cardinality 2. Universal migration need not recover old objects without the embedding hypothesis.
