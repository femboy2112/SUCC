# SWM-A06 — Minimal repair is a coreflection; separate repairs can fail

## 1. Original question
Does finite source-faithful observational repair have a universal property, and can repairs for different action families be composed once?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
`categorical_repair_synthesis.py` already constructs the classical coarsest lawful Mealy quotient and its universal observer factor. The present independent implementation extends the viewpoint to an **arbitrary initial equivalence** and explicit fibred coreflection, rather than claiming new automata minimization. Background: [Rutten, Universal coalgebra, 2000](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/48); classical adjunction theory in [Riehl](https://emilyriehl.github.io/files/context.pdf), §4.1.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Eq(X) uses relation inclusion, so a smaller equivalence means a finer observer. The complete action family A induces stable equivalences StEq_A(X). Initial R is the equality relation of the allowed report tuple, possibly augmented by an original-source target. Define I_A(R) by the all-word intersection of A01. Its quotient is mathematical repair, not an assumed new physical instrument.

## 4. Candidate theorem
I_A is monotone, deflationary and idempotent; it is the right adjoint/coreflection of StEq_A(X)↪Eq(X). For finite |X|=n and initial b blocks, iterative refinement stabilizes after at most n−b strict splitting rounds. Any lawful surjective observer r:X→P with ker r⊆R has a unique map P→X/I_A(R) commuting with the source quotient. In contrast, **I_A I_B need not equal I_{A∪B}**. Exact one-pass composition for every R holds iff I_A sends every B-stable equivalence to a B-stable equivalence. Commutation of I_A and I_B is sufficient.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete derivation and minimal failed composition
A01 proves I_A is greatest among stable E⊆R, giving the adjunction. Monotonicity follows by inclusion of every word-preimage relation. Its output is stable, so applying I_A again fixes it; the empty word proves deflationarity. Each nontrivial finite refinement strictly increases the number of blocks, establishing the bound. Define P→X/I_A(R) by r(x)↦[x]. If r(x)=r(y), stable ker r⊆R implies x I_A(R)y, so the map is well defined. Surjectivity makes it total and unique.

For failed composition use four states, R=(0,0,0,1), A=(0,0,3,0), B=(0,2,0,0). I_B(R)=R; I_A I_B(R)=(0,0,1,2), which is not B-stable because 0 and 1 map under B to 0 and 2. The joint repair is discrete. The mixed history B then A distinguishes original states 0 and 1 with traces (0,0,0) and (0,0,1).

Four states are minimal for this failure: on at most three states an indiscrete equivalence is stable under every total map; a discrete one is also stable; a remaining two-block relation has only one nontrivial pair. Refinement either leaves it unchanged or makes it discrete. Therefore one pass through the two repair operators cannot leave an unstable intermediate relation in that size range.

For the one-pass criterion, necessity follows by taking an already B-stable R: I_B(R)=R, and an exact joint repair must be B-stable. Conversely, if I_A preserves B-stability, I_A I_B(R) is jointly stable and contained in R. Every jointly stable E⊆R lies in I_B(R), then in I_A I_B(R), so this is the greatest joint repair. If the operators commute, I_B I_A I_B=I_A I_B I_B=I_A I_B, proving the required B-stability.

## 6. Crucible
Independent generated-word enumeration checks 1,208 source/initial-equivalence cases, all stable candidate partitions, monotonicity and idempotence. The fixed four-state witness checks both the malformed composition and the actual mixed-word report. An exploratory exhaustive three-state search found no counterexample; the elementary minimality proof above explains that observation. Declared action omission and source-table truncation are rejected, but undisclosed real-world actions remain outside the declared model.

## 7. Verdict
**PROVED LOCAL.** Exact universal repair and a minimal mixed-action composition obstruction. The coreflection ingredients are classical; the surviving source-typed combination is new implementation in SUCC, with academic originality **UNVERIFIED**.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
A single-operator reflector claim depends on choosing the order correctly: under relation inclusion this is an interior/coreflection, while reversing to information order reverses that language. Arbitrary repair operators have no automatic adjoint. The criterion above is exact at the relation-operator level. A source-level structural classification of action families satisfying it remains open; the next lemma should characterize when the actual generator semigroups preserve the other family’s stable equivalences. Finite fair alternating refinement converges, but that is not a one-step identity.
