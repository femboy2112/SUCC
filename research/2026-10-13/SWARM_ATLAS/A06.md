## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Let \(S\ne\varnothing\) be a finite complete stationary carrier, \(A\) a finite
alphabet, \(U_a:S\to S\) total deterministic actions, and \(q:S\to O\) a total
report. Let \(U_w\) execute a word \(w\in A^*\), including the empty word.
Put \(Q=\ker q\). Let \(\mathrm{Eq}_Q\) contain equivalence relations
\(E\subseteq Q\), ordered by **relation inclusion**. Its subposet
\(\mathrm{Cong}_Q\) contains those satisfying
\(sEt\Rightarrow U_a(s)E U_a(t)\) for every \(a\).

Define

\[
R(E)=\bigcap_{w\in A^*}(U_w\times U_w)^{-1}(E).
\]

These are mathematical source contracts; no instrument authenticity or actual
physical reset is assumed.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**A06-T1, PROVED LOCAL / CLASSICAL:** for every stated source and every
\(E\in\mathrm{Eq}_Q\), \(R(E)\) is the greatest action congruence contained in
\(E\). It is monotone, contractive and idempotent. Inclusion
\(j:\mathrm{Cong}_Q\hookrightarrow\mathrm{Eq}_Q\) satisfies \(j\dashv R\).
Thus this is a **coreflector/interior** under inclusion; reversing both orders
gives a **reflector/closure**, the least stable information refinement.

**A06-T2, PROVED LOCAL:** under inclusion, \(j\) has a left adjoint exactly
when \(Q\) itself is action stable. **A06-T3, PROVED LOCAL:** \(R\) commutes
with relational pullback along report-preserving, action-commuting source maps.

## 5. Proof attempt: complete proofs

Each inverse image in the intersection is an equivalence relation, so \(R(E)\)
is one. The empty word gives \(R(E)\subseteq E\subseteq Q\). Prefixing an
action to each future word proves action stability. If stable \(C\subseteq E\),
induction on words gives \(C\subseteq R(E)\). This proves greatestness and

\[
j(C)\subseteq E\iff C\subseteq R(E).
\]

The formula proves monotonicity; greatestness applied to already stable
\(R(E)\) proves \(R(R(E))=R(E)\). The adjunction unit is \(C=R(jC)\), its
counit is \(jR(E)\subseteq E\). Naturality and triangle identities hold because
these are thin categories and both composites have the same endpoints.

For T2, a left adjoint evaluated at the ambient top \(Q\) would require a
stable \(C\) with \(Q\subseteq C\subseteq Q\); hence \(Q\) must be stable.
Conversely, if \(Q\) is stable, the intersection of all stable
\(C\supseteq E\) is a well-defined least such relation: this family includes
\(Q\), and intersections preserve equivalence and stability. This closure is
left adjoint to \(j\). It merges states, whereas \(R\) splits them.

For T3 let \(f:S\to T\), \(q_Tf=q_S\), and \(fU_a^S=U_a^Tf\).
Induction gives \(fU_w^S=U_w^Tf\). Therefore, for every \(E\in\mathrm{Eq}_{Q_T}\),

\[
R_S(f^*E)=f^*(R_T E),\qquad
f^*E=(f\times f)^{-1}E.
\]

Pullback preserves identity/composition, so these are natural coreflections
over the category of lawful sources. Neither surjectivity nor injectivity is
required. This proof also holds for infinite carriers; finite computation
does not certify an unknown infinite source.

**A06-T4, PROVED LOCAL / CLASSICAL:** finite systems with fixed \(A,O\) and
lawful homomorphisms admit the reflector \(L(S)=S/R(Q)\) into the full
subcategory of behaviorally distinct systems. Define \(L(f)([s])=[f(s)]\);
word commutation makes this well-defined and preserves composition. If target
\(T\) is behaviorally distinct, any lawful \(f:S\to T\) sends equivalent
states to equal states. Hence \(\bar f([s])=f(s)\) is the unique lawful
factor through the surjective quotient. This gives the natural Hom bijection,
unit \(s\mapsto[s]\), and identity counit on simple systems (up to canonical
isomorphism); the triangle identities hold on every state class. The quotient
is simple: equal quotient behaviors would identify the original state classes.

For finite \(n=|S|\), iterating \(E\mapsto E\cap\bigcap_a U_a^*E\) reaches
\(R(E)\) after at most \(n-b(E)\) strict refinements: every strict step increases
block count; a plateau is stable and hence the greatest contained congruence.

## 6. Crucible

Take source \(S=\{0,1\}\), \(q_S=(0,0)\), \(U_S=(0,1)\); target
\(T=\{0,1,2\}\), \(q_T=(0,0,1)\), \(U_T=(0,2,2)\), and \(f=(0,1)\).
Reports commute, actions do not: \(fU_S(1)=1\ne2=U_Tf(1)\).
For \(E=Q_T\), \(R_S(f^*E)\) is total, while \(f^*R_T(E)\) is diagonal.
Thus the source merges \(0,1\), while the target separates their images after
one action. No map of these behavioral quotients can descend \(f\).
On carrier \(T\) with the same report, identity updates yield repair \(Q_T\);
the displayed updates yield the diagonal. A universally safe refinement can
exist across sources without being each source's least refinement.

This behavioral-descent witness is cardinality-minimal: a one-state source
has no pair to separate; a
target with at most two states either reports differently immediately, or
has constant reports forever. It also gives T2's smallest same-carrier
obstruction: \(Q_T\) is not stable, so no stable relation can contain it.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

Map a source to its logical behavior
\(\beta(s):A^*\to O,\ \beta(s)(w)=q(U_ws)\).
For formula \(P_{w,c}\), set
\(\llbracket P_{w,c}\rrbracket=\{s:\beta(s)(w)=c\}\).
Equality of all formula truth values is exactly \(R(Q)\). Actions descend
by \([s]\mapsto[U_a s]\); prefixing words transports logical evaluation.
For lawful \(f\), \(\beta_Tf=\beta_S\), yielding the commuting source-to-logic
square. Mealy data enter via the state report vector \(q(s)=(o_a(s))_a\).
This is an automata/logic bridge with explicit maps, not physical ontology.
