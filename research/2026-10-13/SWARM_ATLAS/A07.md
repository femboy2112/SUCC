# SWM-A07 — A strict report-history 2-category with real interchange

## 1. Original question
Can source-changing instruments and report comparisons be composed horizontally and vertically while retaining causal order?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
`noncommuting_proof_transport.py` and `operational_yoneda_descent.py` already preserve source and actual outcome histories. Strict 2-category interchange and category-of-categories examples are classical: [Riehl, Category Theory in Context](https://emilyriehl.github.io/files/context.pdf), §1.7. This dossier constructs a finite report-labelled instance rather than presuming a full bicategory of physical instruments.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Objects are finite nonempty carriers X. A 1-arrow X→Y is (T:X→Y, r:X→O), with O a finite alphabet of fixed-width integer words. A 2-cell (T,r,O)⇒(T,s,P) is a total report map α:O→P satisfying αr=s; the actual carrier transition T is identical. Causal composition uses (U,s,P)∘(T,r,O)=(UT,(r,sT),O×P), represented by concatenated fixed-width report words. Empty report words implement identity arrows.

## 4. Candidate theorem
These data form a strict 2-category under the chosen flattened word-alphabet representation. Vertical composition is report-map composition. Horizontal composition sends (o,p) to (α(o),β(p)); it obeys interchange. Forgetting reports is a functor on 1-arrows that can identify distinct histories; identical endpoint transitions do not certify identical source evidence.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete coherence check
A sequential instrument outputs r(x) followed by s(Tx) and ends at UTx. Threefold composition outputs the concatenation r(x),s(Tx),t(UTx), independent of bracketing because tuple concatenation is associative. Fixed widths make each split unique, so alphabet enumeration also agrees under reassociation. The empty tuple is a strict unit. A horizontal 2-cell maps the output to (αr(x),βs(Tx))=(r'(x),s'(Tx)), so its square commutes.

For successive α,α' and β,β', either side of interchange sends (o,p) to (α'α(o),β'β(p)); hence horizontal-after-vertical and vertical-after-horizontal agree as total functions, including unused declared report labels. Identity comparisons and units follow pointwise. This is an actual two-dimensional law, not an endpoint analogy.

## 6. Crucible
The exact tests use state swaps, binary reports, two stages of noninjective report garbling, and verify equality of both full `ReportComparison` objects under interchange. They verify strict associativity and units. Comparing a swap transition to identity is rejected as a 2-cell. A reset A with reports (0,1) and reset B with constant report 2 have identical endpoint maps under AB and BA, but AB emits (0,2)/(1,2) and BA emits (2,0) for both states. Causal history is therefore not recovered from the endpoint map.

## 7. Verdict
**PROVED LOCAL.** A strict finite report-history 2-category with checked interchange. It models deterministic carrier maps and total report garblings. No stochastic/quantum bicategory or preservation of arbitrary proof meaning is claimed.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
Garbling may erase target meaning even though a report square commutes. The smallest extra obligation is original-target factorization through the garbled report (or origin/current belief). General comparisons that change carriers require actual intertwining source maps; stochastic instruments require a different composition law and conditioning treatment.
