# SWM-A07: Bicategories of instrument histories

Parent: `5eb09e7cba36ec01b8f9d6834b78b5766fd93935`. Scope: finite,

## 1. Original question

Can source-changing observations compose with honest history transformations,
while keeping original source, current state, and actual record distinct?
The faithful proposal is a bicategory whose horizontal operation joins causal
processes and whose vertical operation composes checked changes of process
representation. Equality of presently visible reports is a rival candidate
for its identification rule. Neither candidate asserts that named possible
paths were performed.

## 2. Existing mathematical context

**CLASSICAL.** Spans compose by pullback; maps of spans supply 2-cells,
with associativity expressed by canonical isomorphisms. See Michael Stay,
[Compact Closed Bicategories, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/1301.1053v7#S2).
Finite stochastic matrices compose by summing over intermediate states;
Tobias Fritz gives this framework in
[A synthetic approach to Markov kernels, Example 2.5](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.07021v8#S2).
The construction below adds explicit record words and a mass-preservation
condition, proved directly. Its classical ingredients and repository placement
establish no academic priority.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Fix a record alphabet A and free monoid M=A*. An object is a finite nonempty,
fully typed configuration set. Include source/basis/epoch, resource, clock,
or history fields whenever subsequent laws depend on them. A 1-cell is

\[
P=(X\xleftarrow{s}E\xrightarrow{t}Y,r:E\to M,w:E\to\mathbb Q_{\ge0}),
\qquad \sum_{s(e)=x}w(e)=1\quad(\forall x\in X).
\]

The left leg preserves the input source; the right leg gives the changed
current configuration; r retains the declared event word. A **potential**
path is an apex element. An **actual model path** additionally requires a
selected positive-weight element and an appended immutable receipt. This
extra marking is never supplied by enumerating E. Hardware actualization is
outside the construction.

A 2-cell α:P⇒P′ is a total apex function satisfying
s′α=s, t′α=t, r′α=r, and
\(w′(e′)=\sum_{\alpha(e)=e′}w(e)\) for every e′. These conditions preserve
the full source/current/record law, rather than just its support.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**A07-T1, CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL:** for every finite configuration family
and fixed M, these 1-cells and 2-cells form a bicategory. For all composable
P:X→Y and Q:Y→Z, define E(P;Q)=E(P)×Y E(Q), endpoint legs s(e),t(f),
word r(e)r(f), and weight w(e)v(f). Horizontal 2-cell composition is
(α⊙β)(e,f)=(αe,βf); vertical composition is function composition.
Identity spans have weight one and empty word. This theorem assumes the
declared current configuration is sufficient for the next instrument law;
an omitted physical memory invalidates its interpretation.

## 5. Proof attempt

For each x, composite normalization is exactly

\[
\sum_{s(e)=x}w(e)\sum_{s(f)=t(e)}v(f)=\sum_{s(e)=x}w(e)=1.
\]

For compatible e′,f′, endpoint preservation makes **every** pair in
α⁻¹(e′)×β⁻¹(f′) compatible. Consequently its pushforward mass is
\((\sum_\alpha w)(\sum_\beta v)=w′(e′)v′(f′)\).
Words and endpoints are preserved coordinatewise. Thus horizontal
composition is well-defined. Regrouping finite sums proves vertical
mass preservation; composition also preserves endpoint and record equations.
Identity 2-cells satisfy all four conditions.

For every composable α₁,α₂,β₁,β₂ and compatible (e,f), both sides of

\[
(\alpha_2\!\odot\!\beta_2)\circ(\alpha_1\!\odot\!\beta_1)
=(\alpha_2\circ\alpha_1)\odot(\beta_2\circ\beta_1)
\]

equal (α₂α₁e,β₂β₁f). This is strict interchange of 2-cells, not
commutation of source interventions.

The associator ((e,f),g)↦(e,(f,g)) preserves endpoints, words, and
weights because concatenation and rational multiplication are associative;
its inverse has the same properties. Unit maps delete the unique boundary
identity token. Their naturality follows by applying apex functions
coordinatewise. Both pentagon routes send (((e,f),g),h) to
(e,(f,(g,h))); both triangle routes send ((e,y),f) to (e,f).
These pointwise identities prove coherence for all finite apices, including
zero weights. The receipt checks concrete instances; it does not prove the
universal quantifiers.

## 6. Crucible

**A07-R1, REFUTED:** public-report equality always supports continuation
on the quotient. In the native two-state carrier, Blind reports 0 and flips
the state; Read reports the current bit without changing it. Originals
0,1 produce identical complete Blind public signatures but currents 1,0.
Appending Read gives (0,1) versus (0,0). Thus continuation is not constant
on the report fiber. Two states are minimal among deterministic,
state-dependent continuations: a singleton current carrier supplies the same
next outcome and enabled status. Original-bit recovery after Read uses
1−Read, preserving the distinction between original and current truth.

**A07-R2, REFUTED:** equal support certifies probability-law preservation.
On one input and two outputs, laws (1/2,1/2) and (1/3,2/3) have identical
support and different masses. The identity apex map fails the 2-cell test.
A changed record word is also rejected. Duplicate-token coarse-graining
4→2→1 is the positive control. All witnesses use exact fractions.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

Neither source labels nor replay digests authenticate external apparatus.
Adaptive laws need their policy/history state included at the boundary;
disabled instruments require a restricted typed domain or an explicit
failure outcome. Arbitrary rational quantum states form an infinite carrier:
the finite construction uses a fixed input family and finite horizon only.
Equality of aggregate kernels need not admit a deterministic apex 2-cell.
Next falsifier: omit a resource/history field on which availability depends,
then seek two equal alleged boundary configurations with different next
laws. That would refute the proposed state interpretation, while leaving
the stated algebraic theorem intact.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

Define the record-valued kernel
\(K_P(y,h\mid x)=\sum_{s(e)=x,t(e)=y,r(e)=h}w(e)\).
Then every 2-cell yields K_P=K_P′, and regrouping finite sums gives

\[
K_{P;Q}(z,h\mid x)=\sum_{y,uv=h}K_P(y,u\mid x)K_Q(z,v\mid y).
\]

Summing over h produces an ordinary finite stochastic matrix and preserves
composition. This explicit map forgets causal records and is not faithful.
Conversely, each enabled native deterministic action embeds with one apex
per input, weight one, transition as t, and its source/basis/epoch-tagged
EventRecord as r. Native quantum inputs embed with positive Kraus outcome
edges, exact trace weights, and posterior endpoints. Selected outcome words
replay through QuantumObserver to recover its full receipt; alternatives
remain predictions.
