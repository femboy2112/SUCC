# A08 — Lawvere-enriched observation and resource geometry

## 1. Original question

Can SUCC's causal interaction costs define directed hom-values without treating a
resource bound, debt counter, or observational alias as a metric automatically?
The faithful reading is minimum declared cost of **executable continuation**.
The target is a conditional composition theorem, with a reset-dependent failure
receipt. Parent: `5eb09e7cba36ec01b8f9d6834b78b5766fd93935`.

## 2. Existing mathematical context

This is classical enriched-category mathematics. [Lawvere's original paper,
2002 author commentary, pp. 1–3](https://www.math.buffalo.edu/~sww/0papers/lawveres-metric-space-paper.pdf)
uses extended nonnegative distances, reverse order, addition, and contracting
maps, allowing asymmetry and infinite values. [Kelly, §1.2, pp. 8–9](https://tac.mta.ca/tac/reprints/articles/10/tr10.pdf)
gives actual composition, identity, and functor coherence axioms. These sources
establish prior context; they do not authenticate SUCC's physical cost model.
No academic novelty is claimed.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Fix a small category H of complete causal configurations and admissible finite
histories. Objects retain source identifier, current carrier/resource state,
target contract, and all history variables affecting future menus or costs.
Composition is continuation at the **same complete endpoint**; identity is the
empty history. Costs c(f) belong to [0,∞], with c(id)=0 and
c(g∘f)≤c(f)+c(g). Access restrictions must be closed under this composition.
Stepwise additive costs satisfy the assumption. Simulator configurations are
not hidden-state information supplied to embedded observers. Any observer-policy
version must instead provide a composition-closed category of uniformly
admissible policies and retain their belief/resource interfaces.

Define d_H(x,y)=inf{c(f):f∈H(x,y)}. Set inf∅=∞ and a+∞=∞, including ∞+∞.
There is no subtraction of infinities, symmetry, or separation requirement.
The enrichment base is V=([0,∞],≥,+,0): a base arrow r→s exists exactly when
r≥s in ordinary numerical order.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**A08-T1, CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL paper derivation.** For every H,c satisfying
§3 and all x,y,z, d_H(x,x)=0 and
d_H(x,z)≤d_H(x,y)+d_H(y,z). The unique base arrows give lawful enriched
identities and compositions; their associativity/unit diagrams commute because
V is thin.

**A08-T2, CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL.** For every ordinary history functor
F:H→K preserving endpoints, identities and composition, if
c_K(Ff)≤c_H(f) for every f, then
d_K(Fx,Fy)≤d_H(x,y) for every x,y. Thus its object map is a V-functor.

## 5. Proof attempt

T1: nonnegativity and the zero-cost identity force d_H(x,x)=0. If either
right-hand distance is infinite the triangle bound is automatic. Otherwise,
for each ε>0 choose composable f:x→y and g:y→z with costs less than their
respective infima plus ε/2. Then
d_H(x,z)≤c(g∘f)≤c(f)+c(g)<d_H(x,y)+d_H(y,z)+ε.
Let ε decrease to zero. Attained minima are unnecessary. Thinness supplies
coherence after the inequalities exist.

T2: each mapped history is a legal K-history with no greater cost. Hence
d_K(Fx,Fy)≤c_K(Ff)≤c_H(f) for every f; taking the infimum proves contraction.
If H(x,y) is empty the bound is again automatic. Repeating F then G preserves
the cost bound, and identity functors preserve it, so the asserted maps compose.

## 6. Crucible

Take four complete states X, Y-spent, Y-fresh, Z. Legal edges are
X→Y-spent at cost 1, Y-fresh→Z at cost 1, and optionally
Y-spent→Y-fresh at reset cost r≥0. Define q(X)=X, q(Y-spent)=q(Y-fresh)=Y,
q(Z)=Z and δ(A,B)=inf{d(u,v):q(u)=A,q(v)=B}. Then
δ(X,Y)=δ(Y,Z)=1, but δ(X,Z)=2+r. For r>0, 2+r>2 refutes triangle;
without reset δ(X,Z)=∞. For r=0 this witness disappears. Four states are
minimal for this three-distinct-label representative-switch mechanism: three
states leave one representative per label and inherit the original triangle.
Destructive use or forgotten history can both produce the spent/fresh split;
neither invalidates T1 on the complete states.

Dropping cost compatibility alone has a smaller witness: the three-object
walking-composable-pair category has unique nonidentity arrows f:X→Y,
g:Y→Z, h=g∘f:X→Z. Assign c(f)=c(g)=1, c(h)=3 and identity costs zero.
Its ordinary category is valid, but d(X,Z)=3>1+1. A history surcharge must
therefore be priced through compatible interfaces or excluded by §3.

An independent native temptation also fails. `resource_probe.py` gives least
separating labels D(8,16)=16 and D(8,3)=D(3,16)=2, so 16>2+2. Even setting
D(a,a)=0 does not repair triangle. Distinguishability threshold is not path cost.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

For a weighted transition graph, H is its free path category: objects are
vertices, arrows finite legal edge words, composition concatenation, cost sum.
The identity vertex/word functor into the same graph with halved edge costs
is lawful and contracting; the probe checks every object pair. Access expansion
is similarly contracting when old histories remain legal at no greater cost.
The collapsed q above has no distance target satisfying the asserted δ axioms.
Taking shortest paths on its label graph repairs triangle but assigns X→Z
cost 2, thereby allowing a free representative switch. That is a changed
resource model, not recovered physical access.
