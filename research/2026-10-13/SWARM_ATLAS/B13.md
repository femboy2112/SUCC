## Objects and quantified theorem

Let F be a set of formulas. Fix a deduction calculus whose derivations are
finite trees and every inference rule has finitely many antecedents. Write
Cl(E) for formulas derivable from premise set E. Let {E_i}_{i∈I} be a nonempty
directed family under inclusion (not necessarily a chain).

**Theorem.** `Cl(union_i E_i) = union_i Cl(E_i)`.

A growing calculus may instead be encoded by including rule declarations as
additional licensed premises: the law remains valid when every particular
finite derivation uses finitely many axioms **and** finitely many rule
licenses, and a single directed stage contains them together. It fails for
arbitrary nonmonotone basis/source revisions unless old proofs are revalidated.

## Proof

Monotonicity gives union_i Cl(E_i)⊆Cl(union_i E_i). Conversely, a finite proof
of phi has finitely many leaf premises. Each is in some E_i; directedness
combines the finitely many stage indices into a common E_j. The same exact
finite proof witnesses phi∈Cl(E_j). If rule licenses grow, include the finitely
many used licenses in that support and apply the same argument.
For a chain E_n the index can be chosen as the maximum of finitely many
support indices. `finite_support_has_common_stage` formalizes this support
lemma for arbitrary types, monotone `stage : Nat -> P -> Prop`, and a finite
list, with **no classical choice axiom** in the proof.

## Infinitary crucible

Introduce atomic premises a_0,a_1,... and a genuinely infinitary rule
`{a_n : n∈N} / b`. Let E_k={a_0,...,a_(k-1)}. Every finite stage lacks an
antecedent and cannot derive b; the union has all antecedents and does derive
b. This is a concrete failure of directed-join continuity when rule arity is
changed. It is not an ordinary extra successor observation. A reflection or
consistency axiom likewise changes logical commitments and is not supplied
by the original law.

For a fixed effectively axiomatized arithmetic T satisfying standard Gödel
hypotheses, the true instances "n is not a contradiction proof" may all be
provable although their universal consistency statement is not provable in
T. The support theorem concerns any **supplied finite derivation**; it neither
combines infinitely many instance proofs into the universal statement nor
establishes those Gödel hypotheses about the repository toy language.
Finite decidability of bounded runs is likewise separate from uniform
halting decidability (B11). No conclusion about RH independence follows.

Executable controls locate support {0,3,5} first at stage6 of E_n={0,...,n-1},
leave support {100} unresolved in the supplied eight stages, accept empty
support at stage0, and reject stage retractions. Source/basis identity and
rule soundness remain premises external to this finite bookkeeping function.
