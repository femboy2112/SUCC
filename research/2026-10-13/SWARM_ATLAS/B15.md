## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Work in **Set**. Fix sets of actions \(A\), reports \(O\), and states \(X\). The functor acts on maps by \(F(g)(o,k)=(o,g\circ k)\). A total deterministic source is \(c=(o,\delta):X\to O\times X^A\). Define \(\delta_\epsilon(x)=x\), \(\delta_{aw}(x)=\delta_w(\delta_a(x))\); words execute left to right. Let \(Z=O^{A^*}\), with

\[
\alpha(f)=(f(\epsilon),a\mapsto\partial_a f),\qquad
(\partial_a f)(w)=f(aw).
\]

The native `ActionSource` realizes (c); `action_congruence` realizes its finite behavioral kernel. `nerode_fixpoint` is the one-action specialization. A chronological intervention between different charts is a separate typed object, not automatically this stationary coalgebra.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**CLASSICAL.** For every such \(c\), exactly one coalgebra morphism \(\beta_c:X\to Z\) exists:

\[
\beta_c(x)(w)=o(\delta_w(x)),\qquad
\alpha\beta_c=(\mathrm{id}_O\times\beta_c^A)c.
\]

Its kernel \(K=\{(x,y):\forall w\in A^*,\beta_c(x)(w)=\beta_c(y)(w)\}\) is the greatest report-preserving action congruence. The quotient is isomorphic to the **image** of \(\beta_c\), not necessarily to all \(Z\). No finiteness hypothesis is required for this theorem.

## 5. Proof attempt

Existence: the displayed definition gives the root report and, for every (a,w),
\(\beta_c(\delta_a x)(w)=o(\delta_w\delta_a x)=\beta_c(x)(aw)\); hence the square commutes. If (h:X\to Z) is another morphism, its root equation gives (h(x)(\epsilon)=o(x)). Inductively,
\(h(x)(aw)=h(\delta_a x)(w)=o(\delta_w\delta_a x)\).
Thus (h=\beta_c) by function extensionality, proving finality.

If (xKy), roots agree and \(\delta_a x\,K\,\delta_a y\) by evaluating prefixed words. Conversely, any report-preserving action congruence (R) preserves every word by induction, so (R\subseteq K). Define (Q=X/K), (\bar o([x])=o(x)), and \(\bar\delta_a([x])=[\delta_a x]\). These maps are well-defined; the quotient projection commutes with reports/transitions. The map \(j([x])=\beta_c(x)\) is an injective coalgebra morphism onto \(\beta_c(X)\). For every other congruence (R), the actual map \([x]_R\mapsto[x]_K\) gives (X/R\to Q). These are maps and equations, not diagrammatic analogy.

## 6. Crucible

**Relational control, PROVED LOCAL.** For (O\times\mathcal P_f^+(X)^A), use one action and four states. Reports of (p,q,r) are 0; (d) reports 1. Successors are
\(p\mapsto\{p,r,d\},q\mapsto\{q,d\},r\mapsto\{r\},d\mapsto\{d\}\).
Both (p,q) have exactly the nonempty finite output traces (0^k) and (0^k1^m), (k,m\ge1). They are not bisimilar: the (p\to r) branch cannot match (q\to q) or (q\to d). The first match fails because (q) can report 1 next and (r) cannot; the second already disagrees at the root. This is smallest within complete unary Moore systems: with at most three states, a trace-equivalent pair has equal output; either all outputs agree, yielding universal bisimulation, or the remaining state has a different output. Equal two-report traces then force identical successor supports in the two output classes, which form a bisimulation.

**Probabilistic control, PROVED LOCAL.** Use reports 0 except absorbing (z_1), which reports 1; (z_0) absorbs at 0. Let
\(p\mapsto\tfrac12z_0+\tfrac12u, q\mapsto r, u\mapsto z_1, r\mapsto\tfrac12z_0+\tfrac12z_1\).
Both roots emit 00 and then an absorbing fair 0/1, so every finite trace probability agrees. Probabilistic bisimulation fails: (u) is distinguished from (z_0,r) by next-report probabilities, while (p,q) assign its class masses (1/2,0). No global minimality claim is made for this six-state control.

**History control, PROVED LOCAL.** A first fair bit, followed by 0, then repetition of the first bit, gives histories 00 and 10 with identical last report and different next laws. A last-report state cannot support a stationary successor. Full public history supports the native finite-horizon `OperationalKernel`; its terminal boundary remains explicit.

## 7. Verdict

**SURVIVES / CLASSICAL:** deterministic finality and kernel quotient. **FAILS / REFUTED:** unconditional identification of relational/probabilistic trace equality with branching bisimulation; receipts above. Final semantics depends on the declared category and functor. Suitable trace finality after determinization or Kleisli lifting does not establish finality for the original Set branching functor. Unrestricted powerset functors may lack a Set final coalgebra entirely. **TESTED MODEL:** the executable finite maps and controls.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

History compression requires a sufficient state: conditional next-output **and next-compressed-state** laws must agree on each compressed-history fiber, for every action. Equal current labels alone fail. Clock/remaining horizon must be retained where needed. The theorem concerns a declared mathematical source: κ is simulator contact; φ is relative to its action language; σ is source-contract correctness; commitment ρ supplies no evidence. Neither source authentication nor human computation follows. Universal quantification over finite words is not infinite performed computation.
