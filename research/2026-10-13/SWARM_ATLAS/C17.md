# SWM-C17 — Source-changing joint laws and a split opfibration

## 1. Original question
Which stochastic structure preserves original-source provenance through genuinely changing carriers, and when does a probability-valued successor descend to an old report?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
Finite Markov kernels, Chapman–Kolmogorov composition, conditional distributions and the category of elements of a covariant functor are classical. Primary context: Tobias Fritz, *A synthetic approach to Markov kernels, conditional independence and theorems on sufficient statistics*, Advances in Mathematics 370 (2020), 107239, [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.07021). ## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Fix finite original labels O, a finite current carrier X and a normalized rational joint law J on O×X. An instrument I:X→Δ_Q(R×Y) supplies the joint report/current-state law, with declared source and input/output carrier tags. The current state is sufficient for the next instrument kernel **conditional on the declared complete model**. Persistent latent noise must be included in X. Actual records are finite ordered instrument/outcome pairs, with conditional and path probabilities.

For an observed r of positive probability,

\[
J_r(o,y)=\frac{\sum_x J(o,x)I(r,y\mid x)}{\sum_{o,x,y}J(o,x)I(r,y\mid x)}.
\]

The model does not identify a physical preparation from its string source ID.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For every finite O, F_O:FinStoch_Q→Set, F_O(X)=Δ_Q(O×X), F_O(K)J(o,y)=Σ_x J(o,x)K(y|x), is a functor. Its category of elements has a split opfibration to FinStoch_Q. For every finite I and total reports q:X→A, t:Y→B, a law-valued V:A→Δ_Q(R×B) satisfying V(q(x))=(id_R×t)_*I_x exists on q(X) **iff** that pushed law is constant on every q-fiber. The coarsest refinement preserving q and this law is q*(x)=(q(x),L_x).

## 5. Proof attempt
Normalization follows by summing K's rows. Identity preservation is immediate. F_O(LK)J=Σ_x,z J(o,x)K(z|x)L(y|z)=F_O(L)F_O(K)J by finite sum interchange. In the category of elements, the canonical arrow (X,J)→(Y,F_O(K)J) over K is opcartesian: any arrow over H∘K factors uniquely through it over H; the equality defining its target law supplies existence, and the base arrow H specifies uniqueness. These choices respect composition, giving a split opfibration.

For descent, necessity substitutes two x with the same q-value. For sufficiency choose any representative of each realized q-fiber; fiber constancy makes V well defined. If another refinement h preserves q and L, h(x)=h(x') implies q*(x)=q*(x'), so q* factors through h on its image. Sequential conditioning agrees with conditioning the composed joint instrument whenever the realized joint event has positive mass: intermediate normalization cancels against its predictive probability. The retained original index is never summed out in this proof.

## 6. Crucible
Equal supports fail probability descent: two hidden states with old report 0 emit yes/no with probabilities (1/4,3/4) and (3/4,1/4), with identical singleton post-state. `law_descent` returns witness (1,0); q* has two classes. Two preparations can have identical original and current marginals while differing in their coupling: the diagonal fair law versus the independent fair 2×2 law produce original posteriors (0,1) versus (1/2,1/2) after reading current=1.

The sibling's fixed-carrier factorization T(y|x)E(r|y) is strictly narrower than a general instrument: reset both inputs to one y while reporting their original bit. Then T=1 forces the same E for both inputs, contradicting distinct reports. State augmentation can repair the factorization, but changes the declared post-carrier. An impossible event is refused. Stale source/carrier maps and corrupted replay are refused. Composition is checked against a separate explicit original/intermediate/final path sum.

## 7. Verdict
**PROVED LOCAL** general finite statements; classical mathematics made explicit in SUCC. Exact implementation tested. No Lean kernel check. A total fibration for **realized normalized outcomes** is not claimed: the zero subkernel has no positive-event lift from any preparation. The split opfibration statement applies to unconditioned stochastic pushforward.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
A source-aware category of partial conditioning morphisms, with all null-event domains and preparation comparisons, remains to be formalized. A Markov state that omits persistent latent memory invalidates the declared kernel law.

## 9. Cross-domain connection
The map I↦supp(I) gives the relational successor, but forgets probabilities and cannot recover I. Dirac kernels embed deterministic causal descent. Classical diagonal density matrices embed these laws into C19 quantum instruments; C21 policies use J rather than its marginals. These are explicit maps, not physical identifications.
