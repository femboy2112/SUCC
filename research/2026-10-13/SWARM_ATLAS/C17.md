## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Fix finite nonempty original labels \(S\), current carriers \(Q\), actions \(A\),
and reports \(O\). An actual public history is the ordered word
\(h=((a_1,o_1),\ldots,(a_n,o_n))\), distinct from arrival time or prediction.
An intervention has a complete nonnegative normalized rational joint kernel
\(K_{h,a}(o,q'\mid s,q)\). Original \(s\) remains unchanged; current \(q\) may be
erased. Start with a rational joint prior; the conditional law is the
**joint** \(\mu_h(s,q)\). Every kernel row sums over both \(o,q'\) to one.
A randomized observer policy satisfies \(P(a\mid h,s,q)=\pi_h(a)\), including
seed independence. Apply each actual event once in causal order.
All actions are defined on every declared carrier state; partial admissibility
is excluded. Kernels are mathematical premises, not authenticated instruments.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**C17-T (PROVED LOCAL).** For all such experiments and surjections
\(f:S\to\bar S\), \(g:Q\to\bar Q\), retain the same action/report grammar and
require, for every \(h,a,s,q,o,z\), the law square

\[
\tag{★}
\sum_{q':g(q')=z}K_{h,a}(o,q'\mid s,q)
=\bar K_{h,a}(o,z\mid f(s),g(q)).
\]

Write \(P=(f\times g)_*\), initialize \(\bar\mu_\varnothing=P\mu_\varnothing\).
Every positive-probability finite actual history has identical predictive
probabilities and \(\bar\mu_h=P\mu_h\).
Law squares compose under successive source/carrier maps and chronological
instruments. Equation (★) is necessary for preserving the complete unnormalized
reported law for **every** prior, but not necessarily one restricted prior.

## 5. Proof attempt

Define the unnormalized update

\[
(T_{h,a,o}\nu)(s,q')=\sum_q\nu(s,q)K_{h,a}(o,q'\mid s,q).
\]

Summing over source/carrier fibers and applying (★) gives
\(PT\nu=\bar TP\nu\). Pushforward preserves total mass, so both sides have the
same \(z=P(o\mid h,\operatorname{do}(a))\). Divide by \(z>0\).
The common policy factor cancels on positive realized actions. Induction proves
the history statement; normalizers telescope to the intervention-word likelihood.
Multiplying by the common policy factors gives the complete public-history likelihood.
Expanding products and summing intermediate carriers proves chronological
associativity by finite distributivity.

For another square with maps \(u,v\), partition the composite fiber:

\[
\sum_{v(g(q'))=w}K(o,q'\mid s,q)
=\sum_{v(z)=w}\bar K(o,z\mid f(s),g(q))
=\widehat K(o,w\mid u(f(s)),v(g(q))).
\]

Pushforwards compose; identities satisfy (★). Necessity follows by testing
the unnormalized equality on each Dirac prior \(\delta_{(s,q)}\).

## 6. Crucible

Let \(S=Q=O=\{0,1\}\), \(f=\mathrm{id}\), \(g(q)=*\).
Two joint priors have diagonal entries respectively \(3/8,1/8\), with
off-diagonals respectively \(1/8,3/8\). Both have full support, uniform marginals,
and identical coarse joint priors. The action reports \(o=q\), then sets
\(q'=0\). After the same \(o=0\), both predictive probabilities are \(1/2\), but
\(P(S=0\mid o=0)\) is respectively \(3/4\) and \(1/4\).
Posterior supports and destructive endpoints still agree.
No single coarse kernel acting on that identical coarse prior reproduces both.
Equation (★) fails: the report varies inside the erased carrier fiber.
Each nontrivial coordinate is binary; a singleton coordinate cannot express
this correlated source/report separation.

The exact probe also mutates kernel weights while preserving support and
normalization, rejects floats and zero-mass conditioning, and checks a
hidden-dependent policy changing the original posterior from \(1/2\) to \(1\).

## 8. Remaining obstruction

replace original truth. Delayed packets require established event order.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

Let \(H\) be the free **typed mathematical word** category, containing even
impossible reports, and \(F(h)\) the cone of nonnegative
finite rational measures on \(S\times Q\). Send each edge to \(T\).
Products define a genuine functor \(F:H\to\mathrm{Set}\).
Its category of elements has objects \((h,\nu)\); an arrow over \(w:h\to h'\)
exists uniquely when \(\nu'=F(w)\nu\). Projection is a **discrete
opfibration**: \((h,\nu)\to(h',F(w)\nu)\) is the unique lift; any further
extension factors uniquely through it by functoriality.
Equation (★) makes \(P\) a natural transformation and an
opcartesian-lift-preserving map of these opfibrations.
Normalization is partial on positive mass. Support relations discard the
weights essential to this law-valued construction.

The zero measure remains a legitimate cone object, so impossible report edges
still have unique unnormalized lifts. This solves the categorical closure
problem without inventing a conditional distribution on an impossible event.
Any original predicate lost by the second source quotient is deliberately
outside that quotient's claim domain. Diagram coherence therefore preserves
specified probability laws and factorable targets, not arbitrary source truth.
