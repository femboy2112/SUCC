## 5. Proof attempt: finished finite proof

For supported \(y\), put \(\alpha_y=u(\cdot,y)/s(y)\),
\(\beta_y=v(y,\cdot)/s(y)\). The posterior fiber matrix is

\[
p_y=(s(y)/Z)\operatorname{diag}(\alpha_y)
\ell_y\operatorname{diag}(\beta_y).
\]

On restricted supports both diagonal matrices are invertible, so its rank
equals that of \(\ell_y\). Any Markov-glued fiber is an outer product and
therefore has rank at most one: necessity.

Conversely, a nonzero nonnegative matrix with vanishing minors admits a positive
pivot \(M_{a_0b_0}\), and
\(M_{ab}=M_{ab_0}M_{a_0b}/M_{a_0b_0}\). Thus each nonzero posterior fiber is an
outer product. Its row sums times column sums divided by total mass reproduce
the matrix exactly. Zero fibers reconstruct as zero. This proves sufficiency
without assuming strictly positive likelihoods.

The correct local update maps are explicit:
\(\lambda_A=\sum_b\beta_y(b)\ell(a,y,b)\),
\(\lambda_B=\sum_a\alpha_y(a)\ell(a,y,b)\), and
\(\lambda_Y=\sum_{a,b}\alpha_y(a)\beta_y(b)\ell(a,y,b)\).
They yield \(p_{AY}=u\lambda_A/Z\), \(p_{YB}=v\lambda_B/Z\),
\(p_Y=s\lambda_Y/Z\). Consequently the reconstructed law is
\(q\lambda_A\lambda_B/(Z\lambda_Y)\) where \(\lambda_Y>0\).
An equivalent exact criterion is
\(\ell=\lambda_A\lambda_B/\lambda_Y\) there. If \(\lambda_Y=0\), nonnegativity
makes the entire supported likelihood fiber zero.

For shared \(L\), all three local likelihoods equal \(L\), so one factor
cancels. Keeping the old separator leaves \(L^2\). Independently normalizing
arbitrary side likelihoods is a different map and need not even produce
compatible separators.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

For each separator fiber, target-event marginal probabilities \(p_y,r_y\) admit
exact intersection range
\([\max(0,p_y+r_y-1),\min(p_y,r_y)]\); a four-cell binary law realizes every
point. Markov gluing selects \(p_yr_y\) inside that range. With fair marginals,
both the collider intersection \(1/2\) and reglued \(1/4\) are admissible; marginals
do not select between them.

To attain either summed endpoint, independently choose its binary coupling
inside each supported fiber. Lift each binary cell using the original marginal
conditional distributions within the corresponding event/complement blocks.
Cell masses reproduce the required block marginals; summing the lifted law
therefore recovers \(u,v\). Empty blocks carry zero cell mass. This proves
sharpness, rather than assuming that endpoint laws describe actual carriers.

The forgetful map \(p\mapsto\{x:p(x)>0\}\) connects probability capsules to
EODCS supports. For nonnegative finite kernels,
\(\operatorname{supp}(LK)(x)=\bigcup_{z\in\operatorname{supp}K(x)}
\operatorname{supp}L(z)\). Yet the strictly positive counterexample leaves all
supports unchanged while breaking probabilistic CI. Relational compatibility
therefore cannot certify Bayes/gluing commutation.
