# SWM-C18 — Bayesian gluing: separator correction and duplicated evidence

## 1. Original question
Exactly when does Bayesian conditioning commute with conditional-product gluing, and what happens when two receipts reuse one latent event?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
Conditional independence and conditional products are classical. Primary sources: A. P. Dawid, *Conditional Independence in Statistical Theory*, JRSS B 41 (1979), 1–15, [publisher](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb/article/41/1/1/7027599); Fritz (2020), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.07021). Repository `source_coupling_bounds.py` gives Fréchet intervals; `heuristic_joint_noise.py`, origin commit `45597a5750971130e3399ed69c03e107bdc28c70`, already guards repeated evidence.

Sibling `b2774ca485db4b5b4f7ce92e535220352fb3af2f:observational_succ/source_aware_amalgamation.py`, blob `5ef028a6ab3b8fcaae7285101a373fff91a5a29b`, implements `markov_amalgamate` and `separator_bayes_square` under explicit CI and matching context. The separator-only square is sound; this expedition derives a larger factorized-evidence square and independently reproduces the duplicate counterexample.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
One declared source law P(z,a,b)=p(z)α_z(a)β_z(b), finite alphabets and rational probabilities. The common separator Z and both full conditional kernels are inputs. Evidence likelihood ℓ(z,a,b) is a declared joint law, not a product inferred from marginal supports. Arrival, event and source context must be compatible before using it.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For every such CI law and nonnegative likelihood ℓ(z,a,b)=u(z,a)v(z,b) with positive evidence mass, conditioning and conditional-product reconstruction commute if the **common separator** is updated to

\[
p'(z)=p(z)A_z B_z/Z,\quad
A_z=\sum_a\alpha_z(a)u(z,a),\quad B_z=\sum_b\beta_z(b)v(z,b),
\]

and the conditional factors become α'_z(a)=α_z(a)u(z,a)/A_z, β'_z(b)=β_z(b)v(z,b)/B_z on positive fibers. For each active z on the positive α×β support, posterior CI holds **iff** the likelihood matrix there has rank one. Zero posterior separator fibers impose no conditional-law constraint.

## 5. Proof attempt
Substitution gives p'α'β'=pαβuv/Z, exactly the direct Bayesian posterior. Null fibers contribute zero and may use arbitrary normalized representatives. Conversely, posterior CI means its positive-fiber matrix has rank one. Multiplication of the likelihood matrix by nonzero diagonal α and β matrices and a positive normalization preserves rank, proving necessity. Any nonnegative rank-one matrix has outer-product factors, proving sufficiency on that support.

Separator-only evidence ℓ(z) changes p to pℓ/Z and leaves α,β fixed. Both updated marginals have this **same** p'; Markov gluing divides their product by p', so the shared evidence is counted once. Treating those marginals as independent likelihoods instead would insert ℓ². For different local evidence u,v, their standalone updated separator laws need not agree; recomputing pA B/Z is necessary.

## 6. Crucible
Prior P(H)=1/2 and one latent yes-event with P(yes|H)=3/4, P(yes|K)=1/4 produce posterior 3/4. Repeating the same readout has conditional yes-probability 1 under both hypotheses and leaves posterior **3/4**. Fresh independent conditional draws produce **9/10**. Exact histories retain the latent coupling.

Conditioning two initially independent fair bits on A=B yields posterior diag(1/2,1/2), determinant 1/4: the nonfactor likelihood destroys CI. Equal yes marginals 3/4 under H and 1/4 under K alone permit a posterior interval [2/3,1] for two yes-events, by the sharp Fréchet choices t_H∈[1/2,3/4], t_K∈[0,1/4]. Independence's 9/10 is a selected coupling. Zero evidence and malformed likelihood shapes are refused.

## 7. Verdict
**PROVED LOCAL**, with classical attribution; five exact Bayes controls and independently constructed latent histories. Broad unconditional “Bayes always commutes with gluing” is **REFUTED** by equality evidence. The sibling's stated separator-only theorem survives.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
Unknown source coupling needs a credal family or additional joint observations. Neither separate model names nor distinct event IDs determine that coupling. Nonfactor evidence requires retaining a joint capsule rather than forcing a CI product.
