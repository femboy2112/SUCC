\[
E_n=T_n^*(I_{H_n}),\quad p_n(\rho)=\operatorname{tr}(\rho E_n),\quad

## 4. Candidate theorem

**PROVED LOCAL:**

\[
\bigl(\forall n<\infty\ \exists\rho_n\in C:\ p_n(\rho_n)>0\bigr)
\Longrightarrow
\exists\rho\in C\ \forall n<\infty:\ p_n(\rho)>0.
\]

Moreover, \(I=E_0\ge E_1\ge\cdots\ge0\), and \(\ker E_n\) eventually stabilizes. No compactness or convexity of \(C\) is required. This is a support statement, not source identification or a positive infinite-event probability theorem.

## 5. Proof attempt

Trace nonincrease gives \(0\le\Phi_{n+1}^*(I)\le I\). Positivity of \(T_n^*\) yields

\[
0\le E_{n+1}=T_n^*\Phi_{n+1}^*(I)\le T_n^*(I)=E_n.
\]

For positive operators \(B\le A\), \(Av=0\) implies \(\langle v,Bv\rangle=0\), hence \(Bv=0\). Thus the kernels increase. Each strict inclusion raises dimension, so there are at most \(d\) strict increases. Their finite set of indices has a last member: some \(N\) satisfies \(\ker E_n=\ker E_N\) for all \(n\ge N\).

For density \(\rho\),

\[
\operatorname{tr}(\rho E)=\|E^{1/2}\rho^{1/2}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2=0
\iff\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\ker E.
\]

Choose the premise's witness \(\rho_N\). Kernel equality preserves its positive likelihood after \(N\); decreasing effects preserve it before \(N\). This completes the proof. Under the premise, ranks stay positive, so at most \(d-1\) strict increases occur. The argument actually needs positivity and trace nonincrease; CP additionally licenses the quantum framework.

In its consistent probability law, the fixed infinite outcome event has probability zero by continuity from above. Nothing performs infinitely many measurements.

\[

C=\{\rho_0=|e_0\rangle\langle e_0|\}\cup

This principal-suggested rational parametrization refines the independently found tail-projection countermodel. Since \(\|\rho_k-\rho_0\|_1=2/\sqrt{k^2+1}\), \(C\) is trace-norm compact.
