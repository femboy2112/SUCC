# SWM-C19 — Multi-Kraus causal instruments and retained source labels

## 1. Original question
Can finite quantum instrument histories preserve the meaning of recorded original-source outcomes while the reduced carrier loses its distinguishability?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
E. B. Davies and J. T. Lewis, *An operational approach to quantum probability*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 17 (1970), 239–260, [primary paper](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/communications-in-mathematical-physics/volume-17/issue-3/An-operational-approach-to-quantum-probability/cmp/1103842336.pdf), [publisher DOI](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01647093). CP/Kraus instrument composition is established mathematics. `quantum_records.py`, last originating commit `4e95c4ce9481d79fdd14a777e8eb1abb64d5b594`, has exact real-qubit single-Kraus outcomes and replayable records; it is left unchanged.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Finite-dimensional density matrices ρ and outcome CP maps E_r(ρ)=Σ_i K_{ri}ρK†_{ri}, with Σ_{r,i}K†_{ri}K_{ri}=I. A preparation label θ is classical original-source provenance, not the altered density matrix. The record is the ordered actual outcome path. Our additive implementation restricts matrices to exact rational real entries and dimensions ≤4, with arbitrary finite Kraus multiplicity per outcome.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For every two composable finite instruments E,F, the sequential instrument G_(r,s)=F_s∘E_r is complete and CP, with Kraus family {L_sj K_ri}. For every positive-probability outcome path, staged normalization gives the same posterior and path probability as conditioning the composed CP map once. Given a finite ensemble π_θρ_θ, original-label posterior weights satisfy

\[
P(\theta\mid h)=\pi_\theta\operatorname{tr}(E_h(\rho_\theta))
 /\sum_\eta\pi_\eta\operatorname{tr}(E_h(\rho_\eta)).
\]

Equal terminal carrier states do not imply equal recorded outcome experiments.

## 5. Proof attempt
Kraus expansion directly gives F_s(E_rρ)=Σ_i,j(L_sjK_ri)ρ(L_sjK_ri)†. Positivity persists after tensoring with any identity because each summand is A B A† for PSD B; hence CP. Summing completeness gives Σ_r,i K†_ri(Σ_s,j L†_sjL_sj)K_ri=I. Staged posterior normalization cancels: the second conditional probability is tr(F_s E_rρ)/tr(E_rρ), so its product with the first probability is tr(F_s E_rρ). Induction proves finite paths. Applying the same unnormalized CP map separately to each ensemble block π_θρ_θ and Bayes-normalizing proves the original-label equation.

## 6. Crucible
The reset channel has one outcome and two Kraus operators K0=|0><0|, K1=|0><1|. It sends both |0> and |1> to |0>. Composing a Z measurement **before** reset retains outcomes 0 versus 1 with probability 1 for the respective original states, although both terminal carriers are identical. Replacing that recorded instrument by the unrecorded reset removes the original discriminator.

X then Z has every outcome-pair probability 1/4 from |0>; the independently staged calculation matches composed Kraus probabilities and posteriors exactly. Incomplete Kraus families and a trace-one symmetric non-PSD 3×3 density matrix are rejected. Completeness is exact, and PSD is checked by **all** principal minors, not only leading minors.

## 7. Verdict
**CLASSICAL** theorem, with a **TESTED MODEL** implementation of the stated real rational finite subcategory. Three independent finite controls. No Lean kernel check, no measured hardware, no human cognitive realization, no physical claim about energy, and no arithmetic positivity inference.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
The code evaluates instrument composition and individual normalized density matrices; an immutable unknown-preparation ensemble history object and general complex arithmetic are not implemented. The ensemble equation above is a paper derivation, not an executed ensemble-history formalization.

## 9. Cross-domain connection
The block-diagonal classical embedding ρ_p=diag(p) converts each stochastic instrument atom I(r,y|x) to a Kraus operator sqrt(I(r,y|x))|y><x|. This is a lawful complex/real mathematical CP embedding; arbitrary rational probabilities can require irrational square roots, so it does **not** always land in the rational-Kraus implementation. Classical semantic readout C on recorded outcomes obeys TV(Cp,Cq)≤TV(p,q), by the triangle inequality after summing C's rows. C17 source labels and C24 memory registers supply explicit provenance/erasure comparisons.
