# SWM-C20 — Blackwell order and its backaction boundary

## 1. Original question
When can one source-relative observer simulate another, and does readout informativeness also order source-changing instruments?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
David Blackwell, *Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments*, Annals of Mathematical Statistics 24 (1953), 265–272, [primary publisher](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/annals-of-mathematical-statistics/volume-24/issue-2/Equivalent-Comparisons-of-Experiments/10.1214/aoms/1177729032.full), DOI 10.1214/aoms/1177729032. The finite garbling order and decision comparison are classical. Our exact local theorem below proves simulation and the rational feasibility procedure, not a new Blackwell theorem.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Finite experiments E(α|o), F(β|o) share the **same** original-source rows o. An allowed garbling G(β|α) cannot depend on o. Instruments additionally produce a changed current carrier; their one-step report experiment forgets this carrier. All input rows are declared complete mathematical laws, not inferred empirical frequencies.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For every finite rational E,F on a common source, F is simulable from E by a source-independent stochastic readout **iff** a nonnegative row-stochastic G solves EG=F. If a real G exists, a rational G exists. Exhaustive enumeration of full-rank basic supports in this rational finite system returns a valid rational G or certifies infeasibility; budget exhaustion is an unresolved search. A garbling simulation implies no greater expected loss for E in every finite decision problem. Readout Blackwell equivalence does **not** imply equal future policy value for instruments.

## 5. Proof attempt
Simulation's joint law is E(α|o)G(β|α), so summing α proves necessity and sufficiency. The feasible G form a closed bounded rational polytope. If nonempty, choose a feasible point of minimum positive support. Its positive columns in the equality-constraint matrix must be linearly independent: otherwise a nonzero supported null direction allows a perturbation to the boundary without violating nonnegativity, reducing support. Extend these columns to a basis for the constraint matrix; setting unused coordinates to zero gives a basic solution, whose coordinates are rational by exact Gaussian elimination. Thus enumeration of every such basis is complete. Each returned channel is independently checked against EG=F. For decision rules d(δ|β), composing Gd implements exactly the F rule using E, with identical source-dependent loss.

## 6. Crucible
Noncomparable experiments on source {0,1,2}: A separates 0 from {1,2}; B separates 1 from {0,2}. A's equal rows 1,2 cannot produce B's unequal rows through any garbling. The reverse has the analogous obstruction. Equal support alone is insufficient: the binary (3/4,1/4) versus (1/4,3/4) experiment garbles to (5/8,3/8) versus its reverse through channel ((3/4,1/4),(1/4,3/4)); reverse garbling is infeasible.

**Backaction countermodel:** original (a,b) is a uniform two-bit pair; target is original a XOR b. Two A-instruments both report a perfectly. GoodA preserves (a,b); BadA sends it to (a,0). Their report experiments are identical, so Blackwell equivalent. After GoodA, one B-read certifies original parity. After BadA, the two original b-values for any observed a have the **same current state and opposite target**; every future instrument depending only on current state gives identical output laws. Certification is impossible forever without a source-restoring action or an independent retained b-record.

## 7. Verdict
**PROVED LOCAL**, classical simulation/feasibility mathematics and an exact general counterexample to extending readout order to continuation value. Finite garbling controls and policy contrast pass. No academic originality claim; no Lean check.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
A stronger comparison of instruments must simulate the joint report/current output **and** intertwine every allowed continuation action, including costs and availability. A report-only G does not supply that map. Exhaustive basis enumeration is exponential; the bounded implementation raises `SearchBudgetError` rather than returning a false negative.

## 9. Cross-domain connection
C17's joint instrument is the richer output needed for causal simulation. A source-preserving stochastic carrier simulator T combined with report garbling must satisfy a joint square and action-intertwining squares to transport C21 policies. Without these maps, Blackwell experiment comparison and operational future equivalence are different invariants.
