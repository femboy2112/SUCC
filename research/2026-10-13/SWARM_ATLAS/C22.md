# SWM-C22 — When a causal receipt may be reordered

## 1. Original question
Which partially ordered events can replace sequential receipt order without changing original-source inference or the probability of recorded outcomes?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
Partial commutation and trace monoids formalize interchange of independent events. Primary context: *Theory of traces* (1988), [publisher article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0304397588900515), which describes the trace framework introduced by A. Mazurkiewicz. The publisher search abstract was retrieved; full text was unavailable, so no specific theorem from that paper is used as a premise. Trace constructions are **not** evidence that arbitrary physical measurements commute. The exact finite kernel theorem is derived here. Sibling `source_aware_amalgamation.py` buffers one total causal sequence and explicitly leaves partially ordered streams to additional commutation certificates.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Finite source-preserving instrument endomorphisms on a common carrier X. Let A_r(x,y)=P_A(r,y|x) and B_s(x,y)=P_B(s,y|x) be outcome subkernels. A receipt DAG has fixed uniquely labeled events, with order dependencies. Outcomes and event identities remain attached to events even when a topological ordering changes. Adaptive action choices dependent on earlier reports are dependencies, not free interchangeable events.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For every two finite instruments A,B, swapping the fixed events preserves the full outcome-labeled terminal joint law for **every** initial original/current J **iff**

\[
A_r B_s=B_s A_r\quad\text{for every outcome pair }r,s.
\]

If every pair of incomparable DAG events satisfies this condition on the complete carrier, all topological orders induce the same event-labeled joint law. Hence positive-probability observed outcomes have the same original posterior and joint likelihood, although their sequential histories remain different ordered records.

## 5. Proof attempt
The unnormalized original/current law of outcome pair (r,s) is J A_r B_s. Equality of matrices implies equality for every J. Conversely choose a point-mass J concentrated at each current x; equality of each terminal y atom forces equality of every matrix entry. Conditional posteriors agree whenever the common total event mass is positive.

Any two linear extensions of a finite partial order are connected by adjacent swaps of incomparable elements: move the first event of the desired extension left through the other extension; every crossed predecessor is incomparable, then induct on the remaining DAG. Each certified swap preserves the full subkernel product, proving global independence from the chosen extension. This is a theorem about fixed events and source laws, not permission to sort arbitrary delivered messages.

## 6. Crucible
Nondestructive reads of independent bit coordinates commute at every outcome subkernel; reversed conditional histories have exactly equal J and path probability. Destructive A/B reads fail with an explicit atom witness: source state 1, labeled outcome pair (0,0), terminal state 0 has probability 1 versus 0.

**Weak test refuted:** reading a bit nondestructively has unobserved transition I; flipping it has transition F. I and F commute. Nevertheless reading **before** versus **after** flip reports opposite bits. Thus equality of unobserved transition products is inadequate for receipt interchange. `commutation_defect` checks all outcome atoms rather than just terminal marginals.

## 7. Verdict
**PROVED LOCAL**, established finite partial-commutation mathematics applied to actual joint report kernels; three exact controls. A general asynchronous receipt transport runtime is not implemented. No empirical causal-order authentication or Lean check.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
Variable carrier spaces require a genuinely typed square, not this endomorphism test. State-specific commutation needs coverage of every reachable state. Correlated external noise must be retained in X; otherwise kernels may only appear to commute. Missing DAG dependencies are a provenance defect, not a mathematical theorem of concurrency.

## 9. Cross-domain connection
C17 joint kernels supply the comparison matrices; forgetting outcomes is a functor that loses the distinguishing obstruction. C19 instruments have analogous CP-map commutation conditions, which need their own full operator check. C23 predictable policies require all dependence arrows to remain in the filtration, even when network arrival order differs.
