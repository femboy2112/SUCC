## 4. Candidate theorem

**PROVED LOCAL, conditional.** Declare symmetric irreflexive \(I\). For every \(aIb\) and \(s\in D\), each enabled action preserves the other's enabled status. Whenever both are enabled, require

\[
U_bU_a(s)=U_aU_b(s),\qquad
q_a(s)=q_a(U_b(s)),\qquad q_b(s)=q_b(U_a(s)).
\]

Then every two eligible fixed-schedule words related by independent adjacent swaps have identical enabled domains, endpoints and occurrence-aligned reports, for every initial source in \(D\). Thus original-source target \(t_0:D\to T\) factors through one report map iff it factors through the other. This preserves an original target decoder, not necessarily the target's present-carrier interpretation. It does not assert equivalence of arbitrary adaptive policies.

## 5. Proof attempt

For \(vabw\), let \(s\) be the state after prefix \(v\). Persistence makes \(ab\) executable iff \(ba\) is. The square gives equal endpoints and the same outputs attached to occurrences \(a,b\); equal endpoints give identical suffix \(w\). Induct on swaps. Every pair of finite-poset linear extensions is connected by swaps of adjacent incomparable occurrences: move the first desired occurrence left, crossing only incomparable occurrences, then induct. Here every incomparable pair has labels in \(I\). Therefore evaluation descends to the trace quotient. Equal report maps have equal fibers; \(t_0\) descends precisely when constant on every fiber. The separate semantic gate \(t_0=t_{end}\circ U_{vabw}\) is also invariant under these reorderings, but is not supplied by commutation.

For finite rational kernels, use report-indexed matrices \(K_a^u(s,t)\), with rows summing to one across reports/endpoints when enabled, zero otherwise. Require domain closure, enabledness persistence along every positive-probability transition, and **each** square \(K_a^uK_b^v=K_b^vK_a^u\). The same substitution proves equal occurrence-aligned joint report/endpoint laws. A zero-error original-target decoder exists iff unlike target values never share a positive-probability report. Equal laws preserve this condition. Present-source semantics additionally requires \(t_{end}(t)=t_0(s)\) on every positive-probability terminal transition. This is a law theorem; stochastic samples are not pointwise identical executions.

## 6. Crucible

**REFUTED** endpoint-only reordering: native destructive bit reads \(A:(a,b)\mapsto(a,0)\), \(B:(a,b)\mapsto(0,b)\) end at `00` in either order. At source `01`, `AB` reports `(0,0)`, `BA` reports `(1,0)` in execution order; original XOR collides between `00` and `01` under `AB`.

**REFUTED** summed-kernel sufficiency: nondestructive bit measurement has total kernel \(1\); flip has kernel \(X\). Totals commute, but branch projector \(P_0X\ne XP_0\). **REFUTED** statistical independence from squares: nondestructive separate-bit probes, prior half `00`/half `11`, give joint `00` probability \(1/2\ne1/4\). **REFUTED** ignoring menus: flip followed by a probe enabled only at `0` fails from `0`, while the reverse succeeds. Native reversible shears supply an endpoint-order counterexample at source `01`.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

For separate carriers and rectangular domains, horizontal composition is \(q_A\times q_B\), \(U_A\times U_B\), \(V_A\times V_B\). From \(q'_AU_A=V_Aq_A\) and \(q'_BU_B=V_Bq_B\), their product square follows coordinatewise. With exactly matching intermediate source/basis/epoch/report contracts,

\[
(U_2\times W_2)\circ(U_1\times W_1)
=(U_2\circ U_1)\times(W_2\circ W_1).
\]

Identities and associativity hold under canonical product identifications. Kernels use an **assumed** product intervention law and local menus: row-matrix convention gives \((K_1\otimes L_1)(K_2\otimes L_2)=(K_1K_2)\otimes(L_1L_2)\). Each entry follows by factoring the finite sum over intermediate coordinate pairs. For deterministic product reports, \(\ker(q_A\times q_B)=\ker q_A\times\ker q_B\), restricted to the declared source class. Shared randomness, shared mutable state and synchronized guards need a joint law instead. Correlated initial source laws remain permissible, but product marginal outcomes require further assumptions. These maps connect causal descent to asynchronous composition without identifying covariance with source independence.
