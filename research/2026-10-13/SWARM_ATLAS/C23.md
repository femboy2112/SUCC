# SWM-C23 — Evidence wealth requires actual conditional history laws

## 1. Original question
What can sequential source-calibrated confidence establish, and does reusing marginal likelihoods for dependent receipts preserve anytime validity?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
G. Shafer, A. Shen, N. Vereshchagin and V. Vovk, *Test Martingales, Bayes Factors and p-Values*, Statistical Science 26 (2011), 84–101, [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.4269). Likelihood-ratio martingales, bounded optional stopping and Ville's inequality are classical. `heuristic_evidence_martingales.py`, origin `45597a5750971130e3399ed69c03e107bdc28c70`, already supplies model-probe betting and a finite adaptive Ville audit. Our additive audit filters the evolving null and alternative joint laws separately.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
One public-history filtration and a predictable action policy. Null P and alternative Q have declared source-compatible instrument laws; the implemented audit varies initial joint preparation laws under a common instrument contract. On each reachable history, Q's predictive outcome law is absolutely continuous with respect to P's. The alternative may assign zero to a P-positive branch. All probabilities are exact model laws, not empirical calibration certificates.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For every such pair and predictable policy, L_0=1 and

\[
L_{n+1}=L_n\,\frac{Q(R_{n+1}\mid h_n,A_{n+1})}
 {P(R_{n+1}\mid h_n,A_{n+1})}
\]

form a nonnegative P-martingale. For every c>1,
P(sup_n L_n≥c)≤1/c, and bounded stopping preserves E_P L_τ=1. This theorem controls error against the declared null; it does not prove a mathematical proposition, source ontology or source exhaustiveness.

## 5. Proof attempt
Conditioning on a public h, the chosen action is fixed. Summing the next outcome gives E_P[L_(n+1)|h]=L_n Σ_r Q(r|h,a)=L_n, using conditional absolute continuity. The product of ratios telescopes to the full **path** likelihood ratio, even if observations are dependent or carrier states change. Stop at the first threshold crossing or finite N: bounded optional stopping gives E L_(τ∧N)=1, while nonnegativity and value ≥c on crossing give P(τ≤N)≤1/c. Increasing union over N proves Ville's bound. No integrability assertion for arbitrary unbounded stopping is inferred.

## 6. Crucible
Let P(yes)=1/4, Q(yes)=3/4 and make the second receipt a literal duplicate. Correct second predictive ratios are 1. The true terminal wealth is 3 on yes and 1/3 on no; E_P L=1. Naively multiplying the original marginal ratio twice yields 9 and 1/9, expectation

\[
(1/4)9+(3/4)(1/9)=7/3.
\]

At threshold 9, the fake crossing probability is 1/4>1/9: an exact failure of the claimed Ville calibration. The history-conditioned audit gives crossing probability 0. A genuinely predictable adaptive flip/read policy at threshold 3 gives crossing 1/4 and expectation 1. A singular alternative assigning mass to a null-impossible report is refused rather than divided by zero.

## 7. Verdict
**CLASSICAL** martingale/Ville theorem with three exact model controls. Marginal-likelihood reuse under dependent evidence is **REFUTED** by the 7/3 expectation and 1/4 versus 1/9 crossing witness. No Lean check or empirical statistical guarantee is claimed.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
Actual conditional law calibration, hidden latent memory, model misspecification and source completeness remain external gates. An arbitrary adaptive policy looking at hidden original labels is not predictable in the public filtration. Infinite-horizon correctness is the proof above, not the bounded audit executed as an infinity.
