# SWM-C24 — Exact reversible memory debt versus thermodynamic heat

## 1. Original question
What does source information erasure force mathematically, and which additional physical assumptions are needed before converting it into a heat bound?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
R. Landauer, *Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process*, IBM Journal of Research and Development 5 (1961), 183–191, [primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1147/RD.53.0183). D. Reeb and M. M. Wolf, *An improved Landauer Principle with finite-size corrections*, New Journal of Physics 16 (2014), 103011, [author paper, setup and Theorem 3](https://arxiv.org/html/1306.4352v3). Reversible computation and finite entropy chain rules are established mathematics. No new physical law is proposed.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Finite complete deterministic source map f:X→Y; an extra memory alphabet M and map m:X→M making x↦(f(x),m(x)) injective. Separately, a random X with declared law p defines Shannon entropy in bits. A physical erasure model additionally specifies all interacting registers, reservoir Hamiltonian, initial thermal state, temperature and allowed unitary evolution; these are **not** contained in a stochastic kernel.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For every nonempty finite f, the least cardinality of an auxiliary memory permitting an injective lift is exactly

\[
\min |M|=\max_{y\in f(X)}|f^{-1}(y)|.
\]

For every finite random X, H(X)−H(f(X))=H(X|f(X))≥0. In the distinct physical model with initially product system/reservoir state, thermal reservoir ρ_R=e^(−βH_R)/Z and joint unitary U, the **classical attributed** equality in natural-log units is βQ=ΔS+I(S':R')+D(ρ'_R||ρ_R)≥ΔS for finite positive β. A kernel without those hypotheses entails no energy number.

## 5. Proof attempt
Any fiber f^−1(y) must map to distinct memory labels, proving the cardinal lower bound by pigeonhole. Number each fiber's elements 0,…,|fiber|−1; taking the maximum fiber size supplies an injective lift and proves sharpness. Fixed-width binary memory therefore needs at least ceil(log2 max|fiber|) bits; this is a worst-case source cardinal bound, not an average thermodynamic entropy.

Since Y=f(X), H(Y|X)=0. Expand H(X,Y) as H(X)+H(Y|X)=H(Y)+H(X|Y), proving the information identity. For the attributed thermal equality, unitarity and the initial product give ΔS_R=ΔS+I(S':R'). Expanding D against logρ_R=−βH_R−logZ gives D=βQ−ΔS_R. Combine; nonnegativity yields the heat bound. This short derivation uses the paper's stated physical assumptions, not Python simulation.

## 6. Crucible
Resetting a complete two-state carrier requires two memory labels for an injective lift; an invertible permutation needs one. Fiber sizes 3 and 2 need exactly three; independently enumerating every two-label assignment on the three-element fiber refutes smaller memory. A fair reset bit loses exactly one bit of carrier entropy; a known fixed zero bit has entropy zero and cannot justify a universal “one event costs kT ln2” claim.

**Assumption mutation:** a perfectly correlated fair pair (S,R)=(B,B) with degenerate H_R=0 has a thermal uniform reservoir marginal, but its joint initial state is not a product. The unitary classical CNOT S←S XOR R sends S to zero while retaining fair R, dissipating Q=0. Thus system entropy decrease alone does not imply a positive heat cost after the initial-independence assumption is removed. An external retained record similarly changes the full resource bookkeeping.

## 7. Verdict
**PROVED LOCAL** sharp finite memory theorem and exact controls. **CLASSICAL** information identity and conditional thermodynamic equality. No physical measurement, energy estimate, cognitive claim, RH inference or Lean check. Information lost from a current carrier can remain in the original-label/record joint state.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
Converting SUCC report processing into heat requires an explicitly modeled physical implementation, all correlated memories and the thermal environment. Average coding entropy, zero-error fiber cardinality and bath heat are distinct resource measures. Changing probability support changes entropy but not the complete-source cardinal theorem unless its domain is also changed.

## 9. Cross-domain connection

Projecting away them is an information-losing map; keeping an injective lift explains how backaction and non-erasing history can coexist. C21's no-reset impossibility concerns operational accessibility of that memory, not an energy prohibition.
