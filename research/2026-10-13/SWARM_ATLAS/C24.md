# C24 — Information erasure and conditional physical cost

## 1. Original question

When a finite SUCC carrier or semantic representation forgets distinctions, what
information is lost, what reversible memory would preserve it, and when does a
thermal cost follow? The faithful steelman concerns embodied recording and
reuse. An abstract forgetful arrow alone supplies neither a reservoir nor an
energy scale. This dossier is independent of other first-generation dossiers.

## 2. Existing mathematical context

Finite Shannon chain rules, data processing and finite-set injections are
**CLASSICAL**. [Landauer (1961)](https://sites.pitt.edu/~jdnorton/lectures/Rotman_Summer_School_2013/thermo_computing_docs/Landauer_1961.pdf)
analyzes physical devices implementing logically irreversible functions.
[Bennett (1982)](https://research.ibm.com/publications/the-thermodynamics-of-computation-a-review)
separates reversible recording from clearing a record for reuse.
[del Rio et al. (2011), Appendix E](https://arxiv.org/html/1009.1630v2)
make accessible side information and resource consumption explicit; their work
rates require a thermal/battery setting with degenerate initial Hamiltonians.
Classical conditional entropy here cannot be negative. No novelty is claimed.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Declare finite original source \(S\), retained side information \(Z\), finite
target \(X\), and rational joint prior \(p(s,z,x)\), with marginal \(p(s,z)\).
Work only on its positive support. A total
deterministic map \(R=F(S,Z)\) packages **all accessible retained outputs**:
modified carrier, history, semantic record and provenance where retained.
Entropies use base-two logs. Define \(L=H(S\mid R,Z)\), fibers
\(A_{r,z}=\{s:p(s,z)>0,F(s,z)=r\}\), and \(M=\max|A_{r,z}|\).
These are declared mathematical sources, not authenticated physical data.

## 4. Candidate theorem

For every such law and map, **PROVED LOCAL** by the following derivation:

\[
L=H(S\mid Z)-H(R\mid Z)\ge0,
\]
\[
0\le I(X;S\mid Z)-I(X;R\mid Z)
=I(X;S\mid R,Z)\le L
\le\sum_{r,z}p(r,z)\log_2|A_{r,z}|\le\log_2M.
\]

A deterministic auxiliary \(G(S,Z)\) making \((R,Z,G)\) injective on support
requires \(|G|\ge M\), attainable by indexing each fiber; its conditional
entropy equals \(L\). Hence minimum fixed-length auxiliary storage is
\(\lceil\log_2M\rceil\) bits, which is generally different from Shannon loss.
Retaining another record \(K(S,Z)\) decreases loss by
\(I(S;K\mid R,Z)\), counted once.

## 5. Proof attempt

Because \(H(R\mid S,Z)=0\), expand \(H(S,R\mid Z)\) in both orders to obtain
the first identity. Expand \(I(X;S,R\mid Z)\) in both orders; its deterministic
\(R\)-given-\(S,Z\) term vanishes, proving the information identity. Conditional
mutual information is an average relative entropy and is nonnegative; it is
at most \(H(S\mid R,Z)\). In each fiber the entropy bound follows from
\(D(p(\cdot\mid r,z)\Vert\text{uniform}(A_{r,z}))\ge0\); average it.
The maximum bound follows termwise.

Injectivity forces distinct auxiliary labels within each fiber by pigeonhole;
fiber indices attain the bound. Determinism gives \(H(G\mid S,R,Z)=0\), while
reconstruction gives \(H(S\mid G,R,Z)=0\); the chain rule yields
\(H(G\mid R,Z)=L\). Finally conditional-entropy subtraction proves the record
identity. These derivations apply to arbitrary finite laws, not merely the
enumerated controls. Rationality makes the receipt's logarithms exact.

## 6. Crucible

The probe uses rational masses, symbolic prime logarithms and exact integer
comparisons after clearing denominators; no floating-point signs certify it.
Copying a fair bit into a blank gives loss zero. Resetting without a retained
copy loses one bit. The complete factorial toggles correlated side copy and
retained original-bit record:

| Side information | No record | Record retained |
|---|---:|---:|
| Independent fair coin | 1 | 0 |
| Copy of source | 0 | 0 |

The mixed difference is exactly one bit: duplicate information is non-additive,
not a physical mechanism. Erasing two identical fair bits loses one bit, not
two. A biased full-support bit with \(p(1)=1/1024\) still needs one auxiliary
bit, while its loss is exactly
\(10-(1023/1024)\log_2(1023)<1\).

The actual native `DestructiveCarrier.probe("a")` on four uniform sources,
including its trace, loses the original \(b\) bit and parity information;
retaining original \(b\) restores both. A stochastic report can increase
entropy: constant input and fresh fair output give \(H(S)-H(R)=-1\). This
falsifies removing the deterministic hypothesis, not data processing.

## 7. Verdict

**SURVIVES — PROVED LOCAL:** the scoped classical theorem and storage bound.
**TESTED MODEL:** ten controls, global copy/reset permutations and 256 additional
four-source binary report/target pairs. **FAILS — REFUTED:** billing one lost bit
per overwritten coordinate; receipts are duplicate-bit and retained-copy cases.
**FAILS — REFUTED:** universal heat proportional to lost mutual information:
a fair register correlated with a memory, swapped with an independent uniform
reservoir having \(H_B=0\), loses one bit of correlation with zero heat. This is
the explicit [Reeb–Wolf §5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1306.4352v3) control; it does
not reset the register to a standard value.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

For a physical register \(A\), specify \(T>0\),
\(\beta=(k_BT)^{-1}\), reservoir Hamiltonian \(H_B\), initial Gibbs reservoir
uncorrelated with register plus accessible memory, and joint unitary evolution.
[Reeb–Wolf Theorem 3](https://arxiv.org/html/1306.4352v3) gives, with natural-log
entropy,
\(\beta Q=\Delta S_A+I(A':B')+D(\rho'_B\Vert\rho_B)\).
With preserved accessible classical memory, its conditional extension bounds
heat by conditional entropy reduction. Work additionally requires the
Hamiltonian/free-energy account. For degenerate logical levels and a complete
reset, the ideal isothermal reversible limit yields the usual
\(k_BT\ln2\,H(A\mid Z)\) cost. A finite full-rank Gibbs reservoir with unitary
evolution cannot reduce register rank: exact pure reset needs an idealization.
Count reset of controller, scratch and any consumed correlations when claiming
a repeatable cycle. No such physical implementation is supplied by C24.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

For a source transition \(U:S\to S'\), carrier readout \(q\), and retained history
\(h\), set \(F(s,z)=(q(U(s)),h(s,z))\). Projection drops the auxiliary from the
injective embedding \(s\mapsto(F(s,z),z,G(s,z))\). For bits, global permutations
\((b,c)\mapsto(b,b\oplus c)\) and \((b,c)\mapsto(b\oplus c,c)\) implement
copy-to-blank then reset-with-copy on the correlated support. These are actual
finite maps. Voyager's distinct-source-state counts constrain worst-case
cardinality; they do not determine a prior's entropy or joules.
