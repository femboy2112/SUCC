## 4. Candidate theorem

For any forward-invariant set \(X\), report \(q:X\to Q=q(X)\) admits
\(G:Q\to Q\) with \(qf=Gq\) **iff**
\[
q(x)=q(y)\Longrightarrow q(f(x))=q(f(y))\qquad(x,y\in X).
\tag{D25.1}
\]
For every \(q\in\mathcal L_{\rm cyl}\) on \(J^*\), this identity holds
**iff** \(q\) is constant or \(q=\iota\phi\) for a two-label injection
\(\iota\). Thus the two-phase factor is maximal within this language, at every

factor. All statements are pointwise, without a probability-law assumption.

## 5. Proof attempt: complete proof

Necessity of D25.1 follows from applying \(G\); sufficiency defines
\(G(q(x))=q(f(x))\), well-defined by the implication.

For logistic cells, monotonicity and the endpoint orbit give
\(f(A)=f(B)=C\) and \(f(C)=(a,z)\).
Every legal finite word has a nonempty open cylinder: recursively pull back
an open interval through the corresponding monotone branches. Each inverse
image of \(D\) is finite, so \(E\) is countable. Removing \(E\) leaves points
in every such cylinder. Consequently every allowed word and successor edge is
realized by actual points of \(J^*\), rather than merely by a symbolic model.

Form the higher-block graph \(H_m\): vertices are legal \(m\)-words, with edges
\(u\to v\) when shifting \(u\) and appending a permitted cell gives \(v\).
D25.1 is exactly
\(r(v)=G(r(u))\) on every edge. The graph is strongly connected: join the last
cell of any word to the first cell of another using the strongly connected
three-cell graph, then append the second word. The image graph on \(Q\) is
therefore strongly connected and functional. Every finite strongly connected
functional graph is a single directed cycle: following successors reaches a
cycle, and strong connectivity forces every vertex onto it.

Alternating \(A,C,A,C,\ldots\) supplies a two-cycle in \(H_m\) for every \(m\).
Its image implies the cycle length of \(G\) divides two. Length one means
constant. Length two makes every edge flip labels. Every edge also flips the
first-cell phase; propagation along paths from one vertex shows the label is
determined precisely by that phase. Conversely both claimed reports commute.
The tent branches realize every binary finite word on \(X_4\) by the same open
pullback and countable-removal argument. Its \(H_m\) is strongly connected and has
the all-zero-word self-loop, forcing cycle length one. This proves every width,
not merely the enumerated widths.
