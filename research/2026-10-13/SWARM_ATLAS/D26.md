# SWM-D26 — Resolution and source time commute only in the right types

Frozen parent `5eb09e7cba36ec01b8f9d6834b78b5766fd93935`; owner dynamics cohort D.

## 1. Original question

Does source evolution commute with hierarchical observation depth, and can this
be promoted to logistic renormalization without inventing missing symbols?

## 2. Existing mathematical context

[Milnor–Thurston, *On iterated maps of the interval*, introduction and
§7](https://websites.umich.edu/~kochsc/MilnorThurston.pdf), read 2026-10-13,
develops source-dependent kneading admissibility and piecewise-linear models.
It does not justify replacing all logistic parameters by the unrestricted shift.
Existing tent cylinders and source-specific PCF partition remain the baseline.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

For the full binary one-sided shift `X={0,1}^ℕ`, let `F` delete the first symbol,
`π_m:X→X_m={0,1}^m` retain the first m, `r_m:X_{m+1}→X_m` delete the last,
and `d_m:X_{m+1}→X_m` delete the first. These are exact different maps.
At this abstract stage source words are supplied, not physically queried.

## 4. Candidate theorem

For every `m,k≥0`, `π_m F^k=d_m^(k) π_{m+k}`, where `d_m^(k)` deletes the
first k symbols. The resolution/time square obeys
`r_m d_{m+1}=d_m r_{m+1}` on `X_{m+2}`. For `m≥1`, no same-level
`G_m:X_m→X_m` can satisfy `π_m F=G_m π_m` on the full shift.

## 5. Proof attempt

Both sides of the k-step identity return coordinates k through k+m-1. Both
sides of the square remove original coordinates zero and m+1. For the no-go,
choose two infinite streams with identical first m bits and different bit m;
their next m-windows differ at the final coordinate. No deterministic function
of their equal original reports can return both answers.

This repairs the failed square by changing its domain, rather than pretending
an unseen bit exists. Once `m+k` original bits are supplied, source time and
restriction genuinely commute. The depth burden grows by k for an m-word
prediction; unrestricted terminal uncertainty has `2^min(k,m)` possibilities.

## 6. Crucible

`window_transport` refuses a length-m input for a one-step m-window request.
All binary length m+2 words, m=1,…,4, check the proper square. A restricted
periodic source can have a closed same-depth quotient, so the unrestricted
source language is a load-bearing hypothesis. PCF logistic itineraries require
their actual adjacency/admissibility language; fake arbitrary words are illegal.

## 7. Verdict

**CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL**: the typed shift/refinement identities and
same-depth no-go. **BLOCKED**: a universal source-faithful logistic
renormalization/observation-depth commutation theorem.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

Renormalization requires a specified restrictive interval J, first return
`F^p:J→J`, a legitimate scaling homeomorphism `h:J→I`, and the transported
readout `q_R=q h^-1`. The source-depth map is `R(F)=h F^p h^-1`; until J,p,h
and report domains are supplied, there is no well-typed commutation claim.
For infinitely renormalizable parameters the smallest missing lemma is a
certified nested restrictive-interval family plus compatible readout transport.

## 9. Cross-domain connection

The finite-word tower's bonding maps are the restrictions used by VOYAGER.
Time shifts act via the extra-depth map d, and compatibility yields an action
on the inverse limit. This gives a concrete distinction between finite receiver
resources and ideal limit dynamics. It does not exchange `∀depth ∃source`
with `∃source ∀depth`; source-image closedness remains a separate condition.
