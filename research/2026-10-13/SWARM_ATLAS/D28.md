# D28 — Lyapunov growth and claim-relative resolution debt

## 1. Original question

When does chaotic evolution demand finer initial knowledge to maintain a finite
observer's prediction? Steelman: expansion consumes a certified precision budget
for a specified report and horizon. Stronger reading: positive Lyapunov growth
forces increasing information debt for every observer. These are distinct claims.
The first survives with explicit hypotheses; the second fails below.

## 2. Existing mathematical context

**CLASSICAL.** Lyapunov exponents describe asymptotic growth of tangent vectors,
under invariant-measure and integrability hypotheses, not uniform finite-radius
error. Ruelle's [1978 paper, Theorems 1–2](https://www.ihes.fr/~/ruelle/PUBLICATIONS/%5B51%5D.pdf)
states the relevant multiplicative theorem and bounds invariant-measure entropy
by the integrated sum of positive exponents for a C1 map of a compact smooth
manifold. [Pesin's original exposition, §5](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=rm&paperid=3219&what=fullteng)
treats entropy equality with additional smoothness and measure hypotheses.
Neither result identifies an arbitrary observer's report entropy with source
entropy. These are literature context, not assumptions silently imposed on SUCC.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Declare a source S, convex domains D_i in normed spaces, maps F_i:D_i→D_(i+1),
and reference/approximate states x_i,z_i, with x_(i+1)=F_i(x_i). Let δ bound
initial norm error, η_i bound ||z_(i+1)−F_i(z_i)||, and L_i certify F_i's Lipschitz constant on
the domain containing both trajectories. Let q_i:D_i→Y_i be a K_i-Lipschitz
report into a metric space, with required tolerance ε_i>0. These declarations
are mathematical source contracts, not physical attestation. Distinguish continuous
report error from equality of discrete report labels.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**PROVED LOCAL, classical estimate.** For every such pair and every finite n,

```
P_n = product_{i=0}^{n-1} L_i; P_0=1
E_n = δ P_n + sum_{j=0}^{n-1} η_j product_{i=j+1}^{n-1} L_i
d(q_n(x_n),q_n(z_n)) <= K_n E_n.
```

Thus every report through N is certified if K_n E_n≤ε_n for all 0≤n≤N.
This is a sufficient condition; its failure means that this certificate fails.
With exact steps, constant L>1, K>0 and ε≥Kδ>0, the last certified time is
floor(log(ε/(Kδ))/log L). For F(x)=Lx on R and q(x)=Kx it is also the exact
worst-case horizon over pairs separated by at most δ. Nonconstant bounds may
recover later, so a prefix horizon must check every earlier time.

## 5. Proof attempt

The triangle inequality gives e_(i+1)≤L_i e_i+η_i. Induction unrolls this
recurrence, then report Lipschitzness supplies the final inequality. On convex
domains with C1 maps, an operator-norm derivative supremum supplies L_i; a derivative
sample at x_i does not certify the intervening segment.

A necessary bound needs more: in one dimension, suppose each whole interval
between x_i and z_i stays inside one C1 monotone branch with |F_i'|≥m_i>0,
and the report is co-Lipschitz there with constant k_n>0. The mean-value
theorem gives report distance ≥k_n δ product m_i for a permissible pair
starting exactly δ apart. Any uniform prediction guarantee must accommodate
this pair. This lower statement assumes exact steps and branch regularity;
there is no unrestricted multidimensional derivative-based lower claim.

## 6. Crucible

**REFUTED: universal observer debt.** Circle doubling D([x])=[2x] on R/Z has
derivative 2 and Lyapunov exponent log 2. Its fair binary cylinders have
length 2^-n, giving source itinerary entropy n bits under Lebesgue measure.
Nevertheless q≡0 reports exactly forever, with zero report entropy and no
additional precision demand for that report.

**REFUTED: permanent pair separation.** Under the same doubling map, 0 and
1/32 coalesce exactly at step 5. The circular distance uses
min(|x−y|,1−|x−y|); wraparound destroys unrestricted exponential separation.
Tent states 1/8 and 7/8 both map to 1/4 despite branch slopes ±2.
Logistic f_4 states 1/8 and 7/8 both map to 7/16 despite slopes ±3.
Expansion within a branch and folding across branches must be separated.

**REFUTED: cell width alone ensures identical labels.** For bins of width 1/4,
states 255/1024 and 257/1024 differ by 1/512 but occupy different bins.
Discrete labels instead need certified distance to every relevant boundary;
error smaller than that margin suffices. Constant dynamics also defeat any
necessary exponential horizon inferred only from a loose upper bound L=2.

## 7. Verdict

**SURVIVES / PROVED LOCAL:** finite-time upper budget and the restricted lower
budget. **TESTED MODEL:** exact rational receipts validate the linear horizon,
step-defect recurrence, doubling coalescence, constant reports, folding, and bin
boundary witnesses. Tests do not establish universal dynamical theorems.
For dyadic initial precision δ=2^-p and tolerance ε=2^-r, linear doubling
requires p≥r+n; this is claim- and map-relative, not universal information creation.
The unrestricted slogans are **REFUTED** by the explicit witnesses.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

Actual carriers require independently justified norms, derivative tubes, noise
budgets, source identity and report margins. Logistic f_r on [0,1], 0<r≤4,
has global bound L=r, but critical points and folding preclude a global positive
lower bound. A numerical Lyapunov estimate cannot replace those certificates.
Physical precision limits, brain computation and chaos creating information remain
unclaimed. Next falsification: supply a purported lower-bound tube that crosses
a fold, or a report boundary within its certified error radius.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

The native `FiniteLogisticSource` keeps original labels separate from evolved
values. A target T on originals is recoverable from R_n=q_n∘F^(n) exactly
iff T is constant on R_n-fibers: T=g∘R_n. For the merged logistic pair,
the original-side target fails this test even with an exact terminal value.
Adding genuine branch receipt b distinguishes originals through (b,R_1);
the terminal alone cannot manufacture b. Constant T factors through q≡0.
This links numerical conditioning to source-relative factorization without
equating hidden-state separation, report uncertainty and source authentication.
