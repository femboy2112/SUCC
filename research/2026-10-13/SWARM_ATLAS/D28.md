# SWM-D28 — A sharp finite chaos-resolution budget

Frozen parent `5eb09e7cba36ec01b8f9d6834b78b5766fd93935`; owner dynamics cohort D.

## 1. Original question

How much source precision is required to predict a chaotic observation horizon,
and does that imply a universal creation of information?

## 2. Existing mathematical context

Full-tent expansion, symbolic cylinders and Lyapunov rates are classical.
[Milnor–Thurston's primary interval-map paper](https://websites.umich.edu/~kochsc/MilnorThurston.pdf)
was checked 2026-10-13 for lap growth and symbolic dynamics. Existing
`logistic_observer_tree.py` already constructs exact length-2^-m cylinders;
this dossier extracts a precise resource inequality and its observation-domain
boundary rather than claiming a new chaos theorem.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

T:[0,1]→[0,1] is `T(x)=2x` on the left half and `2−2x` on the right half.
Observer reports a chronological binary m-word, with closed cylinder I_w.
There is no probabilistic prior in this diameter theorem. Boundary itineraries
are convention-sensitive, but closed inverse cylinders and their images are
unambiguous sets. Resource is the number m of original itinerary bits supplied.

## 4. Candidate theorem

For every binary word w of length m and every n≥0,
`T^n(I_w)=I_{w[n:]}` if n≤m, and equals [0,1] if n≥m. Hence

`diam(T^n(I_w)) = 2^-max(m−n,0)`.

For target diameter `0<ε<1`, the full-cylinder worst-case prediction at time n
has diameter ≤ε iff `m ≥ n + ceil(log2(1/ε))`. The logarithm expresses an
integer threshold; certificate values themselves remain rational powers of two.

## 5. Proof attempt

Each inverse branch is affine of slope magnitude 1/2 and maps [0,1] onto one
half. Composing m branches gives an interval of length 2^-m. Forward T removes
the first inverse branch, maps the whole interval onto its suffix cylinder,
and doubles its length until all m branches are removed. Thereafter T maps
[0,1] onto itself. Solve `2^-(m−n)≤ε` for integers m, with ε<1 requiring m>n.

For a point whose orbit avoids the critical nondifferentiability, every finite
n-step derivative has magnitude 2^n, so its Lyapunov exponent is log 2. The
finite-cylinder result is stronger operationally because it prices an entire
declared uncertainty set, including folding and saturation.

## 6. Crucible

An independent test evaluates original T iterates at every needed dyadic
breakpoint and cylinder endpoint, rather than using suffix identities. All
binary cylinders through depth six and horizons through m+2 agree exactly.
At n=m the image is the whole interval, defeating a false continued-small-error
claim. A constant observable has zero report uncertainty even over that whole
interval; therefore this bound is about source-angle resolution, not every
observer. Special period-two phase reports in D25 also evade this source-angle
budget by intentionally discarding microscopic coordinates.

## 7. Verdict

**CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL**: sharp full-tent cylinder resource/horizon theorem.
**REFUTED**: every chaotic observer must incur the same resolution debt or
chaos universally creates observational information. No physical energy law.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

General smooth maps need a specified observed metric, initial uncertainty set,
branch crossings and certified derivative bounds. A Lipschitz upper estimate
`diam F^n(A)≤L^n diam A` is sufficient, not a matching necessary lower estimate.
A positive asymptotic Lyapunov exponent alone does not certify finite-time
worst-case expansion. Next lamp: interval-certified expansion on a specified
PCF source cylinder, with critical-boundary and noisy-readout controls.
