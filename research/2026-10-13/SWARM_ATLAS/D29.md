# SWM-D29 — Invariant-set transport survives; arbitrary Conley preservation fails

Frozen parent `5eb09e7cba36ec01b8f9d6834b78b5766fd93935`; owner dynamics cohort D.

## 1. Original question

Which distinctions between local orbit deflection and structural dynamical
change survive an observational factor, and can a lossy factor preserve Conley
or connection-matrix invariants automatically?

## 2. Existing mathematical context

Conley, *Isolated Invariant Sets and the Morse Index*, CBMS 38 (1978), is the
primary index/continuation source. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/CBMS/038)
was located 2026-10-13, but attempts to open publisher/full text returned errors
or HTTP 403. **The full primary text was not checked.** The local topological
countermodel and conjugacy argument below are given independently; they do not
pretend to establish a generic continuation or connection-matrix theorem.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Let compact metric X,Y carry continuous F,G and a continuous surjective
semiconjugacy `qF=Gq`. In the Conley comparison use continuous flows
`φ_t,ψ_t` and a homeomorphic conjugacy `qφ_t=ψ_tq`, plus an actual index pair
(N,L) for a specified isolated invariant set. Source-model updates F→F' are
changes of the dynamical system, not ordinary changes of its initial point.

## 4. Candidate theorem

For a semiconjugacy, `F(A)⊆A ⇒ G(qA)⊆qA`. Under the compact/continuous
hypotheses, for every x,
`q(ω_F(x))=ω_G(qx)`.

Under a homeomorphic flow conjugacy, `(qN,qL)` is an index pair and
`N/L ≅ qN/qL` as pointed spaces; hence its Conley index is transported.
Under a general lossy semiconjugacy that stronger invariance is false.

## 5. Proof attempt

The invariant-set claim is immediate from `G(qA)=q(F(A))`. If x' is an
accumulation point of `F^n x`, continuity gives qx' as an accumulation point
of `G^n qx`. Conversely, any convergent subsequence of `G^n qx` has a
subsequence of the corresponding original `F^n x` converging in compact X;
continuity maps its limit to the prescribed observer limit. This proves the
ω-limit equality without claiming equal numbers of invariant components.

A homeomorphism transports closures, interiors, invariant orbits and exit-set
conditions in an index pair. It induces the quotient homeomorphism by mapping
noncollapsed points and the collapsed exit-set basepoint. Homotopy type follows.
Injectivity is doing actual work here; it cannot be removed by vocabulary.

## 6. Crucible

Minimal lossy countermodel: X={a,b} with the discrete topology and identity
flow, Y={c} with identity flow, q(a)=q(b)=c. The semiconjugacy is continuous,
surjective and lawful, but two fixed invariant components collapse to one.
Take the isolated entire source X, N=X and empty exit set L. Its pointed index
space is X with a disjoint basepoint, with reduced H_0 rank two. The observed
index space Y with basepoint has reduced H_0 rank one. Thus even the simplest
Conley homology is not preserved by this lawful observer quotient.

The test independently checks both source fixed components and the lawful
one-state factor. The H_0 ranks are a paper calculation, not a nonexistent
homology package run. Changing an initial point between the two fixed points
does not change F; replacing F by a different transition is a structural model
change, irrespective of whether a constant observer can detect it.

## 7. Verdict

**PROVED LOCAL / CLASSICAL**: invariant sets, compact ω-limit transport, and
homeomorphic-conjugacy index-pair transport. **REFUTED**: arbitrary lawful
observer maps automatically preserve Conley indices or component counts.
No psychological/neuroscientific validation of self-update language.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

A less restrictive index-preserving observer class needs fiber/topology
conditions and a specific comparison map on index pairs that is a pointed
homotopy equivalence. Next lamp: construct one noninjective observer with
contractible fibers and a certified index-pair homotopy equivalence; then mutate
its fiber topology. Connection matrices additionally need Morse decomposition
and compatible boundary maps, none supplied by a report label.
