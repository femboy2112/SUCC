# SWM-D30 — A filtered invisible velocity produces a visible solenoidal force

Frozen parent `5eb09e7cba36ec01b8f9d6834b78b5766fd93935`; owner dynamics cohort D.

## 1. Original question

Can a PDE velocity projection support an exact autonomous coarse equation
solely from the present filtered velocity? Preserve the negative control
`Pw=0` while `P(w⊗w)≠0`, and determine whether pressure removes the witness.

## 2. Existing mathematical context

[Germano, *Turbulence: the filtering approach*, JFM 238 (1992), 325–336,
§2](https://www.ams.jhu.edu/~eyink/Turbulence/classics/Germano92.pdf), primary
paper opened and checked 2026-10-13, derives filtered generalized central
moments under linear constant-preserving filters commuting with derivatives.
This is classical LES closure mathematics. Maxwell source/frame transport is
linear and therefore does not itself certify closure of a quadratic velocity
term. No global Navier–Stokes regularity claim appears here.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Work on the smooth periodic carrier `T²=(ℝ/2πℤ)²`. P is the Fourier spectral
projection onto integer modes `|k|≤1`, componentwise for tensors/vectors. It
is a projection, commutes with derivatives, and is distinct from the Leray
divergence-free projection `𝓟`. Let resolved report `U=Pu`; quadratic stress
`τ(u)=P(u⊗u)−U⊗U`.

For a fixed integer `N≥2`, set k=(N,1), l=(N−1,1),
a=(1,−N), b=(1,−(N−1)),
`w(x)=a cos(k·x)+b cos(l·x)`.
Both modes are high and `k·a=l·b=0`, so w is smooth and divergence free.

## 4. Candidate theorem

For every integer N≥2:

`Pw=0`, but
`P(w⊗w) = (aaᵀ+bbᵀ)/2 + (abᵀ+baᵀ) cos(x₁)/2 ≠0`,

and, more strongly,
`𝓟 div P(w⊗w) = (0,(2N−1)/2) sin(x₁) ≠0`.

Consequently no function H of the current resolved velocity alone obeys
`P[-𝓟 div(u⊗u)+νΔu]=H(Pu)` for **all** smooth divergence-free u on T².
The sign of the surviving generator defect is minus the displayed force.

## 5. Proof attempt

The individual modes have squared norms N²+1 and (N−1)²+1, both >1, so P
removes w. Squaring the cosines yields constant terms and modes 2k,2l;
the latter remain high. The product identity gives difference mode k−l=(1,0)
and sum k+l=(2N−1,2); only the former is retained. Thus the tensor formula
follows exactly. Its x₁ derivative gives sin coefficient
`−(abᵀ+baᵀ)e₁/2 = (−1,(2N−1)/2)`.
At Fourier mode (1,0), Leray removes the first component and leaves the second.
This checks that the visible force is not merely a constant stress or a pure
pressure gradient.

Compare original states u=0 and u=w. Both reports are U=0. The zero state has
zero projected generator; w has the nonzero quadratic defect above, while
`PνΔw=νΔPw=0`. Hence H(0) would have to take two different values. This is
the generator counterpart of D27's noncongruent report fibers.

## 6. Crucible

Exact `Fraction` tests cover N=2,3,7,19,101. A second implementation performs
Fourier coefficient convolution directly for N=2,…,11 and derives every
retained tensor coefficient without calling the product-identity constructor.
It recovers exactly modes (0,0),(1,0),(−1,0). A single high-mode cosine would
give only a constant retained stress and no surviving derivative, so it is an
insufficient dynamic no-go witness; the two-mode difference interaction repairs
that weakness. Pw=0 never implies P(w⊗w)=0.

## 7. Verdict

**PROVED LOCAL**: a parametric exact, smooth, divergence-free closure
counterexample, with the stipulated LES negative control and surviving Leray
force. The general closure issue is classical. This is an algebraic generator
test; no numerical PDE solve, empirical fluid experiment, or NS regularity proof.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

Exact closure requires extra source variables (at least a stress/memory law),
a restricted invariant source manifold on which the projected generator is
fiber-constant, or a stochastic closure with calibrated defect. Next lamp:
find a specified restricted source class where the stress is a function of U,
then test invariance of that class under the original generator.

## 9. Cross-domain connection

The relevant pullback algebra is `A_P={g∘P}` on source velocity space. Generator
closure requires it to remain invariant under the PDE Lie generator. The
countermodel gives one coordinate linear observable of U whose time derivative
is not a function of U. A lawful repair adds τ to the observer report, followed
by a new closure test; appending τ alone does not close its evolution hierarchy.
