# SWM-D32 — Finite delay reconstruction has a sharp source-bound certificate

Frozen parent `5eb09e7cba36ec01b8f9d6834b78b5766fd93935`; owner dynamics cohort D.

## 1. Original question

When do future/delay observations recover the original state rather than only
its behavioral quotient, and how does corrupted reporting change the result?

## 2. Existing mathematical context

[Takens, *Detecting strange attractors in turbulence* (1981), Theorem 1,
p.369](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~saito/data/synchrosqueezing/takens_detect-strange-attractors-turbulence.pdf),
primary paper opened and theorem checked 2026-10-13: for a compact m-manifold,
a generic pair of at least C² diffeomorphism and scalar observable has an
embedding in 2m+1 future coordinates. Genericity concerns pairs and is not a
certificate for an arbitrary fixed sensor, noninvertible logistic map, or noisy
dataset. The finite theorem below is elementary partition refinement/Moore
automata mathematics, independent of Takens' smooth transversality proof.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Complete declared finite source S, `|S|=N≥1`, stationary total deterministic
F, fixed q:S→O. For m≥0 define
`D_m(s)=(q(s),q(Fs),…,q(F^(m−1)s))`, with D_0 the empty word.
Let R_m=ker(D_m). Generated future probes are real mathematical source
queries; their physical feasibility/reset rights remain separate.

## 4. Candidate theorem

For every such source, `R_{N−1}=R_N=R_∞`, where R_∞ means equality of every
future report. Thus D_(N−1) is a lawful autonomous observer quotient, and it
recovers the original source iff the full report language separates every pair.
The N−1 bound is sharp in general.

For a fixed length-m delay code with minimum pairwise Hamming distance d_min,
every report corrupted in at most e coordinates uniquely decodes to its original
source iff `2e<d_min`. The single-state code has no competing word and is
handled separately.

## 5. Proof attempt

The equivalences R_m refine each other. If `R_m=R_{m+1}`, then for equal m-words
the length-(m+1) equality implies equal m-words after F, so R_m is an
F-congruence. Iterating F makes every later report equal, and all subsequent
R stabilize. At m=0, equality with R_1 means q is constant, giving the same
conclusion. Before stabilization every strict refinement adds at least one
class; starting with one, at most N−1 strict refinements are possible. If all
N−1 occur, R_(N−1) is already equality and cannot refine further. This proves
the uniform bound. Original-state recovery adds injectivity: behavioral
congruence alone is insufficient.

For sharpness use chain states 0,…,N−1 with successor min(s+1,N−1) and q=1
only at the absorbing endpoint. D_(N−2) does not separate states 0 and 1;
D_(N−1) separates all states. For coding sufficiency, two codewords both
within e of one received word would have mutual distance ≤2e by triangle
inequality. For necessity, if two words have distance ≤2e, split their differing
coordinates into two groups of size ≤e and build a word in both balls.

## 6. Crucible

All 4,330 deterministic binary-report source tables with N≤4 compare the
N−1 quotient against an independently expanded N²-step future table. Chains
through N=8 check sharpness. Two fixed states with a constant report remain
indistinguishable after 100 reports, and mathematically after all reports.

A 3-cycle pulse observation, nine-delay code, has minimum distance six;
two coordinate corruptions decode uniquely. A constructed midpoint at distance
three from two codewords is explicitly ambiguous. An initial midpoint fixture
used the wrong three coordinates and failed the test; the fixture was repaired
by splitting the actual six disagreements. No failed run was relabeled green.

## 7. Verdict

**CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL**: finite delay stabilization, sharp bound and
adversarial noise margin. Takens' continuum theorem is primary-source context,
not a newly proved or automatically applicable SUCC result. No Lean-kernel
status. No finite unknown-state data certificate without the complete N-state
source assumption.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

For a fixed continuum observable, the smallest target is an actual injectivity
and immersion proof (or a separating pair counterexample), not genericity.
For noisy data, exact reconstruction additionally needs a separation margin
and an externally justified error bound. For nonstationary/source-changing
instruments, delays must be action/history-indexed and old proof support
transported; the single fixed F proof cannot silently survive that mutation.

## 9. Cross-domain connection

The map `D_(N−1):S→D_(N−1)(S)` realizes VOYAGER's coarsest lawful future behavioral
quotient in a bounded finite source. D27's invariant algebra criterion proves
its induced update is well-defined. The full source is recovered precisely when
this map is injective; surjectivity onto its image does not supply injectivity,
nor does completion of finite reports remove hidden aliases.
