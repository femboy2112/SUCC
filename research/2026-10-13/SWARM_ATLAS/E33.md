## 5. Proof attempt

First, \(p(x+1)=px+p\), proving \(V_pS=S^pV_p\). Induction gives \(V_aS^d=S^{ad}V_a\). Normalize a word by structural induction: multiplying normal forms gives
\(S^bV_aS^dV_c=S^{b+ad}V_{ac}\).
Every positive integer has a unique finite prime factorization; sorting its dilation factors therefore supplies existence using exactly the stated relations. If two normal forms define equal maps, evaluation at 0 gives equal offsets \(b\), and evaluation at 1 gives equal slopes \(a\). Hence no further monoid identifications occur, and the presentation is faithful.

On the free module with basis \(e_n\), set \(T_{a,b}e_n=e_{an+b}\). This proves algebraic faithfulness, without asserting faithfulness of a universal completed C*-algebra.
