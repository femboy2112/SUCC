## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Source: N_0 with S(n)=n+1 and V_a(n)=an for a>=1. Actions A_(a,b)(n)=an+b, b>=0; multiplication is composition:
A_(a,b) A_(c,d)=A_(ac,ad+b).
Report: r_m(n)=n mod m. Morphism: a bijective frame h:C_m->C_m intertwining S and each V_a. All observations are exact source-relative arithmetic, with no physical-attestation claim. N_0 is discrete; clocks are finite discrete spaces. Implementation limits m<=4096 and stored action parameters <=10^6, while the paper theorem has no such bound.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For every positive m and finite (possibly empty) slope family P, the full group of bijections commuting with S and all V_a, a in P, consists exactly of translations h_c(x)=x+c with
(a-1)c=0 mod m for every a in P.
Put g=gcd(m,{a-1:a in P}), with gcd(m,empty)=m. The offsets are c=j m/g, 0<=j<g. Thus the group is cyclic of order g. Including slope 2 makes it trivial; using only odd slopes leaves at least the offset m/2 when m is even.
On N_0, any map h commuting with S and with one dilation a>=2 is the identity.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete elementary proof
Commutation with S gives h(x)=h(0)+x by induction around the cyclic orbit. Conversely every translation is a bijection commuting with S. Its dilation square differs by ac-c; hence exactly the displayed congruences remain. If m divides every (a-1)c, a Bezout combination of m and the a-1 shows m divides gc. Since g divides m, this is equivalent to m/g dividing c, giving the count g. For N_0, successor induction again gives h(n)=n+c with c=h(0)>=0. At n=0, dilation commutation says c=ac, hence c=0 for a>=2.
The action relation V_a S=S^a V_a follows at every n from a(n+1)=an+a. Faithfulness of the natural-number affine representation follows by testing n=0 for b and n=1 for a. Reduction r_m intertwines every affine action because congruence is stable under addition and multiplication.

## 6. Crucible

## 8. Remaining obstruction
Residue clocks identify affine coefficients only modulo m. To recover full integer actions one needs a separating family of unbounded moduli, or a stated finite coefficient bound. To reach operator algebra completions requires their relations, topology, norm and representation theorem. Next falsifier: permit nonlinear source actions and test whether a proposed frame classification still holds.

## 9. Cross-domain connection
The maps r_m are action-equivariant quotient maps, so they feed an actual multi-action Nerode/source quotient, rather than a diagram analogy. The stabilizer theorem describes exactly which automorphisms preserve the two-action observational structure. On an inverse family with m|n the morphism C_n->C_m is residue reduction; translation offsets must reduce compatibly. No completed source is actualized by merely naming that inverse family.
