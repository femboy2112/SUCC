# E34 — prime jets, valuation geometry, and factor-square dynamics

## 1. Original question

Can authentic prime-power motion couple additive SUCC, multiplicative FUCC,
valuation geometry, and square-root factor windows through actual maps? The
faithful steelman retains the original positive integer and distinguishes its
valuation representation from a lossy observer. It asks for an invariant and
a precise obstruction to extending it. Mathematical source authenticity is
separate from physical authentication. Academic novelty is unclaimed.

## 2. Existing mathematical context

Unique factorization gives multiplicative integers additive prime exponents.
The valuation laws and the convention `v_p(0)=∞` are classical; see
[Evertse, *P-adic Numbers*, §§1–3](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/math5020f11/evertsepadicnotes.pdf).
The [Riemann–Siegel formula](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.10.E3) uses
`m=floor(sqrt(t/(2π)))`, half-density `n^(-1/2)`, and the Gamma-derived phase,
with a remainder requiring separate control. Its origin is discussed in the
[translation of Siegel's 1932 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05198).
These established structures supply context, not a SUCC proof of RH.
The jet gap equals `φ(p^(k+1))`, the unit-group cardinality and an Euler
common exponent. Positive-depth jets are nonunits; this equality does not
identify the two carriers. At `p=2` the least universal unit exponent can
be smaller than that cardinality: modulo eight it is two, versus four units.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Take `n∈N_{>0}`, a verified prime `p`, `k≥0`, integer dilation `m≥1`, and
shift `d≥0`. Define `S_d(n)=n+d`, `V_m(n)=mn`, prime jet `j_p(k)=p^k`,
full word `v(n)=(v_q(n))_q`, diagonal `D(n)=(n,n)`, and product `P(a,b)=ab`.
Thus `Q=P∘D`, `Q(n)=n²`. A single coordinate `v_p` retains neither the
unit nor the integer. The finite observer `J_{p,r}(n)=(v_p(n),n mod p^r)`
has fixed `r≥1`. Full words here have finite support and finite exponents;
their empty word denotes **one**, never zero.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL**, uniformly for the stated domains:

\[
v_p(j_p(k))=k,\quad j_p(k+1)-j_p(k)=(p-1)p^k,
\quad v_p(j_p(k+1)-j_p(k))=k.
\]

Moreover `v(mn)=v(m)+v(n)` and `V_m S_d=S_{md}V_m`.
However no function of `v_p(n)` alone, or of `J_{p,r}(n)` at fixed depth,
returns `v_p(n+1)` for **every** positive integer. Square transport is
`Q V_m=V_{m²} Q` and `Q S_d(n)=Q(n)+2dn+d²`; for `d>0` its increment is
state dependent. These claims concern arithmetic addresses, not curvature.

## 5. Proof attempt

Factor out `p^k` in the gap; `p∤(p−1)`. Unique factorization proves the
full-word multiplication law; expanding both affine composites proves their
relation. For the first no-go, `p−1` and `p²−1` both have valuation zero,
whereas their successors have valuations one and two. For fixed depth `r`,
use `p^r−1` and `p^(r+1)−1`: they have equal input valuations and residues,
but successor valuations `r,r+1`. Any decoder assigns one output to their
common input, contradicting exactness.

Expansion proves the square laws; successive squares already have unequal
gaps `3,5`. On a product fiber `ab=N>0`, `a≤b` iff `a²≤N`. Swapping
factors pairs every off-diagonal point, giving
`τ(N)=2 #{a|N: a²≤N}−1_{N square}`. Positivity of both factors is essential.

## 6. Crucible

The smallest successor witness is `p=2`, integers `1,3`. Full words do
distinguish them, so this does not refute faithful full factorization.
Successor transports through its inverse as a set map, but fails as a
divisibility functor: `2|4`, while `3∤5`.

Composite jets `6^k` satisfy the same gap and affine identities. Exact
characters `χ_j(x)=exp(2πijx/5)` see identical dilation by prime `11` and
composite `6`, because both are one modulo five. Precision twenty-five
separates them at `x=1`; the independent native prime gate rejects six.
Prime seven versus composite four modulo three is a fresh control.

The non-Euler source `a(n)=1+1_{n=6}` has `a(6)−a(2)a(3)=1`. Keeping the
same phase and half-density, its finite packet agrees below square threshold
`t/(2π)=36`, then changes formally by
`(zX_6+z^{-1}X_6^{-1})/sqrt(6)`. Here `z=exp(iθ(t))` and
`X_n=∏_q X_q^{v_q(n)}`, `X_q=exp(−it log q)`: multiplicative phase laws
are preserved. A specialized cosine may vanish; no pointwise detection is
promised. Nor is this packet a Riemann–Siegel expansion for the mutant.

Exact valuation covariance on integers `1..6` remains PSD for both sources:
determinants are `13/108` and `41/343`. Positive semantic geometry therefore
does not authenticate Euler data or identify a completed Weil form.

## 7. Verdict

**SURVIVES — CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL:** the jet invariant, affine relation,
faithful full-word representation, square transport, and factor cone.
**FAILS — REFUTED:** valuation-only successor, fixed-depth exact successor
valuation, fixed square increment, and inference of prime authenticity from
the tested geometric/phase identities. Receipts are the explicit witnesses
above. **TESTED MODEL:** bounded native gates and exact finite controls.
The finite computation supports regression, not universal proof promotion.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

Repair successor by retaining sufficient unit/source data or a justified
bounded source class. Ring successor modulo `p^r` itself remains valid;
the no-go targets its **unbounded exact valuation** output. `v_p(0)=∞` is
an extended-value convention; native finite valuation/product APIs reject
zero. Also `p^k→0` p-adically while it diverges in the real place; no actual
observer performs that infinite limit. Authentic Euler/Gamma/pole completion,
conductor, test domain, and completed-Weil identification remain separate
requirements. No RH progress is claimed.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

`D: n↦(n,n)` followed by `P` transports the integer horizon `n≤H` to
square-source horizon `Q(n)≤H²`. It does not make arbitrary products
diagonal or make square root an integer operation everywhere. In the
analytic window, `n²≤t/(2π)` reproduces the cutoff only after supplying
the standard phase, half-density, source, and remainder. Changing a window
changes its hypotheses. The next discriminator is a source-attested finite
height/unit-residue bound tested against cancellation just beyond that bound.
Here κ is exact source-code contact, φ is bounded/proof-relative survival,
σ requires the independent prime/source gate, and ρ grants no evidential weight.
