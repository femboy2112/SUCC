## 1. Original question
Which actual invariant couples additive successor events to prime-power valuation rays, and can successive factor-square states be transported by a bounded local valuation update?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
Unique factorization and Legendre's factorial valuation are classical. ## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Original arithmetic source n in N>=1, successor n->n+1. Its valuation coordinate nu(n) is the finitely supported vector (v_p(n)) over genuine primes. FUCC V_p increments the p-coordinate. Factor source over n is pairs (alpha,beta) with alpha+beta=nu(n). Log-height h(alpha)=sum alpha_p log p; the lower square-root cone h(alpha)<=h(n)/2 is encoded exactly as divisor d with d^2<=n, without floating logs. Reports retain BOTH source n and valuation vector, so an additive event is never mistaken for a prime-coordinate translation. Topology is discrete; finite code factors n<=10^6.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For each prime p and n>=0,
v_p(n!)=sum_(k=1)^n v_p(k)=sum_(j>=1) floor(n/p^j).
Each sum is finite. Factor-square pairs over n are in bijection with divisors d|n satisfying d^2<=n, through d=prod p^alpha_p, partner n/d.
There is NO source-uniform finite B bounding |v_p(n+1)-v_p(n)|: for any B, choose integer r>B and n=p^r-1. In particular a bounded-jump lattice model for successor cannot be source-faithful on the full integer carrier.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete
For each k, v_p(k)=sum_(j>=1) 1_[p^j|k]. Sum over k<=n and interchange finite sums; exactly floor(n/p^j) terms remain at level j. This constructs a source-faithful additive event sum with a multiplicative jet count. Unique factorization sends a divisor d to alpha=nu(d); nu(n/d)=nu(n)-alpha is nonnegative precisely when d|n. Monotonicity of exp gives h(alpha)<=h(n)/2 iff d<=sqrt(n), equivalently d^2<=n. For the negative result, p does not divide p^r-1 but v_p(p^r)=r, so successor has jump r. This last proof is universal; finite test values are only controls.

## 6. Crucible
Independent divisor enumeration verifies every factor square for n=1..180. Summing valuations of the ACTUAL integers k=1..n independently verifies the jet formula for p=2,3,5,7 and n=1..180. Hostile p^r-1 controls for p=2,3,5, r<=7 exhibit growing jumps. At n=36 the exact lower factor pairs are (1,36),(2,18),(3,12),(4,9),(6,6). The probe gives v_2(100!)=97. Composite p=6 is rejected by the prime-sensitive factorial instrument; an abstract dilation by 6 remains legitimate under E33's different type.

## 7. Verdict

## 8. Remaining obstruction
An arithmetic source-changing morphism must account for unbounded successor carry, not impose a fixed finite shift on valuation vectors. The sharp next task is a source-indexed partial correspondence between factor pairs over n and n+1; no globally canonical nontrivial divisor matching has been constructed. This is a missing map, not a reason to assert impossibility of all correspondences.

## 9. Cross-domain connection
The factorial accumulation map sends the additive event history (1,...,n) to sum_k nu(k), and the jet counting map sends the same source to (sum_j floor(n/p^j))_p. The proof shows these two maps commute exactly. They connect source histories to the prime-coordinate free commutative monoid. They do not turn successor n->n+1 itself into FUCC translation: the jump obstruction proves why history accumulation and current-source coordinates have distinct types.
