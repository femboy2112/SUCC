## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Fix an actual prime p. The source is a complete sequence `g:N_0→Q_p`, with successor difference Δg(n)=g(n+1)−g(n) and
\[
a_j=\Delta^jg(0)=\sum_{k=0}^j(-1)^{j-k}\binom jk g(k).
\]
Clock objects are C_r=Z/p^rZ with actual reductions π. A same-depth source map has F_r:C_r→C_r and πF_{r+1}=F_rπ. This diagram concerns integral-valued sources; arbitrary Q_p outputs need a declared scaling or output lattice.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**CLASSICAL, recovered proof below:** For every such g, these are equivalent:

1. g has a unique continuous extension F:Z_p→Q_p.
2. Uniform precision holds: for every integer r there exists s≥0 such that, for **all** m,n≥0, m≡n mod p^s implies v_p(g(m)−g(n))≥r.
3. a_j→0 p-adically.

Then F(x)=Σa_j binom(x,j), uniformly, with
\[
\sup_{x\in Z_p}|F(x)-\sum_{j<K}a_j\binom xj|_p
\le\sup_{j\ge K}|a_j|_p.
\]
Finite coefficient prefixes provide no bound on that supremum.

## 5. Proof attempt

**PROVED LOCAL derivation of the classical statement.** Binomial polynomials take integral values on N_0, hence on its dense closure Z_p; their norms are ≤1. Thus (3) gives uniform convergence and continuity. Finite binomial inversion reconstructs g(n), and density gives uniqueness. Compactness gives uniform continuity, proving (1)⇒(2).

For (2)⇒(3), approximate g uniformly by h(n)=g(n mod q), q=p^s. Uniform precision also bounds g: finitely many residue representatives plus an error bound suffice. Let T shift q-periodic sequences and D=T−I. Since T^q=I and every intermediate binomial coefficient of p^s is divisible by p, D^q=pB for an integer-coefficient operator B. The endpoint terms cancel for odd q and sum to 2I for even q; q=1 gives D=0. Hence
\[
\|D^{qk+\ell}h\|\le p^{-k}\|h\|\quad(0\le\ell<q).
\]
Moreover \|D^j(g−h)\|≤\|g−h\|. Arbitrarily accurate periodic approximations force a_j→0. This proves the universal criterion; the finite script only audits instances.

## 6. Crucible

**Same-depth counterclock.** For integral F, compatible F_r exist iff
\[
x\equiv y\pmod{p^r}\Rightarrow F(x)\equiv F(y)\pmod{p^r}
\]
for every r: define F_r by representatives; conversely reduction of F gives this law. This is exactly 1-Lipschitz continuity. General continuity permits input depth s(r).

The smallest prime/depth polynomial witness is p=2, r=1, F(x)=binom(x,2): F(0)=0, F(2)=1. It is globally analytic and integral-valued, but fails equal-depth naturality. Depth r+1 suffices because
\[
F(x+h)-F(x)=h(2x+h-1)/2.
\]
Minimal degree is two: an integral-valued affine polynomial has intercept F(0) and slope F(1)−F(0) in Z_2, hence is 1-Lipschitz.

**Analytic jump.** For binary digits d_j(x), define H(x)=Σ4^j d_j(x). Agreement through input depth r gives output agreement through depth 2r, so H supplies compatible clocks. Yet H(2^j)=4^j and H(3·2^j)=5·4^j. Analyticity near zero would force c_0=c_1=0 and a common quadratic limit; the two ratios are 1 and 5/9. Contradiction. All-depth clock coherence does not imply local analyticity.

**Finite interpolation.** Zero and binom(x,N+1) agree at 0,…,N but differ at −1. Alternatively coefficients a_0,…,a_N=0 and a_j=1 afterwards define exact integer data with no continuous extension. For N=2, its values at 2^r are odd for r≥2, although 2^r→0 and g(0)=0. Native parity at p=3 independently supplies Δ^j=(-2)^j, another failure of decay.

Even finite coherence must be tested: constant maps F_1=0 mod 2 and F_2=1 mod 4 are individually valid finite functions but violate their reduction square. This is the smallest two-level counterclock; independent finite tables cannot replace a projective family.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

For distinct primes and M=Πp_i^{r_i}, CRT gives the ring isomorphism
\[
[n]_M\mapsto([n]_{p_i^{r_i}})_i,
\quad (b_i)_i\mapsto\sum_i b_i(M/p_i^{r_i})u_i\pmod M,
\]
where u_i inverts M/p_i^{r_i} locally. These maps commute with reductions, synchronizing genuine residue clocks.

The integers a_r≡0 mod 2^r, a_r≡1 mod 3^r form a compatible CRT tower beginning 4,28,136. Its limit is (0,1) in Z_2×Z_3, not a diagonal rational: a rational equal to zero in Q_2 is zero in Q_3. Independent local limits therefore require an explicit global source identification. Unit-exponent periods also share factors: periods 2 and 4 at primes 3 and 5 cannot synchronize residues 0 and 1. Generalized CRT requires agreement modulo the gcd.
