# SWM-E35 — Finite prime clocks do not identify p-adic regularity

**Immutable base:** VOYAGER 5eb09e7cba36ec01b8f9d6834b78b5766fd93935.
**Owner:** arithmetic expedition cohort.

## 1. Original question
Can finitely many successor samples and exact prime clocks identify a continuous p-adic interpolation, and what regularity must be paid for before passing to an infinite Mahler object?

## 2. Existing mathematical context
Mahler's original [1958 interpolation paper, primary reprint](https://ems.press/books/dms/252/4989), retrieved 2026-10-13, characterizes continuous p-adic functions using null Mahler coefficients. Repo clock_mahler_interpolation.py already implements exact local powers, finite Kummer checks and continuity controls. This expedition targets the information missing from a finite prefix, not a new Mahler theorem.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Source function f:N_0->Q_p; observed causal record f(0),...,f(N). Observer clocks are reductions of inputs modulo p^r. An admissible extension, when stated, is F:Z_p->Q_p continuous, restricting to f. Probes are integer evaluations. The new instrument constructs explicit rival functions with exactly the same finite source record. p must be genuine prime. Code N<=256; proof every finite N>=0.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For every prime p and finite N>=0, the zero prefix on 0..N has at least two distinct continuous p-adic extensions, F_0(x)=0 and F_1(x)=binom(x,N+1). It also has a source continuation on N_0 with no continuous extension to Z_p: choose R with p^R>N and let g(n)=1 exactly when n=p^r, r>=R, and 0 otherwise. Therefore finite samples alone neither determine uniqueness within the continuous class nor decide membership in that class.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete
The binomial polynomial x(x-1)...(x-N)/(N+1)! is a Q_p polynomial, hence continuous. It vanishes at 0,...,N and equals 1 at N+1, proving nonuniqueness even among continuous extensions. The spike g vanishes on the observed prefix and at 0. But p^r->0 in Z_p while g(p^r)=1 for all r>=R. Were an extension G continuous, G(p^r)->G(0)=0, contradiction. This is a topology-based failure, not Gödel independence. For a constructive positive control, Mahler coefficients a_j=(-p)^j are p-adic null; binomial expansion gives the continuous power source (1-p)^x. General continuous extensions require a_j->0, and p-adic analyticity imposes further decay; no finite check of this limit is supplied.

## 6. Crucible
Exact cases N=0,1,9,31,128 and p=2,3,5 verify zero-prefix agreement and the first separating polynomial sample. For N=31,p=3, the spike begins at 81; it vanishes on every observed index but differs on a p-adically convergent input sequence. Composite clock p=6 is rejected. Existing integer_power_clock_continuity distinguishes (-1)^n on Z_3 from its continuous parity behavior on Z_2; those controls remain untouched. No finite Kummer congruence run is promoted to a constructed Kubota–Leopoldt function.

## 7. Verdict
**PROVED LOCAL** for both nonidentifiability claims, by explicit source countermodels; **CLASSICAL** Mahler context. The construction is new to this checkpoint, not claimed academically original.

## 8. Remaining obstruction
Uniqueness needs an additional source class such as a degree bound, known Mahler coefficients, a differential law or an analytic interpolation theorem. Continuity needs a uniform modulus or equivalent coefficient decay premise. Next discriminating instrument: a source contract providing precision-dependent congruence guarantees for ALL future input pairs. A finite prefix alone cannot replace that premise.

## 9. Cross-domain connection
Finite prefix maps E_N:F->(F(0),...,F(N)) have nontrivial fibers even on continuous source functions. Their inverse observational limit recovers a full integer sequence only when every sample is supplied; Mahler extension then still requires topology compatibility. This supplies a concrete arithmetic source pair for VOYAGER's distinction between coherent ideal report and legitimate realization in the declared source class.
