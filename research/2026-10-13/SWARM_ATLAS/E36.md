## 4. Candidate theorem
For normalized a, b=log_*a is supported on prime powers iff a is multiplicative on coprime pairs. The same equivalence holds at horizon N with all quantifiers restricted to uv<=N and n<=N.
Complete multiplicativity is stronger: it is equivalent to
b(p^k)=a(p)^k/k for every prime power, together with zero mixed support.
Degree-one Euler support does not imply unit modulus, correct ramification, conductor periodicity, logarithmic frequency log p, Gamma completion or any Weil sign.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete
If a is multiplicative, its formal Euler factorization is a=*_(p) A_p with A_p supported on p-powers and A_p(1)=1. Each coefficient reads finitely many prime factors, so log_*a=sum_p log_*A_p, hence no mixed support. Conversely, if b has only prime-power support, decompose b=sum_p b_p. exp_*b=*p exp_*b_p; disjoint prime supports and unique factorization give multiplicativity on coprime arguments. Formal exp/log inverse identities hold coefficientwise because augmentation powers are locally nilpotent. All terms affecting n<=N use only divisors <=N, proving the finite version without invoking any future coefficients.
For complete multiplicativity, A_p(X)=sum_k a(p)^k X^k=(1-a(p)X)^-1, whose logarithm is sum_(k>=1)a(p)^k X^k/k. Conversely exponentiating that local series recovers the geometric ray.
Independent recursion: Omega(n)=sum_p v_p(n) is additive under multiplication, so Df(n)=Omega(n)f(n) is a convolution derivation. Da=(Db)*a gives
b(n)=[Omega(n)a(n)-sum_(1<d<n,d|n)Omega(d)b(d)a(n/d)]/Omega(n).
Triangularity computes the SAME log coefficients without formal power enumeration.
