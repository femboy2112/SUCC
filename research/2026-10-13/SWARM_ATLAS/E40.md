## 1. Original question

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects
Source operator T=diag(1,2) on C^2. Observer unit vector u_alpha with squared first-coordinate weight alpha, 0<=alpha<=1. Scalar report R_alpha(z)=<u_alpha,(zI-T)^-1u_alpha>, z outside {1,2}. Full source report is det(zI-T). A basis change U acts by similarity with the observation vector transported as well; changing alpha instead changes the readout. No Mellin continuation or completed arithmetic source is input.

## 4. Candidate theorem
For 0<alpha<1,
R_alpha(z)=alpha/(z-1)+(1-alpha)/(z-2)
=[z-(1+alpha)]/[(z-1)(z-2)].
Its uncancelled zero is 1+alpha and can move across (1,2), while the source spectrum and both visible poles remain {1,2}. At alpha=0 or 1 the displayed numerator zero cancels an unobserved pole, so it must not be counted as a genuine transfer zero.
For every invertible U, det(zI-U^-1TU)=det(zI-T). If u is transported consistently under a unitary basis change, the scalar resolvent report is also unchanged.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete
Add the two resolvent diagonal terms: the numerator is alpha(z-2)+(1-alpha)(z-1)=z-(1+alpha). For interior alpha neither denominator factor cancels, so the zero is real and strictly between the fixed poles. At endpoints the canceled expression is respectively 1/(z-2) or 1/(z-1). Determinant multiplicativity gives
det[U^-1(zI-T)U]=det(U^-1)det(zI-T)det(U)=det(zI-T).
For unitary U, transport u to U^-1u and use (zI-U^-1TU)^-1=U^-1(zI-T)^-1U; the inner product is unchanged. Thus observer-dependent zero motion and coordinate change have different mathematical types.

## 6. Crucible

Binary float weights are rejected. A full rational orthogonal basis U=((3/5,-4/5),(4/5,3/5)) gives the same characteristic polynomial at six exact rational test values. This polynomial identity has the algebraic proof above; finite evaluation is corroboration. No sampled Fourier cancellation is treated as a source spectral zero.
