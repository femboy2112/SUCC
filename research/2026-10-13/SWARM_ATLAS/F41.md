## 5. Proof attempt: complete local derivation

1. Reports from the empty word and the word `A` give `(z,z+y)`. Equality of those two reports implies equality of `z` and `y`, over `Z/nZ` without primality. Conversely every future update of `(y,z)` depends only on `(y,z)`, so induction on word length proves all-word equality whenever these coordinates agree. This proves the exact quotient and its minimality. Different `x` values remain aliased. At least two `n`-valued report results are necessary to separate `n^2` behavioral states on individually prepared sources; the displayed pair attains that counting bound in the specified nondestructive-read model.

2. For `u,v,w in R`, define

   `T(u,v,w)(y,z)=(y+v,z+u*y+w)`.

   With ordinary function composition, a direct substitution gives

   `T(u,v,w) o T(u',v',w') = T(u+u',v+v',w+w'+u*v')`.

   This is precisely multiplication of `[[1,u,w],[0,1,v],[0,0,1]]`. The generators are `a=T(1,0,0)`, `b=T(0,1,0)`. The inverse is `T(-u,-v,-w+u*v)`. Their commutator in the **execution** convention is `b^-1 o a^-1 o b o a = T(0,0,-1)`. Hence every `T` is generated: use powers of `a`, `b`, and the central translation, adjusting the resulting `w` coordinate. All `n^3` triples give distinct permutations: evaluate at `(0,0)` to determine `v,w`, then at `(1,0)` to determine `u`.

3. Commuting with `a` forces `v=0`; commuting with `b` forces `u=0`. Thus the center is exactly `{T(0,0,w)}`. Translation by `-1` has order `n` and no fixed point. This proves the algebraic claims, including the composite-modulus extension.

4. The same triples act on the source by `(x,y,z) -> (x+u,y+v,z+u*y+w)`. This action is regular: the image of `(0,0,0)` is exactly `(u,v,w)`. Identifying the source with `G_n`, the observer is the homogeneous space `G_n/L`, where `L=<a>` has order `n`: right multiplication by `T(u,0,0)` changes only source `x`. The observer action is faithful because its kernel is `core_G(L)`. A direct conjugation gives `L intersect b^-1 L b = {identity}`: elements in the two subgroups are `T(u,0,0)` and `T(u,0,u)`; their equality forces `u=0`. Therefore the core is trivial. A noninjective state quotient can preserve every group-action law.

5. The equivariant map `q` induces a lawful action-groupoid functor `S_n // G_n -> Q_n // G_n`, sending an object to its quotient and an arrow `(g,s)` to `(g,q(s))`. Sources, targets, identities and compositions are preserved by `q(gs)=gq(s)`. One must retain the action label `g`; treating a groupoid arrow as merely its endpoint pair would lose isotropy and proof provenance. Every arrow over a fixed source object lifts uniquely, giving the corresponding action-graph covering.

6. On the bouquet, the loop representation has image all `G_n`. A connected cover corresponds to a subgroup `K` of `F_2`; all pulled-back holonomy is trivial exactly when `K` is contained in the loop representation's kernel. Thus its degree `[F_2:K]` is at least `[F_2:ker]=|G_n|=n^3`, achieved by the kernel cover. The action is transitive on `Q_n`, whose Schreier cover degree is `n^2`; its based residual holonomy is the nontrivial stabilizer `L`, so that cover is not a full trivialization. The regular source action has trivial stabilizer and realizes the kernel cover. A parallel section on the base must be fixed by every holonomy element, impossible for `T(0,0,-1)`.

This is a direct proof. The finite probe is a corroborating implementation rather than the justification for the universal quantifiers.
