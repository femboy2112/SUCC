## 4. Candidate theorem

For every prime p and nonzero λ in F_p, actions A(x,y,z)=(x+1,y,z+λy), B(x,y,z)=(x,y+1,z) with readout z have minimal all-word behavioral quotient (y,z), independent of x. The quotient action group has p³ elements and central commutator of order p. For λ=0 the minimal quotient is z and the induced action group is trivial. Neither coordinate opacity nor this theorem supplies a new-human-theorem claim.

## 5. Proof attempt

Read z then z after A gives z and z+λy, hence determines y,z when λ≠0. Future y,z dynamics never use x, giving both necessity and sufficiency. Induced transforms have unique forms (y,z)↦(y+j,z+i y+k); evaluating at y=0 and y=1 distinguishes (i,j,k), so there are p³. Executing A,B,A⁻¹,B⁻¹ changes z by −λ, hence is central and order p. Setting λ=0 removes every y dependence and every observable action.
