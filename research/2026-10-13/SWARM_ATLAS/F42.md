## 5. Proof attempt

Kernels and inverse-image relations are equivalences; intersections preserve
equivalence. A witnessed stability failure has \(xRy\) but
\(\delta_a(x)\not R\delta_a(y)\), so the chosen replacement splits a block.
There are at most \(|X|\) blocks, proving termination.

Let \(S\subseteq R_0\) be any action-stable equivalence. Inductively assume
\(S\subseteq R\). If \(xSy\), stability gives
\(\delta_a(x)S\delta_a(y)\), hence both pairs lie in \(R\); therefore
\(S\subseteq R_{next}\). At termination the returned relation is stable and
contains every such \(S\), proving the order claim. Constancy on blocks defines
the induced maps; surjectivity proves uniqueness. Generator commutation extends
to every finite action word by induction. These arguments provide the universal
