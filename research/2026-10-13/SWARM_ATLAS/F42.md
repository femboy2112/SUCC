# SWM-F42 — Typed constructor growth and its exact ceiling

## 1. Original question

Can a learner expand mathematical objects rather than fit constants, while retaining semantic verification at every constructor boundary? What ceiling survives even unlimited grammar expansion?

## 4. Candidate theorem

Let E be all-word behavioral equivalence. Every observationally sound term generated from primitive readouts by finite word composition, deterministic tuple formation and deterministic postprocessing is constant on E. Thus these grammar extensions cannot identify a target T varying inside an E-class. A source-dependent quotient constructor is sound precisely when its readouts and action squares descend; a candidate factor Q_f→Q_c exists precisely when E_f⊆E_c and is then unique for surjective source quotients.

## 5. Proof attempt

Primitive word readouts are constant on E by definition. Tuple formation preserves equality componentwise; deterministic postprocessing preserves equal inputs. Structural induction establishes the grammar ceiling. For the factor map define f([x]_f)=[x]_c; E_f⊆E_c proves well-definedness, and source surjectivity proves uniqueness. For a state-changing quotient q, checking q(U_a x)=V_a q(x) for generators gives every word by induction; equality of readouts then gives all experiments.

The implementation actually builds quotient and action-algebra objects from tables, not a hard-coded Heisenberg target. Independent forward refinement verifies the result. It does **not** claim a stochastic genetic proposer: sibling's genuine constrained genetic bridge engine was independently reproduced and audited, while the new constructor engine is deterministic.

## 6. Crucible

`factor_map((0,0,1,1),(0,1,0,1))` rejects a syntactically plausible but nonfunctional transformation. A forged descended action rejects a false commuting square. A new physical action invalidates the old digest. Noninvertible erasure generates a monoid that is not falsely called a group. Finite closure-budget exhaustion returns OPEN rather than a structural certificate. The hidden-copy target in F44 remains unreachable under every old word but becomes reachable after a genuinely new swap instrument.

## 7. Verdict

**PROVED LOCAL** for typed-constructor soundness and the grammar ceiling; **CONJECTURE/OPEN** for evolving an unrestricted metagrammar of verified categorical constructors. The broader goal is not silently reduced to a constants-only genetic search.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

A grammar does not verify its own semantic interpretation. An extensible constructor proof kernel must check constructor denotations, maps, obligations and inherited counterexamples. Smallest next lemma: a typed syntax with an inductive sound interpretation into finite source-relative experiments and quotients; then search its terms fairly. No end-to-end formal refinement is present here.
