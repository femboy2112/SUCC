## 2. Existing mathematical context

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Write $L_s(a^n,y^n)$ for the outcome law of a fixed action word after summing hidden carrier paths. Define $s\sim_A t$ iff these laws agree for every $n\ge0,a^n,y^n$. A public policy uses only recorded history and source-independent randomness. A target $T:S\to V$ refers to the **original** source.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**CLASSICAL, re-derived here.** For every $s\sim_A t$, every public adaptive randomized policy, every finite horizon, and every common source-blind randomized computation of its transcript, output laws coincide. The conclusion also holds for measurable stopping/output rules, counting divergence separately.

Consequently, if (T$s$\ne T(t)), no such procedure identifies $T$ with zero error on both sources. With equal prior weights, any always-answering binary classifier has average success exactly $1/2$.

The law signature (D_A$s$=(L_s(a^n,\cdot))_{n,a^n}) determines $T$ iff $T$ is constant on its fibers. This is distributional identification, not a promise of a finite zero-error experiment.

## 5. Proof attempt

For $h_n=(a_1,y_1,\ldots,a_n,y_n)$, causal composition gives

\[
\Pr_s^\pi(h_n)=L_s(a^n,y^n)\prod_{j=1}^{n}\pi_j(a_j\mid h_{j-1}).
\]

The policy factors are common, so equality of $L_s,L_t$ implies equality of every transcript law. Persistent random memory causes no exception: fix its independent random tape, prove the deterministic-policy case, then integrate over tapes. A postprocessor $R(z\mid h)$ satisfies

\[
\Pr_s(Z=z)=\sum_h R(z\mid h)\Pr_s(H=h)=\Pr_t(Z=z).
\]

Arbitrarily long computation changes $R$, not the information supplied to it. Equality of finite cylinders determines the same infinite transcript measure; stopping and divergence are measurable functions thereof. This mathematical limit is not executed infinite time.

For an actual transcript, retain $B(h)=\{(s,c):\Pr_s(h,c)>0\}$. A universally sound decoder exists at that terminal precisely when

\[
\bigl|\{T$s$:(s,c)\in B(h)\}\bigr|=1.
\]

Necessity follows from two compatible original targets demanding different answers to the same evidence; sufficiency follows by reporting their common value. A singleton current-carrier projection is insufficient.

## 6. Crucible

\[
\rho_B=|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|,
\quad |\Phi^+\rangle=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2,
\quad \rho_M=(|00\rangle\langle00|+|11\rangle\langle11|)/2.

Both $Q$ and $E$ marginals equal $I/2$. **If every allowed evolution factors through common $Q$-local CP instruments**, every unnormalized branch obeys

\[
\operatorname{Tr}_E[(\mathcal I\otimes\mathrm{id})(\rho)]
=\mathcal I(\operatorname{Tr}_E\rho),
\]

so induction gives all $Q$-local history laws equal. Equal initial marginals alone do **not** justify this premise. An allowed CNOT with $Q$ controlling $E$ maps $\rho_B$ to $|+0\rangle\langle+0|$, but $\rho_M$ to $I_Q/2\otimes|0\rangle\langle0|$; subsequent local $X$ gives plus probabilities $1,1/2$.

Indeed $\rho_M\ge\rho_B/2$, so any positive measurement effect with positive Bell probability also has positive mixture probability: single-copy zero-error identification is impossible. Matching $E$ marginal alone adds no discriminator; joint correlation access does.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

The minimal new-instrument gate is **one genuine operational refinement**: an authorized, commonly executable experiment, with explicit acquisition provenance and transition, whose outcome law differs on an old equivalence pair having different original targets. Any experiment assembled solely from old actions and source-blind computations fails this gate by the theorem. For immediate zero-error completion, every resulting original-target fiber must additionally be homogeneous. A deterministic extra readout returning $0$ on $A$, $1$ on $B$ passes both gates; the quantum parity readout passes only law separation. Authorization does not itself establish physical realizability.
