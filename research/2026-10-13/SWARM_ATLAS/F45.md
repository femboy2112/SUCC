# SWM-F45 — Sharp debt thresholds and scheduling versus Zeno limits

## 1. Original question

Can a knowledge/debt growth law force every finite stage to remain unfinished, and how is this distinguished from starvation of an already finite proof task?

## 2. Existing mathematical context

Geometric-series recurrences and fair dovetailing are classical. [Levin's original search paper](https://www.karlin.mff.cuni.cz/~krajicek/levin.pdf) and [author's research page](https://www.cs.bu.edu/fac/lnd/research/hard.htm) locate search/scheduling prior art. The elementary schedule proved below is not claimed to be Levin's optimal universal-search algorithm. VOYAGER O638 already distinguishes finite witness existence from effective extraction and physical cost.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Abstract unresolved debt D_n, growth multiplier a>0, supplied service b r^n with b,r>0, and recurrence D_{n+1}=aD_n−b r^n until the first D_n≤0. All quantities here are rationals; completion is a finite stopping predicate. No empirical proof-complexity law is inferred. A separate scheduler assigns one step to each of tasks1,…,s in round s; task k is assumed to have a genuine fixed N-step proof search.

## 4. Candidate theorem

For every D_0,a,b,r>0:

1. If r≥a, debt reaches ≤0 at a finite stage.
2. If r<a, finite completion occurs **iff** D_0<b/(a−r).
3. At equality D_0=b/(a−r), D_n=D_0 r^n>0 for every finite n, even when r<1 makes its limit zero.
4. The fair triangular scheduler finishes task k requiring N steps by round k+N−1, after at most (k+N−1)(k+N)/2 total allocated steps. A policy never allocating any step to k can fail despite the task being finite.

## 5. Proof attempt

Divide the recurrence by a^{n+1} and telescope:

\[
D_n=a^n\left(D_0-\frac b a\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}(r/a)^j\right).
\]

For r<a the increasing partial service sum approaches b/(a−r) strictly from below. Hence the target threshold is crossed at a finite stage precisely when D_0 is strictly below that limit; equality gives D_n=D_0r^n. For r=a the sum is nb/a and crosses any fixed D_0. For r>a it diverges and does likewise. The smallest n is exactly the first finite partial sum at least D_0; no floating logarithm is needed to certify it.

Scheduler task k receives one step per round k,k+1,…; by round k+N−1 it has N steps. Summing round costs proves the triangular bound. This separates ordinary starvation from the intrinsic chosen recurrence. Neither argument decides an unbounded mathematical theorem.

## 6. Crucible

Exact boundary (D0,a,b,r)=(2,1,1,1/2) gives 2,1,1/2,…,1/2048 at stage12, always positive by the derivation. A lower D0=3/2 gives 3/2,1/2,0 at stage2; higher D0=3 never finishes. r=a=2 and r=3>a reach nonpositive debt. Thirty-six scheduler index/length combinations check the explicit allocation bound. These comparisons discriminate threshold failure from poor scheduling.

Assign elapsed durations 2^{−(n+1)} to the boundary stages: total ideal time is1, but no finite stage supplies the completion predicate. A Zeno accumulation point requires an additional report/limit operation; it is not an executed finite proof. Conversely divergent sum of positive per-stage resource costs is an additional resource theorem, not implied merely by D_n>0.

## 7. Verdict

**PROVED LOCAL** sharp recurrence criterion and fair scheduler bound. **REFUTED**: zero limiting debt automatically means a finite completed certificate. The recurrence is an explicitly designed model, not measured human knowledge growth or a universal proof-search impossibility.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

The load-bearing unproved application premise is that a real task obeys this recurrence with no alternative route. Next verdict-changing probe: derive a lower bound from an actual source/action/target contract that every policy must obey, or construct a policy escaping the assumed debt law. F48's history theorem supplies the correct state variables but does not itself force this cost recurrence.

## 9. Cross-domain connection

Use the lawful-history prefix h_n as stage and attach an independently specified cost cocycle c(h→k), additive under concatenation. Quotient history transport preserves that cocycle only when costs descend through the quotient; hidden carrier costs must be retained. VOYAGER memory lower bounds can constrain storage, but cannot be substituted for this service law without a map from storage state to debt.
