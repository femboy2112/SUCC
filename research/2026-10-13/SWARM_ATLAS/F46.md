# SWM-F46 — Load-bearing features as an exact anchored graph model

## 1. Original question

What rigorous mathematical quantity can model resistance and propagation of an update to an integrated feature, while avoiding an unsupported neuroscience interpretation?

## 2. Existing mathematical context

Quadratic graph energies, anchored Laplacians and rank-one inverse updates are classical. Primary matrix source: Sherman–Morrison (1950), [original article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729893). The identity below is independently derived and checked against normal equations; no psychological claims rely on that citation. Existing `observational_succ/phenomenology.py` describes arithmetic probe regimes, not neural cognition.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Undirected finite weighted graph G, positive edge weights w_ij, nonnegative anchoring weights α_i, and at least one positive anchor per connected component. Let A=L_G+diag(α), baseline model m∈Q^n, and a new evidence term η(x_j−b)²/2 with η>0. The revised representation minimizes

\[
E(x)=\tfrac12(x-m)^TA(x-m)+\tfrac\eta2(x_j-b)^2.
\]

The abstract self-boundary may be a chosen node subset; its existence or personal meaning is not inferred from the graph. Age of onset is absent unless an explicit parameter law is added.

## 4. Candidate theorem

A is positive definite under the component-anchor hypothesis, so the revised model is unique and

\[
x^*=m+\frac{\eta(b-m_j)}{1+\eta R_{jj}}R e_j,\qquad R=A^{-1}.
\]

Own-node susceptibility is ηR_jj/(1+ηR_jj); other-feature propagation is the corresponding Green-column ratio. Node degree alone does not determine either quantity. Increasing anchor α_j at a fixed graph weakly decreases R_jj and therefore decreases own-node susceptibility.

## 5. Proof attempt

x^TAx=Σ_edges w_ij(x_i−x_j)²+Σ_i α_i x_i². Vanishing forces x constant per component and zero at an anchored node, hence x=0. Strict convexity gives a unique solution to A(x−m)+ηe_j(e_j^Tx−b)=0. Write x−m=tRe_j; substituting yields t=η(b−m_j)/(1+ηR_jj), proving the formula. For an anchor increment s>0 the same rank-one identity gives R'_jj=R_jj/(1+sR_jj), strictly smaller when R_jj>0.

## 6. Crucible

Three-node star with all anchors1, baseline0 and evidence b=η=1: center update is (1/3,1/6,1/6); leaf update is (2/13,5/13,1/13). Integration increases propagation while own-node response can be smaller. At exactly the same graph/degree, increasing the leaf anchor from1 to100 reduces its susceptibility. An unanchored connected graph has a constant null mode and is rejected; uniqueness cannot be claimed. Exact Fraction computation independently checks the update against modified normal equations for three evidence nodes and a nonzero rational baseline.

The countermodel kills any unconditional equation “deeper graph integration = harder correction” based only on degree, or any inference of age sensitivity from a timeless graph. It preserves the useful question: which specified anchor/connection metric actually controls revision?

## 7. Verdict

**CLASSICAL / PROVED LOCAL** exact update and sensitivity law; **REFUTED AS INFERENCE**: degree alone or an unspecified age variable predicts update resistance. This is an abstract representation-revision model and supplies no validation of TLICA as neuroscience or psychology.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

The source-to-graph interpretation, anchors, energy and evidence weights are application premises. A threshold for structural reconstruction requires changing the graph/anchors themselves; solving this fixed SPD problem only revises a state. Smallest next mathematical extension: characterize when an evidence-driven graph-update rule changes connectedness, invariant subspaces or identifiability, with retained original history.

## 9. Cross-domain connection

Map a lawful report h→h e to a checked evidence term only through a declared interpretation. The graph update is semantic revision, whereas F48 original support shrinks only under source-consistent events. Distinct graph representations may process the same causal record differently without changing source truth. A feature projection is adequate for a target only if the target factors through it (F44), independent of small prediction error.
