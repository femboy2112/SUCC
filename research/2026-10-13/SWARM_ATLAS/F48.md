# SWM-F48 — A discrete fibration of lawful ORIGINAL-source histories

## 1. Original question

Can original states, changed carriers, histories, future feasibility, policies, proof support and ideal limits coexist in one exact object without assuming resets or confusing a counterfactual branch with a realized causal path?

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

A deterministic source contract consists of original preparation indices O; current carrier C; preparation i:O→C; authorized instruments a with enabling e_a:C→{0,1}, outcome r_a:C→Y_a, and update U_a:C→C. Resource exhaustion may be encoded into C/enabling. U_a is evaluated only on enabled states; the executable model supplies total mathematical tables without licensing disabled executions. No reset exists unless explicitly declared as an instrument with its own outcomes/dynamics. This cohort's sources do not declare one.

Let B be the prefix category of finite typed event words h=((a1,y1),…,(an,yn)); h→k iff h is a prefix of k. Define

\[
P(h)=\{o\in O:\text{lawful replay from }i(o)\text{ gives exactly }h\}.
\]

For each o∈P(h), c_h(o) is its **current** carrier after h. The observer's mathematical alternative set is the indexed graph {(o,c_h(o)):o∈P(h)}. This is neither a claim that every alternative was physically prepared nor a report that every counterfactual action was performed.

For h≤k, define restriction P(k)→P(h) as inclusion of originals. P is contravariant: longer reports eliminate original alternatives. Its category of elements E has objects(h,o) with o∈P(h), and a unique arrow(h,o)→(k,o) when h≤k and o survives k. Projection E→B is the promised **discrete fibration**. The changed carrier is separate evaluation data satisfying c_{h(a,y)}(o)=U_a(c_h(o)), not an invertible restriction map on physical states.

A target is T:O→V. A nonempty history supports a source-relative original-target report v exactly when T is constant v on P(h). A robust policy chooses an instrument enabled at **every** current alternative and may stop only at a target-constant fiber. Source-sensitive availability is included, not inferred from a silently observed guard. Counterfactual policy branches are mathematically represented but only the actual branch is a causal receipt.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**Original-history quotient theorem.** Let q:C→D be a surjective quotient, with descended instruments satisfying, for all c,a,

\[
e'_a(qc)=e_a(c),\quad r'_a(qc)=r_a(c),\quad
U'_a(qc)=q(U_a c),\qquad i'=q i.
\]

Retain the **same** original index set O. Then for every finite typed history h:

1. P'(h)=P(h) as subsets of O.
2. c'_h(o)=q(c_h(o)) for every surviving o.
3. The two original-history presheaves and their discrete fibrations are canonically isomorphic over B.
4. Original-target support and robust history-dependent policy branches are preserved exactly. If action costs are functions of history/instrument or descend through q, the associated policy costs are preserved too.

In addition, finite O guarantees that any infinite history whose every finite prefix has nonempty support has at least one single original realizer. Infinite O requires a separate compactness/closedness condition; an ideal prefix tower need not be actualized.

## 5. Proof attempt

**Presheaf/fibration.** If o replays k it replays every prefix h, so P(k)⊆P(h). Inclusion restrictions satisfy identity/composition exactly. Given h→k and an object(k,o), there is exactly one arrow above it with that codomain: (h,o)→(k,o). This proves discrete fibration directly and supplies actual maps/coherence, not merely a diagram.

**Quotient induction.** At the empty history, both sources admit every o and c'_empty=q i(o). Suppose equality of supports and current evaluations holds at h. For event(a,y), the same o survives on both sides because enabling and outcome descend. For each survivor, the commuting update square gives c'_{h(a,y)}=U'_a(c'_h)=U'_a(q c_h)=q U_a(c_h)=q c_{h(a,y)}. This proves statements1–2 for all finite h. Restriction maps are identical inclusions on O, so the identity on originals defines a natural presheaf isomorphism and hence the category-of-elements isomorphism. Target constancy and universal enabling coincide, proving support/policy preservation; descending costs prove cost equality pointwise along corresponding branches.

**Proof support transport.** If P(h) is nonempty and T=v on P(h), then every lawful nonempty extension k has P(k)⊆P(h), so T=v there too. The source-independent original proposition is not changed when the carrier is erased. Empty support signals a contradiction in the source model/receipt; the software does not report empirical certainty by ex falso.

**Finite actualization.** Along an infinite history, finite original supports form nested nonempty subsets P0⊇P1⊇…. Each strict loss removes an original, so there are at most |O|−1 strict losses. The intersection is nonempty; any element replays every finite prefix and realizes the whole mathematical history. This proves ideal compatibility, not physical execution of infinitely many actions. For infinite O, take eventually-zero binary streams and all-one read/shift history: every finite prefix survives but the intersection is empty, reproducing VOYAGER's obstruction.

## 6. Crucible

**Erasure control:** originals0/1 both evolve to current0 after erase, yielding {(0,0),(1,0)}. Later current read0 identifies neither original. If read1 occurred before erase, retained support is {(1,0)}, and original target1 remains certified despite the altered carrier. Endpoint-only current-state models conflate these distinct supports.

**Branch gluing failure:** one preparation of two bits (a,b); read a erases b, read b erases a. Counterfactual single-event fibers a=1 and b=1 intersect at original11. Yet sequential histories (read a=1, read b=1) and (read b=1, read a=1) both have empty support. Source-fiber overlap is insufficient for an actual single-carrier amalgamation. No sheaf theorem can be claimed just by intersecting semantic branches.

**Feasibility mutation:** two current states with identical outcomes but different availability cannot be merged. `quotient_source` rejects the quotient. This hypothesis is essential for policy preservation even when every numerical report agrees.

**Positive quotient:** duplicate-copy four-state source quotients to its two visible states while retaining four original indices. The receipt checks94 legal histories through depth5. Twenty-five generated duplicate-carrier systems with declared deterministic actions and variable availability check the same invariants through depth3. Prefix restrictions are checked for all nested pairs through depth4. Forged prefixes, unlicensed reset and empty-support certainty are rejected. Certification, ambiguous support and inconsistent support are separate typed verdicts; a legitimate null semantic value is not confused with uncertainty.
