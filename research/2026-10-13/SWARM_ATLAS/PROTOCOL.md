# Investigator protocol

Read AGENTS.md and docs/METHOD.md, relevant current modules/dossiers, and import manifest before touching any imported path. Work only in your assigned worktree. Use new files for native contributions. Browse primary sources for niche mathematics/prior art. Preserve exact source parent, command exits, definitions, hypotheses, quantifiers and counterexamples. Never claim academic novelty from repository novelty.

Dossier sections: (1) Original question (2) Existing mathematical context (3) Proposed SUCC objects (4) Candidate theorem (5) Proof attempt (6) Crucible (7) Verdict (8) Remaining obstruction (9) Cross-domain connection with actual maps (10) Deliverables. Status options CLASSICAL, LEAN-KERNEL PROVED, PROVED LOCAL, TESTED MODEL, CONJECTURE, REFUTED, BLOCKED. Keep subclaims separate.

No reading other first-generation dossiers or intermediate conclusions. No shared ledger/roadmap/architecture edits, no runtime/default changes, no archives. Commit scoped artifacts, do not push. No full suites or parallel compilers; exact probes bounded in space/time. A complete short theorem with hostile witness is preferable to an unbounded speculative essay. Final mathematical acceptance belongs to independent crucible and principal review.
