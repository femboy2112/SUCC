## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Fix sets S,W, a discrete report alphabet O, and Φ:S→O^W. A declared witness
family A assigns a set A_s to each original source. Its total receipt space is
E=Σ_s A_s; an element e=(s,a) retains both identity and receipt. Put
Ψ(e)=Φ(s). Let λ:E→L be a supplied label, with L discrete; source labels
factor through e↦s.

For every finite F⊆W, including ∅, set

\[
X_F=\Phi(S)|_F,\quad
D_F=\{(\Psi(e)|_F,e):e\in E\},\quad
B_F=\{(\Psi(e)|_F,\lambda(e)):e\in E\}.
\]

For F⊆G, restriction sends (r,e)↦(r|F,e) and (r,l)↦(r|F,l).
These are actual maps obeying identity and composition. D_F is a witness
family over X_F; its projection to X_F×L has image B_F, the nonemptiness
truncation. Write J(e)=(Ψ(e),λ(e)) in Y=O^W×L with product topology.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**X01-T1, PROVED LOCAL paper theorem using classical ingredients.** For every
such datum, the canonical maps give

\[
\lim_F D_F\cong E,\qquad \lim_F B_F\cong\overline{J(E)}.
\]

Consequently **the image of the retained-witness limit equals the limit of
stage images iff J(E) is closed**. Equivalently every labelled compatible
profile must have one receipt. Because L is discrete, this holds iff
Ψ(λ⁻¹(l)) is closed for every l∈L. Compact E with continuous Ψ and λ suffices;
compact S alone does not.

More generally suppose E is Hausdorff, every Ψ-coordinate and λ continuous,
and c:E→[0,∞) finite-valued with compact sublevels. For every (z,l), define

\[
M(z,l)=\sup_{F\text{ finite}}
\inf\{c(e):\Psi(e)|_F=z|_F,\lambda(e)=l\}.
\]

Then inf_{J(e)=(z,l)}c(e)=M(z,l), with empty infimum ∞ and attainment whenever
M<∞. Thus a global receipt exists exactly when finite compatible receipts
have **one finite cap uniform over F**, for this fixed (z,l).

**X01-T2, PROVED LOCAL.** The refinement comparison
D_G→X_G×_{X_F}D_F is injective. If every A_s is inhabited, it is bijective
iff X_G→X_F is injective. Report surjectivity alone supplies no cartesian lift.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete direct derivation

Decode a retained inverse family at ∅ to its e. Coherence forces every other
coordinate to retain that same e and equal Ψ(e)|F. Conversely those coordinates
encode e; both inverse equations follow. Receipt multiplicity and λ are
preserved exactly.

Decode a B-family at ∅ to l and at singleton probes to z. Coherence gives
its F-coordinate (z|F,l), whose membership supplies an e_F matching all probes
in F with that label. Conversely these restrictions encode any such pair.
Finite cylinders, with {l} open in discrete L, identify precisely closure
of J(E). The retained limit projects to J(E), proving the closed-image
criterion and the label-slice criterion.

For the cost claim, every global receipt bounds all finite infima. If M=∞,
finite-valued c excludes it. If M<∞, inside compact K_{M+1} use closed sets
{e:Ψ(e)|F=z|F,λ(e)=l}∩K_{M+ε}, 0<ε≤1. Compact sublevels are closed in Hausdorff
E. A finite collection is witnessed by unioning its probe sets and taking its
least ε: its infimum is ≤M, so some receipt has cost <M+ε. The finite
intersection property yields one receipt matching every probe and having
cost ≤M. This proves equality and attainment, without selecting an infinite
physical history.

The refinement comparison sends (r,e)↦(r,(r|F,e)); retaining e makes it
injective. If report restriction is injective, matching small reports forces
the required large report. Conversely two different large reports over the
same small one, plus a receipt realizing either source, exhibit a missing
pullback pair. Inhabited A_s is material to that converse.

## 6. Crucible

**Smallest nontrivial refinement failure.** S={0,1}, one bit probe with
Φ(s)=s, A_s={*}, F=∅, G={probe}. Every stage projection is surjective, but
the pullback has four pairs and D_G only two. The pair (large report 1,
original receipt 0) is missing. A single source cannot produce this failure.
Truth coverage at each stage therefore does not certify witness pullback.

**Compact-source escape.** Let S=N_{≥1}∪{∞} with the convergent-sequence
topology; finite k are isolated. Set q_n(k)=1[k≤n], q_n(∞)=0, n≥1. Every
coordinate is continuous, and S is compact. Set A_k={*}, A_∞=∅. The all-zero
profile is realized by original source ∞, but has no receipt: every finite k
fails at probe n=k. Its first h probes have witnesses k>h. Thus E, the
supported receipt space, is noncompact and Ψ(E) nonclosed. Cost c(k)=k has
compact sublevels and minimum finite cost h+1, tending to ∞.

Constant labels preserve this phantom; injective labels λ(k)=k eliminate it,
since the empty-stage label fixes k and probe k refutes that labelled pair.
Labels solve this abstract identity problem only when actually supplied.
Restoring A_∞={*}, with c(∞)=0, repairs receipt existence; a deliberately
expensive chosen approximating sequence cannot refute that repair.

**Compact positive control.** On Cantor sources, adjacent-bit derivative
q_n(x)=x_n xor x_{n+1} has exactly two preparations for each profile, by
recursive integration from x_0=0 or 1. Full receipts retain both; truncation
erases this multiplicity. Inhabitation does not identify the original source.

## 9. Cross-domain connection with actual maps

The common structure is the diagram D_F→B_F⊆X_F×L. A01-style existential
direct image and nonemptiness produce B_F; B09-style inverse completion
produces closure J(E); the cost gate applies to **E**, not merely S.
The resulting limit/image exchange criterion and noncartesian refinement
diagnostic concern maps absent from any one predecessor dossier. This is a
local synthesis theorem, not a claim that three prior results are independent
empirical bearings. Source identity, receipt identity, and labels survive in

The abstract fixed-base family category exists. Physically realizable histories
need separately certified actions, pullbacks and receipt access; no functor
from arbitrary physical histories is constructed here.
