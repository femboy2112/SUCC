# SWM-X01 — Repair, completion transport, and source actualization

## 1. Original question

When does a strict source/action comparison extend from the repaired actual
behavioral quotients to their inverse-limit completions? Which hypotheses
make this extension onto, injective, or reflective of original-source
realization? This is an independent second-generation synthesis of A01/A06
with B09/B10, including an audit of the compact-policy core.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects and maps

Fix an arbitrary action alphabet A and its full finite-word set W=A*. Sources
X,Y carry total actions T_a and U_a, reports h_X:X→O and h_Y:Y→P, with O,P
discrete. A comparison is an actual function f:X→Y and report code k:O→P
satisfying, for every a,x,

    f(T_a x)=U_a(f x),       h_Y(f x)=k(h_X x).

Let Φ_X(x)(w)=h_X(T_w x), and similarly Φ_Y. Write A_X=Φ_X(X),
K_X=closure(A_X)⊆O^W, and likewise for Y. The repaired actual quotient Q_X
is X/ker Φ_X; identify it with A_X and give it the topology induced by its
finite report coordinates. This topology is explicitly specified; no
unspecified original-source quotient topology is smuggled in.

Let C_k:O^W→P^W be pointwise coding, C_k(p)(w)=k(p(w)). Word induction gives
Φ_Y f=C_k Φ_X. The induced completion map is

    f̂ = C_k|K_X : K_X → K_Y.

Completion carries continuous actions σ_a p(w)=p(aw), where aw means perform
a and then w. These preserve K_X because they preserve A_X and are
continuous. C_k commutes with every σ_a. Actual source traces, repaired
quotients, and their closed completions remain distinct objects.

## 4. Candidate theorem with exact hypotheses

**T1: transport and repair.** For every comparison above, f̂ is a well-defined
continuous action map, natural with the original source traces. It induces
the corresponding continuous map on B09 inverse threads by conjugating with
the explicit continuous inverse maps. Identity and composition of comparisons
give identity and composition of completion maps. Moreover

    ker(C_k Φ_X) = I_X(f* ker h_Y) = f*(I_Y(ker h_Y)).

Thus the transported repaired relation is exactly the kernel of the
completion comparison on original X states. It can be strictly coarser than
native ker Φ_X when k forgets report information.

**T2: exact image and onto criterion.** If O is finite, with no finiteness
assumption on P, then

    f̂(K_X) = closure(Φ_Y(f(X))).

Consequently f̂ is onto K_Y iff the transported actual trace image Φ_Y(f(X))
is dense in K_Y. Surjectivity of f is sufficient, not necessary. If the
reports are exactly preserved (O=P, k=id), surjectivity of f makes f̂ a
homeomorphism even without a finite alphabet: the two actual trace images
and hence the two closed completions are equal.

**T3: exact finite-recovery criterion for injectivity.** Suppose O is finite.
Then f̂ is injective iff

    for every finite F⊆W, there is a finite G⊆W such that for all x,x'∈X,
    [∀w∈G, k(Φ_X(x)(w))=k(Φ_X(x')(w))]
       implies [∀w∈F, Φ_X(x)(w)=Φ_X(x')(w)].

This is uniform finite-coordinate recovery on actual sources. Injectivity of
k is sufficient without finiteness, but not necessary. Injectivity or even
bijectivity of the carrier map f alone is insufficient.

**T4: source actualization square.** Every actual X profile transports to an
actual Y profile. If f is surjective and f̂ injective, then the converse
holds for every p∈K_X:

    f̂(p)∈A_Y  iff  p∈A_X.

Hence an actualization theorem A_Y=K_Y transfers to X under these two gates,
without requiring an a priori topology on X. In particular, exactly
report-preserving surjective source transport preserves and reflects source
actualization. A completion homeomorphism by itself does not suffice.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete local derivation

For T1, equality of action composites proves the trace intertwiner by word
induction. Every finite set of coordinates of p∈K_X has an X witness by B09;
its image under f witnesses the corresponding coordinates of C_k(p).
Therefore C_k(p)∈K_Y. Alternatively use continuity of pointwise coding and
the image-of-closure inclusion. Continuity follows coordinatewise because
k is continuous between discrete alphabets. Coordinate precomposition gives
σ_a continuity and commutation directly. On inverse threads the map is
`profileToThread_Y ∘ f̂ ∘ threadToProfile_X`; all inverse and continuity laws
come from B09's actual maps. Identity and composition are pointwise equalities.
The displayed kernel equality is the all-word characterization of A01 repair,
using the trace intertwiner. This is a typed commuting square, not an analogy.

For T2, finite O makes K_X compact. Its continuous image is compact and
therefore closed in Hausdorff P^W. It contains C_k(A_X)=Φ_Y(f(X)), so it
contains that set's closure. Conversely continuity gives
C_k(closure A_X)⊆closure(C_k(A_X)). These two inclusions prove the equality.
Surjectivity of f makes Φ_Y(f(X))=A_Y. With k=id, this equality identifies
the actual trace images themselves, so compactness is unnecessary.

For the forward direction of T3, fix finite F. In compact K_X×K_X, the set
D_F of pairs disagreeing somewhere on F is closed, hence compact. Injectivity
of f̂ says every pair in D_F disagrees after coding on some coordinate w.
The open sets witnessing each such disagreement cover D_F. A finite subcover
chooses G. Restricting to actual source pairs gives the displayed recovery
implication. Conversely let p,q∈K_X have identical coded profiles. Given F,
choose its G. Finite compatibility separately supplies x,x' matching p,q on
F∪G. Their coded coordinates agree on G, so the recovery premise gives
agreement on F. Thus p,q agree on every finite F, hence p=q. This reverse
implication in fact needs no compactness; finiteness supplies the forward
uniformity implication.

For T4, naturality gives forward preservation. If f̂(p)=Φ_Y(y), choose x
with f(x)=y. Then f̂(p)=Φ_Y(f(x))=f̂(Φ_X(x)); injectivity gives p=Φ_X(x).
This explicitly states the source lift and information-reflection step that
an arbitrary diagram of completed spaces could conceal.

## 6. Crucible and hypothesis failures

**Lose source-image control.** Let X be finite-support binary streams, Y all
binary streams, with shift and head reports, f the inclusion and k=id. Both
completions are the full Cantor stream space and f̂ is identity, but all ones
is Y-realized and not X-realized. Every finite coordinate pattern has the
X witness with exactly those required one bits. Thus a completion
homeomorphism neither manufactures a source lift nor reflects realization.
Dropping injectivity in T4 also fails: map the same X onto a one-point Y,
use constant k, and every completed profile maps to Y's actual point.

**Lose onto source transport.** A one-state constant-zero source included in
a two-state identity-action source reporting 0 and 1 has a non-onto completion
map. If both target states report 0 instead, the same non-surjective carrier
map is onto after completion. This distinguishes the exact trace-density
criterion from unnecessarily demanding carrier surjectivity.

**Lose compactness of the source completion.** Take X=N, T(n)=max(n−1,0),
native report h_X(n)=n in the infinite discrete alphabet N, and k(n)=1_{n>0}.
Let Y={1^n0^∞:n∈N}∪{1^∞}, with shift/head, and f(n)=1^n0^∞. This is a
strict comparison. K_X=A_X: coordinate zero fixes n and therefore every
remaining coordinate max(n−w,0). The image f̂(K_X) omits all ones, although
it is dense in K_Y. Thus the closed-image equality and density→onto inference
need compactness (finite O is one sufficient condition).

**Lose report continuity on a compact source.** Put K={0}∪{1/n:n≥1} with its
usual compact real topology. Label 0 by integer 0 and 1/n by n. The action
sends n to floor(n/2), interpreted back in K, and the fixed binary report is
n mod 2. The action is continuous: all positive points are isolated and
floor(n/2)→∞ as n→∞, hence images approach 0. The report is discontinuous
at 0 because odd n approach that point with report 1. Its trace image is
exactly the finite-support binary streams (the least-significant-first
binary expansions of n), so the all-one completion profile is not realized.
The fixed read-and-update policy that stops at its first zero terminates on
every original source, yet n=2^m−1 requires m+1 readings. A compact source
and continuous actions alone therefore imply neither actualization nor a
uniform stopping bound. This countermodel uses only one readout and action.

**Lose action commutation.** The two-state identity source with constant
report 0 maps report-preservingly by f=(0,1) into the three-state source
T=(0,2,2), h=(0,0,1). Its two states remain equivalent in X but their images
are distinguished after one Y action. The repair/base-change square fails.

**Lose information despite a source bijection.** Two fixed states with
reports 0 and 1, f=id, constant target report, and constant k give a bijective
carrier comparison but a noninjective completion comparison. Conversely,
on the three-cycle T=(1,2,0), h=(0,1,2), the noninjective code k=(0,0,1)
preserves all three whole behaviors: two coded readings recover the position.

The independent probe enumerates all **4,096** binary unary source/report/map
tuples. Exactly **512** are strict comparisons. All satisfy the repair square
and finite-recovery equivalence. Among them, 352 completion maps are
injective, 320 onto, 128 carrier maps onto, 192 completion-onto comparisons
have non-onto carrier maps, and 32 carrier bijections lose completion
injectivity. It also checks 511 finite-support patterns, 1,320 ray coordinates,
and 41 all-one prefix witnesses. The infinite claims above have quantified
proofs; these finite counts do not certify them.

## 7. Verdict

**PROVED LOCAL / CLASSICAL INGREDIENTS.** T1–T4 have complete paper proofs.
`SwarmX01CompletionTransport.lean` kernel-checks completion construction and
continuity, injectivity from an injective report code, source naturality,
T4's reflection gate, and completion surjectivity with exactly preserved
reports. It assumes the explicit trace intertwiner; action-word induction,
T2's compact image equality, and T3's exact uniform finite-recovery criterion
remain paper proofs. No `sorry`, `admit`, or new axiom was introduced.

## 8. Remaining obstruction and next falsifier

The report code is explicit coordinatewise transport, not an arbitrary
possibly discontinuous function of an entire source history. Extending to
changing probe alphabets requires an explicit probe-reindexing map and its
action compatibility. Extending to stochastic instruments needs joint-law
transport, not replacement of probabilities by support profiles. The next
kernel obligation is T3, especially compactness of the disagreeing-pair
space. The next mathematical falsifier is a proposed noncompact source with
injective coded completion but no finite-coordinate recovery; the present
proof identifies exactly where compactness is used.

## 9. Cross-domain connection and dominance boundary

A01/A06's repair is the actual trace-image quotient. B09's completion closes
that quotient, and this mission supplies the source-typed natural map between
both levels plus exact information and realization gates. B10's observational
completeness explains the finite-support counterexample. The compact-policy
theorem uses actual history cylinders on original sources; it is compatible
with these maps only after policy/readout transport is provided. None of T1–T4
constructs a reset, prepares a limiting source, executes all words on one
destructive carrier, proves a physically available decision policy, or crosses
the RH source-authentication firewall.
