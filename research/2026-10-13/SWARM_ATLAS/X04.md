## 3. Common ambient objects, maps, and invariant

Let `S` be a nonempty set, `F:S→S` a fixed total evolution, and `r:S→A` a
fixed report to a finite alphabet. For `m≥1`, let

    D_m(s) = (r(s), r(Fs), ..., r(F^(m-1)s)),   L_m = D_m(S).

The common invariant is the complete set `L_m` of length-m output itineraries.
A finite autonomous surrogate is `(Q,G,a)` with fixed `G:Q→Q`, `a:Q→A`;
**coverage** means `D_m^Q(Q)=L_m`. This only compares sets of finite traces.
A **factor** additionally requires `e:S→Q`, `eF=Ge`, and `r=ae`; it transports
the same original state through every source step. The distinction is exact.

For the full tent map `T(x)=2x` below `1/2`, `2−2x` above it, use branch labels
and their closed inverse cylinders `I_w`. Every finite binary word has a
nonempty interior cylinder; therefore `|L_m|=2^m` for any fixed deterministic
boundary convention. Closed cylinders can share endpoints. No Bernoulli law,
reset permission, physical clock, or microscopic state authentication is added.

## 4. Candidate theorem and status

**PROVED LOCAL, classical ingredients.**

**(A) Exact finite-horizon surrogate theorem.** A nonempty finite set `L⊆A^m`
is the exact m-word language of some finite total stationary source iff every
`w∈L` has some `v∈L` with `w[1:]=v[:-1]`. Whenever it exists, the smallest
such source has exactly `|L|` states. Thus every `L_m` from the declared
stationary source admits a minimal `|L_m|`-state surrogate. The construction
does not generally give a factor from `S`.

**(B) Tent and full-shift consequence.** Exactly `2^m` states are necessary
and sufficient to cover all their m-itineraries. Even requiring the surrogate
to be one cycle does not increase this minimum. Nevertheless, the map `D_m`
on the full binary shift is not an autonomous quotient for any finite `m≥1`.
No fixed finite autonomous factor can preserve the original branch report for
all times. Every finite horizon does have a finite surrogate.

**(C) Resolution-label theorem with explicit report restriction.** Fix integer
time `t≥0` and precision `k≥1`. If a finite collection of full original tent
cylinders covers `[0,1]`, and every reported cylinder must have image diameter
at most `2^-k` after t steps, at least `2^(t+k)` cylinder labels are required.
That bound is attained. The same number is the minimum stationary surrogate
state count for coverage of all `(t+k)`-itineraries. These are different
resources linked by the shared horizon, not an identification of observers
with the source. For arbitrary deterministic reports, the minimum label
count for this one future-diameter task is only `2^k`.

## 5. Derivation and coherence checks

For (A), every state emits one length-m word, so coverage forces
`|Q|≥|L|`. A word emitted from q has successor word emitted from Gq; their
overlap proves the stated no-dead-end necessity. Conversely use `Q=L` and
choose one overlap successor `G(w)` for each w. Set `a(w)=w_0`. For
`0≤j<m`, repeated overlap gives `(G^j w)_0=w_j`; hence `D_m^Q(w)=w` and
the language is exactly L. This constructs the lower bound. Finiteness makes
successor choice elementary. An original stationary source automatically has
the overlap successor `D_m(Fs)` for every represented `D_m(s)`.

For (B), all `2^m` words occur in interiors of the tent cylinders. For the
single-cycle construction use the directed graph on `(m−1)`-words, with an
edge for each m-word from its prefix to its suffix. Every vertex has two
incoming and two outgoing edges; appending target bits gives a path between
any two vertices. Splicing closed unused-edge walks yields an Euler circuit.
Its edge labels give a cyclic binary string with each m-word once. The cycle
has `2^m` states and attains the coverage bound, including `m=1` via the two
loops on the empty vertex.

However two full-shift streams `0^m0...` and `0^m1...` have equal D_m reports
and unequal next D_m reports. There is no G with `D_m F=G D_m`. If some
other finite factor `(Q,G,a,e)` preserved the branch report, induction on j
would give `a(G^j e(s))=r(F^j s)`. Thus Q would cover all `2^m` words for
every m, forcing `|Q|≥2^m` for all m: contradiction. The finite-horizon
surrogates select successors and discard other continuations; they do not
violate this all-time no-go. Finite-language coverage alone supplies no map e
commuting with the original dynamics.

For (C), D28's independently verified formula is
`diam T^t(I_w)=2^-max(|w|−t,0)`. The tolerance `2^-k<1` therefore requires
`|w|≥t+k`. Each permitted cylinder has initial length at most `2^-(t+k)`.
A finite collection covering an interval of length one must have at least
`2^(t+k)` members, even if overlaps are allowed. All depth `(t+k)` cylinders
attain equality. This is a finite-length covering proof, not an information
entropy or thermodynamic argument.

For arbitrary reporting, partition `[0,1]` into `2^k` dyadic bins with one
fixed endpoint convention and report the bin containing `T^t(x)`. Its fibers
are preimages of future bins and can be disconnected unions of intervals;
their image diameters are at most `2^-k`. Conversely, surjectivity of T^t
implies the images of report fibers cover `[0,1]`, so their interval hulls of
length at most `2^-k` force at least `2^k` labels. This sensor is a different
authorized report map; no procedure for obtaining it from less source access
is asserted.
