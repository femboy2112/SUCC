## 1. Original question

When does a bounded observation of a symbolic chaotic carrier admit a finite
deterministic realization? Steelman: distinguish the autonomous current-report
quotient from the finer state needed to retain every future report. A finite
graph of possible transitions need not itself be a finite autonomous source:
the deterministic shift acts on paths, whose futures carry branching choices.

## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Let G=(V,E) be a finite strongly connected directed graph with an edge,
d the gcd of positive closed-walk lengths, and Σ_G its one-sided vertex-path
shift σ. Every legal finite word extends indefinitely, so every higher-block
edge has actual path realizers. Fix a base vertex and define c(v)∈Z/d by the
length of a path to v. This is well defined and c(w)=c(v)+1 on edges.

More generally declare f:X→X and an itinerary i:X→Σ_G with i f=σ i,
realizing **every legal finite initial word**. Reports have a uniformly bounded grammar:
q(x)=r(i(x)₀,…,i(x)ₘ₋₁), m≥1, with finite image Q.
Define π(x)(n)=q(fⁿx), R=π(X), δ(t)(n)=t(n+1), and e(t)=t(0).
Thus π f=δπ and q=eπ. No probability law is assumed.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**X04-T, PROVED LOCAL, classical specialization.** For every such source/report:

1. q f=Fq for F:Q→Q iff, up to cyclic relabeling,
   q=ι(c(i(x)₀) mod k), where k divides d and ι is injective.
2. R is the minimal output-preserving deterministic abstraction of X;
   ker π is the greatest forward-stable equivalence contained in ker q.
3. R is finite iff q=a(c(i(x)₀)) for some a:Z/d→Q.
   Its cardinality is the primitive period k|d of the cyclic word a.
   Therefore a bounded report outside the phase grammar has infinite R.

## 5. Proof attempt

Two base-to-v paths become closed after the same return path; their lengths
differ by a multiple of d. This proves c well defined and its edge law.
Following a closed walk traverses every residue, so c is onto.

The m-block graph H_m is strongly connected: connect the final vertex of one
word to the first of another, then append that word. Full finite-word
realization makes report autonomy equivalent to r(w)=F(r(v)) on **every**
H_m edge v→w. Its image is a strongly connected functional finite graph,
hence one directed k-cycle. Repeating any G closed walk gives an H_m closed
walk of the same length, so k divides every such length, hence d.
Along a path between blocks, its length modulo k equals the difference of
their first-vertex c-coordinates. Propagating F therefore determines r exactly
from that coordinate modulo k. Conversely every claimed phase map commutes.

Equality of entire traces is an equivalence, is forward stable, and implies
current-report equality. Any forward-stable equivalence inside ker q implies
equality of q(fⁿx) for every n by induction, proving greatestness.
For any abstraction p:X→S with p f=Up and q=hp, define
ν(p(x))(n)=h(Uⁿp(x)). Then νp=π and νU=δν on p(X);
distinct traces require distinct abstraction states. π achieves the bound.

If R is finite, choose N≥1 so its distinct traces have distinct N-prefixes.
π is consequently a bounded-window report of width m+N−1, and is autonomous.
Part 1 makes π an injected k-phase cycle; q=eπ depends only on c.
Conversely phase-dependent q has the d rotations of a as its traces.
Their number is exactly a's primitive cyclic period, proving part 3.

## 6. Crucible

The smallest finite-residual/current-autonomy counterexample has a three-cycle
with reports 001: its three rotations are distinct, yet equal current report
0 has successors 0 and 1. One- and two-phase cyclic reports are always
autonomous, establishing minimality of this obstruction. On the full binary
shift q(x)=x₀ gives π(x)=x: every binary prefix followed by 0^ω is a source
realizer, yielding 2ⁿ distinct trace prefixes for every n. Finiteness of the
report alphabet or presentation graph cannot bound R.

The exact probe enumerates partitions at widths 1–3 for period-two, full-binary,
branching period-three, and six-cycle graphs; cycles of lengths two and three
and a synthetic C self-loop force period one. These mutations are not logistic
source attestations. Both inherited probes are replayed against saved receipts:
D25's fixed boundary still forbids an everywhere binary flip; B16's redundant
six-state constant cycle still has one residual, and prefix 0010 still makes
truncated-suffix compatibility nontransitive. No failed gate is weakened.
