## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Six **simulation assumptions** fix the narrower contract:

1. Finite nonempty sets \(S,X,Q,A,Y,Z\); immutable original \(s\in S\);
   enabled sets \(E^I_a\subseteq S\times X,E^J_a\subseteq S\times Q\);
   rational normalized instruments \(I_a:E^I_a\to\Delta(Y\times X)\),
   \(J_a:E^J_a\to\Delta(Z\times Q)\).
2. A deterministic carrier map \(g:X\to Q\); any joint preparation
   \(\lambda\) on \(S\times X\) is matched by
   \(P\lambda=(\mathrm{id}_S\times g)_*\lambda\), never marginal products.
3. Enabledness satisfies \((s,x)\in E^I_a\iff(s,g(x))\in E^J_a\). Policies choose
   actions safe at every compatible pair; menus supply no extra observation.
4. A stochastic decoder \(D_a:Y\to\Delta Z\), using only the named action
   and reported \(y\), obeys the joint square below. Fresh seeds are independent
   of hidden coordinates. It never reads \(x,g(x')\), or \(s\).
5. Policies and terminal labels depend only on projected public history
   \(h=((a,z),\ldots)\); the target is the same \(t:S\to L\).
   Action selection satisfies \(P(a\mid h,s,x)=\pi_h(a)\).
   Decoder outputs are recorded public computations. Zero-error means every
   supported execution stops with \(t\) constant on compatible originals.
   No reset or extra probe.
6. For cost comparison, declared nonnegative public charges, including decoder
   work, satisfy \(c_I(h,a,y,z)\le c_J(h,a,z)\) on every supported edge.
   Private actual costs are outside this comparison.

For every enabled \(s,x,a,z,q'\), require

\[
\tag{CS}
\sum_{y,x':g(x')=q'}I_a(y,x'\mid s,x)D_a(z\mid y)
=J_a(z,q'\mid s,g(x)).
\]

## 4. Candidate theorem

**X05-T — PROVED LOCAL, conditional finite mathematics.** Under assumptions
1–5, for **every** joint preparation and every public-history randomized policy,
all finite projected history/source/current joint laws agree under (P).
Positive-history posteriors and source supports agree. Every finite-depth,
zero-error (J)-policy transports to a public-history (I+D)-policy of the
same depth bound. Under assumption 6, its worst supported cumulative public
cost is no larger. Consequently the minimum winning depth on (I), allowing
public decoder computation, is at most that on (J). Equality of optimal
depth or cost is not asserted.

## 5. Proof attempt

For a nonnegative measure \(\nu(s,x)\) supported on \(E^I_a\), let

\[
(T^D_{a,z}\nu)(s,x')=\sum_{x,y}\nu(s,x)I_a(y,x'\mid s,x)D_a(z\mid y).
\]

Define \(T^J\) analogously. Finite summation and (CS) give
\(PT^D_{a,z}=T^J_{a,z}P\). Conversely, testing this identity on each enabled
Dirac measure proves (CS): necessity is for this all-prior full-law contract,
not arbitrary restricted initial laws. Matching safe menus makes every chosen
map defined. Multiply by the same public policy factor \(\pi(a\mid h)\);
induction from matched preparation gives the entire subprobability joint law
at each \(h\), including its likelihood. Equal positive normalizers give
posterior equality; zero-mass branches have no posterior.

Nonnegative sums cannot cancel: every supported fine execution and decoder
choice projects to a supported (J)-execution with the same original.
The support of an additional fine (y)-record can only narrow the original
set at projected \(h\). Thus a \(J\)-terminal constant \(t\) remains sound;
all branches stop by the inherited depth. Summing edgewise public-cost
inequalities along that very path proves the bound. Decoder randomization
does not obstruct a deterministic guarantee: select any positive decoder
entry for each (a,y). This preserves support, depth and the bound, although
it generally loses exact probability-law equality.

**New simpler-bridge failure.** Initialize \(x=s\in\{0,1\}\). Action a draws
a fair bit \(r\), replacing the carrier by \(r\). Machine I reports \(y=r\);
machine J reports \(z=x\mathbin\oplus r\). Both next-state and report marginals
are uniform, on every input. Action b reads the current bit. I's ab records
are \((r,r)\), independent of the original; J's are \((s\oplus r,r)\), revealing
it. No original-blind history postprocessor recovers those distinct source
laws. Separate readout/state marginal squares therefore fail even together.
The joint (CS) square rejects them immediately.
