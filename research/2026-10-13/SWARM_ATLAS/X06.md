\((M,+,0)\) is a monoid with **left cancellation**:

Words execute left to right. Define \(C_s(\epsilon)=0\),
\(C_s(aw)=c_a(s)+C_{U_as}(w)\),

labels, not semantic entropy, peak storage, energy, or proof authority.

greatest equivalence preserving reports, immediate costs, and all actions.
The minimum reachable stationary cost-faithful realizer size is
\(|\pi(S_{\rm reachable})|\); finite realization exists exactly when this is finite.
Realizers reproduce reports and cumulative charges at every word, with zero
initial charge and no terminal correction.
The minimum counts reachable stationary weighted control states for supplied mathematical cost labels. It excludes storage and execution costs for label encodings, monoid operations, accumulation/querying of totals, observation history and input scheduling. Every such variable affecting future reports, charges or enabledness belongs in the complete state. These qualifications are required by the independent review.

## 5. Proof attempt

Equality of B at \(\epsilon\) gives equal reports; equality at a gives equal
\(c_a\). Equality at aw then gives equal successor reports and
\(c_a(s)+C_{U_as}(w)=c_a(s)+C_{U_at}(w)\).
Cancel the common prefix. Thus \(\ker\pi\) is action stable. Conversely, any
report/cost/action-stable equivalence preserves B by word induction.
Define \(V_a(B_s)=B_{U_as}\), \(\bar q(B_s)=q(s)\), and
\(\bar c_a(B_s)=c_a(s)\); these maps are well defined and attain the bound.

For any other pointed realizer and history u, its prefix charge equals the
source's. Equality of charges at uv and left cancellation therefore identify
the continuation charge after u. Equal reached realizer states force equal
source B-residuals; distinct residuals require distinct states.

Under cancellation, unary B-equality is exactly equality of both shifted
report and immediate-cost streams: consecutive prefix totals recover each
next cost. A joint periodic tail cannot begin before either least preperiod;
both tails repeat from their maximum with lcm period. Any joint tail period
is a period of each component, hence a multiple of each primitive period.
B16's distinct-suffix argument then gives the displayed sharp count.

H is the free action-history category, arrows \((s,w):s\to U_ws\), priced
\(\lambda(C_s(w))\). Its residual counterpart has arrows \((\pi s,w)\).
F sends states by \(\pi\) and keeps w; identities and concatenation commute.
Every residual path lifts from **every** representative with identical charge.
Consequently its hom-cost infimum equals the displayed target-fibre infimum.
Same-endpoint concatenation proves triangle; the thin Lawvere base supplies
coherence. No infimum needs to be attained.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

\(2^{-n-1}\) have total 1 but infinitely many residuals, distinguished by
their first costs. Conversely constant reports and unit charges require one
state but have divergent cumulative cost. F45's summability and B16's memory
index therefore remain independent; no entropy distribution was specified.
