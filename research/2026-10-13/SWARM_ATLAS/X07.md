## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

An object of `PosFaith` is a real vector space, convex cone \(K\), and linear
functional \(u\), positive and **faithful** on \(K\): \(u(x)=0\Rightarrow x=0\).
Morphisms are positive linear maps; identities and composition are ordinary
linear ones. Its mass-contractive subcategory additionally requires
\(u'R\le u\) on \(K\).

Fix an occurrence poset \(P\) on one declared conflict-free occurrence set.
Let \(\mathcal I\) contain **all finite downward ideals**, ordered by inclusion.
Their union makes \(\mathcal I\) directed.
Each event being covered by an ideal requires finite past; without that
hypothesis the theorem covers only events in the declared ideals. Alternative
conflicting schedules are not silently unioned.

For every ideal \(I\), specify a complete current interface \((K_I,u_I)\).
Write \((K_0,u_0)=(K_\varnothing,u_\varnothing)\).
It includes all memory, resources and public context determining later laws.
Every eligible occurrence \(e\) has one fixed action/report branch
\(R_{I,e}:K_I\to K_{I+e}\), common to every candidate original source.
All maps are total; impossible reports have zero output. Partial menus require
an explicit failure outcome or declared restriction. For simultaneously
eligible \(e,f\), demand occurrence-aligned report squares

\[
R_{I+e,f}R_{I,e}=R_{I+f,e}R_{I,f}.
\]

For legal schedule interpretation, enabledness must persist along every
positive-probability transition of the other occurrence. Incomparability
alone supplies neither condition. Policies depending on ordering or hidden
source must satisfy these same obligations, not select different branches.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**X07-T — PROVED LOCAL derivation; independent acceptance pending.**
Assume the selected-branch data define a common, total positive ideal functor, as constructed from the typed squares and legal enabling obligations above. Suppose the face poset of the original cone \(K_0\) has finite height and
\(C\subseteq\{x\in K_0:u_0(x)=1\}\) is any fixed nonempty class. No convexity
or closure of \(C\) is assumed. Set \(T_I=R_{\varnothing,I}\),
\(\ell_I=u_IT_I\), \(Z_I=K_0\cap\ker\ell_I\). There exists a finite ideal
\(I_*\) such that

\[
Z_J\subseteq Z_{I_*}\quad(\forall J),\qquad
\bigcap_J\{x\in C:\ell_J(x)>0\}=C\setminus Z_{I_*}.
\]

Consequently \((\forall I\,\exists x_I\in C:\ell_I(x_I)>0)\) implies
\(\exists x\in C\,\forall I:\ell_I(x)>0\). Every extension of \(I_*\) has the
same zero face. No bound on its cardinality or discovery time follows.

## 5. Proof attempt

Two linear extensions of a finite ideal connect by adjacent swaps of
incomparable occurrences: move the desired first occurrence left, then
induct. The displayed, correctly typed squares preserve each swap's map.
Thus \(R_{I,J}\) is presentation-independent; concatenation proves
\(R_{J,L}R_{I,J}=R_{I,L}\) and \(T_J=R_{I,J}T_I\).

Each \(\ell_I\) is nonnegative, so its zero set is a face: if \(x+y\in Z_I\)
with \(x,y\in K_0\), both nonnegative summands \(\ell_I(x),\ell_I(y)\) vanish.
For \(I\subseteq J\), faithfulness gives
\(\ell_I(x)=0\Rightarrow T_Ix=0\Rightarrow T_Jx=0\); hence \(Z_I\subseteq Z_J\).
These faces are inclusion-monotone, **not necessarily a chain**.

Finite height supplies a maximal attained \(Z_{I_*}\). For any \(J\), take
\(U=I_*\cup J\). Then \(Z_{I_*}\subseteq Z_U\), so maximality gives equality;
also \(Z_J\subseteq Z_U\). A witness outside \(Z_{I_*}\) therefore lies outside
every \(Z_J\), proving the exact intersection formula and conclusion.

Mass contraction separately gives \(\ell_J\le\ell_I\); it is needed for the
probability interpretation, not the zero-absorption proof. It can alternatively
establish zero nesting without faithfulness. Neither CP nor tensor copying
appears in this proof.

**REFUTED:** individual threads suffice. On \(K_0=\mathbb R_+^2\),
\(C=\{e_0,e_1\}\), two incomparable, always enabled branches \(P_0,P_1\)
commute. Each one-event thread has a source; their union has zero map.

**REFUTED:** fresh terminal experiments are one ideal diagram. Alternating

**REFUTED:** total channels suffice. Z/X Lüders totals commute, but their

**REFUTED:** marginal/endpoint transport suffices. Full-support binary joint

\(3/4,1/4\). **REFUTED:** rank plateau certifies finality; 23 identity steps

**REFUTED:** omitted original memory preserves finite height. Sources with
the same reduced qubit and memory label \(k\in\mathbb N\), followed by tail

is not a function of the reduced qubit. The correct original cone has
unbounded height. Source-selected identity-until-\(k\), then-zero branches
have the same defect of missing a common map. Compactness does not repair
arbitrary classes, as C19's reviewed compact pure countermodel shows.

For the orthant, \(Z_\ell\) consists of coordinates where \(\ell_j=0\);
strict inclusions add coordinates. For PSD states,
\(\ell(\rho)=\operatorname{tr}(E\rho)=0\) iff
\(\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\ker E\), by
\(\|E^{1/2}\rho^{1/2}\|_{HS}^2=0\). Thus strict zero-face growth increases
kernel dimension, giving at most \(d\) strict inclusions (\(d+1\) nonempty faces when both endpoints are counted). All effects live on the
**original** space even when current output dimensions grow.

The diagonal map \(J(p)=\operatorname{diag}(p)\) intertwines a nonnegative
matrix \(B\) with
\(\Phi_B(X)=\sum_{y,x}B_{yx}X_{xx}|y\rangle\langle y|\):
\(\Phi_BJ=JB\), matching branch masses \(\operatorname{tr}(\Phi_BJp)=\sum Bp\) and the corresponding zero faces on diagonal sources. B itself preserves total mass only when its column sums equal one, and contracts mass when they are at most one.
On commutative diagonal algebras this is an identity-preserving functor;
on full matrix algebras \(\Phi_{\mathrm{id}}\) dephases, so claiming that
full-matrix embedding as a functor would fail.

This bridge grants no quantum copying. \(\rho\mapsto\rho\otimes\rho\)
is nonlinear: copying the average of \(P_0,P_1\) gives diagonal
\((1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4)\), while averaging their copies gives
\((1/2,0,0,1/2)\). Basis copying also loses the coherent \(P_+\) marginal.
Original quantum states are semantic inputs, not physically stored duplicate
states. Original-target decoding remains a separate constancy condition on
compatible sources.
