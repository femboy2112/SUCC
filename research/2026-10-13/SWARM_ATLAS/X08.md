## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Fix a Gaussian prime `π ∈ Z[i]`. It is **not necessarily a rational prime**:
the principal probe uses `π=1+i`, and a fresh-place control uses inert `π=3`.
Let `F=Q(i)_π` be the completion, `O` its valuation ring, and normalize
`v=v_π` by `v(π)=1`, with `v(0)=∞`. The exact embedding `Q(i)→F` does
not identify local completion with finite elapsed computation. Put

\[
B_r(z)=z+\pi^r O,\quad r\in\mathbb Z,\qquad
\nu_L(w)=\min\{v(w),L\},\quad L\in\mathbb Z_{\ge1}.
\]

Negative depths and negative valuations are allowed and denote larger
fractional balls. They are not silently passed to a finite integral-residue
API. Infinity is capped to `L`, including at zero.

The common ambient structure is the affine group

\[
G=Q(i)^\times\ltimes Q(i),\quad
(a,b)(c,d)=(ac,ad+b),\quad
(a,b)^{-1}=(a^{-1},-a^{-1}b).
\]

Products act rightmost first. Write `g z=az+b` and `λ(g)=v(a)`. Let
`P⊂Z[i]` contain all genuine Gaussian primes and their associates. The
restricted action groupoid `Γ_P` has arrows `(g,z):z→gz` precisely when
`z,gz∈P`. Its identities are `(1,z)`, inverse is `(g⁻¹,gz)`, and composition
is `(h,gz)∘(g,z)=(hg,z)`. This is the full endpoint restriction, not a claim
that primes are invariant under every affine map.

For integer squared-step bound `K≥0`, form a labelled source graph with dart

\[
e=(g,z):z\longrightarrow gz,
\quad z,gz\in P,\quad 0<N(gz-z)\le K.
\]

Its reverse dart is `(g⁻¹,gz)`. Every endpoint is retained as an actual
Gaussian integer with its arithmetic classification. For a fixed label,
the partial generator domain is exactly
`D_g^K={z∈P:gz∈P, 0<N(gz−z)≤K}`. Step-bounded domains themselves are
not a global action: composing two short darts need not give a short dart.

An **observation itinerary** is an unreduced finite composable dart sequence
`η=(g₁,…,g_m;z)` with every intermediate prime and step checked. Its prefixes
are `G₀=1`, `G_j=g_j⋯g₁=(A_j,B_j)`, including the initial report at `j=0`.
Its exact domain retains every prefix requirement, not just the final prime.
Itineraries form a category under concatenation. The free source **path
groupoid** `Π_K` additionally cancels adjacent reverse darts; identities are
empty paths, inverses reverse the dart order and labels. The distinction is
load-bearing: an observation itinerary can contain reports that disappear
after groupoid reduction.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**X08.1 — CLASSICAL ambient maps, recovered locally.** The functor
`Π_K→Γ_P`, `[η]↦(G_m,z)`, preserves identities, inverse and composition.
There is a depth-lifted groupoid with objects `(z,r)∈P×Z` and arrows
`([η],r):(z,r)→(G_m z,r+λ(G_m))`. Its functor to the affine action groupoid
on actual local-field balls sends `(z,r)↦B_r(z)` and its arrow to
`(G_m,B_r(z))`. Both functors preserve the full affine label, including `B_m`.
Forgetting the source tag is permitted as a map but does not create new
prime paths.

**X08.2 — PROVED LOCAL, sharp whole-itinerary precision.** For every Gaussian
prime `π`, every `z∈Q(i)`, every finite affine word with all `a_j≠0`, every
integer cap `L≥1`, and every integer depth `r`, define

\[
s_L(y)=
\begin{cases}
v(y)+1,&v(y)<L,\\
L,&v(y)\ge L,
\end{cases}
\qquad
R(\eta,z,L)=\max_{0\le j\le m}
\bigl[s_L(G_jz)-v(A_j)\bigr].
\tag{1}
\]

Here `s_L(0)=L`; equivalently it is `min(v(y)+1,L)` with infinity handled
by the stated convention. Then

\[
\forall x\in B_r(z)\ \forall j\in\{0,\ldots,m\}:
\nu_L(G_jx)=\nu_L(G_jz)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad r\ge R(\eta,z,L).
\tag{2}
\]

The universal quantifier in (2) is over the **ambient local field**. At a
certified prime itinerary it supplies a sufficient valuation-report bound
on source-realizable alternatives. Its necessity is not promoted to the
sparse source: a hostile ambient point need not be a Gaussian prime or
belong to every generator domain.

**X08.3 — PROVED LOCAL, transported max-plus composition.** For matching
itineraries `β` followed by `γ`, with total affine label `G_β=(A_β,B_β)`
and a common cap `L`,

\[
R(\gamma\beta,z,L)=\max\{
R(\beta,z,L),\ R(\gamma,G_\beta z,L)-v(A_\beta)\}.
\tag{3}
\]

The shared boundary report is included by both sides harmlessly. Equation
(3) is associative: demands accumulate by maximum while input precision
transports by slope valuation. `R` is an invariant of a retained ordered
itinerary and its report specification; it is **not** an invariant of the
reduced groupoid arrow. Together (1)–(3) give an exact, composable report
criterion unavailable from affine normal form, valuation multiplication,
clock coherence or prime adjacency considered separately.

## 5. Proof attempt

**Maps and coherence.** Since `a≠0`, `aO=π^{v(a)}O`. Hence

\[
gB_r(z)=az+b+\pi^{r+v(a)}O=B_{r+\lambda(g)}(gz).
\]

The affine product satisfies `λ(hg)=λ(h)+λ(g)`; inverse negates `λ`.
Endpoint matching supplies the source groupoid composition. Cancelling a
dart with its reverse replaces the affine product by the identity and the
depth change by zero. Thus the action and ball functors descend to `Π_K`
and satisfy the actual composition squares and inverse laws. Distinct
source paths can share one affine label; the functor is not asserted
faithful on histories.

**Single-ball discrimination lemma.** Fix `y∈F` and integer radius depth `s`.
If `t=v(y)<L`, then `ν_L` is constant on `B_s(y)` iff `s≥t+1`.
For sufficiency use the strict ultrametric inequality: perturbations of
valuation greater than `t` cannot change `t`. If `s≤t`, then `0∈B_s(y)`
has capped valuation `L≠t`. If `t≥L`, including `y=0`, constancy holds
iff `s≥L`: every member then has valuation at least `L`; if `s<L`, the
point `π^s` lies in the ball and has smaller capped valuation. This proves
the exact threshold `s_L(y)` without numerical precision.

Apply this lemma to the exact ball image
`G_jB_r(z)=B_{r+v(A_j)}(G_jz)`. Each prefix condition is exactly
`r≥s_L(G_jz)−v(A_j)`; their conjunction is their maximum, proving (2).
For sharpness choose a maximizing prefix at `r=R−1` and the lemma's hostile
target `y'=0` or `y'=π^{r+v(A_j)}`. Pull it back by
`x=(y'−B_j)/A_j`. This is in `B_{R−1}(z)` and changes that report. For
`z,a_j,b_j∈Q(i)` the witness is in `Q(i)`, so the probe does not need an
infinite p-adic digit sequence to realize ambient sharpness.

For (3), a prefix of `γβ` is either a prefix of `β` or `H_kG_β` for a
prefix `H_k=(C_k,D_k)` of `γ`. In the second case its threshold is

\[
s_L(H_kG_\beta z)-v(C_k A_\beta)
=[s_L(H_kG_\beta z)-v(C_k)]-v(A_\beta).
\]

Taking both maxima proves (3). Applying it to three matching itineraries
gives the same maximum of three transported demands in either grouping.
The offset recurrence `B_j=a_j B_{j−1}+b_j` enters each target valuation;
omitting its cross-term changes the criterion. This is an exact finite-word
proof for all stated words, not an inference from the bounded search.
