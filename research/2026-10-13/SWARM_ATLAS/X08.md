## 4. Candidate theorem

For every m≥1, every report r and every family P of integer dilations:

1. An equivalence on C_m is S-stable iff it is congruence modulo a unique
   divisor d|m. Thus every S-stable equivalence is also stable under every
   affine action x↦ax+b on C_m.
2. If d is the least positive cyclic period of r, then d|m and
   I_S(R)=ker q_d. Moreover
   I_{S∪P}(R)=I_S(R), and both I_S I_P(R) and I_P I_S(R) equal I_S(R).
3. The induced maps D_{a,d} and S_d commute iff d divides a−1. Adding D_2
   therefore adds no distinctions after successor repair but remains
   noncommuting on every nontrivial repaired clock. This distinguishes
   information content from the actual action algebra.
4. Every integer dilation preserves the truncated p-adic valuation relation
   on C_{p^k}, but its successor repair is discrete: all p^k original states
   become distinct. The original k+1 valuation reports cannot support a
   lawful successor quotient except when they were already discrete.

There is also an infinite-source closure statement: every reflexive,
transitive successor-stable relation on N_0 is stable under all natural
affine maps n↦an+b. Equivalence is a sufficient stronger hypothesis. Three
lemmas for this statement are **LEAN-KERNEL PROVED**; the finite cyclic
classification and the repair identities are paper proofs.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete derivation

Suppose E is S-stable on C_m. Powers of S include every translation and
S^{-1}=S^{m−1}, so x E y iff 0 E (y−x). Let H={h:0 E h}. Translation,
symmetry and transitivity show H is a subgroup: if h,k∈H, translating
0 E k by −k and using symmetry gives 0 E −k; translating 0 E h by −k
gives −k E h−k, and transitivity gives 0 E h−k. Conversely the coset
relation of any subgroup is translation
stable. A subgroup of Z/mZ is dZ/mZ for a unique d|m (lift it to a subgroup
of Z containing mZ and take its least positive generator). Therefore E is
exactly equality modulo d. Multiplying or translating congruent residues
preserves that equality, proving all affine squares descend.

Define H_r={h:r(x+h)=r(x) for every x∈C_m}. This is a subgroup by composing
and inverting translations. The all-S-word relation identifies x,y exactly
when y−x∈H_r, so its cosets are the kernel of q_d for the least positive
period d. Let J=I_S(R). By the first paragraph J is P-stable. Hence J⊆I_P(R),
and monotonicity/idempotence of I_S gives
J=I_S(J)⊆I_S I_P(R)⊆I_S(R)=J. Also I_P(J)=J. The joint repair is J because
J is jointly stable and every jointly stable relation contained in R lies
inside J. This is an explicit source class satisfying A06's absorption
criterion; A06's generic four-state counterexample remains valid outside it.
For any other lawful surjective report-preserving observer q:C_m→Q whose
kernel lies in R, its stable kernel lies in J, so the unique map
Q→C_d sends q(x) to q_d(x). This states the direction of the universal
factorization explicitly; an arbitrary coarse report is not a source map.

For commutation, D_a S(x)=ax+a whereas S D_a(x)=ax+1. Their difference is
the constant a−1, so equality of these maps on C_d is precisely d|(a−1).
For truncated valuation, multiplication sends a report j to
min(j+v_p(a),k); slope zero sends everything to k. Reduction modulo p^k
preserves this truncated valuation. The report fiber at k is {0}. Any period
h preserving the entire report must therefore send 0 back to 0, forcing
h=0 in C_{p^k}; the least positive period is p^k. Thus the repaired source
is fully separated. This is a universal proof, not extrapolation from tests.

On N_0, successor stability iterated b times gives stability under x↦x+b.
If x R y and R is reflexive and transitive, induction on a gives ax R ay:
combine ax+x R ay+x with ay+x R ay+y, using translated copies of the
induction hypothesis and of x R y. One final translation gives ax+b R ay+b.
This proof needs neither finiteness nor symmetry. The finite divisor
classification, unlike affine closure, uses cyclicity.

Three falsifiers are retained:

- The smallest prime-power valuation failure is C_3: reports (1,0,0) identify
  original states 1 and 2, but S sends them to 2 and 0, whose reports are 0
  and 1. C_2 has a discrete valuation report, so cannot fail. Within the
  p=2 family the smallest failure is C_4, at states 1 and 3.
- The smallest cyclic carrier allowing a nonlinear action to invalidate
  successor absorption is C_4. Parity R=(0,1,0,1) is S-stable, while
  T=(0,0,1,0) sends the same-report pair 0,2 to different parities. Joint
  repair is discrete. C_1,C_2,C_3 have only universal/discrete S-stable

## 8. Remaining obstruction and next falsifier

This theorem assumes a complete mathematical source and a genuinely
available full successor action. A repaired partition specifies necessary
state information; it does not construct a new physical sensor or make an
unknown modulus observable. Full reports over m successor positions suffice
mathematically, but no optimal physical cost is claimed. The next useful
falsifier is a partial-successor carrier with unavailable transitions: find
the exact replacement for subgroup periods when the full translation orbit
is absent. A separate next theorem could kernel-formalize the finite cyclic
classification and derive the quotient squares from it.
