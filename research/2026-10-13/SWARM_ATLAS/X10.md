## 3. Proposed SUCC objects and common ambient

Let S be a nonempty **complete finite** source, |S|=n, with total stationary
actions T_a:S→S. Let q:S→O=q(S) be the original report, with b=|O|. The ambient
algebra is F^S with pointwise multiplication, for F=ℚ or ℝ. Each action induces
a unital algebra endomorphism U_a(f)=f∘T_a. Let A_0=q*(F^O), and recursively

`A_(k+1) = alg(A_k ∪ ⋃_a U_a(A_k))`.

Let T_w mean the transformation obtained by actually executing word w in its
stated order. Put `R_k = {(s,t): q(T_w s)=q(T_w t) for every |w|≤k}` and
`I = ⋂_k R_k`. Write Q=S/I and p:S→Q for the quotient. This I is precisely
A06's greatest stable equivalence contained in ker(q). Refining reports means
reducing their kernel relations; I gives the **coarsest lawful refinement**.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**Algebra–repair duality.** For every k≥0,

`A_k = {f:S→F : f is constant on each R_k class}`.

The least unital algebra A_* containing A_0 and invariant under every U_a is
exactly `p*(F^Q)`. Its dimension is |Q|. The sequence stabilizes by k=n−b; at
stabilization the induced maps `G_a([s])=[T_a s]` obey `pT_a=G_ap` and
`U_a p*=p*U_(G_a)`.

**Universal augmentation.** Suppose a surjection r:S→Y augments q, so q=βr,
and is autonomous under every action, so rT_a=H_ar. Then there is one and only
one π:Y→Q satisfying p=πr. In observable terms, `A_*⊆r*(F^Y)`. The quotient
p is therefore minimal among autonomous refinements in information content.
This quantifies over the complete declared action family, not just actions
observed so far.

**Finite hierarchy obstruction.** For polynomial sources with no degree bound,
translation `T_t f(x)=f(x+t)` is an exact, total source evolution. No finite jet
report `J_m(f)=(f(0),f'(0),…,f^(m)(0))` supports an autonomous derivative law or
even a deterministic unit-translation update, for any m≥0. If the source is
restricted to polynomials of degree at most d, J_d is a closed exact report,
with derivative `(x_1,…,x_d,0)`; every J_m with m<d still fails.

## 5. Proof attempt — complete derivations

Every generator of A_k is a function of the reports along words of length at
most k, hence is R_k-constant. Conversely, fix an R_k class C. For every state
t outside C, choose s∈C and a word w_t of length at most k whose report separates
s from t. The function

`1_C(x) = ∏_(t∉C) 1_{q(T_(w_t) x)=q(T_(w_t) s)}`

is an exact equality: every point of C has all the same word reports as s,
and each outside t is excluded by its own factor. An empty product is one.
The factors lie in the generated algebra by induction, so every class
indicator lies there. Every R_k-constant function is a linear combination of
these indicators. The recursion's pullbacks add exactly the required action
prefixes, and multiplication forms the simultaneous tests. This proves the
finite-level identity without assuming a linear span is already an algebra.

A strict algebra inclusion increases dimension by at least one. A_0 has
dimension b and F^S has dimension n, so there are at most n−b strict rounds.
If A_k=A_(k+1), it is invariant under every action and no later round changes.
Its equality kernel is stable; it equals I because it contains every generated
report and every generated report belongs to it. Quotient updates are then
well defined. This proves the displayed intertwiner on actual functions.

For universality, equal r-values remain equal after every word by the assumed
commuting updates, and therefore have equal q-word reports. Thus ker(r)⊆I.
Define π(r(s))=[s]. Kernel containment proves this is well defined; surjectivity
makes it total and unique. Equivalently each p-observable is an r-observable.

For the hierarchy obstruction compare f=0 and g(x)=x^(m+1). They have identical
J_m=0. The final component of the jet derivative is f^(m+1)(0), which is zero
for f but (m+1)! for g. Therefore no H_m on reported jet values can agree with
both source derivatives. Moreover the first component after unit translation
is f(1)=0 versus g(1)=1, ruling out a discrete update too. For degree ≤d, ordinary
differentiation gives the stated nilpotent shift matrix and preserves the
source class. Taylor's finite identity gives its exact flow. For m<d the same
g belongs to the restricted source and is still a counterexample.
