## 5. Proof attempt

Q is compact as the continuous image of compact X, and π is a quotient map.
Its coordinate functions separate Q. Their complex unital *-algebra is uniformly
dense in C(Q) by Stone–Weierstrass. Pullback π* is isometric, giving A=π*C(Q).
Composition shifts its generators, so UA⊆A. Any invariant closed unital algebra
containing q contains every Uⁿq and hence A. A fiber-constant continuous τ
descends continuously through the compact-to-Hausdorff quotient π; the converse
is immediate. Equality A=C(X) is equivalent to separation of all distinct points.

Continuous functions are dense in L²(Q,π_*μ), and π* is a Hilbert isometry;
thus their closed range is precisely H. Continuity of U gives UH⊆H and UW⊆W.
If f⊥W, then `⟨w,Lf⟩=⟨Uw,f⟩=0`, proving L-invariance; the same proof applies
to H. Moreover `C Lⁿf=(⟨1,f⟩,⟨Uⁿq,f⟩)`, so vanishing for all n is exactly
f⊥W. For |z|>1 the norm-convergent Neumann series gives
`R_z=(zI−L)⁻¹`, `||R_z||≤1/(|z|−1)`, and preserves the closed L-invariant
N_linear. Hence f∈N_linear implies all delayed responses vanish. Conversely,
if they all vanish, r=R_z f lies in N_linear, and
`f=(zI−L)r∈N_linear`. No finite truncation is used in this proof.

For the strict gap, take X={−1,+1}², uniform μ,
`T(a,b)=(b,a)`, `q(a,b)=a`. Two actual delays `(q,Uq)=(a,b)` recover every
point. But W=span{1,a,b}, whereas H contains ab and equals all four-dimensional
functions. The mode f=ab has norm one, is orthogonal to W, and Lf=f.
Therefore `R_z f=f/(z−1)` and all scalar delayed responses vanish, while
`⟨q·Uq,f⟩=1`. Products also give the actual point indicators
`(1±a)(1±b)/4`, proving full algebraic reconstruction.
Indeed the positive stationary densities `1±ab/2` have identical one-time
binary sensor marginals but joint-delay product means ±1/2. Such a joint
statistic, including a sensor autocorrelation, detects the mode; the no-go
concerns the specified **linear mean/resolvent channel C**, not every possible

Minimality for a **binary** sensor: any three distinct binary future profiles
