## 3. Proposed SUCC objects

Fix a finite nonempty complete declared interface set
\(Z\subseteq\Sigma\times X\times M\). A vertex \(z=(\sigma,x,m)\) retains the
**original source**, **current carrier**, and sufficient **resource/history
memory** for stationary future laws. Public history is a separate ordered
record; vertices are not full private paths. Every action is a deterministic
partial map \(U_a:D_a\to Z\), preserving \(\sigma\). A reset, budget change,
or intervention exists only if explicitly included in these tables.

Fix report \(q:Z\to O\), current target \(F:Z\to V\) in a metric space,
tolerance \(\epsilon\ge0\), and allowed decoder values \(D\subseteq V\)
containing \(F(Z)\). Original target \(T:\Sigma\to L\) is separate from \(F\).
An original predicate is not silently replaced by \(F(U_wz)\).

A nonempty subset \(C\subseteq Z\) is immediately feasible when:

1. \(q\) is constant on \(C\);
2. for each action, either \(C\subseteq D_a\) or \(C\cap D_a=\varnothing\);
3. some \(d\in D\) satisfies \(\max_{z\in C}\operatorname{dist}(F(z),d)\le\epsilon\).

These subsets form a hereditary family \(K_0\), or an abstract simplicial
complex after adjoining the empty face. Exact homogeneous legality is a
contract requirement, **not a free observation of offered menus**. A safe
planner can retain mixed-legality beliefs and choose fewer actions; that
larger policy problem is outside this universal-language reconstruction.

## 4. Candidate theorem

**X12-T1, CLASSICAL construction / PROVED LOCAL specialization.** Define

\[
K_{n+1}=\{C\in K_n:\ \forall a,
C\subseteq D_a\Rightarrow U_a[C]\in K_n\}.
\]

For every stated system, the limit \(K_\infty\) is the unique greatest hereditary
subfamily of \(K_0\) stable under every enabled exact subset image. It stabilizes
after at most \(|K_0|-|Z|\) strict rounds. Every rejected subset has a finite
action-word witness ending in an immediately infeasible subset; every
intermediate action is enabled throughout its then-current subset. No infinite
experiment is performed.

Equivalently, \(C\in K_\infty\) iff \(U_w[C]\in K_0\) for **every** finite word
whose successive actions are enabled throughout the successive subset images,
including the empty word. This is a complete future-feasibility invariant under
the stationary declared grammar, rather than a fitted finite-horizon radius.

The face poset has exact partial action maps \(W_a(C)=U_a[C]\), with
\(W_vW_w(C)=U_{wv}[C]\) whenever the word is lawful. Original support
\(I(C)=\operatorname{pr}_\Sigma[C]\) satisfies \(I(W_aC)=I(C)\).
Thus constancy of an original target on \(I(C)\) survives such an update.
This does not infer constancy from a small current-target error.

**X12-T2, PROVED LOCAL.** Let \(f:Z\to Z'\) satisfy reflected enabled domains
\(D_a=f^{-1}D'_a\), \(fU_a=U'_af\) on \(D_a\), and \(q=q'f,F=F'f\), with
the same decoder space, metric and tolerance. Then

\[
C\in K_\infty\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f[C]\in K'_\infty.
\]

Hence feasible-complex repair commutes exactly with source-map pullback.
Direct-image face maps commute with all enabled actions and with composition.
Injectivity and surjectivity are unnecessary. If original projection commutes
through \(\tau:\Sigma\to\Sigma'\), then \(I'(f[C])=\tau[I(C)]\).

## 5. Proof attempt: finished finite proof

Immediate feasibility is hereditary: restrictions preserve a common report,
homogeneous enabledness, and any witnessing decoder. Every singleton is feasible
because its target value belongs to \(D\). Suppose \(K_n\) is hereditary and
\(B\subseteq C\in K_{n+1}\). Homogeneous enabledness on \(C\) makes every action
enabled on \(B\) also enabled on \(C\). Then \(U_a[B]\subseteq U_a[C]\in K_n\),
so heredity gives \(B\in K_{n+1}\).

The finite sequence decreases. Singletons cannot be removed: their enabled
images are singletons. Every strict round removes at least one other face,
proving the bound. At a plateau, all exact images remain inside. Any stable
family \(J\subseteq K_0\) lies in every \(K_n\) by induction, proving greatestness
and uniqueness. Following a removal cause backwards yields a finite bad word;
its length is bounded by its removal round. A subset outside \(K_0\) already
has the empty-word witness. Conversely a stable family cannot contain the
start of a lawful word ending outside \(K_0\).

This proof supplies an action-stable feasible **complex**, not an equivalence
relation. Its unique universal inclusion is a property of families; it chooses
neither a partition nor a decoder center. Exact image preserves subset inclusion,
and function composition proves word coherence. Original-support equality follows
because each original tag has a representative, every action is defined on
the whole face, and the tag is unchanged in its image.

For T2, immediate feasibility of \(C\) equals that of \(f[C]\): the reports,
target values and enabled signatures coincide. Also
\(f[U_a[C]]=U'_a[f[C]]\). Induction proves equivalence at every finite stage;
intersecting stages gives the claim. Source-map identities and compositions
act as literal direct-image identities and compositions. Target transport
alone, without the action and domain squares, proves none of this.

## 6. Crucible

The standalone exact probe compares deletion with independent breadth-first
search for a reachable bad subset. It checked **11,440 models**, **76,488
face memberships**, and **456 lawful two-to-three-state source maps**. Scalar
center enumeration independently checks F46's half-diameter formula. Seeded
two-action four-state cases rotate the action decomposition. All are declared
tables sharing this author and implementation environment, not external
source or independent reasoner evidence.

* **No partition repaired by naming a cover.** For \(F=(0,1,2)\), constant
  \(q\), \(\epsilon=1/2\), and identity actions, the facets are \(01,12\).
  Their union is infeasible. The canonical family retains both; the original
  incomparable-partition obstruction remains.
* **Facets invent histories.** Vertices are
  \(z_0=(s_0,0),z_1=(s_0,1),z_2=(s_1,1)\); omitted memory is constant.
  With the preceding targets and \(U=(1,1,2)\), both facets are stable.
  Exact \(U[01]=\{1\}\) has two maximal containers. Choosing \(01\) invents
  current endpoint \(z_0\); choosing \(12\) invents original \(s_1\).
  Tolerance survives both choices, while lawful endpoint reconstruction fails.
* **Legality guard is load-bearing.** On four states, targets \((0,0,0,2)\),
  zero tolerance, and \(U=(0,3,\mathrm{undefined},3)\), an unguarded rule keeps
  \(012\), since no action is universally safe there, but deletes subface
  \(01\), whose enabled image \(03\) is infeasible. The proposed family would
  not be hereditary. Homogeneous legality excludes this false construction.
* **Changed laws require reconstruction.** Identity dynamics retain \(01\)
  above; \(U=(0,2,2)\) removes it after one action. Equal current target and
  report tables therefore do not transport the old certificate.
* **Deflection is different.** Under the latter unchanged \(U\), intervention
  \(\delta=(2,1,2)\) moves the orbit \(0,0,\ldots\) to \(2,2,\ldots\).
  It does not certify the failed subset \(01\) for \(U\).
* **Higher-order geometry.** Rational points \((0,0),(2,0),(1,2)\), tolerance
  \(6/5\), have feasible pairs but no feasible triple. Weighted squared radius
  has lower bound \(25/16>36/25\) about \((1,3/4)\). A pair graph misses this
  nonface; the full feasible-subset construction retains it correctly.
* **Original/current erasure.** Erasing a correlated current bit makes current
  value zero exactly recoverable while originals \(0,1\) remain possible.
  Current zero error cannot certify their different original targets.

## 8. Remaining obstruction

The complex may contain exponentially many faces and offers no minimum-memory
observer theorem. Computing geometric feasibility additionally needs a correct
oracle or finite declared decoder class; finiteness of \(Z\) alone supplies no
effective arbitrary-metric oracle. Added source actions or changed source laws
can shrink the complex, so certificates must retain the table digest.

Nondeterministic public outcomes require branch-indexed exact subset updates.
Complete private histories cannot be recovered from endpoints; F48's incidence
interfaces and tokens remain necessary for path gluing. Resources are explicit
memory coordinates, not a measured cost theorem or F45 fairness certificate.
An actual observed belief outside \(K_\infty\) cannot be repaired by deleting
uncomfortable alternatives. A justified new report may refine it; a stipulated
counterfactual feature is not an acquired event.
