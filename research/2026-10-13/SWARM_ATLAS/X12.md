# SWM-X12 — Changing representations without changing original evidence

## 1. Original question and exact hole

F48 fixes one carrier quotient for every history. Can an observer revise its representation after a lawful report, compose several such revisions, and preserve what the report establishes about the original preparation? The proposed bridge is **history-indexed representation transport with cross-stage commuting squares**. Initial status was OPEN; final status is **PROVED LOCAL, written proof**. No claim about human cognition or psychology is made.

The weakest sufficient condition is local: impose the squares only on current states reachable at the relevant lawful history, retain the original index, and keep the representation schedule a function of recorded history. This closes `fixed quotient → adaptive representation → original-target/policy preservation`. It does not authenticate the original source contract.

## 3. Common objects, maps, and hypotheses

Fix original indices O, preparation i:O→C, and deterministic instruments a with enabling e_a, report r_a and update U_a. No undeclared reset or observation is available. For a finite typed event history h, let P(h)⊆O be the originals that lawfully replay h, let c_h:P(h)→C be their current-state map, and let R_h=c_h(P(h)). Empty support means an inconsistent record, not a certified target value.

For every feasible h, choose a surjection q_h:R_h→D_h. The family and all model-update rules are fixed functions of the recorded history, not functions of the unknown actual original. Require:

1. Enabling is constant on each q_h-fiber. On enabled fibers, reports are constant too. Thus ē_h,a and r̄_h,a are well-defined on D_h.
2. For every feasible extension k=h(a,y), the function q_k U_a on R_h,a,y factors through q_h, where R_h,a,y={c∈R_h:e_a(c)=1,r_a(c)=y}. Equivalently, for c,d in that set,
   q_h(c)=q_h(d) implies q_k(U_a c)=q_k(U_a d).
3. For cost preservation, a nonnegative per-action cost κ_h,a on enabled R_h also factors through q_h. This is an additional premise.

Hypothesis 2 defines a unique map V_h,a,y:q_h(R_h,a,y)→D_k satisfying

`V_h,a,y(q_h(c)) = q_k(U_a(c))`.

Uniqueness follows because q_h is onto its displayed event domain; V is onto D_k because R_k=U_a(R_h,a,y). This is a transition between different representations, not an inverse to an erased physical transition. The quotient starts with d_empty(o)=q_empty(i(o)); it retains the same O and filters the same event labels.

## 4. Candidate theorem and quantified claims

**History-indexed transport theorem.** For every source contract and every family satisfying §3, and for every finite typed history h, compressed replay has exactly original support P(h), and every surviving original has compressed current state d_h(o)=q_h(c_h(o)). Consequently its original-support presheaf is identical to the original one, including inclusion restrictions along prefixes; the induced categories of elements are isomorphic over the prefix category.

For every fixed target T:O→V, target constancy on nonempty support is preserved. Every policy whose decisions depend only on recorded history has exactly the same robust availability, lawful branches, and correctness for T. If hypothesis 3 holds, the cost of every corresponding finite execution agrees. This is conditional preservation of a supplied policy, not existence of an identifying policy.

**Composition theorem.** If p_h:D_h→E_h is a second surjective history-indexed transport satisfying analogous enabling/report/transition/cost conditions, then p_h q_h is a transport from the first source to E. Its replay, original-support isomorphism, and path costs equal sequential application of q then p. Such transports have identity families and associative composition.

## 5. Proof and semantic-revision corollary

At the empty history every original is present and the current-state equation holds by preparation. Assume it holds at h. An original o survives event (a,y) on one side precisely when its current state is enabled and reports y. Hypothesis 1 and the inductive equation make these conditions identical on both sides. For a survivor the next compressed state is

`V_h,a,y(d_h(o)) = V_h,a,y(q_h(c_h(o))) = q_k(U_a(c_h(o))) = q_k(c_k(o))`.

Induction proves the support and state equations for every finite history. Prefix restrictions are inclusions of the identical subsets of O, so they commute identically. Robust action legality quantifies enabling over these same originals; terminal correctness quantifies the same T over the same nonempty support. Policy branch correspondence follows by induction on policy execution, since the same history entails the same decision. Cost equality holds at each enabled step by hypothesis 3 and therefore for each finite sum.

For composition, let W_h,a,y be the second transition. Its commuting square gives, for every lawful c,

`W_h,a,y(p_h(q_h(c))) = p_k(V_h,a,y(q_h(c))) = p_k(q_k(U_a(c)))`.

Thus W itself supplies the transition on the composite representation. Enabling, reports and costs descend by two substitutions. The original-support maps in both steps are identity on O, so sequential and direct maps agree. Pointwise associativity and identity functions prove the coherence laws. Keeping this type distinction explicit prevents an attractive but false “compose the update arrows” diagram.

**Declared semantic revision.** Let m_empty be a model state and m_h(a,y)=Ψ(m_h,a,y) a deterministic update from actual reports; Ψ may, for example, be the declared rational graph update of F46. The enlarged representation `(d_h,m_h)` has reachable carrier D_h×{m_h}; transitions send `(d,m_h)` to `(V_h,a,y(d),m_h(a,y))`. Projection to D_h and insertion of the known m_h are inverse on this reachable carrier. Therefore this semantic revision preserves P(h) and all fixed original-target claims. It adds no source discrimination merely by changing internal coordinates.

If instead a model state depends on unobserved original data m_h(o), it is not a free history function. It must be retained as source data, supplied by a lawful observation, or proved to factor through the retained representation. Likewise changing the question from T to a different T' requires a new constancy check. These are typed restrictions, not psychological assertions.

## 6. Crucible, cheapest falsifiers, and executed receipts

**A local quotient unavailable globally.** Eight originals/current states c=4v+2b₁+b₂ carry visible bit v and two hidden bits. `flip` reports v and flips v; `erase` reports a constant and sets v=0; `one` is available only at v=1 and reports a constant. Hidden bits persist. Initially retain v; after any nonempty lawful history, v is determined by that history, so a one-point current representation is adequate locally. The global one-point quotient is invalid because `flip` reports different bits and `one` has different guards. Original indices remain eight separate possibilities after erase. A `flip=1` report retains originals4–7, certifying the original visible bit while leaving each hidden bit ambiguous.

The independent probe explores **97 lawful nodes through depth4**, **96 edges**, **464 pointwise cross-stage squares**, **291 robust-availability comparisons**, and **97 composition comparisons**. The two composed maps are genuinely noninjective: discard one hidden bit, then discard the other; at later histories also drop the already-known visible bit. Costs depend on current v and descend locally; pointwise descent is checked at every node.

**Smallest missing-square countermodel.** C=O={0,1}, preparation identity, one identity action with a constant report, always enabled. Both q_empty:C→{*} and q_after=id_C are valid static quotients considered separately. No transition { * }→{0,1} can satisfy q_after U=V q_empty for both originals. Hence a sequence of individually lawful static representations need not be a lawful online representation change. This refutes the simpler bridge before any asymptotic argument.

**Target and provenance controls.** On support {0,1}, T=0 is constant but T'(o)=o is not. Preserving support does not carry a certificate from T to T'. Compressing O to one point also loses T' because it does not factor through that compression. Identical reports with unequal availability disallow policy transport; identical reports with costs1 and2 disallow cost transport. A schedule selecting mode0 for original0 and mode1 for original1 before receiving a report would itself reveal the hidden bit: it is not a history-measurable schedule and is excluded.

The F43 audit uses its own coordinate producer, a closed-form pair-equivalence oracle and an independent finite group closure; it reproduces p=2/5, zero-coupling and dihedral distinctions. Details and scope qualifications are in X12_REVIEW.md.

## 7. Verdict and claim states

**PROVED LOCAL:** history-indexed support/current-state transport, associative composition, fixed-target/policy preservation, and the conditional cost and declared-semantic-update corollaries. General proof is written above; finite controls corroborate it. **REFUTED:** arbitrary switching among static quotients preserves online evidence; support preservation certifies a changed target; report equality alone preserves guards or costs. **UNVERIFIED:** Lean-kernel formalization, implementation refinement, academic priority, empirical source authentication, and any human-psychology interpretation.

## 8. Remaining obstruction and next lamp

This theorem preserves already available information. It does not show how to find an economical schedule, when a stochastic state update preserves a joint law, or how to soundly forget history itself. The next discriminating lemma is an exact criterion for compressing h to a finite controller state while retaining both P(h)-relevant target support and future transitions. Countermodels must include histories with the same current endpoint but different original support. Finite enumeration cannot establish that an unbounded source admits such a finite controller.

## 9. Cross-domain payoff and dominance boundary

The common ambient object is the prefix category equipped with original-support fibers, reachable current carriers, and representation maps. F48 contributes source-relative evidence; A07 contributes causal composition and the distinction between reports and endpoints; F46 contributes an explicitly declared semantic update. The new edge is the cross-stage square, which permits representation change while proving exactly what it preserves. It dominates the fixed-quotient treatment for history-local compression, and stops at source/target changes or a forgotten history. It provides no RH-facing positivity or arithmetic inference.
