## IH-03 — a naive spectral gap cannot repair it (PROVED LOCAL)

If a bounded operator on an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space is compact and self-adjoint, it cannot satisfy <v,Kv>≥c||v||² for a fixed c>0 and all v. Otherwise K maps an orthonormal sequence to vectors of norm ≥c and its inverse on the range is bounded, contradicting compactness (equivalently, the eigenvalues of a compact self-adjoint operator tend to 0).
