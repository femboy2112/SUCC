# Spectral-response authority and source-application contract

The new `spectral_tail_contract` adapter records an **open** analytic target.
The independent `arithmetic_resolvent` adapter computes actual source matrix
enclosures. They have different authority. Neither adapter proves an analytic
source theorem by loading a metadata hash.

The tail packet contains the original Git blob and SHA256, origin epoch,
radius1/2, active address2, Lebesgue mean-zero carrier, modes1..4 versus5+,
the Legendre metric diag(1/3,1/5,1/7,1/9), and all five signed source channels.
It records mu(E)=B1_E(D)B*, the actual inverse-power domain, optional conditional
fractional parameters, separate spatial/Gamma/derivative/regularization budgets,
evidence files, and the inherited complete proof dependency graph.

The following obligations cannot be erased by this schema:

1. `SOURCE_MODEL_IDENTIFICATION`: mathematical comparison of the executable
   source with the intended signed Suzuki operator and its hypotheses.
2. `SOURCE_SPECTRAL_TAIL_RATE`: an actual source theorem bounding weak inverse
   spectral mass, not a numerical slope or a finite diagonal example.
3. `ZERO_SHIFT_REGULARIZATION_GAP`: a quantitative bound connecting a computed
   positive-shift response to the zero-shift response.

Their names identify proof debt, not three newly independent physical inputs.
The first has reviewed paper and source provenance evidence; no semantic proof
checker in this adapter discharges it. The latter two remain mathematically
unresolved in this round. The existing qualitative square-root range theorem
does establish a finite response and convergence, with no effective rate.

`make_open_tail_packet` may record eta in (0,1/2] and a proposed C² as a
conditional premise. `audit_tail_packet` retains that premise and returns
`source_tail_rate_proved=false`, `semantic_authority_issued=null`. It never
outputs a global sign. `source_lower_matrix` and `source_upper_matrix` are
explicitly unavailable in this **tail** packet; the source calculation's
separately replayable matrix packet supplies its valid upper/lower bounds.
Unsupported transplantation of those matrices into the open-tail schema is
rejected. A conditional Lean implication cannot supply a missing rate.

The verifier recomputes the source/radius/metric/channel map, checks the actual
source file in the evidence root, checks evidence bytes, and delegates all
transitive premise tracking to `source_proof_certificate.audit_capsules`.
Exact finite models, numerical experiments, paper implications and kernel
declarations keep the inherited distinct authority labels. No embedded shell
command is executed. A fresh valid hash for altered prose still proves no
semantic assertion: the corresponding adversarial test accepts structural
integrity while requiring an empty list of theorems proved by the audit.

Hostile tests alter the source radius, inject primitive composite6, omit signed
channels, replace the low metric by identity, swap the O(J^-1) derivative tail
with the O(J^-2) operator tail, and erase debts after recomputing a matching
packet hash. All are rejected. The historical OPEN_TARGET and transitive
premise-laundering regressions remain unchanged and are rerun.

The separate finite rational YEAN extension proves actual operator identities
with explicit solve, symmetry, order and nonzero-denominator hypotheses. A
rational diagonal spectral presentation is supplied data. It is not an
extracted spectrum of the Suzuki source, and no finite declaration is labelled
as a proof of the infinite spectral theorem. Compiler receipts and their axiom
budgets are linked from the final round report after fresh local execution.
