## 2. Common minimal object and assumptions

The deterministic core has a set of source/history states X, a source map s:X→S, a declared report map o:X→O, a request alphabet A, and partial transitions t_a:X⇀C×X with nonnegative additive cost C. States may encode whole histories; no finite sufficient state is assumed. A path is hypothetical until separately journalled as actual. A rejecting request is an observation only when that instrument is explicitly supplied; otherwise an availability mismatch is a structural legality witness.

Designated proof tasks are outputs of an explicitly fixed verifier or report contract. Preserving those outputs does not prove the verifier sound, decide arbitrary truth, or add proof axioms. Source authenticity, truth of a theorem, and acceptance of a supplied proof remain separate predicates.

The linear/analytic adapter additionally declares vector spaces, topology, form domains, source coefficients and morphism laws. A signed quadratic form cannot be replaced with a covariance. Existence of a global report, realization by one source, and effective construction remain distinct.

## 3. Discriminating verdicts

**PASS:** full written proof under explicit hypotheses plus calibrated exact or kernel evidence where applicable. **FAIL:** exact countermodel to a stated inference, preserved with its responsible assumption. **AMBIGUOUS:** bounds straddle the required sign or the probe cannot distinguish the claims. Finite checks never establish an unproved infinite lift. Scholarly priority stays **UNVERIFIED** unless independently established.

Agents use disjoint artifacts and differing mathematical decompositions. Same-model reviewers are not blind independent witnesses; root review, exact alternative implementations, formal kernel checks and primary sources provide different failure modes. No invented independence or runs.
