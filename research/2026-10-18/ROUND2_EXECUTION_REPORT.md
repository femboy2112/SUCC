   a nonzero bounded forward intertwiner between the atomic multiplication

   either direction iff sum_(p in S)1/p converges. In the convergent case a
   unitary exists. In the divergent case every such map is zero, without any
   adjoint-intertwining assumption. The finite-group inverse-residue argument
   proves the required monoid harmonic divergence; Fourier/Bessel bounds kill
   every atomic component. Reverse-direction adjoint kernels have Haar-null
   support. [Complete operator manuscript](ROUND2_OPERATORS.md).

2. **Completion cost and sharp regularity (INS-16–18, paper).** For F subset G,
   normalized avoidance vacua have overlap sqrt(alpha_G/alpha_F). Every choice

   divergent prime exhaustion, despite preserving norms. Positive finite
   GCD Grams do not yield bounded global synthesis: their norms diverge,
   with exact spectral certificates and stronger divisor/tensor lower bounds.
   For every positive finite weight sequence, the Dirac map extends
   ell2(w)->weak Dprime iff sum1/(nw_n)<infinity; its exact TV operator norm
   is the square root of this sum. Power s>0 and logarithmic beta>1 are sharp.
   Source-action ratio bounds and weighted adjoints are separate obligations.
   [Completion manuscript](ROUND2_COMPLETION.md).

3. **Nondeterministic source descent (INS-19–20, Lean kernel).** One fixed public
   policy is belief-safe iff it is source-safe for every compatible hidden

   [Paper](ROUND2_CAUSAL.md), [kernel](../../formal/round2/PublicBelief.lean).

4. **Decisive causal obstruction (INS-21, paper/exact model).** Trace-equivalent
