## 1. What has actually crossed the bridge

There are now two substantive positive routes, with a deliberate distinction:

1. **Arithmetic quantum source reconstruction.** Complete prime-mirror
   relations reconstruct a diagonal bipartite state uniquely up to phase,
   with necessary and sufficient summability conditions. A quantitative
   residual inequality bounds distance from that state; a proved tail estimate
   makes some finite oracle outputs genuine infinite-state fidelity bounds.
2. **Arithmetic code on a geometric carrier.** A specified finite prime-mode
   subspace embeds isometrically into the boundary of a genuine Bruhat–Tits
   ball. A proper boundary region recovers its full noncommutative logical
   algebra through a unitary decoder and a CPTP channel. Arithmetic thermal
   weights have an exactly classified effect on access sets and root symmetry.

The second route supplies an explicit finite correspondence. The lattice
carrier and encoding tensor are independently declared structures. We have
not derived their choice uniquely from the arithmetic state, or constructed
a spacetime satisfying gravitational field equations. This is the dominance
boundary of the construction.

## 4. The three competing constructions

### Candidate 1: independent-prime EPR bonds

The free TFD is a product over paired prime modes, with zero mutual information
between distinct prime pairs. Its coefficient data carry no incidence relation
between different primes. This is a proved limitation of the given data, not
a denial that extra structure could define a graph.

Even a full boundary encoder need not determine its metric network. The
four-output copy isometry |i>↦|iiii> can be implemented by one four-leg star
or by a binary tree of two-output copy isometries. All boundary input/output
operators agree, while opposite boundary distances are 2 and 4 respectively.
The hostile dossier H8 supplies this exact nonuniqueness receipt. Specifying
a BT carrier and its contact metric removes that particular ambiguity by
adding geometric data; it does not derive the geometry from EPR weights.

**Verdict:** state construction survives; unique or connected geometric bulk
from these data alone is REFUTED. A new intrinsic incidence/contact observable
is the missing input.

### Candidate 2: interaction-generated correlations

H_g=H0+g(S+S*) is self-adjoint on D(H0), has compact resolvent, and obeys
log n−2|g|≤λ_n≤log n+2|g|. Its trace threshold remains exactly β>1.
For g≠0 every β>1 Gibbs state is nonproduct under valuation-2/odd-part
factorization. Actual unitary evolution of (e_1+e_2)/√2 creates entanglement
on a proved nonzero time interval. These statements overcome the independent
prime gas's absence of internal correlations.

They do not produce an intrinsic graph metric, tensor decomposition, geometric
field equation, or boundary reconstruction map. Integer-prefix truncation
already creates correlations even at g=0, so finite correlation plots alone
would not establish the stronger infinite theorem. Mixed Gibbs entanglement
is still open; the proved nonproduct theorem is weaker.

**Verdict:** genuine quantum-interaction step survives. The next edge is an
explicit correlation-to-geometric-invariant reconstruction theorem, not a plot
of correlated primes.

### Candidate 3: p-adic boundary reconstruction

There are two lawful interfaces that must not be conflated. The first sends
continuous p-adic boundary functions into diagonal arithmetic observables;
it preserves characters but has trivial free modular dynamics. The attempted
coherent map |n>↦|n mod p^r> is unbounded on ℓ²(N). Its CPTP measurement
replacement destroys quantum coherence and cannot encode the EPR state.

The second restricts explicitly to K_p and encodes the full finite logical
algebra by the arithmetic-weighted qutrit code. For f_r=p^(−βr/2), its
isometry and privileged-pair recovery are exact. For p=2,β=2, weights are
(16,4,1)/21; erasing a different site leaks logical information with exact
trace distance 5/7. Full symmetric single-erasure recovery holds iff weights
are uniform. Positive nonuniform weights also reduce the geometric root
symmetry from S3 to the two-element subgroup fixing the privileged direction.

**Verdict:** a substantive finite arithmetic quantum code on a separately
constructed geometric carrier is PROVED LOCAL. Natural frame-independent
geometry from arithmetic remains open. This is stronger than a network
picture and weaker than an emergent metric or ER bridge.

## 5. Adelic/source gluing: positive example and actual obstruction

Same-source finite residues at coprime prime powers glue uniquely modulo their
product. The compatible diagonal observable algebras glue faithfully into
C(Zhat); at the critical Gibbs boundary their state is Haar. This is a genuine
positive local-to-global result in a declared category.

But the adele with 2-adic coordinate 0 and every odd-prime coordinate 1 is not
the diagonal image of any rational: zero in Q2 forces the rational itself to
be zero. Every finite finite-precision congruence sample still has an integer
CRT realization. Thus successful finite descent and global original-source
realization are different assertions. Moreover no continuous F:Zhat→R can
satisfy F(n)=n for every positive integer, since a compact image is bounded.
An Archimedean place, a diagonal rational source, restricted-product conditions,
and compatible quantum encodings are independent missing structures.
