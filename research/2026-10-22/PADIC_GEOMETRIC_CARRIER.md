## 1. Lattice theorem: the finite ball is not an arbitrary tree

Fix a prime p. Let L0=Z_p^2. A lattice is a free rank-two Z_p-submodule spanning
Q_p^2; identify L and aL for a in Q_p^*. Vertices are these homothety classes.
Two classes are adjacent when representatives satisfy pL ⊊ L' ⊊ L. Both
inclusions are strict. This is the lattice definition of the Bruhat–Tits tree
of PGL_2(Q_p).

**Theorem G1 (canonical finite spheres).** For each integer r>=1, vertices at
distance r from [L0] are canonically in bijection with P^1(Z/p^r Z). Write q=p^r.
Unique disjoint representatives are

```
affine a:   (a,1),   0 <= a < q,
infinity b: (1,pb),  0 <= b < q/p.
```

The corresponding lattices are the inverse images of these cyclic lines under
L0 -> (Z/q)^2. Integral basis columns are respectively

```
((q,0), (a,1)),       ((1,pb), (0,q)).
```

Their parent is reduction of the projective line modulo p^(r-1), with the
depth-one parent equal to [L0]. A root has p+1 children, and every nonroot
vertex has p children and one parent. Consequently the radius-R induced ball
has 1+sum_{r=1}^R (p+1)p^(r-1) vertices; boundary vertices have degree one
inside the truncated ball when R>0. The radius-zero ball has degree zero.

*Proof.* In a primitive vector over Z/q at least one coordinate is a unit. If
the second is a unit, normalize it to one. Otherwise the first is a unit and
normalization makes the second a multiple of p. Uniqueness follows from the
unit coordinate; the two cases cannot overlap. The inverse-image descriptions
give the displayed bases and determinant q.

For completeness, elementary divisors over the discrete valuation ring give
each homothety class a unique representative L contained in L0 but not in pL0,
with elementary divisors (1,p^r). Its image modulo p^r is exactly a primitive
rank-one direct summand, recovering the line. Scalar-equivalent normalized
lattices coincide because the scalar has valuation zero. Thus no vertex is
lost or counted twice.

To check all edges, write L=Z_p v + p^r Z_p w for a basis (v,w) of L0. Its
index-p sublattices correspond to the p+1 lines in L/pL. For r>=1 exactly one,
generated by pv and p^r w, equals p times the normalized parent. The other p
are generated by v+t p^r w and p^(r+1)w, t modulo p, and are precisely the
projective lifts. At the root all p+1 index-p sublattices are forward.
Therefore every edge is a parent-child edge. There are no cycles (a deepest
vertex in a cycle would have two parents), and every vertex reaches the root
by repeated reduction. Path length to the root is r, proving the distance and
count assertions. ∎

**Theorem G2 (boundary and metric).** The inverse limit of these projective
spheres is P^1(Q_p). For normalized primitive vectors v,w representing distinct
ends, their common initial ray length is v_p(det(v,w)). The visual distance
at the chosen root is p^(-v_p(det(v,w))). On the affine Z_p chart this is
|x-y|_p. For finite vertices u,v, graph distance is

`depth(u)+depth(v)-2*common_depth(u,v)`.

*Proof.* Compatible affine labels give one x in Z_p. Compatible infinity labels
give one y in pZ_p, represented by (1,y), including infinity y=0. These disjoint
charts cover P^1(Q_p): normalize a projective vector to max p-adic coordinate
norm one, then use its unit coordinate. Primitive vectors determine the same
line modulo p^k exactly when their determinant vanishes modulo p^k; this is
immediate after normalizing a unit coordinate. The determinant valuation is
therefore the common-prefix length. The tree path runs from u to the last common
ancestor and out to v. The ultrametric inequality follows because sharing k
digits with an intermediate end forces sharing k digits with each other. ∎

**Theorem G3 (actual boundary-to-bulk reconstruction).** Fix p and R>=1. Given
the complete labeled finite boundary P^1(Z/p^R), and its determinant contact
matrix

`K(v,w)=min(R,v_p(det(v,w)))`, with `K(v,v)=R`,

the entire rooted unit-edge radius-R ball is determined up to the unique
boundary-label-preserving rooted isomorphism. The vertices at depth r are the
equivalence classes of K(v,w)>=r. An edge joins a class to its containing class
at the preceding depth.

*Proof.* By G2 these classes are exactly projective reduction fibers. Every
depth-r vertex has descendants in the boundary, and two such vertices have
disjoint nonempty fibers. Thus sending a lattice vertex to its descendant
fiber is a bijection at each depth. Inclusion is precisely the parent relation
by G1, proving existence and uniqueness. ∎

This reconstructs **a geometric tree from the full projective contact data**.
It does not recover that matrix from a list of prime entanglement entropies.
`reconstruct_boundary_clusters` computes the contact matrix from determinants,
without reading parent pointers; tests independently compare all recovered
clusters to lattice reductions. Unit edge lengths, root, full boundary and
depth R are load-bearing. Unrecorded edge subdivision defeats uniqueness if
unit edge lengths and depth are omitted.

## 2. Arithmetic maps, Fourier sign and refinement

Let Q_{p,r}=Z/p^r Z. The arithmetic observation is

`o_{p,r}: Z -> P^1(Z/p^r), n |-> (n:1)`.

Its image is exactly the affine chart, not the whole sphere. For r<=s,
projective reduction satisfies `red o_{p,s}=o_{p,r}`. On functions, the pullback
is a unital injective *-homomorphism

`C(Q_{p,r}) -> ell_infinity(N_{>=1}), f |-> (n |-> f(n mod p^r))`.

It is injective because every residue is represented by a positive integer.
The positive-orientation character chi_{k,r}(a)=exp(2*pi*i*k*a/p^r) pulls back
to the precise arithmetic operator E(k/p^r). Refinement sends k to p^(s-r)k,
so its phase is unchanged. These equalities hold for every integer n and every
integer k, not merely tested prefixes. The executable chart uses source ID and
epoch contracts to reject relabeled reports from different sources.

The matrix [[1,1],[0,1]] lies in GL_2(Z_p), acts as successor on the affine
chart and acts on the whole rooted ball. Every integral matrix with unit
determinant acts on primitive vectors, preserves lattice index and reduction,
and therefore preserves graph distance. A multiplier m prime to p gives such
an action by diag(m,1). In contrast diag(p,1) moves the lattice root in the
full tree and is **not** an automorphism of a fixed rooted sphere. The residue
map a->pa at a fixed modulus is noninjective.

There is nevertheless a different, lawful quantum isometry between finite
affine charts:

`J_r: C^(p^r) -> C^(p^(r+1)), |a> |-> |pa>`.

Distinct indices remain distinct, so J_r^*J_r=I. If T_r denotes cyclic
successor, then `J_r T_r = T_(r+1)^p J_r`, including the wraparound index.
This is a finite source-typed affine braid. It is not a unitary identification
of two unequal-dimensional spaces, and not an isometry from the entire
arithmetic Hilbert space onto a finite residue clock.

## 3. A sharp quantum interface obstruction and its lawful repair

**Theorem G4 (no bounded coherent residue quotient).** For any integer q>=1
there is no bounded linear operator T:ell^2(N_{>=1})->C^q satisfying
T|n>=|n mod q> for all n.

*Proof.* For any M>=1, let x_M=M^(-1/2) sum_{j=0}^{M-1}|1+jq>. Its norm is
one, whereas T x_M=sqrt(M)|1 mod q>. The operator norm would have to exceed
sqrt(M) for every M. ∎

For a finite subset F of integer basis labels, the same map restricted to
span(F) has norm sqrt(max_a #{n in F:n=a mod q}); it is isometric precisely
when at most one label in F lies in each residue class. Indeed the Gram
matrix splits into all-ones blocks of those sizes. This gives an exact
admissible code-subspace criterion, rather than a qualitative truncation warning.

**Theorem G5 (CPTP measurement repair).** The Kraus family
K_n=|n mod q><n| defines a normal trace-preserving completely positive channel
on trace-class operators:

`M_q(rho)=sum_n <n|rho|n> |n mod q><n mod q|`.

*Proof.* Finite partial Kraus sums are completely positive and their effect
operators sum strongly to I. For positive trace-class rho the output series
converges in trace norm, since the sum of the traces is Tr(rho). Extend by
linear decomposition to all trace-class operators. Complete positivity follows
at each finite matrix amplification from the same Kraus construction; trace
preservation follows from summing the diagonal. ∎

This channel discards all input off-diagonal entries and is
entanglement-breaking: on a joint input its output is a sum of positive
conditional states tensored with orthogonal residue projectors. Consequently
it preserves all finite character expectations and residue probabilities, but
cannot serve as a coherence-preserving arithmetic EPR encoding. Passing a
measurement law to a geometric boundary is weaker than encoding a quantum
logical algebra.

## 4. Completed local boundary algebra and a missing-prime obstruction

**Theorem G6 (faithful local observable realization).** Define

`j_p:C(Z_p)->B(ell^2(N_{>=1})), j_p(f)|n>=f(n)|n>`.

This is an isometric unital *-monomorphism. Its range is exactly the norm closure
of finite p-power residue observables, equivalently the algebra generated by
the E(k/p^r). For beta>1, the arithmetic Gibbs state restricts to the probability
measure `mu_(beta,p)=sum_{n>=1} n^(-beta)/zeta(beta) delta_n` on Z_p.

*Proof.* The operator norm is sup_{n>=1}|f(n)|. Positive integers are dense in
Z_p because every residue cylinder contains a positive integer; continuity and
compactness give equality with sup_{Z_p}|f|. Pointwise algebra operations prove
the *-homomorphism assertion. Locally constant cylinder functions are uniformly
dense: uniform continuity lets a sufficiently fine residue partition oscillate
by less than any prescribed epsilon. Finite Fourier inversion expresses each
cylinder function through the stated characters. The Gibbs expectation formula
is absolutely convergent and is exactly integration against the displayed
countable atomic probability measure. ∎

The full projective boundary C(P^1(Q_p)) pulled back along integer arithmetic
has a nontrivial kernel: every continuous function supported in the clopen
infinity chart vanishes on all positive integers. Thus the same realization
does **not** faithfully observe the full tree boundary.

For f in C(Z_p), direct evaluation on |n> gives

```
V_p^* j_p(f) V_p = j_p(x |-> f(px)),
V_p j_p(f) V_p^* = j_p(x |-> 1_(p Z_p)(x) f(x/p)).
```

The second function is continuous because pZ_p is clopen; it vanishes outside
that set. These are genuine arithmetic/local-boundary compatibility identities.

**Theorem G7 (other-prime projection obstruction).** If q is a prime distinct
from p, the range projection P_q=V_q V_q^* is not in j_p(C(Z_p)).

*Proof.* P_q has diagonal 1 when q divides n, and 0 otherwise. Both sets are
dense in Z_p: for any p-power residue cylinder, the CRT gives positive
integers in that cylinder with residue 0 modulo q, and also with residue 1
modulo q. A continuous function equal to one on the first dense set must be
identically one, contradicting the second. The finite-cutoff falsifier is
n=q and n'=q+p^r: their p^r observations coincide but q-divisibility differs.
Hence no single fixed p-adic affine boundary algebra contains the full
arithmetic range-projection family. ∎

**Theorem G8 (free modular flow is trivial on this geometric algebra).** For
the faithful beta>1 arithmetic Gibbs state, the type-I modular automorphisms
`sigma_t(X)=rho_beta^(it) X rho_beta^(-it)` fix every j_p(f).

*Proof.* Both rho_beta and j_p(f) are diagonal in the integer basis, so their
bounded unitary conjugations commute. ∎

Therefore this explicit boundary interface supplies no nontrivial modular
geometric motion. Any proposed flow intertwiner with this particular abelian
image must have trivial induced motion on its faithful observable algebra.
An interacting Hamiltonian or a larger noncommutative algebra is a new problem,
not a consequence of the free construction.

## 5. Local-to-global scope, including infinity

For distinct primes p_i and positive depths r_i, same-source residues glue by
the CRT to one residue modulo M=product p_i^r_i. Existence follows from
orthogonal CRT idempotents; uniqueness follows because divisibility by every
coprime p_i^r_i implies divisibility by M. The executable iterative CRT retains
source ID and epoch. For nested observations at one prime, compatibility must
be checked separately: x mod 2=0 and x mod 4=1 are individually legal but do
not descend from one integer. They are a finite obstruction to arbitrary
local gluing.

At the level of continuous **observable algebras**, replacing Z_p with
Z-hat=product_p Z_p repairs G7. Positive integers are dense by the CRT, so
`j:C(Z-hat)->B(ell^2(N))` is faithful/isometric by the G6 argument. Rational
characters E(r), r in Q/Z, generate this algebra, and every range projection
P_m is the continuous clopen-cylinder function 1_(m Z-hat). The formulas of
G6 for V_m hold on this global profinite algebra. This is an authentic positive
gluing example, not an identification with the completed Bost–Connes C*-algebra.

There is no continuous real-coordinate reconstruction F:Z-hat->R satisfying
F(n)=n on positive integers: the compact domain would have bounded image,
whereas the prescribed integer values are unbounded. Even a single finite CRT
chart leaves n and n+M indistinguishable with distinct Archimedean values.
The restricted adelic product, diagonal rational source, Archimedean test space,
global normalization and a compatible quantum encoding are additional data.
No global gravitational geometry follows from the profinite positive example.
