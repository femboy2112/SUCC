## Exact dependency cut

For a declared source action alphabet and observation, characterize the coarsest
refinement on which every future source action descends. For one-based successor
and block quotient this should require the full residue. For Hilbert compression
the corresponding target is an exact visible-plus-defect factorization, not a
claim that measurement preserves arbitrary coherence.

Hypotheses: deterministic total actions for the general quotient theorem;
positive integer source for arithmetic; specified bounded Hilbert maps for
operator factorization; explicitly chosen embeddings and topology for limits.
Every extension beyond these types needs a separate theorem.

Truth state at start: standard finite carry and future-bisimulation mechanisms
already exist in SUCC. Full adjoints, instrument distinctions, all-time compressed
dynamics, and their analytic comparison remain to be derived and audited here.
The original signed Weil four-mode Schur target remains OPEN.

Weak sufficient form: prove exact all-time compressed successor moments and
exhibit one failed semigroup law. This closes the false identification of a
one-step compressed action with its future evolution, independently of RH.

Proof leverage: retained carry can then be checked against mixed-radix phase,
continuum heat, arithmetic source coefficients, and future-accessible probes.
Novelty tax: quotient congruences, Cuntz/polyphase algebra, realization theory,
and Gaussian Mellin factors are classical. New-to-project leverage must be an
explicit missing equation, bound, or calibrated counterexample; no academic
priority is inferred from rederivation.

## Predeclared falsifiers and controls

- Radix 2: quotient aliases 1 and 2, then successor separates them.
- All-time compression: compare direct two-step source evolution with the
  square of its one-step compression. A test must reject equality.
- Boundary/origin: terminal one-based residue is a−1, not zero; compare adjoint
  zero sectors and independent basis calculations.
- Quantum: coherent carry versus measured/dephased carry on opposite relative
  phases. A retained classical record is not a unitary quantum repair.
- Arithmetic: composite 6 satisfies overlap/affine identities. Reject it only
  with genuine primitive-factor provenance, preserving legitimate prime powers.
- Limits: noninjective cyclic multiplication cannot be an isometric FUCC;
  profinite completion cannot be silently equated with a pointed metric blow-up.
- Fourier: same magnitudes with wrong origin, orientation or twiddle phases
  must fail source-labelled composition.
- Signed source: preserve original completed Gamma, poles, contact and prime
  data. Unitary re-expression cannot improve an exact Schur complement.

Fresh finite regimes use primes 5,7,11, mixed/repeated radix words and carrier
sizes beyond the construction examples. Finite tests calibrate universal paper
proofs; they do not prove them. Small Lean theorems have exact exported types and
axiom audits; analytic claims have no inherited Lean authority.

## Lamps and boundary

If quotient repair succeeds but source-to-Weil transport fails, expose the exact
intertwining equation and investigate a weighted/distributional alternative.
If compressed generators fail, retain memory and compare full semigroups before
taking a limit. Neither failure licenses a no-go theorem for all possible
correspondences. Physical access and measured parameters are separate from
mathematical preparation. No merge, main modification or paper publication.
