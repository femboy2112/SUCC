## Novelty tax and known barriers

A source-specific exact equidistribution clause is equivalent to the desired
normality and must be labelled as such. Weak topological density proves only
digit occurrence. Finite families of primary phases admit the parent changed-
source clustering controls; equality at all primes identifies phases, not their
frequencies. The latest Pascal/Lucas quotient discards positional fibers.
Joint constraints can recover information, but neither a uniform artificial
observer ensemble nor a growing unconditioned path average is the selected law.

## Preregistered falsifiers and fresh regions

Compare b=2,3,16, held-out b=5. Controls: constant and periodic selected
orbits; biased Bernoulli and parity-block laws; uniform words; rational torsion

period grows with the checking window but whose infinite completion is
periodic. A frozen finite prefix cannot certify an infinite hypothesis.
