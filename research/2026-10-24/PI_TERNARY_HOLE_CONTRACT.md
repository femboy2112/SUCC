## Question and live frontier

The user asks whether Pascal's triangle generates 3^n and whether that helps
the pi-normality proof. The classical identity sum binom(n,j)2^j=3^n is exact.
What needs new work is its effect on the authentic BBP source, compatible
real lifts, and selected empirical laws.

The inherited source is U_0=0, U_n=16U_(n-1)+r(n), with the fixed four-pole
BBP rational forcing and alpha=pi/16. Its tail is a positive Hausdorff moment.
The earlier rational filter (E-1)/5 already produces a base-3 shadow, with a
5^k division cover. Real half retention, 1/16 retention, and the necessary
same-law prime symmetry remain OPEN.

## Exact dependency cut

**Candidate statement:** a Pascal-derived fixed rational polynomial P with
P(16)=3^m can produce a rational source P(E)^k U_0 whose absolute difference
from 3^(mk) alpha tends to zero, while reducing the previously known carry
cover or retaining stronger dyadic source structure. Seek the minimum
possible denominator over all polynomial degrees, not merely affine filters.

- **Truth state at registration:** explicit denominator-4 ternary and
  denominator-3 base-81 candidates derived in the separate lanes; proofs
  under audit, no source-frequency theorem assumed.
- **Quantifiers:** a fixed polynomial, all k>=0; all-degree lower bounds
  concern fixed rational polynomials with the stated absolute-error property.
- **Weakest useful form:** a local finite-window transform with certified
  O(n^-2) source error and explicit finite cover; statistical preservation of
  the same law remains a separate obligation.
- **Leverage:** lowers a concrete arithmetic/carry cost for an independent
  symmetry. Positive all-scale collision control plus the appropriate
  symmetry would enter the inherited rigidity argument.
- **Novelty tax:** a new formula for 3^k alpha alone is only a construction.
  A sharp denominator theorem, proved dyadic profile, weaker symmetry target,
  or calibrated obstruction is a genuine local proof step. None is a proof
  of normality without the stated real statistics.
- **Known no-go:** general Pascal profiles and positive moment kernels admit
  completely clustered changed sources; averaging observables does not
  produce phase independence; independent-base normality does not transfer
  by binomial identities.

3. Check binary multiplication by 3 as a finite circle operation separately

4. Do not infer infinite equidistribution from any finite source sample.
   Do not claim minimum denominator outside the fixed-polynomial mechanism,
   or remove a compatible lift because its denominator is dyadic.
