## Competing mechanisms and falsifiers

1. **Recorded twists preserve information.** Exact Gaussian-integer sequences
   with all wire relabelings must recover the same lag products and sum energy.
   Omitting the transport must fail on a constant signal with alternating labels.
2. **Agreement alone produces cancellation.** Compare independent and
   anti-diagonal joinings on odd finite clocks. Match both marginal and entire
   differential-trajectory distributions while checking the sum distribution.
   These are countermodels to a weakened observation contract, not to BBP y0=0.
3. **Single-gain agreement is stable.** Check exact odd-denominator states near
   the hidden half-turn. Test two coprime gains against the explicit sharp 4/3
   coercivity identity, including a smaller-constant mutation.
4. **A persistent antiphase copy obeys the same source.** Test even and odd
   radices separately, including the sharp radix-16 9/17--8/17 two-cycle.
5. **Signed temporal energy can be measured faithfully.** Use authentic prime
   windows p=41,97,257,1009; frequencies h=1,3; smoothing lengths H=2,4,8,16
   whenever H<=window length. Use exact rational BBP states and outward
   Machin/Taylor phase boxes. Report every prescribed row, without selecting
   favorable primes or fitting a decay exponent. Constant and alternating
   signals are calibration controls. These finite rows cannot establish an
   asymptotic estimate.
