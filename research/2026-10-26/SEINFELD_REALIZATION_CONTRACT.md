## Exact target and strongest faithful reading

The author asks whether Seinfeld's retained zero relations can connect inverse
branch averaging to actual selection, whether information erased by averaging
survives in a probability law, and whether realizing a path changes the future
field in the manner suggested by lightning/material growth.

The mathematical target is a lossless finite interface from a declared path law
to its coarse image **plus a retained signed residual**, followed by an exact
account of conditional selection and separately declared feedback dynamics.
The interface must evaluate the authentic arithmetic character, not replace it
with the uniform inverse character. Physical collapse and arithmetic randomness
are not assumptions. A joint law, a marginal and a realized path are distinct data.

## Preregistered rival outcomes

1. **Disintegration:** pass only if coarse mass plus the retained fiber-null
   coordinates reconstructs every rational branch row; an average alone must fail
   on an explicit pair. Dropping each claimed necessary channel must have a witness.
2. **Selection innovations:** pass only if exact likelihood and observable
   increments recover the signed expectation difference. Positive energies alone
   must not silently replace that bilinear pairing.
3. **Resolution:** independently enumerate small inverse fibers and compare their
   character projections at every revelation level. Include constant-fiber modes,
   negative frequencies, held-out source windows, and corrupted source histories.
4. **Realization feedback:** unchanged sampling, Bayesian learning and changed
   geometry must be separate laws. A different predictive probability is not by
   itself proof of a changed physical mechanism. Compare equal-terminal paths.
5. **Arithmetic use:** an auxiliary stochastic lift preserving the deterministic
   arithmetic factor must preserve its character evaluations. A purported gain
   that alters them fails source authentication; none is promoted to normality.
