# Observer-N: arithmetic source versus sampling geometry

Research-only successor to SUCC draft PR #355. The natural-number **arithmetic structure** is not a free observer coordinate, but the order, encoding, filtration and acceptance protocol through which an observer samples that structure *are* choices.

A native SUCC Pólya sample can be postselected with probability \(a^k\) to reproduce the **exact** apparently controlled tilted distribution \(q_j(k)=a^k/Z_j\) without altering source dynamics. A two-time readout distinguishes that particular snapshot selection from persistent intervention; unrestricted **history-dependent** postselection can mimic either at any fixed horizon, but its success rate tends to zero at infinite depth because the two history measures are singular.
