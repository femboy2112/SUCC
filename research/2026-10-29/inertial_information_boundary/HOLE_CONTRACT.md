## Author proposal and faithful target

The author proposes a fundamentally inertial carrier: selecting zero requires
the least additional information relative to that carrier; selecting -1 or -16
requires an additional boundary instruction. A neutral actualized state may
retain a nonempty virtual field. Do not replace this relative claim with an
unrestricted claim about every coding language or every notion of information.

**Principal statement sought:** for a pointed source carrier with a declared
class of free preparations, characterize when zero is the unique zero-cost
actualized boundary; retain the virtual neutral fiber; connect causal boundary
selection, explicit prefix codes and relative entropy without identifying their
different costs. Determine exactly which boundary information the arithmetic
phase observation erases.

**Current state at selection:** candidate elementary theorems and countermodels;

## Six-part frontier

1. Object: pointed carrier, preparation programs, initial seed law and causal
   response to source prefixes.
2. Observables: preparation cost, framed code length, relative entropy,
   conserved homogeneous seed and selected Fourier phases.
3. Mechanisms: positive-cost reachability, Kraft inequality, KL chain rule,

   long-time signed cancellation.
6. Closure obstruction: generic information or positive-field statements can
   hold for coherent zero-seeded sources; integer seed changes are already
   invisible to the BBP phase observation.

## Proposed lemmas and boundaries

1. Free operations preserve the neutral actualized fiber; every operation that
   can leave it has cost bounded below by a positive constant. Then any reachable
   nonneutral seed has strictly positive preparation cost, while zero is free.
   Full virtual support inside the neutral fiber is allowed.
2. A prefix code carrying several outcomes cannot include the empty codeword.
   A default/no-message protocol must account for its framing separately.
3. Causality plus unprepared neutrality determines the retarded response for
   every forcing sequence. The original BBP source and U0=0 are unchanged.
4. For a reference with nonneutral mass epsilon, uniformly bounded KL forces
   nonneutral mass to zero as epsilon tends to zero, with an explicit binary
   coarse-graining bound. This is a probability-law theorem, not a guarantee
   about an arbitrary chosen individual path.
5. For fixed authentic forcing, integer seed offsets produce identical
   integer-frequency phases at every time. Eventual phase equivalence is typed
   by the exact denominator and observation start; real seed distinctions remain.
6. A zero-seeded positive rational source with the same leading n^(-2) scale
   can remain coherent. Preserve this control rather than defining it away by
   an unspecified simplicity condition.
