# Next frontier: noncircular signed carry tomography through the Oily logarithm

## What this research supplies

- **Source-sensitive analytic log:** canonical Lévy exponent of exactly the original positive four-pole source, with the strict 59/(30a) complex first correction.
- **Intrinsic source reciprocal log:** a↦16/a, positive shifts alpha*beta=16, exact odd bounded artanh defect, first and second native source clocks on opposite sides of the inversion fixed point.
- **Seed-sensitive branch instrument:** a q=3 third-root-of-unity character of two symmetric seed observers yields x_n={U_n(c)} exactly, with two-side triangular geometry; coherent s=-pi/16 counterseed converges to omega-bar.
- **Analytic signed cumulant log:** root-free |eta|<log2, and source/carry-dependent mixed covariance without a division by an unknown possibly vanishing Weyl mean.
- **Universal no-go:** logarithmic seed smearing produces gamma and quarter-turn for every seed and forcing; the analytic source Lévy log has identical values for every seed. None of this proves equidistribution of a marked orbit.

## Falsification checks before theorem promotion

1. Same source and **coherent counterseed** `c=-pi/16`: full positive/log source identities survive, while `z_n -> 1` and `B_(n,3) -> omega-bar`; a claimed generic cancellation theorem must fail its stated hypotheses here.
2. Gamma-matched fake `Q_fake=15x`: keeps rank-two 90° endpoint, loses original 59 source jet and the exact 241/16 reciprocal numerator.
3. Wrong pole, coefficient, or clock: must alter the original source passport and foil the finite exact rational tests.
4. Missing integer carry: integer-frequency exponentiation alone is blind to `k_n`.
5. Only q=2 branch character: does not distinguish x and 1-x. q=3 retains orientation.
6. Haar-random seed: source-only or seed-integrated moment cancellation holds irrespective of the marked zero seed; this is an ensemble statement, not selected-path equidistribution.
7. Numerical finite orbit windows or source-connected covariance alone: not sufficient to establish a global Fourier cancellation theorem.
8. Native `a_n=8n-7` does not map under `a↦16/a` to another native clock; don't mistake the continuous-source hyperbolic involution for an actual BBP step symmetry.
