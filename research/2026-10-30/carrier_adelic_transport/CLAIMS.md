## Earned novelty and dependency status

This round closes concrete source/observer transport and identification
edges. The complete all-order shadow is a stronger countermodel along the
asymptotic-jet axis than the earlier arbitrary-finite-jet families. It does
not retain every hypothesis of earlier positive Hausdorff source modules.
The first-jet prime-frame operator supplies an operational measurement that
fixed prime valuations lacked, with an explicitly paid accuracy cost.

The original CRT reconstruction, umbral/Gamma identities, rational
summability principles and finite moment method remain classical or
inherited. The new common-source organization is not an external novelty
claim. The normality-equivalent transport in CAT-05 is explicitly classified
as a reformulation with a useful evaluator, not a proof step closing Weyl.

## Source-map verdict

The fixed-pole reconstruction assumption resolves source identity inside
its declared class. The full initial jet resolves the selected finite
state. The carry seam resolves the local-to-global integer observation.
These do not resolve the infinite-time signed estimate. Source adequacy
and mathematical truth-indistinguishability are therefore tracked as
different obligations, not collapsed into one confidence score.
