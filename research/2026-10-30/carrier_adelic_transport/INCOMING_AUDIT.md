## Findings that change the research choice

1. **The carrier exists in the new work, but its observation must retain
   derivatives.** #416/#417's B_n(0) and every prime valuation at that fixed
   point are unchanged by replacing B0(u)=1 with 1+s*u. The selected first
   jet changes by 16^n*s. This is the concrete reason to build a joint value
   and first-jet carrier, then a varying rational-frame observation.
2. **The zeta direction already reached beyond the scalar -1/12.** The
   inherited negative-integer tangent ladder and reflected prime weights
   survived the focused replay. This round extends the source family to
   every residue and mixed derivative, supplies Pascal translation and
   connects it to the original BBP polylogarithmic endpoint.
3. **The latest Pisot geometry includes a significant no-go omitted by its
   stale PR body.** #419's body still names 519fc1c; actual head 5a4acddf adds
   PIG-06b. Two multiplications in one commutative number field commute;
   their plaquette holonomy is identity. Its nonflat chosen pair uses an
   additional identification between two different quadratic fields.
   Source-authenticated gluing remains unproved. Our ordinary spectral and
   source-shift derivatives likewise commute; no curvature is asserted.
4. **Pisot coordinates are useful, but preserve their inverse.** #418's
   cubic B=2I+A+A^2 and A=2I-B^-1 pass the exact replay. The observer must
   retain that signed inverse and its original time generator; calling
   the new generator Pisot does not create mixing of the old output.
5. **The strongest selected finite-band work is an error-transport result.**
   #415 pays the Green error more sharply and performs certified finite
   signed experiments. Its actual original-source asymptotic energy bound
   remains open. This round does not silently import that missing bound.

## Authority correction in #417

The fresh count is 57/57 PASS, but its PR phrase “57 exact focused tests”
overstates the arithmetic authority. Reading the methods gives:

| Scope | Arithmetic-only exact | Includes float or mpmath |
|---|---:|---:|
| Universal carrier |20|4|
| Log jet |10|0|
| All-grade zeta tangent |1|7|
| Negative-integer tangent |1|7|
| Source-twisted pole |3|4|
| Total |35|22|

The correct description is “57 focused methods combining exact arithmetic
with floating-point and high-precision numerical checks.” A paper error
bound may be rigorous while the quadrature/differentiation used to calibrate
it is not an interval certificate. No mathematical parent theorem is
refuted by this metadata correction.

The #418 replay likewise has mixed authority: 13 methods use exact integer
or rational arithmetic, while `test_quadratic_shift_family` uses `math.sqrt`
and floating tolerances. Its separate oracle combines exact symbolic
identities with numerical conjugate moduli. The report therefore calls it
a 14-method probe, not 14 exact arithmetic certificates.
