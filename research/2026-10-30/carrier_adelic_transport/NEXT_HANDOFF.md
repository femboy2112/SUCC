## Principal exact hole: weaker than normality, stronger than logarithmic loss

Seek an unconditional constant eta>0 and N0 such that, for the authentic
zero-seeded source and every integer N>=N0,

\[
\left|\frac1N\sum_{n=N}^{2N-1} e(U_n(0))\right|\le1-\eta.
\]

The starting target is fixed h=1. If it is reached, generalize the
frequency and improve the contraction; do not report the initial gap as
normality. This is genuinely weaker: nonnormal periodic or biased systems
can have a strict first-character modulus gap. It nevertheless defeats
the inherited loss that shrinks to zero with N and would constitute a
real selected-distribution advance.

PF4 allows replacing e(U_n) by the explicit prime-frame phase with only a
constant unnormalized error. The exact CRT first-jet product is another
common basis. Either route must estimate the **complete signed product at
the actual aligned natural times**, not each prime marginal separately.
