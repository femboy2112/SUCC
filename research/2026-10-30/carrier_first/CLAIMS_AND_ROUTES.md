## D1: the smallest declared new signed milestone

For at least one fixed integer h!=0, prove from the authentic four poles and
U0=0 that there are eta_h>0 and A_h such that for every integer A>=A_h,

    |sum_(n=A)^(2A-1) e(hU_n)| <= (1-eta_h)A.          (D1)

This improves the inherited logarithmic absolute loss. It is deliberately
weaker than the full normality target and is not called a normality proof.
ST4 transforms D1 into the same assertion about the explicit signed
prime-log array with error at most `2pi|h| sum_(n>=A)1/(120n^3)`. That error
is O_h(A^-2), so a constant saving transfers with arbitrarily small loss.
The stencil does not average seeds or discard complementary phases.

**Executed attacks.** The high-order prime representation was derived and
tested on fresh frames; exact CRT products were measured on four prime-birth
windows at h17 and19. The full product was compared with both the primary
factor and a separately reconstructed real phase. The p7001 counterexample
rules out the proposed primary-only pointwise domination. Fibonacci tiling
was tested as a coverage mechanism; the explicit cyclotomic control rules
out prefix-only coverage. The observer contraction was used with actual
counts at four horizons; its conditional cost remains unbounded by the
source. Finally, moment reconstruction was pushed to degree19 and tested
against prefix-identical coherent sources; finite matching cannot supply D1.

**Why no proof emerged.** In the prime representation the weights include
16^(n-k), signed derivative stencils and factorizations of shifted source
integers. Multiplicative identities identify these weights but do not control
their complex time sum. Absolute-value bounds lose the cancellation being
sought. Positive source order lives on the unwrapped line, while D1 lives on
the circle. Classical irrationality controls unusually close rational
approximations, not enough of these pair phases to yield positive density.
No verified trace-function, large-sieve, stationary-phase or measure-rigidity
hypothesis was found for this actual array. Those theorems were not invoked
outside their domains, and no new implementation of them is claimed.

The coherent seed has completed phase zero and violates D1 at every h for
large A. Rational translations need not violate it and are not false
negatives. The Gaussian shadow violates it at its own denominator frequency;
that frequency depends on the shadow. These types matter when comparing a
single fixed h with a theorem quantified over all h.

## Coverage and the larger target

If D1 holds on every large dyadic block, partition prefixes into such blocks
and a bounded initial remainder to obtain a constant all-horizon saving.
This is still not o(N). A normality proof needs savings tending to total
cancellation for every nonzero fixed h, or the established finite-band
subexponential condition at the safe moving depth. A collection of isolated
prime windows has neither positive-density coverage nor a vanishing leftover
term merely because each window passes a constant threshold.

The inherited moving-cylinder theorem from #410 and comparisons from
#411/#415 are conditional bridges. We did not modify their theorem contracts
or substitute observed logarithmic growth for their required little-o bound.
All twelve requested fronts have a scoped outcome above; the remaining
analytic work is an open mathematical problem, not an unexecuted roadmap
presented as a completed proof.
