# Observer front: predictions before new execution

Parent PR417 and read-only PR414 already establish the fiber criterion and
stationary inverse-branch deficiency. This front targets their finite-horizon
quantitative interface, not a new claim of stationary entropy rigidity.

1. Exact conditional digit transport, including finite future resolution and
   endpoint drift, bounds the Wasserstein distance of any actual radix orbit
   from Lebesgue measure. PASS means a proof and all exact rational fresh
   cases obey the bound; one exact violating orbit REFUTES the constants.
   An authentic computed small defect is only OBSERVED, never asymptotic.
2. The number of states of a clockless autonomous deterministic digit emitter
   matching an irrational finite-exponent prefix is at least N/mu-O(1).
   The original pi/16 boundary inherits this; coherent and rational controls
   admit bounded-state emitters. A hidden external clock invalidates the model.
   This is state cardinality, not entropy or a Weyl bound. Kempner with finite
   exponent is expected to survive it while remaining nonnormal.
3. Conditioning on unique sampled futures yields maximal empirical deficiency,
   even on normal controls. Periodic de Bruijn can have zero finite-depth defect
   but nonnormal infinite repetition. Both are required FAIL controls for any
   claim that one fixed finite depth alone certifies normality.
4. A finite depth-m observation requires exactly b^(m+L-1) distinguishable
   states to retain all L future outputs on the unrestricted radix carrier.
   This is a finite observational refinement, not a finite closed simulator.

Discovery cases: binary words up to length7, rational seeds 1/7,1/17,1/257,
base16 original N=128,256 at future depths0..3. Fresh holdouts: denominator
263,509,521; original N=512,1024 and depth4. Keep all literal carries distinct
from completed digits. Exact rational aliases are not expected to fail normality.

References and provenance will distinguish same-model reconstruction, different
implementation, analytic proof and finite observation. No data-dependent
selection of thresholds after inspection.
