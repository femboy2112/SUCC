## Dependence and independence boundaries

- SB2 is a general Diophantine theorem, not a four-pole-specific miracle. SB1 is the authentic source leg; the finite irrationality exponent of π is the selected arithmetic leg.
- Replacing π by any rational translate preserves finite irrationality exponent, so the result cannot single out the literal zero seed among rational translations.
- The exact coherent seed `s=-pi/16` violates the Diophantine premise and has finite energy, so the source-only argument could never yield SB3–SB5.
- A normal sequence has average lag energy 1, but energy 1 alone does not prove normality: it tests Fourier frequencies `15h`, not all test functions and all moving windows.
- A logarithmic absolute saving is much smaller than a linear saving and has no demonstrated implication for a Weyl `o(N)` bound.
- The proof uses published Zeilberger–Zudilin 2020; newer unrefereed π irrationality-exponent claims are NOT used.
- Independent finite rational oracles and unit tests are calibration, not independent proofs of any infinite theorem. No Lean proof or external referee is claimed.
