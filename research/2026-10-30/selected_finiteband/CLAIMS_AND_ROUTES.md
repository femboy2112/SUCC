## Alternative frameworks actually checked and their limits

Large-sieve reasoning needs an Archimedean separation or pair-count hypothesis;
the exact denominator growth gives a mesh far finer than1/H. No such required
spacing estimate was obtained. Unweighted finite-field/Gauss bounds do not
control the full cofiber weights, as the retained restoration counts show.
Global seed-averaged spectral moments and transfer-operator gaps lose the
marked boundary (PR404/406); the coherent seed is an exact counterexample.
Ordinary invariant-measure entropy needs the selected energy premise, rather
than deriving it. Finite irrationality exponent cannot supply positive slip
density, as the inherited Kempner controls demonstrate. These are distinct
failures, not repeated implementations counted as independent proofs.

Dwork/Frobenius, hypergeometric motives and p-adic stationary phase were
considered as possible source descriptions. No verified realization identifying
their trace/oscillatory weights with the actual complementary complex phase
was constructed. Their applicability here is **UNVERIFIED**, not refuted;
no outside bound is invoked. No new Lean theorem was attempted because the
remaining selected analytic premise was not established. Elementary paper
proofs and interval algorithms are labeled as such.
