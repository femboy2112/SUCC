3. **Gamma moments vs actual selected digit entropy**: a Gamma observer is a law on an auxiliary source, not a proof of fair selected digits. The same r but changed seed s=-pi/16 is a decisive countermodel.
4. **Complex s continuum vs chronological integer time**: zeta's analytic variable s is not automatically the observer's physical clock or the BBP iteration n.
5. **Zeta at -1 vs convergent prime series**: Euler product is valid for Re s>1, and prime-power weighted derivative at the reflected positive argument 2 is convergent; `sum n` at -1 is divergent without explicit continuation.
6. **Fibonacci vs general Pisot**: x²-x-1 has Pisot dominant root; x²-x-3 does not. The Fibonacci-to-radix16 factor239 is a *mismatch*, not a new equality of clocks.
7. **Theta/Poisson vs RH**: theta transformation provides functional equation; Weil or trace positivity for nontrivial zeros remains missing.
8. **Pure fair-branch simulator vs concrete selected carrier**: both normal and nonnormal invariant measures can satisfy ×16 invariance; measure disintegration selects actual conditional branch kernel, not the uniform simulator's law.
9. **Source correctness vs π-specific cancellation**: signed four-pole `U0=0` must enter any claimed source-selected inequality; growing denominator/Green transport alone do not bound Weyl sums.
