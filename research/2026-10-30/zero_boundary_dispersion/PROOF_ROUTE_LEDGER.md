## Why the strongest survivors do not yet meet the main target

The selected inequality is stronger in frequency/lag quantifiers, not in
fixed-frequency growth order. The new fractional signed estimate moves h
with the observation scale, and the coherent seed satisfies it. Neither can
be substituted for fixed-h SC-06 or a Weyl o(N) theorem.

Prime-local arithmetic now imposes stronger transport-complexity restrictions,
but real near-coherence is compatible with every observed odd-place mark
under explicitly changed rational seeds. Exact authentication of zero is
therefore essential; a local-character census cannot silently supply it.

The all-frequency energy threshold and its finite Gram implementation expose
a concrete way to assemble future signed information. They remain equivalent
or sufficient certificates, not arithmetic proofs. The actual finite floor1
counterexamples prevent promoting the most tempting stronger finite claim.

Raw outputs and failed source snapshots are linked by each front's validation
and manifests. Repeated tests are not independent proof votes; source families,
prime classes, frequency regimes and seed types retain separate identities.
