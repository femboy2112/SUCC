## Refuted claims (keep their receipts)

1. **REFUTED:** Any number of positive paths with phases only `Φ(γ)=F(t)−F(s)` can self-cancel. Receipt: all paths have identical scalar phase; for positive weights magnitude equals total weight (CRPM-03).
2. **REFUTED:** Exact root-of-unity cancellation of a model Pascal path ensemble automatically implies π digits normal. Receipt: the same model ensemble can be assigned alongside the explicit altered-seed nonnormal BBP orbit of CRPM-06; no map transports its q-phase to the selected π orbit (CRPM-04).
3. **REFUTED:** The original source's affine prime dilation square, or its summability, implies selected normality independently of the initial seed. Receipt: square independent of seed; CRPM-06 retains square while yielding `S_N/N→1`.
4. **REFUTED:** Perfect opposition of 2-adic residue bits forces the corresponding real-circle phases to be antipodal. Receipt: exact rational real-phase mismatch for original BBP first 4..64 states, and an explicit zero-sum phase permutation with nonzero prescribed-pair mismatch (CRPM-07).
