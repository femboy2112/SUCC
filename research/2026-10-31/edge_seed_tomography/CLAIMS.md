### Steelman → crucible → verdict

* **Steelman:** Carrier-relative path distance can be strengthened into a source-labelled edge difference measure plus a virtual signed measurement of seed-response paths, with finite exact spectral tomography. It must retain seed, signed phases and the actual measurement status.
* **Crucible:** All-path positive measure is phase blind; a static 15-fold covering sends a non-Haar density to Haar; uniformly averaged seed intensity has exactly N for *every* phase source; a genuine same-forcing changed seed is maximally nonnormal; p=73 fails for h multiple 73. Every failure has an explicit receipt.
* **Verdict:** Exact finite geometry and two equivalent original-source normality observables survive. The selected π asymptotic gate remains OPEN. Neither a Lie/quantum interpretation nor a physical source measurement has been proved.
