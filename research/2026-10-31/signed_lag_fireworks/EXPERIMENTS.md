# Predeclared exact probes and interpretation

Written before running `probe.py`. No fitted inequality will be promoted.

1. Replay parent selected h=1,K=4,N=512,1024,1536 with the independent
   combined-source/BBP-pi/angle-doubling instrument. Require intersection
   with every published rational interval, not identical rounding.
2. Fresh cases: h=3,5,7,11 at N=769,K=4; N=2048,K=5; N=4096,K=6.
   Also N=1024,K=17,h=3,11, including k=9 and15 conductor strata.
   Preserve each individual lag interval and v3/v5 class. A positive score
   does not refute an asymptotic limsup claim. A negative score does not
   prove it. The deeper-lag bridge may be vacuous and must be reported so.
3. Measure Stoneham alpha_(16,3), alpha_(16,5) completed orbits at
   N=256,K=4,h=1,3,5. A rational finite sum is accompanied by a uniform
   rigorous infinite-series tail budget. Normality is a classical theorem
   for these constants, not a conclusion from these finite scores:
   Bailey and Crandall, [Random Generators and Normal Numbers](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/bcnormal.pdf).
   No demand that their small finite scores be negative is legitimate.
4. Exact source algebra, tiny-angle cosine roots, independent Machin
   interval overlaps, Gaussian window identities, periodic conductor
   controls and symbolic dyadic shadows test the new proofs. The inherited
   q-Pascal chosen phases, coherent irrational seed, nearzero shadows,
   rational finite-coherence spoofs, aliasing and one-mode non-Haar controls
   remain in the separately executed parent suites. They are source-family
   controls, not independent observed realizations of pi.

Failure criteria: mismatched source identity, disjoint certified intervals,
incorrect exact conductor counts, a violated finite theorem, or inaccurate
source labels. Such failures require a repair and rerun. Passing finite
tests certifies the implementation on those cases only.

The preferred hypothesis is that the zero-selected signed lag mean tends
to zero. Its rivals include periodic completed orbits with arbitrarily
large exact low-harmonic gaps and dyadic shadows coherent at h=1. Finite
data can favor a candidate while leaving these rivals observationally
indistinguishable at the declared precision. Exact boundary arithmetic
remains a pathway gap; it is not resolved by assigning probabilities to
the finite samples.
