## Three central fail-closed squares

1. **[4] lift:** `[4]G_n = F_n[4]` is valid only after source `r_n` is transported to its specific fourth root. Keeping label unchanged FAILS in general.
2. **Chiral J:** `J F_r = F_(1/2-r) J`, not `J F_r=F_r J`. The derived source label carries a parity/2-primary correction; its native 2-adic integrality FAILS. The augmented torsor is an admissible repair.
3. **Source sphere → circle period:** the direct Möbius identification FAILS by unequal j values. Exact Mellin source completion followed by quotient modulo zero-boundary exact forms is the actual, source-attested arrow. The need to preserve true real-circle observables is not repaired by a source-blind isometric clock alone.
