## Descending a target through a source quotient

A probe or claim `P:X->Y` descends through a report `q:X->Z` iff P is
constant on every q-fiber. The HG-08 mutant shows that the predicate
"completed base-16 normality" is not constant on reports retaining
only any fixed first M source moments—even with strict positive measure
and actual zero seed. The HG-07 seed shadow shows quantitative
indistinguishability of the first K controlled real-circle phase echoes
under same-forcing but altered seed.

**No claim of physical gravity, metaplectic quantization of a real
arithmetic substrate, or π normality is authorized.** The exact first
nontrivial signed source/seed bound remains an open obligation.
