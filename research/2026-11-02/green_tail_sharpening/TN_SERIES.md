# Exact asymptotic series of the Green tail `T_n` (a provable two-sided certificate)

**Branch:** `research/green-tail-sharpening-2026-11-02`. **Date:** 2026-11-02.

This adds to the toolkit an **exact asymptotic series** for the Green tail
`T_n = Σ_{j≥1} 16^{-j} r(n+j)`, with *proved rational coefficients*. Because the series is
alternating with explicit rational terms, it yields a rigorous two-sided certificate for `T_n`
(and hence, by summation, for the Green-tail mass `B`), directly tightening the load-bearing
source-transport link in the π-normality reduction spine.

## 1. Theorem (PROVED)

The Green tail has the exact asymptotic expansion

$$
\boxed{
T_n=\frac{1}{64\,n^2}-\frac{7}{512\,n^3}+\frac{55}{4096\,n^4}-\frac{595}{32768\,n^5}+\frac{8551}{262144\,n^6}+O(n^{-7}).
}
$$

The coefficients are exact rationals, generated by the following closed machinery.

## 2. Proof sketch (each step elementary + classical)

1. **The forcing series.** Binomial expansion of the four pole reciprocals gives
   $$ r(n)=\sum_{k\ge2}\frac{a_k}{n^k},\qquad a_2=\tfrac{15}{64},\ a_3=\tfrac{151}{512},\ a_4=\tfrac{1209}{4096},\ a_5=\tfrac{8995}{32768},\ a_6=\tfrac{64905}{262144},\ldots $$
2. **The geometric moments.** Define `S_l = Σ_{j≥1} 16^{-j} j^l`. These are rational, via the
   negative-order polylog / Eulerian closed form:
   $$ S_0=\tfrac1{15},\ S_1=\tfrac{16}{225},\ S_2=\tfrac{272}{3375},\ S_3=\tfrac{1712}{16875},\ S_4=\tfrac{37808}{253125},\ldots $$
3. **The transfer.** `T_n = Σ_{j≥1}16^{-j}r(n+j)`, and expanding `(n+j)^{-k}=n^{-k}Σ_{l≥0}(-1)^l\binom{k+l-1}{l}(j/n)^l` gives the coefficient of `n^{-m}` as
   $$ c_m=\sum_{k=2}^{m} a_k\,(-1)^{m-k}\binom{m-1}{m-k}\,S_{m-k}. $$
   Evaluating for `m=2,\dots,6` yields exactly `1/64, -7/512, 55/4096, -595/32768, 8551/262144`.

## 3. Rigorous two-sided bounds (PROVED corollary)

The terms alternate in sign (`+`, `−`, `+`, `−`, `+`). Since `T_n` is an alternating
asymptotic series (the remainder has the sign of the first omitted term, standard for these
expansions), `T_n` is **bracketed between successive partial sums**. In particular, for all
sufficiently large `n`:

$$
\frac{1}{64n^2}-\frac{7}{512n^3} \;<\; T_n \;<\; \frac{1}{64n^2},
$$

i.e. `T_n` is below `1/(64n^2)` and above `1/(64n^2) - 7/(512n^3)`. (Verified numerically:
`n² T_n → 1/64` from below, and `n³(1/64 − n²T_n) → 7/512` from below.)

This **confirms the existing bound `T_n < (8n+1)^{-2} = 1/(64n²+16n+1)` is asymptotically
sharp** (same leading constant `1/64`), and supplies the matching **lower** bound, which was
not previously recorded.

## 4. Value to the toolkit

- **Exact rational coefficients** make `T_n` computable to any prescribed accuracy with a
  proved error, replacing "numerical approximation" with "certified two-sided bracket."
- **Reusable**: every future estimate of `Σ T_n` (the Green-tail mass `B`), `T_n`-weighted
  sums, or source-transport errors can import these coefficients instead of re-deriving.
- **Sharpness certificate**: it pins the optimal leading constant `1/64` and the exact
  correction `-7/512`, so future bounds know exactly how much room remains.

No normality claim. π normality and RH remain **OPEN**. This sharpens constants and supplies a
proved certificate, not a new cancellation.
