# Hannibal source experiment declaration

Declared before new source computations in this directory. Parent campaign:
`1478b51cf1370411aea6ec42c09d080598384528`, PR #476. This declaration is
same-team preregistration, not a blind external holdout. Parent observations
through 8191 and earlier ancestor horizons are development data.

Immutable input: all four signed poles, U0=0. No pi digits are construction
inputs. Integer/Fraction paths retain the selected real cofactor and carries.

1. Development: dyadic A=2,...,4096; compare the tempting IID-mean ceiling
   E <= 1+(m-1)/A at the explicit logarithmic mesh. This is a candidate
   deterministic inequality, not a statistical confidence bound. Any exact
   counterexample refutes it; passing finitely many A cannot prove it.
2. Fresh-to-this-execution dyadic epochs A=16384 and 65536, with
   m=max(2,2^floor(log2(log2 A))). Stream the original full-modulus source,
   retain exact histogram vectors, literal carry digest, and source modulus
   hash. Report E exactly; test E<9/8 only as a declared finite observation.
   Also record meshes 64 and 256 to resolve annuli that the slow mesh cannot
   yet resolve. These auxiliary meshes do not change the asymptotic target.
3. Independent Fraction/full-modulus/odd-modulus agreement through n=384.
   Oracle agreement is a pass/fail gate, never mathematical independence of
   the shared source identity.
4. Fresh prefix N=12288, h=7,17,255,256: exact sorted mirror transport and
   feasible rank-half-turn costs, with rational outward enclosures and
   hashes of the exact costs. No vanishing-cost premise is inferred.
5. Fresh annulus/carry census: epoch A=512, meshes 64,256, lags 1..511.
   Record exact outer-shell counts, inner-core counts, and run-spacing
   violations against the derived theorem; compare an independent quadratic
   pair oracle. Earlier source data at this A are development data; the
   shell decomposition is a new diagnostic, not a fresh source holdout.
6. Fresh all-lag witness blocks A=128,256: for every 1<=d<=A search exact
   source differences on [2A,24A], retain a witness at distance >1/32 and
   validate backward propagation at m=256 and 4096, when its explicit tail
   budget permits it. Finite witness search does not determine the onset
   in the theorem using a published irrationality measure.

Required controls: changed coherent completed seed 1/5, delta_0, uniform
fifteenth roots, conjugate +/-1/6, exact antipodal pair, finite-history source
mutant, prime-free cofactor locking, PSD-but-not-pointwise-positive kernel,
and frequencies divisible by sixteen. Use rational comparisons for claimed
signs; inherited floating tests remain diagnostics only.

Resource limit: one long native suite, one substantial source pass at a time;
stream large horizons rather than storing huge common-modulus states. Keep
every failure and any reduction of scope explicit. Do not rewrite this file
after observing results; record deviations separately.
