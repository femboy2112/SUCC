# The remaining inequality, after the falsifiers

The exact original target remains

    Lambda_A M_A^0(|h|16^j_A) -> 0 for every fixed h != 0,

on all sufficiently large dyadic epochs. F4 still converts it to full-prefix
fixed-character Weyl. This round does not claim a rate for the selected orbit.

The new falsifiers make the next required discriminator concrete. Any proposed
signed cofactor/carry estimate must use information absent from BDE-3: exact
forcing at its sparse nonbirth times, the omitted ternary numerator orientation,
or the genuine literal carry history. Merely adding first-prime Gamma pins,
nonternary principal parts, exact denominators, or the good-pair deletion does
not fix it. The exceptional pair cost is already o(1); that cost is not the gap.

On the real side, CFG-2 exhibits one dangerous alignment:

    || {16^(n+j_A)y} - H_A T_n || <= 1/30+o(1)

over a contiguous block of length A/sqrt(log A), apart from a paid switch set.
A source-selected upper bound on such alignments would distinguish this
control, but excluding this one pattern alone is still insufficient: every
signed maximizing interval must be controlled. This is a discriminating
mathematical target, not a claimed reduction to a solved estimate.

The radial channel needs a different calibration. It already has critical
signed decay by curvature, while its correct retrieval cost grows like H/A.
Do not require it to fail the original scalar ratchet or apply original F4 to
it. Use its coarse concentration to test denominator/measure arguments instead.

The immediate numerical tool is now an exact maximal-sum certificate at the
specified depth. Larger finite epochs can falsify explicit finite inequalities
with stated constants. They cannot settle the asymptotic through a fitted trend.
No remaining frozen holdout, proof contract, or historical receipt is modified.
