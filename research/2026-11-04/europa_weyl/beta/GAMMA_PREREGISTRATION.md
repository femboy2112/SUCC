# Two-Mellin-moment holdout

After deriving the covariance bound and before constructing the probe, select
N=2,7,20,38. Enclose the exact residual ratio using four alternating powers
of x^2 and factorial/harmonic integrals. Expected: nonzero lower and upper
differences above H_(8N+1), with upper strictly below
3/[(8N+2)(8N+3)−2]. Record outward intervals for (8N)^2 times the
difference. A rational 1/5 seed in the lifted endpoint adds 4/5 and makes
the inferred first residual negative, at every positive N. All source
markers and measurements must be distinct from fitted-output validations.
