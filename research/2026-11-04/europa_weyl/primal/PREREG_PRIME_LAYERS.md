# Fresh arithmetic holdouts

Sealed protocol before running the accompanying independent probe. Source tap formulas and historical #497/#499 examples are development material. These holdouts test implementation and signs; they do not certify asymptotics, priority or external review.

* Use a positional common-denominator polynomial for the direct oracle at the literal zero seed. Compare its top prime digit with the prefix formula for every rational standard tap basis, the original, angular, and hostile signed combination, over p=3,5,7,11,13,17,19 and e=1,2,3 whenever the horizon stays at most 1600. Use p-unit rational weights and horizons floor(p^e*m/8), one step either side, for m in {1,2,4,7,8,11,16,22,29} with 8N<p^(e+1). Include two prime levels, their wrong character, and modulus-p versus modulus-p².

* For N=83,211,389,631 test every simple prime p with p²>8N in the two genuine cumulative conductors. Record every cancellation. For each verify division of the appropriate universal numerator and the loss budget; check all other simple residues too.

* Use the exact source mutant (1,-2,0,0,0,0,0,0) over p=1 mod8 and exactly two surviving summands. Predict a zero against positive source conductor valuations for every such generic prime. Use the sign-flipped (-2→+2) positive control, a legitimate p-integral altered seed invisible at every tested layer, and seed 1/p^e visible at that precision.

* Recover all eight tap basis columns using exact inversion of the group action. Check character eigenspace dimension and the alternative generator's angular period by rational integration substitution; no pi digits enter any finite test.

* Failure means any in-domain residue differs, a charge is missing, or a nonzero rational pulse is found to vanish in Q. A top digit that vanishes modulo one prime is expected and must be recorded. Do not change inputs after seeing outputs.

Before running any numeric holdouts, add a pair test at ALL four named horizons. For every large endpoint prime p dividing the final denominator and every m<n count precisely the row hits p(Y_n−Y_m)=0 modulo p, and bound the count by floor(n/ord_p(16))+1+floor(8n/p). For U and Y, compute every gap denominator independently by integer subtraction and preserve the maximum and sum of exact conductor losses in bit lengths. Record failure if a row violates the multiplicative-order cap.
