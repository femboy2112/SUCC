# Competing mechanisms at the selected boundary

The object is the unwrapped chronological numerator Q_n U_n, read on the real circle by the ordered-pair tent. The proposal was that identifying authentic injections at primes forces the intermediate realized phases apart. The test needs to separate a constraint on the full numerator from a constraint that permits a coherent orbit.

| Proposed mechanism | Prediction or discriminator | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Birth-site congestion controls the obstruction | Delete all pairs touching births | Changes J by O(1). A divergent multiple of K has to come from the interior. |
| The signed change of r across a lag forces real dispersion | At w below the quadratic scale, two visits to a given pair of arcs at short time separation can occur at few start times. Null forcing fails. | Proved and sharp over same-forcing rational seeds at the stated fine scales. |
| Precise accumulated checkpoint phases determine bulk occupancy | Copy all completed checkpoint words, protecting p-primary parts and pair heights, and test a nonbirth block | A polynomial-precision checkpoint control clusters. At precision A^-4 for accumulated states this construction gives no general obstruction. |
| Full order/denominator data in one frame determines the selected real statistic | Keep all additive relations and all integral-combination orders on a silent-prime fiber | Both extremes survive. Marking a mixed-frame denominator can split these worlds exactly. |
| The maximal moving-frequency sum beats the coherent crossover rate for the selected boundary | Use exact finite stencils; vary the depth and maximize over subintervals, compare with both same-forcing coherent controls | An averaged upper bound and the coherent rate are proved. The selected rate at the specified depth stays open. |
| The radial conductor supplies independent reinjection suppression | Evaluate the exact angular/radial decomposition on the same numerator history | The radial component tends to zero with a maximal conductor. The archimedean sum shares that component with the angular term with opposite sign. |
| Zero seed, all jets, sparse defects and exact height suffice | Preserve those features while changing old numerator orientations at rare composite times | A single rational control has asymptotically maximal J. Checking the exact local injection grid detects the mutation. |

Exact features and finite ranges in that specification take precedence over the shorthand. Full chronological numerator history is a separate discriminator. No higher-order quantitative interaction is inferred from survivor counts. The signed Fourier transport has an explicit common basis e(h·) and orientation U_m−S_j(m); the radial finite part uses V−2U. No statistical phase is assigned across unrelated prime embeddings.

**Remaining rivals.** The original zero seed might disperse by cancellation among the retained large-denominator pairs, or those pairs might have correlated centered numerators despite satisfying every conductor bound here. Prime orientation and the mixed denominator identify the path. Present inequalities on those measurements do not decide between the rival real distributions. The toolkit is saturated only for the specified invariant-only implications with explicit counterexamples.
