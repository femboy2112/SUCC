# Gamma Prybar: real short returns of the four-pole orbit

**Research draft · November 4, 2026.** The original orbit has a sharp seed-uniform microscopic dispersion bound. The growing dyadic energy and fixed-character Weyl estimates remain open. Authority throughout is paper mathematics except where a receipt is explicitly described as finite. No new kernel declarations or external referee review.

## 1. Birth pairs and the precise sufficiency arrows

Use natural logs and integer epochs A≤n<2A. Write ||z|| for distance to the nearest integer; absolute values of unwrapped differences would define a different problem. With r(n)=4/(8n−7)−2/(8n−4)−1/(8n−3)−1/(8n−2), set U_0=0, U_n=16U_(n−1)+r(n). Every arithmetic comparison uses these unwrapped rationals unless a changed seed or forcing is explicitly specified. Q_n is the LCM of the reduced injection denominators up to n. The numerator Q_n U_n retains the whole integer lift. The common character is e(hx)=exp(2πihx).

K=floor(log_16 log A), q=16^K/2. All scheduled bounds refer to sufficiently large A. The count b of first-pole birth indices is the number of primes 1 mod 8 from 8A−7 through 16A−15 inclusive. Fixed-progression PNT gives b=(2+o(1))A/log A. The difference between full and interior tent energies, normalized by A² in both, lies between 0 and q(2Ab−b²)/A²=O(1)=o(K), since q≤(log A)/2. Deleting these indices changes the ordered near-pair count by at most 2Ab−b²=O(A²/q). Thus each little-o target is equivalent to its interior version; likewise the stronger big-O pair target. The same normalized count argument adds at most O(A/q) births to any interval, preserving Target B in both directions. No short-interval prime hypothesis enters.

More precisely for any subcloud, if P counts pairs at distance strictly below 1/q, then
$$J\le qP/A^2\le 10J.$$
Partition into 2q equal half-open arcs with counts c_i. Same-arc pairs have tent weight ≥1/2. A close pair occupies arcs at cyclic index distance ≤2, so P≤5∑c_i² by Cauchy–Schwarz on each cyclic shift. This proves both inequalities. Also J is the circle integral of the square of q/A times the number of points in the moving arc [x,x+1/q). Hence a uniform occupancy bound CA/q gives J≤C, including for arcs containing either endpoint by consistent half-open convention.

There is a larger disposable set than the birth indices. For a deletion E of size e, positivity of the tents and the triangle inequality on the moving-arc count in L² give
$$0\le \sqrt{J_{\rm full}}-\sqrt{J_{\rm retained}}\le\sqrt q\,e/A.$$
The upper bound for the deleted cloud alone uses its e² pairs. Thus **every** deletion of size o(A sqrt(K/q)) preserves the little-o gate in both directions. In particular one can remove all times within L steps of a birth for L=o(sqrt(K log A)), by the same union bound. A point mass on all deleted sites has exactly q(e/A)² self-energy, showing the sharp cardinality threshold for arbitrary deletions without distribution assumptions.

The inherited implication is J=o(K) ⇒ normality, while vanishing of every fixed-character prefix mean is equivalent to normality. The converse to the energy implication fails over the same-forcing family of real seeds: start with any base-16 normal digit sequence and replace the next floor(A(K/q)^(1/4)) digits beginning at position A+1 by zeros on each late dyadic epoch. The edited positions have asymptotic density zero by geometric summation, so every fixed word still has its normal frequency (at most its length times the edit count windows are affected). Call the new number y and take seed y−π/16. Except for O(log q) positions at the right of each planted block, the completed phases are in [0,q^(-2)]; the actual phases differ by O(A^(-2)). Thus J/K ≳ sqrt(q/K)→∞ on those epochs while every fixed character mean tends to zero. This is a changed real seed, and extends the planted-word warning in main's LS-10. It supplies no converse assertion for the unique original seed.

## 2. The actual real-circle estimate and its sharpness

Let N_A(w) count ordered pairs including the diagonal, with ||U_n−U_t||≤w. For every fixed B>2, all sufficiently large A and
$$A^{-B}\le w\le A^{-2}(\log A)^{-4},$$
the authentic orbit obeys
$$\sup_{|I|=w}\#\{n:\{U_n\}\in I\}\ll A/\log A,
\qquad N_A(w)\ll A^2/(\log A)^2.$$
The upper bounds also hold for smaller widths by monotonicity and for all real seeds under the identical forcing. For every width in the displayed range, there is a rational changed seed with denominator dividing Q_A attaining both orders (lower constants may depend on B). For every w=o(A^(-2)), even outside the stated range, N_A(w)=o(A²). The proof works at fixed positive δ for w=A^(-2−δ). At width 1/(64A²) the fixed coherent seed places the entire epoch in one arc, identifying a genuine crossover. All constants in the upper bounds at the displayed logarithmic cutoff are absolute.

**Proof of the upper bounds.** The polynomial identity
$$r(s)=\int_0^1 u^{8(s-1)}(1-u)(u^2+2)(u^2+2u+2)\,du$$
follows by expansion and integration. In particular the positive polynomial after (1−u) lies between 4 and 15. Multiplying by 1−u^8 and integrating bounds r(s)−r(s+1) between positive absolute multiples of A^(-3) on [A,2A], since 1−u≤1−u^8≤8(1−u). Also r(s)=O(A^(-2)). For D=floor(log_16 A), a return after j≤D steps from [a,a+w] to [b,b+w] requires
$$16^ja-b+R_j(n)\in\mathbb Z+[-16^jw,w],\qquad
R_j(n)=\sum_{i=1}^j16^{j-i}r(n+i).$$
The integer term follows by iterating the actual unwrapped recurrence and choosing integer representatives in the two arcs. R_j is decreasing, has total range O(16^j/A²), and changes by at least c16^(j−1)/A³ per integer step. Only O(1) integer windows can occur, since 16^j≤A. Each admits O(1+A³w) start times. All these bounds hold uniformly over a,b, including wraparound intervals.

Let m=ceil(log A). For a union of m such arcs, divide time into blocks of length D; every visit after the first in a block yields a distinct short return between some ordered pair of arcs. Therefore its relative mass is at most
$$O(1/D)+O(Dm^2(A^{-1}+A^2w))=O(1/\log A).$$
Partition into ceil(1/w) equal bins. The largest m bin masses together are O(1/log A), so their sum of squares is O(1/log² A). Every other bin has mass at most this total mass divided by m, hence O(1/log² A); their sum of squares is bounded by the same maximum. A w-close pair occupies bins at cyclic index distance at most two. Cauchy–Schwarz on the five shifts proves the asserted count. Taking a single arc proves the uniform occupancy bound. For general o(A^(-2)) widths, the signed difference along a lag also gives N_A(w)/A² ≪ A²w+1/log A; see the complete independent lag proof F5 and the Fourier cross-check F5b [here](front4/FINE_SCALE.md).

**Matching rational construction.** Set M=3A and α_M=16^(-M)U_M. The exact finite relation is U_n(s)=16^n(s+α_M)−R_(n,M), where 0≤R_(n,M)=16^nα_M−U_n≪A^(-2) in the epoch. Choose L=ceil(log_16(8/w)). For each n=A+jL<2A, prescribe the next L completed digits to lie in a radix cell contained in [R_(n,M)+w/4,R_(n,M)+3w/4]. Such a cell exists since its width is ≤w/8. These digit windows are disjoint; their concatenation prescribes an entry point x at time A. The source clock Q_A is odd. By the first occurrence of the primes p≡1 mod 4 in the offsets 8k−7 and 8k−3, it includes all such primes 5<p≤8A−7; each has a single p-pole at its first occurrence and its coefficient is a unit. Fixed-progression PNT gives log Q_A≥(4+o(1))A. Pick b mod Q_A by rounding x−16^Aα_M on the grid and inverting 16^A modulo Q_A on its numerator. Then
$$\|16^A(\alpha_M+b/Q_A)-x\|\le1/(2Q_A).$$
The deviation at every sample is ≤16^A/(2Q_A)=o(A^(-B)); every prescribed point lies in [0,w]. There are at least A/L such points, and hence at least (A/L)² ordered pairs. Since L=O_B(log A), both lower bounds follow. If the rounded seed is zero one may move one grid step without exhausting the margin. This construction supplies an extremal member of the family, without locating the original seed within it. At A=367 and w=A^(-3), for example, the exact zero-seed count was 367 (all diagonal) and the constructed rational control gave 2,537 pairs.

The coherent seed −π/16 has U_n=−T_n, T_n=Σ_(i≥1)16^(−i)r(n+i), with 0<T_n<1/(64n²) from the integral bound. At the normality mesh every one of its pairs is close. In contrast, the zero forcing with zero seed fails even the fine-scale bound. This isolates exactly which part of the source the estimate uses: the chronological change of the four-pole forcing. The exact selected boundary has yet to yield a comparable theorem at w=1/q.

## 3. Two-time prime arithmetic, signed frequencies, and the valve

* [Chronological two-time conductor](front2/CHECKPOINT_THEOREMS.md), §§1–2. Fix η>0. Except for a bounded number C_η of lag diagonals per epoch, every genuine difference U_t−U_n with A≤t<2A and 0≤n<t has log denominator ≥(6−η)t. Unlike a one-point conductor bound, this controls joint cancellation of actual residues across time. Their exceptional energy is O_η(q/A). §3 constructs a same-forcing rational seed that approximates every **accumulated** first-birth phase to A^(-1), protects entire prime families, and can still cluster a fixed fraction of the epoch. It distinguishes exact state data from finite precision and does not extrapolate to A^(-4) state precision.
* [Signed Fourier sieve and reverse estimate](front4/FOURIER.md), F1–F4. Sparse high frequencies pay for their spacing through the finite source stencil. The maximal-subinterval sum of squares across the ladder is bounded, while the coherent seed realizes the sharp backward-transport cost. The crossover at A² is derived separately from the positive Green tail.
* [Valve leakage and reinjection](front3/VALVE_AND_FILTER.md), V1. Both normalized positive flows are O(q/K) separately, and some same-forcing rational seeds attain that order. Small signed net flow alone supplied no such bound. This rate and a bound on transitions still permit large central occupancy.
* [Gaussian and radial arithmetic](front5/front5_proofs.md). All three frames satisfy a square-root average bound on the prime mass lost in temporal differences. The radial frame Z has conductor exponent 8 and 0<Z_n<1/(32n+16), hence J(Z)/q→1. The finite part uses the signed orientation V−2U. [Mixed denominator](front1/COFACTOR_PROOFS.md) 2Y−U detects every nonzero seed in the marked silent-prime fiber, even though its timewise marginal orders can survive. Thus the extra frame can identify a lost orientation; a real inequality at that orientation remains due.
* [Same-forcing arithmetic automorphism and occupancy](front1/COFACTOR_PROOFS.md). On the full silent-prime fiber, all integral relations between the labelled epoch states and the epoch injections can be identical. Almost every member has asymptotically correct occupancy uniformly over intervals of width 1/q, yet an admissible member has J≫q. Here and throughout, J≫q means a lower bound by a positive constant times q. The original seed belongs to this fiber. The Haar comparison is explicitly restricted to a declared seed ensemble.

## 4. A single zero-seed counterfeit across all epochs

The next construction uses a different forcing sequence. It unifies the sparse reset and silent-prime arguments without requiring the state-denominator saturation theorem.

Fix any prefix H. Choose a sufficiently late first multiple s_0 of 7 after H; iterate s'=7 floor(5s/28). Gaps are ≤s/4 and asymptotic to s/4, so there are O(log N) resets through N. All 8s−7 are composite. Take C=16^(−2s_0) floor(U_(2s_0)), a fixed dyadic rational; its distance from α=π/16 is ≤2·16^(−2s_0). Before s_0 take Y_n=U_n. At each reset form h equal to the product of primes p≡5 mod 8 with 4s<p≤8s−11. Each was born at (p+3)/8≤s−1. The multiples 2p,3p have unusable offset classes and 4p>16s. Thus no such prime fires from s to 2s, but all have nonzero p-primary state there. Also h|Q_(s−1) and log h=(1+o(1))s.

At s write Y_s=U_s+w/h. Choose w within s²+1 of h(16^s C−16^(−s)U_(2s)), omitting each residue that would cancel the nonzero p-primary part of U_s. Each p excludes one class. In any interval of s² consecutive integers the excluded count is at most s²Σ_(p|h)1/p + ω(h)=o(s²), by PNT and p>4s. In [s,s') put Y_(s+i)=U_(s+i)+16^i w/h. This gives a single rational path with seed zero and forcing f_n=Y_n−16Y_(n−1). All its terms can be computed from the stated finite original prefixes.

The reset error relative to 16^s C−T_s is at most (s²+1)/h+16^(−s)T_(2s). Within the block the error multiplies by at most 16^(s/4), leaving exp(−cs) for any fixed 0<c<1−(log16)/4 and sufficiently large s. In terms of the time n one may for instance take c=1/10 for large resets. At the very first reset the incoming discrepancy from C is also exponentially small by the choice of C. Hence f=r except at resets and |f_n−r(n)|=O(exp(−n/10)); by taking s_0 later if necessary, f stays positive and strictly decreasing everywhere. It agrees with r at every designated first-pole birth, and has all its asymptotic Laurent coefficients. But 16^n C is an integer eventually, so ||Y_n||=O(n^(-2)) and J(Y)/q→1.

At any reset, den(f_s−r(s)) divides Q_(s−1) by the old denominators of both successive shifts. The same preceding LCM joined with f_s or r(s) therefore yields the same next LCM. Induction proves equality of every prefix LCM. On each reset block, multiplication by a unit on each donor p-component takes U_s to Y_s and acts as the identity elsewhere. It fixes the original injections inside the block, and transports all epoch states *in that block*, preserving the reduced denominators of all their integral combinations. In particular den(Y_n)=den(U_n) at every time. All p≡1 mod 8 state passports agree globally, and for every fixed p the two trajectories have identical p-primary components eventually. All primes firing in any authentic injection at n retain their actual primary state and forcing at n: the current donors are silent, and so are the previous donors at the switching instant. Extra poles at previously silent old primes can occur at resets. A single global automorphism or agreement of chronological integer numerators or carries is not asserted. The per-injection denominator is a decisive discriminator: if den(f_n) divides den(r(n)), a nonzero difference has magnitude ≥1/den(r(n))≫n^(-4), impossible for all large altered resets.

[Causal sparse reset](front3/SPARSE_RESET.md) uses only past taps for its rational approximation to the Green tail and protects all fresh prime passports for a linear age. [One omitted prime](front7/SPARSE_CONTROLS.md) preserves all other primary parts indefinitely. [Ternary address](front7/TERNARY_DEPTH.md) resolves an actual growing primary depth. Each control has its own quantifiers; their properties cannot be combined by voting.
