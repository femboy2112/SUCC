## V1. A sharp separate positive-flow bound

For integers A≥2, K≥1, q=16^K/2, every seed obeys

    ⟨I_q⟩ ≤ 1/K + 2/A + 16q/(64A²).
    ⟨L_q⟩ ≤ 1/K + 4/A + 17q/(64A²).

Proof. Use the exact completion X_n=16^n(s+π/16)=U_n(s)+T_n and 0<T_n<1/(64A²) throughout the epoch (SC-04). Thus pair differences change by less than 1/(64A²). I_q is 16q-Lipschitz, since its disjoint tents are continuous piecewise linear with these slope bounds. L_q has slope magnitude at most 15q in its inner tent and q elsewhere. Fix positive lag d and consider completed gaps z_n=X_{n+d}−X_n; the next gap is 16z_n. A lateral hit I_q(z)>0 means ||z−j/16||<1/(16q) for j≠0. For each 1≤i<K one has ||16^i z||<16^{i−1}/q≤1/128, whereas every lateral hit has ||z||>(1−1/q)/16≥7/128. Thus hits are separated by at least K sites, and their number is ≤(A−d)/K+1. Each weight is ≤1. Sum over both orientations and use I_q(0)=0. This gives the first bound on completed phases, then the displayed transport cost. On completed phases I_q−L_q=k_q(16z)−k_q(z), so telescoping over all n on a lag diagonal leaves two endpoints with difference bounded by one. The ordered-pair average differs by at most 2/A. Transporting L_q with the preceding slope bound proves even the second displayed inequality with 15 in place of 17.

At the scheduled K=K_A this improves the trivial O(q) upper bound for positive flows individually to O(q/K). The signed net-flow bound alone did not give this improvement.
