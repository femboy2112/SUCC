# Sparse rational interventions in the four-pole recurrence

These are infinite, deterministic **altered-forcing controls**, with literal initial value zero. They attack two proposed projection arguments for the original trajectory. Neither control is admissible as its replacement. All times and mesh sizes below are integers. A principal part at p means the class of a rational number in Q/Z_(p), where Z_(p) denotes rationals of p-integral valuation. This retains the whole polar part, including higher powers.

Let r be the four original signed fractions, U_n=16 U_(n-1)+r(n), U_0=0, and Q_n the lcm of the reduced denominators of r through n. Write V_0=0 and V_n=16V_(n-1)+r(n)+e_n. The following constructions maintain their own genuine LCM equal to Q_n. Whenever den(e_n) divides Q_(n-1), this follows inductively from lattice equality: adjoining r(n)+e_n or r(n) to (1/Q_(n-1))Z generates the same additive subgroup of Q. Thus both trajectories admit full chronological integer lifts and literal carries. Their actual numerators and carries can differ.

## S1. All principal parts except 3 at all times

There is a rational deterministic control with O(N/log N) designated correction times through N, exact original injections at EVERY prime birth 8b−7>5, identical Q_n for all n, and identical principal parts at ALL p≠3 simultaneously for every state, lag and forcing value. Nevertheless V_n=O(n^(-1/2)) as unwrapped reals. Its near-pair fraction tends to one at q=O(log A) and its normalized triangular energy is q(1−o(1)).

Take t_0=2 and t_(i+1)=t_i+3 max(1,floor(log_16(t_i)/6)). Every t_i is 2 mod 3 and 8t_i−7 is composite. The gap is asymptotic to (1/2)log_16 t_i, bounded above by 3+(1/2)log_16 t_i. Counting gaps in [sqrt N,N] gives O(N/log N) resets. At each t set D=3^v3(Q_(t−1)) and x=16V_(t−1)+r(t). Set e_t=−round(Dx)/D, and e_n=0 elsewhere. Fix any deterministic nearest-integer tie convention. At k=(3^a+1)/2 for every a≥2, the second pole of r has valuation −a, the first −1 and the remaining two are 3-integral. Hence D≥3^floor(log_3(2t−3))>(2t−3)/3 for all sufficiently large t. Consequently |V_t|≤1/(2D)=O(1/t). For t≤n<t_next, positivity and r(j)=O(t^-2) yield |V_n|≤16^(n−t)(|V_t|+O(t^-2))=O(t^-1/2). As n/t→1 this proves the stated bound. All e_n and V_n−U_n belong to Z[1/3]. The same applies to any state difference and all integer characters' arguments. This is a single path in all epochs. The unwrapped bound implies every pair distance in [A,2A) is O(A^-1/2) and therefore every kernel is 1−O(q A^-1/2).

The control loses the 3-primary numerator and exact forcing on the designated indices. The frequency of changed carries is uncontrolled. Equality of principal parts does not assert equality of all p-adic digits. In particular, it does not preserve the p-integral component at every prime. Corrected Gamma windows hold at births because their forcing values coincide literally. This global construction extends #487 OMC-07's separate finite clouds with a single zero-seeded, sparsely edited recurrence. It cannot disprove a theorem that uses the full four-pole formula at each step.

## S2. Exponentially small sparse changes and linear memory of every newborn prime

There is a second rational control with at most 1+log_2 N designated resets through N, e_n=O(exp(−c n)) for some c>0, and the same Q_n at each step. It agrees at every 8b−7 prime injection. For any prime p first entering Q at b, its complete principal part at every time b≤n<2b agrees with U_n. Even so V_n=O(n^-2), all near-pair counts saturate at q=O(log A), and J/q→1. This control matches the original forcing to every finite asymptotic order, including at nonbirth times.

Write T_n=sum_(j>n)16^(n−j)r(j)>0, with T_n=O(n^-2) by the rational source bound and geometric summation. The identity T_(n−1)=(r(n)+T_n)/16 gives W_n=V_n+T_n=16W_(n−1)+e_n. At t=7·2^i, i≥0, let D=Q_floor(t/2) and H_t=sum_(j=t+1)^(3t)16^(t−j)r(j). Put e_t=−round(D(16V_(t−1)+r(t)+H_t))/D. Elsewhere set e_n=0. This uses only a finite rational evaluation of the declared source; it uses 2t terms of lookahead. The post-reset |W_t|≤1/(2D)+16^(-2t) T_(3t). By #492 AS-01, log D≥3t+o(t), hence |W_t|≤exp(−(3−ε)t) eventually for any ε>0. For t≤n<2t, |W_n|=16^(n−t)|W_t|. Choosing 0<ε<3−log 16 shows exponential smallness throughout every epoch as a function of n (n≤2t). At the next reset the pre-correction completed state has the same bound by one additional multiplication by 16. Thus e_n, the difference of pre- and post-correction values, decays exponentially, with dependence on the finite initial transient absorbed in the constant. V_n=−T_n+W_n gives the assertion.

Every designated t has 8t−7 divisible by 7 and greater than 7. For n<2b every earlier intervention denominator divides Q_floor(n/2), hence its denominator is prime to p. Consequently V_n−U_n is p-integral for every n<2b, as is the corresponding injection error. The statement compares actual principal parts, even if a prime undergoes reinjection; it assumes no unproved prime-gap property.

The price is explicit: at a reset the intervention may borrow an OLD denominator forbidden by the original individual forcing denominator. The exact per-step rational injection, even at one sparse set of nonbirth indices, is stronger than all asymptotic jets plus the authentic cumulative denominator. If an alternative injection also lies in the genuine (1/den(r(n)))Z and its absolute deviation is o(n^-4), then for large n it equals r(n) because den(r(n))=O(n^4). Sparse changes of complete input may change the completed linear sum: here summation of 16^(-n)(r(n)+e_n) telescopes to zero since V_n/16^n→0.

## Conditional transport of the missing carries

For rational forced trajectories with fractional lifts x_n={U_n}, y_n={V_n}, define the literal carry c_n=floor(16x_(n−1)+r(n)), and its counterpart d_n for r+e. Backward iteration of y_n−x_n=16(y_(n−1)−x_(n−1))+e_n−d_n+c_n gives, for every H≥1,

|y_n−x_n|≤16^(-H)+sum_(j=1)^H 16^(-j)|e_(n+j)|

whenever the next H literal carries agree. This identity explains which observable could defeat S1/S2. A source-specific bound on the frequency of carry words of length H≈log_16 q would access real occupancy. The constructions do not furnish that bound; counting matching carry words requires further arithmetic.

## The one-prime detection threshold

The 3-primary part of Q_n is at most 8n, since Q_n divides the lcm of all four original individual term denominators up to n. Therefore a perturbation in (1/Q_n)Z ∩ Z[1/3] having magnitude strictly below 1/(8n) must vanish. The polynomial exponent 1 is sharp for this recovery test even with sparse interventions: in S1 replace the gap by 3 max(1,floor(sqrt(log_16 t)/3)). It still tends to infinity, equals o(log t), and preserves the composite reset class. The same geometric estimate gives |V_n| ≤ n^(-1+o(1)) and the next correction |e_n| ≤ n^(-1+o(1)). The proof gives a zero-density support (O(N/sqrt(log N))) and the same loss of real dispersion. This concerns recovery of injections from omitted-prime data; knowing all but finitely many exact injections still leaves the completed initial contribution to analyze.

## S3. Arbitrarily late start, exponentially small first change, positive forcing

S2 as written may have a large first intervention. This stronger construction fixes that issue. Given any sufficiently large T=7·2^k, there is an altered zero-seeded recurrence agreeing with the original at every n<T, sharing S2's Q_n, birth injections and linear lifetimes. There exist constants C,c>0 **independent of T** such that |e_n|≤C exp(−cn) for every n≥T, while all e_n vanish before T. All altered injections can be taken strictly positive. The real-circle orbit still concentrates at zero, with ||V_n||=O(n^-2) for n≥3T. The exponential majorant permits any fixed 0<c<(3−log 16)/2.

Let alpha_m=sum_(j≤m)16^(-j)r(j), alpha=pi/16, and choose the dyadic rational
theta_T=16^(-3T) round(16^(3T) alpha_(4T)).
Then |alpha−theta_T|<16^(-3T) for all sufficiently large T since alpha−alpha_(4T)=16^(-4T)T_(4T). At t=T·2^i make the S2 finite-tail correction to the **moving target** 16^t theta_T, that is

e_t=−round(Q_floor(t/2) (16V_(t−1)+r(t)+H_t−16^t theta_T))/Q_floor(t/2).

Define W_n=V_n+T_n−16^n theta_T. Its pre-reset value at the FIRST reset now has magnitude ≤16^(-2T) since V was the original lift there before correction. After each reset |W_t|≤1/(2Q_floor(t/2))+16^(-2t)T_(3t), exactly as before. Uniformly for t<n≤2t before the next correction we have |W_n|≤16^(n−t) exp(−(3−ε)t) eventually, hence ≤exp(−(3−ε−log16)n/2). A correction equals post-reset minus pre-reset W. These bounds hold with uniform constants for all sufficiently large starting T. As r(n)~15/(64n²), every added correction is smaller in absolute value than r(n) for n≥T provided T is large. For n≥3T the moving target is an integer, and ||V_n||≤|T_n|+|W_n|. All other checks in S2 apply verbatim. The completed boundary is exactly the dyadic rational theta_T because 16^(-n)V_n→theta_T. Thus the mutation retains arbitrarily long exact prefixes, a uniform exponential envelope even on the first change, and all birth injections. The finite source evaluation used to select theta_T is lookahead; the construction makes no assertion of pointwise causality from past measurements alone.

For any fixed finite order k, the same uniform bound on e_n and its forward differences preserves the eventual sign of the first k successive finite differences of r (direct Laurent expansion). Choosing T sufficiently large also preserves each of these signs at the finitely many transitions across T since the genuine kth difference has magnitude asymptotic to a positive constant times T^(-k-2). This does not assert simultaneous complete monotonicity in all orders.

The complete reduced state conductor supplies a further ablation in S1. Both denominators divide Q_n and their prime-power factors agree at every p≠3. The ratio of the larger reduced denominator to the smaller is therefore at most 3^v3(Q_n)≤8n. Thus |log den(V_n)−log den(U_n)|≤log(8n) at **every** n. The same bound holds for a pair difference with n the later endpoint. In particular, accepting #497's original log den(U_n)=(6+o(1))n transfers the identical exponent 6 to this concentrated control. This last corollary is conditional only upon that inherited paper result; the uniform logarithmic comparison above uses elementary arithmetic alone.
