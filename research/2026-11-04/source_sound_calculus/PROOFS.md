## 1. What the calculus is allowed to claim

A typed judgement is conceptually

\[
\Gamma;\ S;\ \mathcal T;\ B\ \vdash e:\varphi\quad [\alpha],
\]

where `Γ` is a finite source-tagged hypothesis list, `S` a declared model or the exact original arithmetic source, `T` the available probe toolkit, `B` the finite budget, `e` a proof/witness tree, `φ` the exact conclusion, and `α` its surviving *authority/scope* label. There are three importantly distinct authorities:

- `MODEL_EXACT_NOT_EMPIRICAL`: all relevant finite states, readouts, actions or invariants checked **within a declared synthetic source**. It does not assert that the outside world instantiates that source.
- `EXACT_FINITE_ARITHMETIC_OR_LOGIC`: a closed derivation under registered exact finite arithmetic operations and restricted logical rules. A general mathematical theorem beyond this checked fragment requires separate proof authority.
- `CONDITIONAL`: the derivation retains **open assumptions**. No amount of repeated hypothetical inference upgrades it.

There is **no rule** allowing the text of a source ID to authenticate Nature, a simulated event to become an externally attested event, or a finite empirical prefix to become an unrestricted future theorem. External empirical attestation and actual formal-kernel trust must be provided by independent systems; this research interpreter does not fabricate either.

### The actual finite proof rules

`ASSUME`: only source-labelled hypotheses registered in Γ; emits an open dependency. `AND_I`/`AND_E`, `IMP_I` (discharging exactly the named Γ-hypothesis), and `MP` require exact source and proposition identity. Cross-source implications require a separately proved source morphism—not supplied by this calculus.

`MODEL_TRACE`: run an explicitly declared finite instrument word from an explicit candidate state. The output is a **simulated model trace**, not a physical observation. `MODEL_JOURNAL`: append the resulting source-labelled action/outcome records only from a checked trace. `MODEL_FILTER`: enumerate compatible initial states, without claiming which one Nature actually selected. `MODEL_REFINEMENT`: source-relative observational equivalence classes refine as toolkit/depth/budget expand (under explicit containment of instrument menus). `MODEL_INDUCT`: certify a predicate `P` for all words generated by specified finite-model instruments by checking (i) `P(s0)` and (ii) `P(s)⇒P(T_a(s))` for each model state and admitted action. This is **legitimate mathematical induction under the declared total source law**, not induction from finitely many *observations*.

`ARITH_LCM`, `ARITH_CYCLIC_GLUE`: introduce only exact positive-integer joins and finite cyclic-group join receipts; the latter exhaustively checks its finite image/kernel under a bounded budget. `BBP_ORIGIN`/`BBP_STEP`: construct the genuine **finite** four-pole zero-seeded BBP unwrapped register, integer numerator, growing odd LCM clock and literal carry without accepting arbitrary prior seed inputs. Every successor must carry a checked predecessor.

**Trust boundary:** All proof nodes are re-evaluated recursively from declared source models, exact arithmetic, and the closed rule table; proofs with malformed conclusions, unregistered rule tags, incompatible source IDs, forged instrument results, or unearned integer carries are rejected. This is a research interpreter verified by local tests, not a proof assistant's small formally verified kernel.

## 2. LCM as the universal **least common phase carrier**

In the divisibility poset on positive integers,
\[
\boxed{a\vee b=\operatorname{lcm}(a,b),\qquad a\wedge b=\gcd(a,b).}
\]
LCM is the **least** positive integer divisible by both: any common register `d` with a|d and b|d also has lcm(a,b)|d. Its prime-power valuation is `v_p(lcm(a,b))=max(v_p(a),v_p(b))`; the overlap is `v_p(gcd(a,b))=min(...)`. These are exact classical lattice laws, not experimentally determined facts.

For a positive integer `m`, let `C_m={x∈Q/Z : m x=0}` be its finite cyclic group of rational phases, of cardinality m. If m|L, `C_m↪C_L` is literally the inclusion of rational phases; on numerator addresses `u mod m`, the common-clock address is `u (L/m) mod L` (not the naïve residue `u mod L`). Thus `C_m+C_n=C_lcm(m,n)` and `C_m∩C_n=C_gcd(m,n)`.

### LC-01 — exact **overlap-and-join** theorem (classical, paper proved)

\[
\boxed{0\longrightarrow C_{\gcd(m,n)}\xrightarrow{\ z\mapsto(z,-z)\ }C_m\oplus C_n\xrightarrow{(x,y)\mapsto x+y} C_{\mathrm{lcm}(m,n)}\longrightarrow0.}
\]

*Proof.* Both finite subgroups are contained in `C_L` with `L=lcm(m,n)`. Their intersection is `C_gcd`, since a rational phase killed by both m and n is killed by gcd (Bézout), and every phase killed by gcd is killed by both. Their sum is a subgroup of `C_L` containing the two cyclic subgroups. Since it has `|C_m||C_n|/|C_gcd|=mn/gcd=L` elements, it is all `C_L`. The kernel of addition is the indicated opposite diagonal of the intersection. This also proves exactness and cardinalities. `cyclic_join_finite` checks the map explicitly for its declared finite budget (the paper proof is not restricted to that budget).

Example: `m=6`, `n=10` gives `gcd=2`, `lcm=30`, a two-element kernel and a 30-element image. **What is shared** is the torsion subgroup `C_2`, not a quantum entanglement entropy.

If `gcd(m,n)=1`, the map `C_m⊕C_n≅C_mn` is an isomorphism, compatible with CRT. For overlapping moduli the common information is the kernel; the exact sequence is one concrete categorical explanation of what LCM means as a join.

## 3. Source LCM is the minimal **group generated by genuine BBP injections**

Let the original forcing be exactly
\[
r(j)=\frac4{8j-7}-\frac2{8j-4}-\frac1{8j-3}-\frac1{8j-2}
=\frac{120j^2-89j+16}{(2j-1)(4j-1)(8j-7)(8j-3)}.
\]
Let `b_j=den(r(j))` after reduction and
\[
\boxed{Q_0=1,\quad Q_n=\operatorname{lcm}(Q_{n-1},b_n).}
\]
Since each `r(j)` reduced denominator is odd, each `Q_n` is odd. The subgroup generated by all source injections up to time n is **exactly**
\[
\boxed{\langle r(1),...,r(n)\rangle_{\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z}=C_{Q_n}.}\tag{LC-02}
\]
*Proof.* A rational phase with reduced denominator b has additive order b and generates `C_b`. Inductively join those subgroups by LC-01. Their sum is `C_lcm(b_1,...,b_n)`; no arbitrary new primes enter.

This is the exact *virtual arithmetic carrier* of all phases generated by the available injections. The **actually selected path** is merely *one element* of that carrier at each time. The group capacity is not a probability distribution over paths, and the LCM does not assert that every possible phase is realized by the zero-seeded dynamics.

### LC-03 — integer LCM successor and literal carry (paper proved)

Represent the original unwrapped state as `U_n=A_n/Q_n`, with `A_0=0,Q_0=1`. For `r(n)=a_n/b_n` in lowest terms and `Q_n=lcm(Q_(n-1),b_n)`, the exact update is
\[
\boxed{A_n=16\frac{Q_n}{Q_{n-1}}A_{n-1}+\frac{Q_n}{b_n}a_n.}\tag{LC-03}
\]
Its ordinary integer carry, with `R_(n-1)=A_(n-1) mod Q_(n-1)`, is
\[
\boxed{\kappa_n=\left\lfloor\frac{16(Q_n/Q_{n-1})R_{n-1}+(Q_n/b_n)a_n}{Q_n}\right\rfloor.}
\]
Substituting the exact fraction recurrence proves the identity directly, without dropping the unwrapped integer lift. The research implementation is independently coded and has a **hosted-checkout bridge test** against the existing SUCC `integer_bbp_carrier` and `bbp_natural_carrier`, which remain the canonical implementations. Full family induction is elementary; executable certificates assert only each checked finite prefix.

Because the clock is odd, `×16` is a bijection on every *fixed* `C_{Q_n}`, not a mixing operator or a source of dissipative entropy. The enlargement `C_{Q_n} ↪ C_{Q_(n+1)}` incorporates the next genuine injection; one selected zero-seed section is evaluated through those changing carrier spaces.

## 4. Why **zero seed ≠ maximal entanglement** (calibrated negative theorem)

`U_0=0` gives the distinguished zero phase and the **smallest possible initial denominator** `Q_0=1` for an unforced source. It prevents importing a nonzero initial phase or extra prime-support assumption. That is a useful *source-selecting boundary condition*, not an information-theoretic probability measure or a guarantee of unbiased future observations.

An exact example shows the required distinction. The same `C_6` clock admits:

- Uniform measure on all six residues: independent CRT coordinates in `C_2×C_3`.
- Point mass at zero: every coordinate is deterministic, hence mutually independent in the degenerate statistical sense (all variable entropies and mutual informations zero).
- Equal mass on phases `0` and `5/6`: the associated local pairs `(0,0)` and `(1,1)` are correlated, with nonzero mutual information.

All three measures share the **same arithmetic LCM=6**. Consequently an LCM clock does not choose a statistical coupling, let alone quantum entanglement. More source constraints or an explicit ensemble are required to define the latter. The exact-fraction `prior_independence_countermodels()` tests this claim without fitted floating-point entropy values.

The zero seed is **neutral with respect to adding an extra denominator**, not neutral with respect to the dynamics' eventual empirical phase frequencies: the actual forcing can introduce source-specific structure. No “all elements are maximally entangled” conclusion is valid without a precise state/measure and operational discriminator.
