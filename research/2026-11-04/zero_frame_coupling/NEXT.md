# One decisive next obligation: original carry-conditioned mixed correlation

Fix h!=0 and use the original critical H_A(h), Lambda_A. Set
`D_A=ceil(log^3 A)`. For each sharp interval I in [A,2A), define

\[
\mathcal K_A(I)=\frac1{D_A}\sum_{d=1}^{D_A-1}
(1-d/D_A)\Re\left[\frac1A\sum_{n,n+d\in I}
e\!\left(H_A[(16^d-1)x_n+S_d(n)]\right)\right],
\]

where x_n and S_d are the *actual* zero-seed rational state and original
chronological forcing sum. Empty pair sets contribute zero. Keep the literal
carry cells from [SIGNED_WEYL.md](SIGNED_WEYL.md) in any decomposition.

The prospective hypothesis is

\[
\boxed{\max_I\max(0,\mathcal K_A(I))=O_h(\log^{-3}A).}
\]

For |I|<D_A the trivial bound already gives `M<=D_A/A`. For larger I,
the displayed van der Corput inequality gives
`M_A^2 <= (1+D_A/A)[1/D_A+2 max_I max(0,K_A(I))]`.
The proposed bound would therefore prove
`Lambda_A M_A=O_h(log^-1/2 A)->0` for every fixed h and imply normality
through the inherited retrieval theorem. This is an explicit sufficient
mixed-correlation obligation, not a theorem obtained in this campaign.

epoch, its K_A tends to 1/2, since all d<=D_A have phase defect
`O(D_A Lambda_A/A)=o(1)`. Thus it fails the proposed hypothesis even though
the radial lag Gram bound holds. The 1/5 seed shares every lag character with
the original; it must share the outcome and is an authentication control only.
Normal wrong-seed examples defeating the ratchet cannot all satisfy this
stronger sufficient estimate. The ternary mutant must be rejected by the
actual injection/carry hypotheses, not by a denominator heuristic.
