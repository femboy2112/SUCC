1. **Reciprocal observer inversion.** For arbitrary finite complex data, the
   maximal sharp-interval norm and its reciprocal-weighted version satisfy
   `M<=3 M_rec` and `M_rec<=(8/3)M`. A whole-window Fourier zero will fail to
   imply either maximal norm vanishes. Exact rational coordinate vectors,
   monotone weight decompositions and an interior partial-sum adversary will
   check this; finite checks do not prove the universal norm inequality.
2. **Sharp weighted boundary survives every fixed smooth observer.** For any
   fixed nonzero C² weight w and fixed integer lag ell, the same-forcing
   coherent source U=-T, with unchanged endogenous radial Z and seed assigned
   to the angular channel, should satisfy
   `Lambda M_w(U+Delta_ell Z) ->
    32 max_[1,2](x³|w(x)|)/(pi |2ell+1|)`
   whenever Lambda->infinity and Lambda²/A->0. The coefficient 2ell+1 never
   vanishes for integer ell. The radial-relative counterpart should have
   constant `16 max x³|w|/(pi |ell|)` for ell!=0. These are paper claims,
   not claims about the original zero seed. Tuned full-window sinc zeros and
   compact-smooth full-window rapid decay are mandatory hostile comparisons.
3. **Ordinary infinite-shift reconstruction fails at the radial density zero.**
   A fixed convergent infinite future-difference stencil cannot reconstruct
   original forcing at all positive integer n: the Mellin radial density
   vanishes at t=2-sqrt(2), while the original density is positive. The exact
   issue is whether conditional series convergence suffices to justify the
   generating-symbol and Mellin interchange. Prove that step or leave the
   statement restricted to absolute convergence.
4. **Original coupling remains a live hypothesis.** None of the above excludes
   a source-selected correlation estimate using U0=0. A saving for original U
   is not inferred from a radial-only response, fixed-h Abel limit, finite jet,
   or positive observer change.
