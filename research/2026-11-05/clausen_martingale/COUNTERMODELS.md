# Countermodels: why categorical randomized arrows do not settle selected π

**Scope:** genuine original SUCC source remains untouched. These are scoped falsifiers for logical inferences, not refutations of π normality.

1. **Uniform marginal ≠ uniform digit blocks.** Gaussian AR(1) with rho=1/2 has stationary N(0,1) and uniform probability-integral-transform marginals. But consecutive threshold-high hexadecimal digits have probability 1/3, not the 1/4 required by hexadecimal normality. Its stationary digit process is ergodic but not base-16 normal, with at least 0.056633 nats of adjacent digit information.

2. **Haar-typical seed ≠ selected zero seed.** The conditional Fekete martingale has zero means for all K≤A and strong source-independent cross-boundary cancellation. The unforced zero orbit is fixed at phase zero, and its selected determinant has logarithm -infinity while the Haar-seed ensemble variance is O(A^-2).

3. **A^(2/3) threshold is genuinely sharp for the generic class.** At K=A+floor(c A^(2/3)), the unforced zero-cylinder conditional energy tends to -(log16/3)c^3. Therefore a universal o(1) conditional-mean bound farther into K cannot hold under the available hypotheses.

4. **Original BBP conditional pressure is NOT monotone.** At preregistered A=7,11,13, the past-past Clausen signed contribution changes sign as K increases. Neither causal pointing nor introducing a new condition necessarily increases any proposed measure of equidistribution.

5. **The cyclotomic Clausen primitive does not imply positive regulator values.** It yields exact differences of dilogarithms at source-derived roots of unity. Their signs depend on the selected numerators/carries. Availability of a closed form does not prove a global lower bound.

6. **The same original four-pole forcing with coherent wrong seed.** For s*=-pi/16, U_n(s*)=-T_n tends to zero. All source-uniform cut bounds hold but the Fekete exclusion energy of the selected epoch diverges negatively. A theorem using only forcing but not actual seed cannot certify π normality.

7. **Another rational seed is not a free proof shortcut.** Wall's theorem makes normality invariant under rational addition. Therefore proving normality for ANY original-forcing rational seed is equivalent to proving it at zero. Terminating hexadecimal seeds produce literally identical eventual circle orbits; other rational seeds supply periodic twists.

8. **Causal generation is not statistical time asymmetry.** A stationary Gaussian forward Markov chain with symmetric Gaussian covariance is reversible in law. Conditioning and causal realization are distinct mathematical operations, and no physical Casimir mechanism has been inferred.

9. **Fekete lower bound is stronger than necessary.** Weyl normality permits sparse exact duplicates or ultra-close pairs, while the full Vandermonde determinant becomes zero. The source-selected lower bound is an optional sufficient route, not an equivalent formulation.

**Verdict:** the remaining obstruction lies in authenticated earlier-pair Archimedean phases, not in the universal future-crossing field. A new original-source inequality must address the literal zero-seed historical carry and remain incompatible with the coherent wrong seed.
