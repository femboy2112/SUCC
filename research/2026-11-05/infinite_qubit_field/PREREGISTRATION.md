Can this be generalized to entangled groups of arbitrarily many two-level components, **all compatible as restrictions of ONE infinite global state**, while the genuine SUCC BBP angular/radial phases and dynamics are retained?

In the quasilocal limit every fixed finite proper subsystem forgets θ. Thus naïve infinite GHZ coherence is unavailable to all local measurements.
- Finite linear graph/cluster state \(|C_N\rangle=\prod_{j=0}^{N-2}CZ_{j,j+1}|+\rangle^{\otimes N}\) has local stabilizers \(K_0=X_0Z_1,\ K_j=Z_{j-1}X_jZ_{j+1}\) (j>=1 in the semi-infinite limit), contiguous-cut Schmidt rank 2 and entanglement entropy one bit. - A single local Z rotation on site 0 with angle θ gives the local complex phase witness \(\omega_\theta((X_0+iY_0)Z_1)=e^{iθ}\). For two independently source-labelled rotations at even sites 0 and 2, the 3-body witness \(B_2=(X_0+iY_0)(X_2+iY_2)Z_3\) gives \(e^{i(θ_0+θ_1)}\). These identities were calibrated for unrotated N=4,5,6 and a 2-rotation N=4 example BEFORE preregistration, hence no holdout claim for these cases.

**IQ-03**: For each source time n, integer h and arbitrarily many finite source-labelled angles θ_(j,n) on even sites, define \(\omega_{\theta,n}\) by compatible local rotations of \(\omega_C\). For m>=1, let
\[
B_m=\left(\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}(X_{2j}+iY_{2j})\right)Z_{2m-1}.
