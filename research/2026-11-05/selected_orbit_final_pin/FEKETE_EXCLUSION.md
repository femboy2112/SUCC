# Regularize before paying the selected sign

The singular determinant Gate C is stronger than normality and retains its close-pair debt. This round does not extend #501 microscopic exclusions to a full multiscale determinant lower bound.

[Delta RC3–RC4](front_delta/PROOFS.md) replace log|1-e(t)|^2 by g_r(t)=log|1-r e(t)|^2 at one fixed 0<r<1. With c_A(h)=A^-1 sum e(hU_n), the exact energy identity is

    E_(A,r) = -sum_(h>=1) r^h |c_A(h)|^2/h - log(1-r)/A.

Therefore E_(A,r)->0 is equivalent to every fixed-h Weyl mean vanishing. Sparse exact collisions destroy the singular determinant but leave this criterion intact. This is a strict weakening of the singular Fekete route, not a new bound on the selected orbit. No algebraic norm of all conjugates is used to control the selected embedding.
