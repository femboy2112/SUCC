# Hostile controls: source point ≠ source-time distribution

2. **A source-covariant circle state is not a Haar state.** The original source diagram paired/really/circle character commuting equations are true after any homogeneous seed change \(U_n(s)=U_n(0)+16^ns\). 3. **Asymptotic ×16 invariance is insufficient.** Both Haar and δ0, δ1/15 are invariant; so are uncountably many biased Bernoulli digit measures. The original source enforces only invariance of subsequential measures without a source-specific regularity theorem.

4. **Finite loose collision bound does not force Haar without invariance.** An empirical half-circle grid has P_m=2 for m=1,2 when sampled with N=16^m points, and its limiting measure has L² density 2 on half the circle. But that measure is not invariant under T(x)=16x mod1. Source dynamics are essential to the combined theorem.

6. **Finite observer refinement does not commute with infinite source time.** A finite N sample remains atomic, and its histogram pressures diverge with m→∞. 9. **Original sphere/odd-torsion injection does not imply normality.** The #515/#516 deck/CP¹ observers encode the same source phases, but an injective geometric observation of rational roots can carry nonuniform measures. Their unbounded inverse distortion/weak-limit caveats remain.

A generic observer theorem must not silently inherit arithmetic truth from a mutated source.

For every fixed observer depth m the limiting cell pressure remains 1, so the new all-fixed-m gate is immune to this particular false negative.

These are scoped falsifications of invalid proof routes, not refutations of π normality.
