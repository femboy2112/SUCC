# The source-selected arithmetic gate: bounded 16-adic observational collision pressure

**Primary target:** exact authentic zero-seeded original \(U_{n+1}=16U_n+r_o(n+1)\), full unwrapped integer carries, and the 16^m equal circle cells. Prove there EXISTS a finite C independent of m such that
\[
\boxed{\forall m\ge1,\quad
\limsup_{A\to\infty}
\frac{16^m}{A^2}\sum_{k=0}^{16^m-1}
\#\{A\le n<2A:U_n\bmod1\in I_{m,k}\}^{\,2}
\le C.}\tag{OPEN, desired}
\]
The corresponding prefix version is equivalent as a source-normality criterion. This is an UNSIGNED all-radix-depth upper collision condition. A near-maximal Vandermonde determinant, uniform all-scale logarithmic energy, or chosen high-depth critical chirp rate are NOT REQUIRED by this sufficient criterion. The source's asymptotic ×16 invariance plus any finite uniform C forces every empirical limit to be absolutely continuous, hence Haar.

**Why it may be productive:** Unlike #513's logarithmic Slater criterion, this tolerates sparse exact repeated points and only requires a crude upper pressure constant, not the optimal value1. But under the source-recurrence constraints this is logically equivalent to normality as an infinite limit condition—do not disguise the unpaid source estimate.
