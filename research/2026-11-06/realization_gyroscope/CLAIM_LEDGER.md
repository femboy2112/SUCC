## Steelman → crucible → verdict

**Steelman.** Radix-16 is a particular observer's arithmetic representation, not an ontological requirement. A real observer must represent its own support, chosen instruments, actions and induced future path in the same carrier it is trying to understand. Information discarded by marginalization can live in a mathematically precise joint/conditional object. This yields the correct research problem: show a carrier-selected theorem is preserved by a self-realized observational fibration.

**Crucible.**

1. **Wrong source:** mutate one pole-derived source value by `1/37`; the predicted model state no longer equals the physical world after even the first step. Exact equality is falsifiable.
2. **Wrong completed seed, same source:** formal `s=1/5-π/16` remains internally coherent but is non-normal in both bases. Closed-loop coherence is not enough.
3. **False Haar from policy:** constant world plus irrational staircase action has an exactly Haar-generic observed marginal despite only `O(√N)` chart switches. Dynamic consistency and slow chart motion are not enough.
4. **Joint decoder recovers truth:** the same false-Haar marginal has `y=a`, so the joint Fourier witness is 1, rejecting Haar at every nonzero frequency.
5. **Coarse nonclosure:** inputs `x=0` and `x=1/32` have the same first hex digit 0, but after the same first original source step their first hex digits differ. A coarse state cannot silently be treated as a lossless observer.
6. **Marginal blindness:** the 31×31 product grid and 31-point diagonal grid have identical one-coordinate marginals, but `e(x−y)` has mean 0 versus 1. Joint correlations carry the missing information.
7. **Base-2 recoding:** four phase slots interleave exactly for every rational β tested. Does not infer distribution in the selected π orbit.
8. **Run scope:** focused local tests + separate exact oracle only, no native full SUCC suite, Lean/CI or external refereeing.

**Verdict.** The recovered joint action/observation object and two-connection bound rigorously correct a missing support condition in the observation architecture. They also sharpen the firewall: normality *is* invariant under legitimate fixed radix-power recoding, while it can be fabricated by a time-varying **unlogged observation chart**. A logged policy makes fabrication detectable. None of this proves the source-selected nontrivial signed cancellation needed for π-normality.
