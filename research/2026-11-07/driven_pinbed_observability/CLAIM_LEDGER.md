## Dominance and provenance boundary

The new frame strictly repairs the missing-mode and conditioning obstruction
of a single rectangular aperture under the declared full-profile access.
It adds quantitative H1 and occupancy targets, source-bound acquisition, and
a bounded explicit affine model. PR541's B2 bed still has a better general
point-translation/source-norm modulus. No single bed dominates every task.

The agents and root share a model family. More informative diversity comes
from endpoint algebra versus Fourier multipliers, direct finite masks versus
quadrature formulas, rational cyclotomic samples versus analytic alias tails,
and the four-fraction recurrence versus the integer source engine. Root replay
of the independent programs is recorded in VALIDATION.md.
