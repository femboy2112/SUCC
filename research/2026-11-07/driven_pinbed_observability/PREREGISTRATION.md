# Prospective local protocol

Written before the new exact drive-protocol experiments. This is a local
prospective record, not an external timestamp or blinded experiment.

## Hypotheses and decision rules

- For every finite clock m>=2 and proper window 1<=L<m, the unnormalized
  undriven and tone-one translated profiles admit the stated adjacent-profile
  inverse. Four nonnegative phase-cycle readings recover those channels.
- Source, protocol and phase labels must agree before the decoder combines
  readings. Mixing distinct source snapshots must be rejected even if a visual
  aggregate looks reasonable. A rebuilt authentic BBP snapshot must match its
  source trace and binned masses exactly.
- The continuum inverse, frame, range, dynamic and source-tail claims rest on
  proofs. Finite computations check formulas and implementation only.

## Fresh finite regions

- Calibration clocks m=4,6,8 with every proper L; recover every point mass and
  a nonuniform probability. Recover an alias under the static m=6,L=3 bed.
- Holdout clocks m=7,9,11,13 and L=1, floor(m/2),m-1; nonuniform rational
  probabilities formed from weights (j+1)^2. Not used to fit constants.
- Original BBP epochs A=19,37,73,137; observation clocks m=8,12,16 and proper
  windows near one half. Preserve U0=0 and all four signed source poles.
  Keep overlapping source-prefix counts explicit. These are finite authentic
  measurements, not a test of asymptotic normality.
- False controls: mixed snapshot, changed protocol, duplicate/missing phase,
  altered gain or phase, phase-erased clock, negative reconstructed probability,
  an incoherent two-channel pair, changed BBP seed/source trace, full aperture.

## Independent bearings

An independent exact Gaussian-rational oracle will enumerate source/reference
histories on a four-site carrier. A separate B2 scan oracle will compare an
analytic alias identity against rational sampled surfaces and calibrated
elementary transcendental bounds. Their exact enumerations are recorded in their
own receipts before any universal conclusion is drawn.

## Pass / fail / ambiguous

An exact mismatch refutes the implementation or the corresponding formula.
A mutated invalid certificate accepted by the decoder fails calibration.
Numerical agreement without a proven enclosure is display-only. A source
estimate that changes the seed, discards clock phase or assumes the desired
Weyl cancellation is inadmissible as a proof of normality. Pi base-16 normality
and the source-selected all-epoch decay estimates remain OPEN.
