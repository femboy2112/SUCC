## Corrections that materially affect the claims

1. A recovered positive law is insufficient for a two-channel representation.
   Channel coherence is checked separately; an h=1 ghost has positive Haar
   inverse but violates realization.
2. Real probability-law differences use the symmetrized Fourier weight.
   An arbitrary one-sided complex mode does not prove the sharp real-law norm.
3. The surface identity operator is an identity on the compatible range.
   On arbitrary L2 channel pairs it is a projection. The categorical domain is
   declared accordingly.
4. Two quadratures are a sufficient clean construction, not universally
   necessary under full translated access. Static plus a real cosine channel
   can already restore all rectangular blind modes.
5. A fixed unknown gain/phase can self-calibrate from known source mass.
   Spatially varying phase can instead produce another completely realizable
   law; an explicit G4 source substitution is preserved.
6. A four-phase cycle over a changing source is not a fixed-law measurement.
   The decoder binds source snapshots; the sequential alternative conditions
   on the actual source and declares its independent reference sampling.
7. The interval discrepancy bound is against Haar (or a bounded-density
   reference), not an arbitrary atomic reference. Nearby atoms refute the
   unrestricted statement.
8. Rectangular driven profiles have a square-root point-translation modulus.
   Their O(A^-2) energy transport does not become an O(A^-2) norm estimate.
9. Vanishing unreferenced scan average is a universal B2 sampling identity.
   A locked atom is a sharp false-uniformity control; signed demodulation is
   the source-mode measurement.
10. The wrong-seed scan control uses one fixed rational seed, K=154, across
    all its source epochs. It is not retuned for each epoch, and fails U0=0.
