## Independence and dominance boundary

The root derivation, constructor, Fourier specialist and adversary share the
same base model family and classical mathematical sources. Their agreement is
not four independent witnesses. More useful diversity comes from exact spatial
integration versus Fourier expansion, dual subgroup closure versus transition
partition refinement, and the authentic integer engine versus the four signed
fractions. Root replayed the independent programs. None is formal verification.

Compared with an unspecified pin analogy, this frame adds realization,
naturality, explicit error, a minimal completion and falsifiers. Compared with
SUCC's earlier target-descent and characteristic-kernel work, its added
dependency edge is **controlled dynamical transport of the chosen observation
geometry**. It does not dominate prior golden-aperture work on every task:
different beds trade spatial regularity, frequency sensitivity and dynamics.
