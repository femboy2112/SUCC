lawful source dynamics, and target-relative truth certificates. Accept the exact

## Hole contract

- Proposed statement: Fourier pin observations admit a constructible completion
  whose equivalence relation is preserved by every declared affine source step,
  together with quantitative error and finite-truncation certificates.
- Current state at registration: elementary candidate proofs; implementations
  and fresh source probes not yet run.
- Domain: Borel probability laws on the circle, affine maps x -> bx+a with
  integer b>=2, and symmetric nonnegative summable Fourier weights. State pins,
  moments of laws, and empirical histories are distinct types.
- Weak sufficient form: add only future pullbacks of the chosen pins; global
  injectivity is optional. An all-positive bed gives full law identification.
- Missing edge closed if successful: static observer agreement -> autonomous,
  quantitatively controlled internal law dynamics. This is an instrument/proof
  transport result, not a source-selected equidistribution estimate.
- Novelty tax: target descent, finite behavioral closure, characteristic kernels,
  and BBP circle reconstruction already exist on main. The intended addition is
  the sharp weight-ratio stability criterion, discounted future completion,
  least stable majorant, and continuous rationally evaluable surface with an
  explicit realization inverse. Classical mathematics; priority unverified.
- Known controls: equal first moments need not mean equal laws; a static blur
  need not predict its successor; a characteristic aperture need not have a
  bounded one-step Lipschitz constant; no finite source experiment proves an
  infinite decay estimate.
- Mathematical boundary: original BBP zero seed and four signed poles remain
  fixed; no proof contracts, archival imports, formal files, or main edits.

integration and Fourier/Bernoulli energy formulas, calibrated positive and
