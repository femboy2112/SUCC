# Proof and implementation audit

## Independent bearings and root synthesis

Three bounded assignments were run: a categorical constructor, a Fourier
specialist, and an adversary. They initially worked without one another's
conclusions. The root checked their claims against the existing main frontier,
primary sources, explicit algebra, and executable controls. These are same-model
agents with shared mathematical provenance, not independent academic referees.

The useful differences in method were:

- Curvature reconstruction and Fourier reconstruction of the continuous surface.
- Bernoulli Fourier covariance versus direct integration of piecewise rational
  quadratics, split at their actual seams.
- Dual character closure versus direct finite transition partition refinement.
- Original integer conductor engine versus independent four-signed-fraction
  unwrapped BBP recurrence.
- Ambient operator estimates versus sharpness witnessed by genuine probability
  laws, including finite grid laws.

## Changes made after adversarial review

1. Probability-law moments are distinguished explicitly from point-state pins.
   A single circle character already gives an exact nonlinear point model;
   absence of finite linear moment closure does not refute that model.
2. The generic law dynamics retain a common source time. BBP empirical epochs
   mix times, so their transfer uses the pointwise authentic tail, not a single
   falsely averaged forcing translation.
3. The quadratic pin profile is called continuous, Lipschitz and piecewise
   quadratic. It has a derivative jump and is not described as smooth or C1.
4. The probability mass pin at zero is explicitly included in finite spectral
   support, even when the profile itself has zero mean.
5. TRUE_COMPATIBLE remains a positive output when the original target is
   ambiguous. Both witnesses and both source/target fingerprints are retained.
6. The discounted sum completion and the least maximum-envelope majorant are
   separated. Only the latter is called minimal.
7. The completion's precision cost is quantified, including the necessary loss
   of a Lipschitz point-translation modulus at rho*b²>=1. The base B2 source-tail
   estimate is not silently transferred to a differently weighted metric.
8. Golden-aperture instability is stated as failure of a global Lipschitz bound,
   not failure of continuity or of PR #540's specific source estimates.
9. `FuturePinBed` now rejects malformed pair shapes consistently; source trace
   verification describes exact canonical state values, not file-byte hashing.
   Separate receipt hashes bind the actual implementation files.

Final read-only mathematical and implementation reviews found no blocking
error. This is the scope of their audit, not a theorem that no error can exist.

## Replayed exact controls

The root replayed both committed independent programs:

- `independent_surface_oracle.py`: 2,722 exact equalities/mutation rejections.
  Its primary integration code does not use Fourier inversion or B4. It rejects
  eight deliberately wrong formulas, including missing scale, wrong offset,
  wrong covariance sign and a mutated BBP pole.
- `independent_f2_oracle.py`: 28,864 affine models on F2²/F2³, comparing two
  separately implemented equivalence algorithms for every pair of states.

The repository tests additionally cover actual module APIs, sharp finite-law
expansion, composition, incomplete-but-autonomous observers, all-future sampled
Haar aliases, off-ray invisibility, exact discount tails, and changed source
seeds. The source probe checks all 468 executed recurrence steps across four
overlapping fresh prefixes, and compares pair and prefix-moment energies on the
two smaller epochs. The count includes repeated prefixes and is labelled so.

All-parameter claims rest on the written proofs. Finite computation checks the
implementations and falsifiers within its enumerated domain. Source-specific
asymptotic energy decay and pi normality remain OPEN.
