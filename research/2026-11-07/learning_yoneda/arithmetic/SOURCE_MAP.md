The new AR1 theorem depends only on the literal four-pole source, its elementary
positive finite tail bound, rational recurrence, floors, and finite-place
arithmetic. It does not depend on the 2-adic torsion-log theorem, PNT/AP,
irrationality measures, a Gamma identity, an unproved normality premise, or
numerical pi digits. The primary algorithm reuses main's `selected_orbit.original_forcing` and keeps
unwrapped `Fraction` states. An independent test evaluates the four literal
rational pole fractions. The independent finite oracle uses the combined
numerator `120n^2-89n+16` and common-denominator integer evolution. The main
`SelectedOrbit` projection is also checked against the unwrapped states. Inputs
fail closed: integer chronology, exact rational precision/seed, and distinct
genuine odd primes are required; floats, bools, duplicates and composites fail.

Provenance dependence: parent source contracts are shared; the proof and two
implementations were produced in one model family. Distinct representations are
useful error checks, not independent external scholarly verification. Root proof
review led to a stronger geometric perturbation bound and the explicit complete
prime-support exclusion boundary. No Lean certificate is claimed here.
