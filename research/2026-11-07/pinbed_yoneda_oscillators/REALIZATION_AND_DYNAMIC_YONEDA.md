## RY-1 — A complete algebraic criterion for legitimate paired pin surfaces (PROVED LOCAL)

Retain the normalized modulated arcs \(g_{a,k}=a^{-1}1_{[0,a)}e^{2\pi i kt}\), k=0,1, with \(0<a<1\). Their Fourier coefficients are

\[
G_a(r)=\int_0^a e^{-2\pi i r t}dt/a
=e^{-\pi i r a}\operatorname{sinc}(\pi r a),\quad r\in\mathbb Z.
\]

Define \(g_h=(G_a(h),G_a(h-1))\in\mathbb C^2\), \(w_h=\|g_h\|^2>0\). For a probability μ, let the **centered two-channel surface** \(F(\mu)=(g_{a,0}*(\mu-\lambda),g_{a,1}*(\mu-\lambda))\in L^2(\mathbb T;\mathbb C^2)\). Its Fourier coefficient vector obeys

\[
\widehat F(\mu)(0)=0,\qquad \widehat F(\mu)(h)=g_h\,m_h,\quad h\ne0,
\]
where \(m_h=\int e^{-2\pi i h x}d\mu(x)\), \(m_0=1\).

**Necessary exact cross-channel syzygy:** for every nonzero h,
\[
\boxed{
\Delta_h(F):=G_a(h-1)\widehat F_0(h)-G_a(h)\widehat F_1(h)=0.
}
\]

It enforces that the two complex readings are generated by the **same single carrier Fourier coefficient**. It is not automatic for two separately invented pin surfaces. Since w_h>0, define the unambiguous candidate coefficient

\[
\boxed{
m_h(F)=\frac{\overline{G_a(h)}\widehat F_0(h)+
\overline{G_a(h-1)}\widehat F_1(h)}{w_h},\quad h\ne0;\quad m_0(F)=1.
}
\]

**Full mathematical realization theorem.** A pair \(F_0,F_1\in L^2(\mathbb T)\) is the two-tone centered surface of a **genuine Borel probability** μ **if and only if**:
1. both F modes have zero mean (\(\widehat F(0)=0\));
2. every cross-channel syzygy \(\Delta_h(F)=0\);
3. the recovered bi-infinite moment sequence \(m_h(F)\) is positive-definite: for every finite list \(c_0,\ldots,c_{N-1}\in\mathbb C\), \(\sum_{j,k=0}^{N-1}\overline{c_j}c_k m_{j-k}\ge0\) (with the proper sign convention fixed by the Fourier transform).

**Proof.** Necessity: convolution gives the rank-one Fourier formula, and \(\sum\overline{c_j}c_k m_{j-k}=\int|\sum_j c_j e^{2\pi i jx}|^2d\mu(x)\ge0\), with conjugation adjusted to the declared Fourier sign. Sufficiency does not require assuming the answer: positive definiteness gives nonnegative Fejér densities \(\rho_N(x)=\frac1N\sum_{j,k=0}^{N-1}m_{j-k}e^{2\pi i(k-j)x}\), which integrate to 1. Probability measures \(\rho_N(x)\,dx\) have a weakly convergent subsequence by compactness; their fixed Fourier coefficients converge to m_h because the Fejér weight is \(1-|h|/N\). The resulting probability μ has Fourier sequence m_h, and uniqueness follows from density of trigonometric polynomials. The syzygies imply \(\widehat F(h)=g_h m_h\) for every h≠0, and zero means handle h=0, so the two L² surfaces agree. Uniqueness follows also from w_h>0. The Fejér construction is classical Fourier–Herglotz; this is an explicit observer realization test, not a claim of a new abstract theorem.
