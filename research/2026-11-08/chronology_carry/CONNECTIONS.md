# What the carry observer connects, and what it leaves unpaid

## Existing SUCC proofs, read rather than inferred from their titles

1. [The prior signed clock quotient](../frontier_connections/crucible/THEOREMS.md)
   identifies cancellations within arithmetic polynomial orbits. Its QCO5
   rank-one theorem still rules out calling a canonical fit of the target an
   independent estimate. Our frozen predictor avoids fitting the later target,
   but its residual must be charged. The ancestor-reading construction openly
   proves an approximate equivalence with that target, rather than concealing it.
2. [Signed covariance round two](../signed_covariance_round2/lag_instrument.py)
   already supplied the correct padded-window identity. We retain its endpoints
   and signed cross terms, insert a causal control variate, and independently
   verify its normalization by exact Gaussian-rational pair expansion.
3. [History-shell proofs](../../2026-11-05/history_shell/PROOFS.md) distinguish
   an ensemble of unknown seeds from one authenticated deterministic seed. HCP3
   gives an explicit late-forcing ensemble prediction floor; CCV5 separately
   assumes a normal selected trajectory. Neither transports ensemble typicality
   to zero. Knowing the full source plus unlimited replay is a singleton, so
   memory lower bounds refer to declared observation fibers or model classes.
4. [Selected-branch arithmetic](../../../observational_succ/selected_branch_arithmetic.py)
   and its [source proof](../../2026-10-28/CARRY_POLYA_SOURCE_LOCKED_SUMMARY.md)
   give an unconditional sparse carry16 budget. CCV6 pays its cost if an observer
   projects literal carries modulo16. CCV1-L instead retains the literal symbol16
   as a transient state of the reference kernel and avoids that projection loss.
   This is a concrete benefit of keeping the authenticated carrier large enough.
5. [Two-point moment memory](../frontier_connections/observer/THEOREMS.md)
   supplies the finite geometry behind FR1--3: a nonzero mean of two unit phases
   determines their unordered pair. Combined with actual time evolution and
   Haar marginals, it excludes a finite-range folded approximation of vanishing
   L1 error. Normality and infinitely many q2 clocks are explicit hypotheses.
6. The finite Yoneda/history discipline is respected: for a fixed ancestral
   time, the full carry prefix and forcing define a belief fiber by imposing
   every retained fractional-state constraint. Appending a carry intersects
   that fiber with another constraint. Moving the ancestral time is a different
   target and does not assert nested intervals. The source signature and forcing
   correction accompany each map. No incompatible new category class is introduced.

## Classical context, checked against primary records

Finite-state predictability is closely connected with normality. Bienvenu,
Gimbert and Pulari extend the Agafonov and Schnorr--Stimm results to probabilistic
automata in their [FSTTCS 2025 paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSTTCS.2025.16).
That supports the choice to audit learning and normality together; it supplies
no missing BBP arithmetic estimate. CCV5 is proved locally for the particular
circle-phase loss and authentic forcing, without importing the automata theorem.

The reference-kernel decomposition resembles a martingale/coboundary method,
whose classical source is [Gordin's 1969 paper](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/dan34921).
Here P is an explicitly stipulated finite operator. Its innovation is a measured
deterministic carry contrast. No stationary law, zero conditional expectation,
martingale theorem, central limit theorem or almost-sure statement for seed zero
is silently assumed. The finite Poisson identity is proved by telescoping.

## Source, toolkit and independence

The exact source map is the four signed poles and U0=0. Models can agree under
the current toolkit while having different seeds; their agreement does not
authenticate the source. In the Aletheia terminology, contact, observational
indistinguishability, source correctness and commitment are separate coordinates.
Our enthusiastic target does not strengthen any inequality.

Three agent fronts and root share a model family, repository and inherited
mathematical inputs. Different proof architectures (finite arc counting versus
compact invariant measures), arithmetic representations (common-denominator
recurrence versus bounded dyadic modular sums), and phase evaluators (Taylor
rectangles versus a separate interval library) give useful cross-checks. They
are not four independent external referees. The later source clock is a fresh
declared window of the same deterministic source, not an independent random draw.

The observer can now retain a small, source-authenticated representation of an
ancestor and transport it to an explicit signed carry statistic. Proving that
statistic decays at the original zero source is the remaining task. Uniform
convergence of an observation instrument or exact finite truth descent does not
establish its arithmetic time average.
