# Source-cocycle restrictions on a retained resonance model

A single low-degree nonlinear unit chirp cannot approximate the authentic
folded complement arbitrarily well. Its L1 error has the explicit floor in
[CR2](THEOREMS.md), asymptotically at least 1/289 when its arithmetic budget
vanishes. This supplies a constraint on approximation rather than assuming
approximation from the Weil bound.

The surviving affine models must have common anchors approaching fifteenth
roots of unity. The inverse slope -h survives and carries the actual signed
Weyl signal. Same-forcing completed orbits demonstrate that this exception
is necessary; rational matched-source controls reproduce it on finite epochs.
**No original-zero retained resonance decay is proved.**
