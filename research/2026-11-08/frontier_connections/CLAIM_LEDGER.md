## Failed attempts retained

1. Enlarging monomials to all mixed quadratics did not lower the original
   atomwise cost sufficiently at the tested clock. Both successful scans
   (before and after input-guard changes) are retained, with identical
   mathematical payloads.
2. Canonical Fourier orbit compression looked dramatically cheaper, but
   QCO4–5 shows its invariant outputs are scalar multiples of W. It cannot
   be treated as an independent decay premise.
3. Global polynomial moments and unlabeled intensity geometry lose the
   source-selected coefficient. Exact amplitude and phase-twist controls
   preserve those observations while changing the target.
4. The initial generic one-bit chronology wording was too strong for a
   deterministic known source. It is now explicitly toolkit/fiber-relative;
   unlimited replay requires no added information bit.
5. The initial normal-seed wording did not force alpha!=pi/16. Choosing the
   first digit0 fixes that point without changing the construction or proof.
