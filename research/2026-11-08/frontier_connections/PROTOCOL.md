methods can improve the actual route from original-zero signed covariance
to Weyl decay. No asymptotic source estimate is presumed.

Four independently assigned fronts, sharing model family and repository:

1. Root: a sharp two-replica energy floor via an explicit trigonometric
   coboundary inequality, and synthesis/audit of the other fronts.
2. Arithmetic: the complete mixed-quadratic dictionary, exact finite-field
   moment identities, and a new fixed-point instrument. Its separate
   [protocol](arithmetic/PROTOCOL.md) fixes the existing source data and
   coefficient menu before any mixed-source measurements.
3. Observer: equal-weight conditional laws, Newton moment reconstruction,
   arithmetic nondegeneracy and the history information needed for transport.
4. Crucible: archived coherent-zero/coboundary certificates and a possible
   orbit-grouped arithmetic budget that preserves signed cancellation.

Root's conjectural finite target, to prove or refute: for even integer b>=2,
`b cos(theta)+cos(b theta) >= -(b+1) cos(pi/(b+1))`.
Its equality candidates are theta=pi +/- pi/(b+1). For b=16 this could give
the sharp invariant two-replica energy floor `sin(pi/34)^2`; finite source
transport must separately pay endpoint and forcing errors. Tests: exact
critical-point enumeration for even b=2,4,6,16,32; fresh grid checks; odd-base
negative control; exact original source differences on existing A=1103,2203
windows. Numerical success is not the proof. Existing source data are reused,
so these windows are not new source holdouts.
