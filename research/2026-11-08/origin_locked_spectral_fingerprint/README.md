## Steelman

A realized source path should be identified through its **pointed, time-labelled spectral response**. The origin and its relative transport must not be lost by passage to an unmarked prime or circle phase. A stratified family of digit/harmonic observations is a fingerprint: normality is not decided by any finite layer, and the fingerprint need not uniquely authenticate the forcing if distinct sources represent the same completed constant.

## Strongest outcomes

1. **OSF-01–02 (exact)**: `16^-n(U_n(s)+T_n)−s=π/16` for every real seed. The seed acts on Fourier mode `(n,h)` by the character `e(h16^n q)`. Tail-only observations retain exactly `16^N` initial-seed aliases once history before time N is discarded; observing the pointed origin removes them. Exact rational split certificates reconstruct the same π invariant along any finite history/future partition.
2. **OSF-03–04 (exact + classical Wall theorem)**: any *rational* seed translation has finitely many temporal DFT sidebands and its clock-stratified spectral **power is exactly invariant on every eligible finite epoch**. For `q=1/17`, there are two lines `cos(2πh/17)` and `i sin(2πh/17)`; rational translations preserve base-16 normality, while arbitrary real translations do not.
3. **OSF-05 (classical / inherited SUCC valve)**: all digit-depth characters form a complete finite Fourier fingerprint. Incremental spectral power is a nonnegative sum of squared conditional digit defects. A 16-periodic simply-normal word has `E_1=0` but `E_2=15` and a maximal two-digit line: an adversarial example of why one stratum never suffices.
4. **OSF-06–07 (exact cohomology + new-to-this-repo assembly)**: every decaying rational source representation with the same completed constant belongs to a vanishing coboundary class. Construct an explicit second, everywhere **positive**, **odd-denominator** rational forcing with the **same π/16**, whose real time orbit differs by `g_n=n/[257(2n+1)^3]`. Every fixed-frequency Weyl mean differs by `O_h(A^-2)`; nevertheless its local 257-primary forcing component detects the altered source.
5. **OSF-07b (new exact resolution certificate)**: a **relative path** oscillator at `h_A=2056 A²` has a provable nontrivial signature: `Re D_A<=13/16` for **every A>=24**, and `D_A` converges to `∫_1^2 e(1/x²)dx` with error <=`5π/A`. Frequencies `h=o(A²)` cannot resolve the source perturbation in the ordinary (uncompensated) Fourier averages. The signed **original zero-seed** normality gate is untouched.
6. **OSF-08 (strict boundary)**: 32 hexadecimal digits of `π/16` are certified by exact BBP-tail intervals and an independent Machin calculation; they are a finite proof of digit values, not statistical normality. Digit-depth `k~2log_16 A` is the natural threshold for resolving a source-null `n^-2` defect; source-selected boundary anti-concentration at that moving resolution is OPEN.
