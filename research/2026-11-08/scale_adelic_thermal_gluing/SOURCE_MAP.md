## Observation types and forbidden identifications

- `F_(n,k) ∈ Q` is a signed/positive *transport defect*; its adelic product formula fixes positive real magnitude and finite valuations.
- `U_n ∈ Q` is an *unwrapped state*; the seed enters as `16^n s`.
- `{U_n}∈R/Z` is the **selected real phase**; its Fourier law is not determined by F's valuations alone.
- A real-analytic interpolation `g(x)` for noninteger x is a *declared source model*, not automatically a p-adic function on arbitrary real x.
- Heat-smoothing or relative-frame averaging cannot be substituted for the original Weyl character.
