## Failed attempts and repaired hypotheses

- Learning more does not force signed covariance down. It can reveal coherent
  signal; FD/FC preserve it. We retained the signed coefficient instead of
  replacing it by the size of the removed error.
- Gamma normalization alone is not a decay estimate. SC's exact critical
  chirps specify the bandwidth at which the residual must still be measured.
- An abstract polynomial representation of the complement is insufficient:
  interpolation can require degree m-1. PR4 requires a certified weighted
  degree budget and a retained resonant coefficient.
- Integer itinerary, magnitude-only Fourier data, and finitely many full-pin
  readings each have explicit aliases. Oriented anchors and declared bandwidth
  repair the relevant finite reconstruction statement.
- The triangle's mean is not one physical sample. Exact law tomography has a
  mean-acquisition/precision cost. Minimal algebraic dimension is not a
  uniform condition-number theorem.
- Equal grade masses do not identify a general unknown posterior. Source
  family/known-prior assumptions and retained posterior fields are explicit.
- Source probabilities are not golden arc lengths. GT gives a constructive
  source-preserving decoration and quantitative separation, not their equality.
- Native priors changing with depth cannot be passed to a fixed-prior inverse
  limit. Exact first-bit probabilities demonstrate the failure of projectivity.
- The Farey boundary map is Cayley, not x->e(x); the latter identifies0 and1.
  Euclidean and hyperbolic triangle areas are kept in their own metrics.
- A finite irrationality-measure anti-lock route was inspected but already
  exists in europa_weyl/boundary; it is not counted as new progress here.

Execution failures and fixes are retained in the relevant VALIDATION documents:
