## Recovered objects and hypotheses

1. Distinguish (a) Haar of the 2-adic source state V(n) mod16^k as n varies over residue classes, (b) pairs of **consecutive times** (V(n) mod16,V(n+1) mod16), and (c) two consecutive REAL hexadecimal digits of pi. None is substituted for another.
2. Source-derived prediction: `V(a+16^k t) = V(a) +16^k t*r'(a) (mod16^(k+1))`, k>=1, a>=0, t=0,...,15; `r'(a) mod16` lies in {3,11}, hence each 16-fold fiber is bijective. Check the stronger fully explicit first-digit table and slope against independent finite rational recurrence, and prove via p-adic convergence and derivative.
3. The temporal one-digit residue process has exact one-digit Haar on 16 samples, but pairs have support only 16 out of 256: E1=0, E2=15. The period-16 counterexample must be retained as a decisive obstruction to 'one-digit Haar automatically lifts to chronological two-digit Haar.'
4. For the real original pi orbit, the exact two-digit word is `floor(256*frac(U_n+T_n))`; derive authenticated rational-tail enclosures, all-prime CRT boundary realization, and an exact 16x16 one-to-two transition matrix and its signed current. One-digit Haar is an OPEN source-specific premise for real pi.
5. Prove the exact two-digit Fourier decomposition `E2=E1(row)+E1(col)+sum_(u,v=1)^15 |mixed_uv|^2` for a linear (nonwrapped) chronological window, and `E2=2 E1+mixed` for a cyclic finite window. Refute full Haar lift from source-independent stationarity/carry alone. Distinguish Markov/iid reference from actual deterministic source.
