## Hole contract: original-zero binary future fairness

**Proposed statement.** For the frozen four-pole recurrence, literal U0=0,
and every fixed odd integer k, the completed all-integer-epoch mean
`A^-1 sum_(A<=n<2A) e(8k(U_n+T_n))` tends to zero as A tends to infinity.

**Truth state:** OPEN. **Proof leverage:** B05 would give every fixed parity
word its fair frequency and would justify shrinking-boundary passage. By
the inherited prime-sign marginal estimate, one-digit parity balance would
also force the existing Aphex two-fiber coefficient to vanish. This last
identification retains its original prime-selector hypotheses and the
explicit one-step index/endpoint contract in NEXT.md (at most 2/A).

**Novelty tax:** this is strictly weaker than full hexadecimal normality;
the iid 0/1 hexadecimal law satisfies it and is not Haar. It is stronger
than one-bit parity balance or even fair parity words; the dependent digit
process D_n=2X_(n-1)+X_n disproves the converse. It is an explicitly useful
conditional reduction, not a proved arithmetic saving.

**Weakest sufficient form for just one parity mean.** It would suffice to
bound the signed finite Fejer mean at selected H_A together with the paid
boundary and completion errors, or directly bound the existing original-zero
two-fiber contrast. Requiring every supported pin is one structured route,
not asserted logically necessary for one-bit balance.

**Failed shortcuts to evade:** seed-blind contraction; prime marginals
without their real complement; arbitrary cofinal clock growth; L2-to-atomic
evaluation; inferred independence from equal marginal frequencies.

**Cheapest discriminators:** known same-forcing rational prefix counterseed,
PPP-08 p-integral controls when a prime claim is made, the 2/4 irrational
orbit, and the D_n dependent-digit countermodel. A proposed estimate that
cannot reject the relevant control has not used its advertised source mark.

**Next lamp:** a source-selected signed estimate for the real folded phase
or its conditional future response, using the actual zero-seed history.
Finite arithmetic, numerical agreement and observer completeness alone do
not pay the all-epoch quantifier.
