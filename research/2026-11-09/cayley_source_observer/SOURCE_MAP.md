## Verification independence

The construction, adversarial analysis and implementation review used
separate agent assignments but the same model family and shared source
history. Agreement among them is not an independent external referee result.
The useful independent failure mechanisms are exact four-pole versus combined
polynomial arithmetic, binomial versus repeated-squaring projective powers,
named-variable/direct-environment versus de Bruijn reduction, generic AST
versus compressed omega addresses, and explicit sixteen-preimage enumeration
versus the two-fiber recurrence. High-precision numerical crosschecks remain
diagnostics; rational certificates and analytic proofs carry the exact claims.
