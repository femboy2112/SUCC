## What a correct composite must actually commute

For each finite source horizon L and time n, record the exact square

```
U_n(0) --source step--> U_(n+1)(0)
  | +T_n^(L)                | +T_(n+1)^(L)
  v                          v
x_(n,L) --- ×16 ----------> x_(n+1,L)
  | q                         | q
  v                           v
[x_(n,L)] --- T ----------> [x_(n+1,L)]
  | refine L                  | refine L
  v                           v
completed observed x_n ----> completed observed x_(n+1)
```

Where n<L, every top and middle square is exact. The vertical source-dependent translation is *not* a fixed natural transformation of arbitrary source recurrences: it is constructed from the authentic `r`, source horizon, and seed. At limit L→∞ the bottom observation follows by continuity; the naturality and source typing must be implemented, not inferred from a picture.

## A plausible theorem-building order

1. **Proof/typing foundation:** make a small exact source-functor adapter into existing categorical spine, retain seed, clock, all carries; prove the existing CW-03/04 squares and negative alias.
2. **Lambda compiler:** construct finite strongly normalizing **code of each finite step**, with a separate coinductive Ω trace. Prove simulation and reflection for tested fragments; ensure raw Ω non-normalization is explicit.
3. **Genuine C-monoid:** choose an established nontrivial model; prove C-monoid equations and a faithful source-action representation (or provide precise obstruction), then exhibit original time functor and observer idempotents in Karoubi.
4. **Higher cells:** generate squares/cubes from real equalities and explicitly typed 2-dimensional rewrites; check pentagon/interchange where applicable; do not claim higher dynamical data from nerve alone.
5. **Scott-to-Yoneda completion:** describe source-labelled compact information and Scott-continuous observer functors; prove exactly which probes commute with completion and which do not.
6. **Signed bridge:** attack original-zero complementary contrast or source-specific Weyl means using already-paid carry/CRT/Fejér Fourier/Gamma terms; any claimed contraction must reject the rational p-integral coherent wrong seed, periodic and Gamma-matched controls.
7. **Lean:** formalize one *finite* source square, one compact nested interval lemma and one negative counterexample before aspirational analytic completion.

The order matters: it puts the proof methodology on an actual carrier, rather than making attractive but uncheckable diagrams.
