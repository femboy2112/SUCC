## Classical external families

1. J. Lambek & P. J. Scott, *Introduction to Higher Order Categorical Logic* (1986), §§15–18, Theorem 16.1 (C-monoids and CCC Karoubi), and Rodenburg–van der Linden, *Manufacturing a cartesian closed category with exactly two objects out of a C-monoid* (Studia Logica 48, 1989). Classical model theory, not a new SUCC result.
2. Alessandro Berarducci, *Infinite λ-calculus and non-sensible models*, intro/§§1–3. Explicit Ω₃ and an infinitary β⊥ normal form that distinguishes some head-unsolvable terms; `https://people.dm.unipi.it/berardu/Art/1996Nonsensible/non-sensible.pdf`. Strong convergence conditions matter.
3. van der Leer, Wullaert, Ahrens, *Scott's Representation Theorem and the Univalent Karoubi Envelope*, ITP 2025, DOI `10.4230/LIPIcs.ITP.2025.33`, formalization in UniMath. Not a substitute for implementing SUCC's concrete C-monoid.
4. Basic compact Hausdorff domain/powerdomain, CRT, covering-space `R→R/Z`, Yoneda, currying, Gaussian curvature and Gauss–Bonnet. These are classical and have explicitly stated domains.
