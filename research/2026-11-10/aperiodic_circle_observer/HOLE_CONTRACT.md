# Round V contract: finite rules, productive aperiodic execution

Baseline: SUCC branch `aletheia/cursed-categories-dedekind-yoneda-2026-11-10`,
commit `4e311d03e3ac9c3dbcbdc32477cd113f2ac0743b`. Publication authorized on
the existing non-main branch/draft PR. New work is staged separately while
the required baseline regression suite runs.

## Exact targets

1. Prove all-time nonrecurrence of the circle/LR observer's semantic interval
   state, without appealing merely to an appended step counter.
2. Prove normalized residuals are pairwise different and the LR stream is not
   eventually periodic, retaining the classical nonquadraticity dependency.
3. Construct a finite exact algorithm which, for every proposed start N and
   period p>=1, emits a finite certificate and mismatch witness refuting that
   periodic tail. The run is productive and need not have a preset horizon.
4. Strengthen the witness search with a source-derived finite escape deadline:
   turn the proposed cycle into a rational/quadratic fixed point, certify its
   separation from the geometric target, and bound how many repeated blocks
   can survive. Seek exponential contraction for mixed-letter blocks and exact
   parabolic denominators for single-letter blocks.
5. Construct rational probes separating normalized states, and a productive
   whole-run observer which computes natural-number LR block lengths.

## Hypotheses and truth states

The source is the proved Euclidean beta-matrix interval name from Round IV,
with exact rational arithmetic and strict shrinking enclosures. Pi irrationality
proves comparison productivity; nonquadraticity (available from transcendence)
proves all proposed eventual periods fail. These are imported classical
theorems, not conclusions extracted from finite tests. Targets 1-2 have direct
candidate proofs; implementation, finite escape bounds, and all boundary cases
are to be audited. No normality, parity cancellation, or RH promotion is sought.

## Dependency cut and novelty boundary

The missing edge is from qualitative nonperiodicity to an auditable observer
which generates finite source-derived separation and escape certificates.
Computability and aperiodicity themselves are classical. The proposed local
contribution is the exact quantitative certificate interface, its geometry,
and its implementation. An eventual-period test alone is not a signed
statistical estimate. Any claimed uniform runtime/Diophantine bound must have
its own proof, not a finite prefix fitted as validation.

## Weakest sufficient form

A computable strict interval name of a nonquadratic irrational in (3,4), with
the fixed Farey charts, suffices for total candidate rejection and pairwise
residual separation. The actual observer uses the Euclidean source. A rational
interval excluding zero from the relevant fixed-point polynomial supplies a
checkable finite separation certificate; no symbolic transcendence oracle is
called at runtime.

## Discriminating controls and acceptance

- Positive: independently verify pi-source certificates and mismatches.
- Negative: a quadratic with actual periodic LR itinerary must preserve its
  valid cycle; source interval refinement alone must not be miscalled output
  aperiodicity.
- Boundary: single-letter periods, prefix N=0, nonprimitive periods, scalar
  matrix representatives, changed endpoint convention, equality guards,
  source precision, and rational denominator signs.
- Mutation: alter period, polynomial, gap, deadline, or witness; the verifier
  must reject the corrupted claim.
- Holdout: use source-derived deadlines before inspecting the corresponding
  future LR output, then verify on a separate implementation/name.

The finite-program claim permits unbounded integer registers and runtime.
Finite prefixes validate code, while the proof quantifies over all stages and
all candidate periods. If no useful deadline survives, retain the total
search theorem and name the missing separation estimate explicitly.
