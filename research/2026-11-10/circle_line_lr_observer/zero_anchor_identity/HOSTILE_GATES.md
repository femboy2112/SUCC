# Hostile controls: an identity theorem is not an equidistribution theorem

**Scope:** explicit counterexamples and falsifiers for the zero-anchored rational fingerprint. All actual-source identities retain the original four-pole forcing and `U0=0`. No claim is promoted using a finite fitted example.

## H-01. The coherent irrational wrong seed

`16^n(x+s)=0` for every n, so all completed Fourier means are `1` and every positive-length hexadecimal word is zero. This is maximally nonnormal. Reject the whole wrong-seed section before computing its finite Haar or Fourier certificate; do not accept a numerical phase fit as authentication. ## H-02. The 1/16 alias and classified rational seeds

Seed `s=1/16` has `16^n s in Z` for n>=1. Its later completed circle orbit is indistinguishable from the zero seed, but its initial phase and recovered state are not the same. More generally the rational-affine typed module has a provable exact alias classifier: two seeds alias from clock n iff their difference has **zero** irrational coefficient and rational denominator dividing `16^n`. This is an exact theorem depending on x irrational, not a finite empirical coincidence.

## H-03. Two definitions of π must not be mistaken for two proofs of BBP

A circle-beta interval name and the authentic BBP name agree at finitely many checked stages. Their *mathematical* identification uses classical Euclidean-circle geometry and the BBP identity. If the BBP evaluation theorem were absent, finitely overlapping rational intervals would not prove equal limits; a different real can remain in finitely many intersections. Their independent implementations detect arithmetic slips but are not logically independent witnesses of the imported equality theorem.

## H-04. Mutated source with a correct finite prefix

For any finite tested horizon L, a forcing mutant may agree with the authentic `r(n)` for n<=L and differ at L+1. Its finite interval and word certificates may then be identical through the tested prefix. The fingerprint's *universal* source passport requires the exact original formula for all n; a different sequence is detected at its first differing index. The independent source algebra can check a given stage; neither finite tests nor hashing by itself establishes all-stage authenticity. Positive extra-pole source mutants, equal Mellin prefixes, or a merely matching rational output cannot replace the source contract.

## H-05. A computable nonnormal irrational also has a complete cut

The factorial-sparse real `z=3+sum_(j>=1)16^(-j!)` has strict computable rational enclosures, is irrational, and is nonnormal. It admits source/height-style rational separation and an infinite L/R path with every fixed prefix computable. It does **not** have the authenticated four-pole source and zero-section passport. Therefore productive computable cut reconstruction, π-like prefix geometry, or a Dedekind/Yoneda equivalence cannot prove normality without additional source-specific estimates.

## H-06. The ordinary interval Wasserstein seam trap

A translation `z->(z+a/16) mod1` is **not globally continuous** in the ordinary interval metric. For exact rationals `u=1/16-1/65536` and `v=1/16+1/65536`, `|u-v|=1/32768` but the 16-point translated laws have `W1=2047/32768` (checked by independent rational CDF). Any claim of `W1(Qmu,Qnu)<=W1(mu,nu)` on the **whole interval** is refuted by this example. The positive transport theorem in ZI-08 requires source-certified phase intervals inside a *single* hexadecimal digit chart, which the implementation verifies.

## H-07. One signed statistic can vanish without product independence

The #593 2:3 joining countermodels have full Haar marginals but a nonproduct coupling; an exactly zero mixed h=8 coefficient can coexist with maximal second-digit dependence. The source-locked ratio-fiber budget transfers precision, but no single mixed character certifies complete normality, product joining, RH, or positive Weil identity.

## H-08. Small finite overlap is not asymptotic cancellation

The current exact-rational shadow of the actual zero-seed orbit shows strictly positive, varying signed adjacent-block overlaps at M=4,8,16,32. Those finite values may go up or down. They are source-attested observed numbers with rigorous finite error bars, not proofs of a uniform positive relative overlap or an all-N contractive defect inequality. In particular the M=32 observed overlap exceeds M=16, so monotonicity of the overlap itself is already contradicted by these finite certificates if their intervals are disjoint.
