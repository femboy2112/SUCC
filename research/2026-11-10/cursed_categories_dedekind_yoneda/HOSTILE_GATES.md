# Crucible: failures, witnesses, and the first serious new SUCC target

**Status:** Established elementary counterexamples and explicit UNVERIFIED analytic interfaces. A finite computational certificate is a local fact, not normality or RH.

## HG-01. Finite-cut-probe indistinguishability (PROVED PAPER)

Let `F subset Q` be finite. The sorted elements of F divide the real line into nonempty rational-open cells. Choose any cell `I`. Every irrational `x,y in I` has the same vector `([q<x])_(q in F)` even if `x != y`. Hence a finite restriction of the rational-Yoneda observer is never faithful on all irrationals. The proof does **not** contradict full Yoneda faithfulness, which requires every rational probe.

**Stronger normality witness.** Inside any such cell choose a base-16 rational cylinder contained in I. Put a finite hexadecimal prefix in front of a base-16 Champernowne sequence: the resulting irrational is base-16 normal. Put the same finite prefix in front of a sparse factorial-position-1 digit tail (other digits zero): its value is irrational and nonnormal. Both values have identical finite rational probe answers, even though one is normal and the other is not. Standard Champernowne normality and elementary irrationality of the sparse Liouville tail are classical external inputs; this is a finite-probe separation *impossibility*, not a counterexample for authentic BBP.

## HG-02. Irrational-only domain misses the essential counterseed (PROVED)

The genuine completed original-zero phase is `x=pi/16` irrational. The coherent same-forcing wrong seed `s*=-pi/16` produces completed phase `x+s*=0`, a **rational**. A category whose objects are only irrational cuts cannot even express this hostile orbit as an object. Therefore every source-security theorem involving these controls must extend the observer to the full real-cut category (and preserve source seed labels), even if the accelerated irrational-only digit compiler is used internally.

A rational seed `s=1/16` produces the *same fractional orbit for every n>=1* because `16^n s=16^(n-1) in Z`. The exact digit compiler distinguishes time zero, but a tail-only observer aliases it. This control is implemented in focused tests.

## HG-03. Numerical arrow groupoid can lie by being too equivalent (PROVED)

The action groupoid of rational affine transformations partitions irrationals into countable orbits. Since each orbit component is equivalent to a point category, a plain equivalence of groupoids would erase **which** rational matrix, which source value, and which digit carry produced an arrow. Thus an equivalence of *undecorated* computational pathways has no automatic preservation of original-zero observables. Keep the rational `(a,b)`, source digest, seed witness, and signed phase character as typed decoration.

## HG-04. Fractional part is not an order functor (PROVED)

For e.g. irrational `x<1/16<y`, with x just below and y just above 1/16, `{16x}` can be close to 1 and `{16y}` close to 0. So `x<y` does not imply `{16x}<={16y}`. The global map cannot be treated as an endofunctor of `Irr_<=`. Its restrictions to open rational digit cylinders **are** order-isomorphisms, glued only with explicit branch/carry records. A bogus implementation that silently uses a global monotone map should fail a mutation test.

## HG-05. An exact value certificate is not an ergodic theorem (PROVED scope)

A source-only algorithm may certify any fixed number of digits of pi, and hence any fixed finite Weyl mean using rigorous transcendental phase bounds. It may not conclude that the means decay as the window grows. In #591, an arbitrary vanishing remainder in a statistical contraction is equivalent to the original normality gate unless independently bounded. In #592, coarse seed cylinders anneal generically while deep cylinders retain the exact zero-selected Weyl difficulty. In #593, nonproduct normal joinings and nonnormal mixed-mode cancellations falsify generic implications from marginal Haar or one mixed character. The Dedekind presheaf faithfully represents **point identity/order**, not the distribution of its radix orbit.

## HG-06. Complete source and carry protection contract (UNVERIFIED as compiler)

A future typed compiler must produce a commutative diagram:

`authenticated BBP finite source term -> certified rational interval name -> irrational cut presheaf -> labeled digit/carry branch -> signed observer`.

Required independent replay gates: check four frozen poles and literal zero seed; compare source exact fractions against original module; verify interval strictness using paper bounds; refuse forged or mutation-tainted certificates; retain `k_n` and original integer CRT winding; pay every finite phase error; distinguish rational and irrational cut objects; record nontermination as UNKNOWN; verify normal and innermost beta strategy agreement where both terminate; reject source gauge that changes real observation. This is a proposal, not a claim that all gates were run.

## HG-07. A falsifiable load-bearing theorem target

For the authenticated original-zero BBP orbit `theta_n={16^n pi/16}`, let empirical laws on adjacent M-blocks produce signed CDF defects `H_M^-`, `H_M^+` against their recursive 16-child future-conditioned Haar reconstructions (per #591). Define the signed overlap

`Omega_M=int_0^1 min(|H_M^-|,|H_M^+|) 1_{H_M^- H_M^+<0} dt`.

The exact identity already proved in #591 is

`D_(2M)=(D_M^-+D_M^+)/2 - Omega_M`.

A genuine new route would prove, **from the original source plus cut/carry morphisms**, lower bounds on Omega of the right order and upper bounds on the second block, with independently controlled errors and odd-N endpoints. Merely expressing the identity as a functor, proving a contraction of the *source tail*, or computing finite overlaps does NOT establish a statistical contraction.

**Discriminating experiment:** freeze non-dyadic epoch windows, original zero, odd-eight `h=8`, source identity, and 2:3 paired labels; compare exact/certified selected signed overlaps and contrasts against (a) `s=-pi/16` coherent completed-zero, (b) `s=1/16` tail alias, (c) zero forcing with zero seed, (d) modified poles and moment-matched positive sources, (e) the normal-but-locked and mixed-null joinings of #593, (f) nonnormal rational periodic 1/9. Register the chosen statistic before inspecting holdouts. If the statistic accepts an adversary that was supposed to be excluded, the claimed source-selection frame fails. This is a **proposed** probe; it was not executed here.
