# Next research: pay the source-specific pointwise estimate

## Earned frontier

The filter apparatus is no longer missing a generic way to isolate the
zero sector. The preceding round supplied stable geometric lower/upper
filters. This round supplies a nearly sharp information cost for their
fixed node family, a one-channel moving-node alternative, and angular
projection with finite alias accounting. Those routes all identify the
target once the right authentic responses are known accurately enough.

The obstruction has also become sharper: a fixed finite scalar
exponential bank in the punctured closed unit disk cannot maintain
positive Goldbach certificates at every large target. Its genuine
arithmetic aggregate becomes negative, even with complex phase twists.
This theorem does not cover arbitrary ordered operator probes or a
different authenticated source observable.

## Principal remaining hole

Find a **source-derived estimate or discriminator** that gives new
pointwise information about the genuine coupled midpoint source. A
candidate has to explain why the actual source cannot occupy the
remaining bad response fibers. It must do more than rename the prime
pair count or fit its observed finite values.

The moving-node criterion

\[
F_n(1/[4(n-1)])>1/4
\]

is exactly Goldbach. It is a useful target specification, but presenting
it as the missing lemma with no additional structure leaves the whole
conjecture inside the lemma. At the complete geometric resolution the
same issue appears as `B_(d_n,t)(n)>0`. A universal coarse-resolution
positive lower bound would be stronger than the target on the generic
histogram class and remains unproved for the arithmetic source.

## Required hostile regimes

Preserve exact mass, the unordered prime diagonal, endpoint removal,
phase conjugation and the positive/negative sign convention. Test
grade-one-only sources, high-grade alias masses, hidden normalization
scales, the smallest integer-gap twins, and an infinite sparse family
of failed targets compatible with every current density bound. A
Chen-shaped grade-at-most-one premise does not split grade zero from
grade one. An exact contour identity does not establish its positive
value at every arithmetic target.

For a new analytic estimate, state in advance whether its pass condition
is average, almost all, every sufficiently large target, or every target.
No conversion between those quantifiers is implicit. An observed
finite pass is never the evidence for a universal sign claim.

## Dominance boundary and stop rule

A new frame earns preference if it retains the prior valid finite
certificates, explains the fixed-bank negative mean, respects the
integer and noise counterexamples, and adds a genuine source-specific
discriminator. Otherwise it is either a useful new representation or a
change of access model, with that status stated explicitly.

Continue on the published research branch with reviewable additive
proofs, exact controls and draft PR updates. Do not merge or modify the
archival imports. The present branch is a research record, not a claim
that the arithmetic program is complete.
