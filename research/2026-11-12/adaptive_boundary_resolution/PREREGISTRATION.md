## Frontier and object

The preceding dossier established bounded-coefficient geometric damping
filters, an all-grade error bound, exact integer recovery under adequate
resolution, and a negative arithmetic average for every fixed admissible
finite bank of positive real damping nodes. A growing common degree can
recover a positive aggregate; this alone proves no individual Goldbach
case. Goldbach and the BBP R0 bound remain open.

The object is the authenticated grade response
`F(s)=sum_h c_h s^h`, with nonnegative integer counts and declared total
mass `M`. For Goldbach, `M=n-1` and
`h=Omega(n+a)+Omega(n-a)-2`, `0<=a<=n-2`. Its zero count is the
unordered representation count of `2n`. The proposed boundary includes
joint source coupling; separate marginal distributions do not replace it.

## Frozen discriminating controls

1. Exact integer twins: bases `2,3,5` and degrees `1,2,3,4,5,6`.
   Independently multiply the annihilating polynomial, split positive and
   negative coefficients, verify equal mass, every rational response,
   different zero counts, and the proposed mass bounds. At `(b,d)=(2,2)`,
   compare the raw polynomial with its multiple by `1+z`; the first
   construction is not allowed to masquerade as the minimum.
2. Fixed phase controls: explicit conjugate-paired rational complex nodes;
   compare histogram-side responses with the filtered combination and
   compare ordinary triangular arithmetic reindexing with midpoint sums.
   Check the adversarial prefix filters at even `N=8,32,64`: all grades
   `1..N` pass, grade `N+4` fails. A finite pass cannot certify all-grade
   admissibility.
3. Integer threshold controls: synthetic histograms with zero count zero,
   one, and more; the equality example with mass `M=1+epsilon^-1` when
   integral; and the strict-threshold case. Test rational noise using
   explicit interval endpoints, not a rounded noisy center.
4. Separate arithmetic integration sample: midpoints `67,263,521,1033`;
   degrees `2,4,6`, base `2`. These are fixed before seeing their responses.
   Factorization and direct primality counting will be separate algorithms.
   This small sample checks wiring and exact identities; no asymptotic
   slope, exceptional-set rate or universal positivity is inferred.

**Pass:** all exact identities and promised inequalities hold, source
imports cover the precise domains, mutation controls fail as predicted,
and saved outputs reproduce. **Fail:** preserve the counterexample,
repair the theorem or implementation, and record the status change.
**Ambiguous:** inadequate precision or an unavailable source stays
unverified; it does not become a negative theorem.
