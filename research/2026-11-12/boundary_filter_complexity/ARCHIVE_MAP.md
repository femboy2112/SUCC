## New cut selected here

The finite ordered-closure theorem raises a testable structural question:
**can the actual nonconstant completed BBP phase admit a finite stationary
linear realization at all?** [HANKEL_THEOREMS.md](HANKEL_THEOREMS.md)
answers no by an elementary polynomial-root argument and gives a finite
rectangular rejection horizon. It separates this result from already known
infinite Green-memory rank and from generic Fourier-tower nonclosure.

This is a classification of an instrument's possible complexity, not an
arithmetic normality theorem. The same file proves that irrational sources
can have arbitrarily poorly conditioned finite phase Grams, and that a
sparse irrational can have infinite phase rank while failing R0 maximally.
Thus the surviving proof obligation concerns a quantitative, source-selected
signed margin, not rank alone.

Promising work must now either use the nonlinear/source-labelled dynamics
directly, or pay the growing-dimension and error costs of a linearized
observer. A new proposed filter should be evaluated by what estimate it
adds after these archive identities are removed.
