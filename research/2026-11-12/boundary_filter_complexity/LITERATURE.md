# Primary-source comparison

Checked 2026-11-12. No academic-priority claim is made for this dossier.
The proofs here are elementary and self-contained; these sources identify
classical context rather than supply a missing selected-orbit estimate.

1. David H. Bailey and Richard E. Crandall,
   *On the Random Character of Fundamental Constant Expansions* (2001),
   [author-hosted manuscript](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/baicran.pdf),
   Hypothesis A and Theorem 1.1. The rational-polynomial forcing and
   zero-initialized expanding recurrence are the classical backdrop. The
   proposed distribution dichotomy is a hypothesis, not a result imported
   as proved here. The present finite filters do not establish it.

2. Moody T. Chu and Matthew M. Lin,
   *On the finite rank and finite-dimensional representation of bounded
   semi-infinite Hankel operators* (2014), DOI 10.1093/imanum/dru001,
   [author-hosted paper](https://mtchu.math.ncsu.edu/Research/Papers/imanum.dru001.full.pdf),
   section 2. It reviews the classical links among finite rank, recurrence
   and rational generating functions. Our unit-modulus sequence is treated
   as an **algebraic matrix**, with rank the supremum of finite-section ranks;
   we do not assert that it defines a bounded operator on l2. HBC01 supplies
   its own direct polynomial argument, so bounded-operator hypotheses are
   not silently transferred.

3. Noam D. Elkies, *On Finite Sequences Satisfying Linear Recursions*,
   New York Journal of Mathematics 8 (2002), 85--97,
   [journal page](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2002/8-5.html).
   The finite-sequence relation between recurrence constraints and Hankel
   rank is classical linear algebra. The elementary phase-orbit specialization,
   source-grid cost and finite certificate assembly in this round are not
   advertised as a new general realization theory.

## Physical analogy and measurement scope

4. CERN, [How did we discover the Higgs boson?](https://home.cern/science/physics/higgs-boson/how).
   Higgs bosons are produced in collisions and inferred through their decay
   products against competing background processes. This supports the analogy
   of calibrated production and response, not a theorem that every hidden
   distinction requires the same mechanism. CMS's
   [HIG-17-034 publication](https://cms-results.web.cern.ch/cms-results/public-results/publications/HIG-17-034/),
   figure 1, explicitly includes gluon fusion, vector-boson fusion and
   associated production. A quark-only production assertion is too narrow.

5. G. M. D'Ariano, P. Perinotti and A. Tosini,
   [Information and disturbance in operational probabilistic theories](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2020-11-16-363/),
   Quantum 4, 363 (2020). Their distinction includes disturbance of correlations,
   not merely a change in the system's marginal state. This is the relevant
   physical context for the declared finite quantum instrument; it is not a
   universal positive-energy-injection law or a classification of pi/RH.

6. L. Mišta, Jr. and R. Filip,
   [Quantum nondemolition measurement saturates fidelity trade-off](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.72.034307),
   Physical Review A 72, 034307 (2005). The abstract establishes the specific
   QND information/disturbance setting as a classical antecedent. We do not
   import an unread detailed optimality proof. The elementary two-qubit
   formulas in the present dossier have their own derivation and exact tests.

Physical evidence has calibrated statistical uncertainty. An exact mathematical
proof certifies a statement under explicit axioms/model assumptions. These
forms of authority are not interchangeable, and a difficult number-theoretic
proof is not automatically a physical quantum measurement experiment.
