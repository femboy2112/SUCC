## Faithful proposal

Reconstruct the dynamics visible at a boundary through the *joint, ordered,
phase-calibrated responses* of filters. A sufficient family should either
reconstruct the accessible interior up to an explicit equivalence, or certify
the desired property directly. The assertion that every property of every
interior follows from any such interface is not a hypothesis of this round.

Retain the recent observer contract: compare present outputs at a shared
meeting frame. A historical sample enters only after its transport to that
frame is declared. An adjoint, transpose, sign, time order, or orientation may
not be silently discarded. The boundary is a mathematical observation
interface; no literal radiation or gravitational duality is assumed.

## Hole contract HBF

- **Exact finite statement:** Given a source-generated family of filtered
  vectors `V`, certify closure by `Q_i-H_i^*G^{-1}H_i=0`, where
  `G=V^*V`, `H_i=V^*A_iV`, `Q_i=(A_iV)^*(A_iV)`. Under `G>0`, this is equivalent
  to invariance of `range(V)` under every declared filter. Nonzero residuals
  identify a missed reachable direction. Quantify error for finite words.
- **Target alternative:** For a finite carrier, characterize which linear
  targets are determined by a filter map, and give dual lower certificates
  and nullspace countermodels when full reconstruction is unnecessary.
- **Initial status:** proposed local theorem/proof audit; not an arithmetic
  estimate. Classical ingredients must be attributed.
- **Quantifiers:** arbitrary Hilbert carrier with bounded declared filters
  for the closure theorem; finite carrier/probability laws for the target
  theorem. The data must be genuine joint responses of one source.
- **Weakest useful form:** one target lower certificate robust to observation
  error. No requirement to reconstruct an unobservable complementary sector.
- **Leverage:** differentiates an adequate instrument, an inadequate probe,
  a source problem, and an unproved statistical estimate.
- **Novelty tax:** classical projection/duality/bispectrum ingredients;
  new-to-this-round combined certificates, exact adversarial instances and
  arithmetic applications. No worldwide priority claim.
- **Known obstructions:** source-blind spectra; phase loss; finite prefixes;
  absent support connectivity; same-forcing counterseed; finite rank fitted
  from samples without a closure law; all-order computation versus a uniform
  asymptotic estimate.
- **Cheapest falsifiers:** sign/order mutants, hidden-sector extension,
  equal-power unequal-additive-count interiors, zero-Fourier-support aliases,
  mass/moment-preserving counterfeit grade histograms.
- **Next lamp:** a source-derived lower certificate family whose margin
  remains positive for every sufficiently late BBP shell or every even
  Goldbach target. This is still an OPEN arithmetic obligation, not implied
  by the finite theorem.

## BBP probe, frozen before execution

Use the original rational forcing, `U_0=0`, integer fixed-point enclosures
whose rounding is propagated under multiplication by 16, and the rigorous
tail `0<T_n<=47/[225(n+1)^2]`. Do not use supplied digits of pi.

- Calibration shells: `B=32,64`.
- New fixed evaluation shells: `B=137,271,547,1091`.
- Filter resolutions: `q=16,32,64` in every shell. No tuning after seeing
  results; increasing dyadic refinement should not decrease the lower bound.
- Precision guard: 48 binary bits beyond the last amplification exponent.
- Report rational lower bounds for `1-Re W_B(1)` using the cellwise minorant
  of `8*dist(x,Z)^2`, with the future-tail payment displayed separately.
- Positive controls: evenly spaced circle cells; negative controls: the
  coherent completed orbit `0`, and a one-point law. Ambiguous cells must
  contribute only a justified lower bound (zero is safe).
- Independent oracle: direct Fraction recurrence on short prefixes. Reject
  mutated counts, omitted source/tail payments and insufficient precision.

**Pass:** every theorem/control check succeeds; interval bounds contain the
independent source values; output is reproducible. A positive shell margin
is only a finite fact. **Fail:** one soundness or source mismatch; repair and
record it before delivery. **Ambiguous:** unresolved intervals or nonpositive
bound, not a claim of no escape and not a failed normality conjecture.
