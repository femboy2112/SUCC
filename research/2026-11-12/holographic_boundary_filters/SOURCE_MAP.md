### Accessible realization and source authentication

The main modules [dynamic boundary inverse][main-inverse] and
[spectral boundary rigidity][main-spectral] already distinguish an
accessible realization from invisible extra structure. Their interfaces,
opening claims, and relevant constructions were inspected; that is not a
fresh audit of their entire implementations. Donor #604's
[observer-ladder theorems][donor604-theorems] already give a positive Gram
kernel and a cyclic GNS/RKHS realization. Its
[bidirectional-channel analysis][donor604-channels] separates a universal
response structure from a selected arithmetic orbit: Haar average energy
does not select the π seed, and finite-grid limits need not commute.

The present ordered result combines a declared word basis with
`G=V*V`, `H_i=V*A_iV`, and `Q_i=(A_iV)*(A_iV)`. Its explicit defect
`Q_i−H_i*G^−1H_i` tests closure under **every declared generator**.
The local work is the simultaneous closure certificate, the metric-aware
defect budget, finite-word error propagation, and hostile controls. It is
not the first finite realization theorem, nor reconstruction of an
inaccessible orthogonal summand. The approximate word bound additionally
assumes contraction of the ambient operators; finite observations alone
do not authenticate that assumption.

Donor #601's [coherent-channel contract][donor601-coherent] and
[path-transport argument][donor601-transport] already require a declared
current frame, source/code contract, and paid transport error. Relevant
passages were inspected. The present dossier retains that discipline;
it does not turn shared terminology into a physical entanglement claim.

### Phase, target separation, and conditioning

The main [zero-frame bispectrum proof][main-bispectrum] already cancels
source curvature with delayed character products while retaining a signed
angular factor. Its full proof was inspected. Relevant inverse and
stability sections of the [driven-pinbed continuous theorems][main-pinbed]
already show how two coherent response channels recover a source under
specified nondegeneracy assumptions.

The new target-first account makes the following distinctions explicit:
row-span completeness versus a target-specific certificate; ambiguity
diameter versus one-observation error; full-support bispectrum recovery
versus support relations with torsion; and identifiability versus uniform
conditioning near Haar. Exact finite counterexamples calibrate each
boundary. Complete decay of all nonzero Fourier **powers** still implies
equidistribution; phase loss obstructing an additive target does not
invalidate that criterion. These statements have different quantifiers
and targets.

This is a synthesis and certification layer over classical linear
duality and harmonic analysis. The Z6 pair/triple ambiguity and the Z3
near-Haar obstruction are locally derived examples. Their derivations
do not establish that no equivalent example or theorem exists elsewhere.

### Goldbach, CRT, and ordinary additive coupling

Donor #600's [prime fixed-point cylinder][donor600-cylinder] already grades
prime pairs by `Ω(a)+Ω(b)−2`, with zero grade equal to a Goldbach count.
The [carrier coupling][donor600-coupling] already identifies the squared
norm of a mixed additive/multiplicative operator with the ordered count.
Those identities are exact encodings; positivity at every target is the
remaining arithmetic assertion.

The inspected [legacy CRT note][donor600-crt] gives Fréchet bounds inside
CRT fibers and monotone refinement, so the existence of a coarse-marginal
overlap lower bound is prior repository work. The inspected
[signed-sieve proof][donor600-sieve] has `q*s=R−c*c≤R`, a genuine signed
lower estimate whose pointwise positivity is still open. Its average
positivity does not supply positivity at every midpoint. The inspected
[resonance theorems][donor600-wave] and
[boundary no-go][donor600-boundary] explain why shared low-frequency
structure and source truncation can mislead; the truncation counterexample
does not refute the full Goldbach problem.

The present coarse-measurement theorem is the finite nonnegative dual
certificate, with explicit mass/support contracts and response intervals.
Its finite quadratic example distinguishes a real zero-ground completion
from the observed arithmetic data. The main [no-ghost module][main-noghost]
already implements rational Farkas reasoning; convex separation is not
new here. The exact homometric example, cyclic-alias example, and forged
support control identify where source or measurement claims can fail.
Ordinary integer convolution is retained: a cyclic coefficient sums
ordinary coefficients over congruent target sums.

### The π application

The forcing and tail estimate in [duck_bbp.py][main-bbp] and the selected
seed `π/16` are inherited. The inspected source states the literal
four-pole forcing and the tail bound `47/[225(n+1)^2]`; this round does
not derive a new BBP identity.

The added result transports certified residue intervals into a lower
bound on `1−Re W_B(1)` with a paid tail and a conservative cell filter.
The experiment gives finite bounds at preregistered blocks. A uniform
all-large-block lower bound remains the R0 obligation. Replacing it with
an equivalent uniformly positive cell response does not by itself lower
the arithmetic difficulty.

### Damped filters and the Borwein overlap

The geometric-node coefficients in the damped filter are classical
Lagrange extrapolation weights, equivalently finite q-product
coefficients. The local certificate adds a sharp one-sided all-integer-
grade tail envelope, coefficient amplification below nine, interval
propagation, monotone upper/lower refinement, and a source-authenticated
finite Goldbach application. It does not require a grade ceiling.

There is a particularly strong prior overlap. Donor #601's inspected
[Borwein ratchet, BRT-06][donor601-borwein] already states

\[
T=P_1+\tfrac12P_2,\qquad T^r=P_1+2^{-r}P_2.
\]

Orthogonality of those grade projectors immediately gives

\[
\boxed{P_1=2T^2-T.}
\]

Consequently this round cannot claim the first finite exact prime
projector, or automatic superiority to that donor's functional calculus.
The comparison is between access models:

| Declared observation | What the algebra provides | Unpaid source obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Borwein `T` and `T²` | Exact `P_1` in the supplied two-level spectral model | Authenticate and acquire that functional-calculus readout. |
| Joint damped responses `F_n(t^r)` | A signed lower estimate, an explicit tail budget, and exact integer recovery when the budget is below one | Obtain the coupled responses with sufficient absolute accuracy without already performing the target count. |

All scalar grade filters commute. Their gain is cancellation on a known
integer spectrum, not physical ordering. Ordered matrix responses belong
to the separate realization part of the dossier.

## 4. Provenance and the completed arithmetic cut

The audit files record separate derivations and implementations. They
share a model family, this research session, the mathematical proposals,
and portions of the source corpus. They are useful error checks, not
external refereeing or independent-source corroboration. Replaying the
same script adds reproducibility, not another independent proof. The
universal filter statements rest on displayed proofs, while the recorded
arithmetic windows support only the stated finite observations.

The continuation proves a **fixed-resolution obstruction** to the
proposal that one fixed filter will eventually become positive at every
midpoint. The classical imported mean

\[
\sum_{m\le x}s^{\Omega(m)}\sim
C_s x(\log x)^{s-1},\qquad
C_s=\Gamma(s)^{-1}\prod_p\frac{(1-1/p)^s}{1-s/p},
\]

is transported through parity and the ordinary additive triangle.
For fixed `0<s<1`, FR01 yields a positive constant times
`X²(log X)^(2s−2)` for the sum of genuine joint responses over `n≤X`.
The largest fixed damping dominates a finite signed bank; its coefficient
must be negative for a pointwise zero-grade minorant. FR02 proves the
negative summatory response for the even geometric family, and FR03
proves it for every fixed finite damped bank satisfying that minorant
condition. The complete derivation and precise limitations are in
[FIXED_RESOLUTION.md](FIXED_RESOLUTION.md).

This continuation rules out an attractive but invalid fixed-filter
route using genuine arithmetic and a classical imported theorem. It does
not imply pointwise eventual negativity, refute Goldbach, or provide a
uniform estimate for a growing or adaptive filter bank. After this cut,
the next positive route must specify how resolution changes and how its
joint response is estimated; [NEXT.md](NEXT.md) records that adaptive
continuation contract. Exact factorization of every endpoint is
a valid finite measurement procedure, not a new analytic source estimate.
