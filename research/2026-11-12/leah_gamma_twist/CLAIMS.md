   The root repaired it; a native regression retains the witness.

   reversed. Exact intervals failed that assertion; the corrected signed
   interval 1493<1072231967-341301560*pi<1494 is retained.

   (4^d-1)(4^d+1) also permits the old worst-case loss allocation.
4. Pure source reconstruction, corrected-loop holonomy, positive Gram objects,
   and prime marginals do not pay the selected signed estimate. These failures
