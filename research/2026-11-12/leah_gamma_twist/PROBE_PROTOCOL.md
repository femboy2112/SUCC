- Pass: inequalities hold exactly on rational outputs and finite enclosures

  implementation or theorem pending diagnosis.
- Fail: an exact counterexample to a universal displayed inequality; no silent
  adjustment of sample shells or constants.
- Ambiguous: a wide interval, finite agreement, or absent asymptotic estimate.
  Finite π/non-π separation is a calibrated instrument, never all-scale evidence.

The proof obligations are universal paper arguments. Same-model agent review and
methodologically different exact code are not external mathematical refereeing.
