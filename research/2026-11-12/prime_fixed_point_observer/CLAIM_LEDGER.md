## Still open / unverified

- A genuinely source-derived uniform lower bound for every large composite
  midpoint, beyond the equivalent R(N)>0 / nonzero degree2 condition.
- An asymptotic algorithmic advantage over ordinary prime sieving or direct
  pair counting. No benchmark establishes such an advantage.
- A physical realization, noise model, or quantum speedup.
- The pre-existing pi-normality, source-selected cancellation, and RH gates.

The finite tests and exact spectral manipulations do not promote these items.
