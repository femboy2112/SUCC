## Faithful reading and permitted interface

The user proposes two correlated observers in a larger carrier, transported
from opposite ends to one meeting event. They compare only **present model
outputs** in a calibrated common frame. Historical observations from distinct
patches cannot be directly equated. The new hypothesis is that a coherent
path avoids carrier interactions that destroy the protected twin relation,
so the designated final comparison remains clean.

The steelman is preservation of a declared **logical information space**
through carrier interactions, followed by a present comparison. A physical
collapse law is not assumed. Internal coherent records may be used locally
for computation/recovery but are never silently supplied to the comparator.
The comparator's permitted output algebra must be specified: two local
classical reports, two reduced states, and a joint present quantum output
are different interfaces. Joint present diagnostics are explicitly named.

## Hole contract

- **Proposed statement:** for a finite protected code, characterize exactly
  which larger-carrier isometries preserve its entangled reference, either
  directly or after an explicit recovery. Compose those conditions along
  finite typed paths, and specialize them to authentic arithmetic returns.
- **Initial status:** UNVERIFIED for the combined refinement. Existing
  vectorization, deferred-readout and partial-trace identities are accepted
  antecedents with their earlier proofs; quantum error correction is a
  classical source, not a new discovery claim.
- **Domains and quantifiers:** finite-dimensional codes and finite paths;
  bounded isometries on the arithmetic Hilbert carrier; countable or infinite
  paths only where a separate quantified theorem is provided.
- **Weakest useful form:** a necessary-and-sufficient finite-code criterion,
  one explicit nontrivial arithmetic encoding, and a current-output probe
  that separates coherent mismatch from environmental distinguishability.
- **Dependency cut:** one-step information-preserving extension -> protected
  logical transport under restricted access -> calibrated current diagnostic.
  This does not close the separate prime-pair/normality remainder edge.
- **Leverage:** tell whether the proposed path is a reversible mismatch,
  a correctable interaction, or an actual loss relative to the permitted
  current interface. Identify a realizable repair rather than imposing
  complete absence of interaction.
- **Novelty tax:** the parent already has a CNOT decoherence/reversal example,
  a general two-record overlap, naturality and frame transport. Repeating
  these alone earns no new construction. New work must supply code-level
  characterization, arithmetic coherent sectors/encodings, a separating
  present diagnostic, or a rigorous propagation bound.
- **Known boundaries:** entanglement does not enforce equal arbitrary
  readouts; a relative unitary can rotate a Bell state to an orthogonal Bell
  state without destroying its entanglement; an exact final state cannot
  uniquely determine all past interactions. Ordinary frame changes are not
  time evolution. Both twins can share an incorrect source interpretation.
- **Cheapest falsifiers:** pure phase mismatch versus a dephased pair with
  the same Bell-return probability; identical local marginals; an environment
  recording the logical label; a nontrivial interaction with an independent
  syndrome; same versus differing arithmetic record words; final arithmetic
  output collisions; a failure of fixed-code all-success transport at depth.
- **Preregistered outcomes:** direct factorization is sufficient only after
  its code/frame hypotheses are checked. A successful same-output control
  with distinct targets refutes that interface's sufficiency. Distinct
  current phase quadratures with equal return scores expose a probe gap.
  A protected arithmetic code is a construction result, not an asymptotic
  distribution theorem. Exact degeneracies remain countermodels.
- **Controls and fresh regimes:** exact rational unitary rotations, identity,
  orthogonal and partially overlapping records, invalid phase identification,
  explicit recovery, and literal arithmetic walks independent of derived
  sector formulas. Use additional return parameters and longer schedules
  after the symbolic statements are fixed. Never tune a universal claim on
  finite outcomes or report synthetic counts as experimental evidence.
- **Candidate proof languages:** Stinespring isometries and Kraus maps;
  Knill--Laflamme conditions; typed code embeddings; exact path products;
  environmental Gram matrices; integer divisibility and finite record words.
- **Finite boundary:** there is no physical quantum experiment in this task.
  Exact symbolic probabilities differ from finite sampled observations.
  No finite report proves equality on every infinite arithmetic input.
- **Next lamp:** source-faithful current observables with a proved target
  separation/error theorem and controlled computation/access budget.

## Later steering: external carrier bond and ER=EPR

Before publication the user adds an outside view: the two observers may be
the endpoints of one carrier defect or wormhole, sharing information as
they walk, equivalent to the inside entangled-observer description.

Faithful testable reading: build an exact outside **encoded bond** and a
dictionary preserving the inside twins' current observable probabilities,
then ask whether it also preserves their permitted interventions and
communication resources. Do not silently strengthen a state identity into
an operational signalling channel or an Einstein--Rosen geometry.

- **Candidate exact equivalence:** an isometric encoding sends the logical
  twin bond to a carrier state; pulling back each declared present observable
  gives the same expectation. Coherent encodings transport the bond through
  both paths. This is a representation theorem with specified access maps.
- **Operational question:** can one endpoint choose a new message that
  changes the other endpoint's present output while they remain restricted
  to local operations on a shared entangled state? Compare with a supplied
  channel or joint coupling. The allowed interventions must match under any
  claimed equivalence.
- **Cheapest falsifier:** two chosen input bits, identical receiver marginals
  in the entanglement-only model, and distinct receiver outputs through the
  proposed message wire. An outside drawing cannot erase this difference.
- **Positive construction:** teleportation with explicit Bell resource,
  outcomes and correction messages, or its fully coherent implementation
  with actual controlled interactions/communication. Keep normalized
  cup/cap factors and all branch probabilities; no silent postselection.
- **Shared-memory guard:** two disjoint endpoints cannot both implement an
  arbitrary full noncommuting logical algebra on a code through commuting
  local physical algebras. State-only mirror identities are weaker and do
  not imply arbitrary actions or a communication capability.
- **Physical boundary:** compare freshly retrieved ER=EPR and traversable
  wormhole papers with the actual model. A metric, gravitational dynamics,
  causal dictionary and applicable physical regime are not supplied by a
  generic finite tensor bond or by the existing arithmetic thermofield pair.
- **Outcome labels:** exact state/observable construction; conditional
  protocol-level channel simulation; refutation of entanglement-only free
  messaging if the intervention probe distinguishes it; gravitational
  realization UNVERIFIED. The source-faithful arithmetic estimate remains
  its own open dependency.
