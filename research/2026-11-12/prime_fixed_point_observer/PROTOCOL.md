## Target and scope

Construct a category with an explicitly specified operation whose fixed
arithmetic objects are precisely primes (with 1 separately identified), and a
finitely described, indefinitely productive computation reaching every prime.
Use the existing SUCC additive/multiplicative distinction to determine what the
construction actually supplies to prime-midpoint / Goldbach observers.

Initial truth state: candidate constructions, not yet execution-verified.
Binary Goldbach, pi normality, and RH remain OPEN. Existing theorem status is
not changed by this dossier.

## Candidate statements

1. In the divisibility poset category, `F(n)=n^(tau(n)-1)` is an endofunctor
   with fixed objects exactly 1 and the primes; all quantities use finite
   divisor arithmetic. This is an elementary construction, not a new
   primality theorem or a fast algorithm.
2. In the category of decidable represented sets and total computable maps,
   the least-divisor map is an idempotent with prime equalizer. It is not
   silently promoted to a functor of the divisibility poset.
3. A finite recursive source program unfolds a chronological observer which
   emits each prime exactly once. Prove totality of every finite demand and
   completeness for each prime; no infinite elapsed computation is used.
4. A factorization-incidence operator has a prime kernel. Its resolvent
   gives a bounded contraction whose fixed subspace is the prime span.
   Derive an exact finite error for a two-register, fixed-sum observer.
5. The reflection commutator records missing prime overlap exactly. A strict
   all-target estimate that is merely equivalent to Goldbach is labeled a
   reformulation, not a proved missing edge.

## Dependency cut and novelty tax

Finite divisor source -> prime fixed-point object -> productive prime stream
-> sum-constrained pair observer -> **missing uniform nonempty-pair theorem**.
The first three arrows may be unconditional. Their existence alone cannot
close the last arrow. The weakest useful intermediate target is a certified
cellwise overlap inequality from independent marginal counts, with explicit
source, resource budget, and quantitative error.

The anticipated payoff is a rigorously typed interface and exact finite
certificates. No historical novelty or improved asymptotic complexity is
claimed. A diagonal spectral recoding of pair counting pays no Goldbach
novelty tax by itself.

## Discriminating probes (before execution)

Pass requires exact agreement of divisor-based predicates, a separate marking
sieve, and the recursive stream. The entire prescribed finite region must be
tested, with the bound recorded. A single mismatch fails the implementation.

Positive controls: 2, 3, prime squares, semiprimes, prime midpoint, and an
unequal prime pair. Boundary controls: 1, 4=2+2, zero requested stream outputs,
and invalid integer/type inputs. Spectral checks use exact rational numbers.

Hostile controls:

- An autonomous orbit of a fixed-point map remains at the first fixed point.
- A decidable infinite counterfeit atom set has a finite retraction and a
  productive enumerator but misses some even sums.
- Finite factor screens accept a square of an omitted prime.
- Counting beta expansions as distinct witnesses changes syntax count without
  changing semantic pair count.
- A positive soft spectral trace need not contain a zero-energy eigenvector.
- `P` and `J P J` commute for every indicator source, so their commutator
  cannot detect Goldbach; the informative operator is `[P,J]`.
- Altered source certificates, stream omissions, wrong prime-1 policy, and
  off-by-one square cutoffs must be rejected by independent replay.

Fresh evaluation region: after a small construction window through 256,
test arithmetic objects through 10,000 and all even targets through 2,048;
use additional selected targets beyond that window. This is an exact finite
implementation test, never a universal number-theoretic proof.

## Boundaries and next lamp

Fixed arithmetic objects, fixed programs, and fixed vectors are distinct.
Finite program size does not imply finite state or bounded memory. General
lambda recursion is not an inhabitant of the existing strongly normalizing
simply typed compiler. State the evaluation strategy and arithmetic delta
rules explicitly; a Python generator is not by itself a beta evaluator.

Next verdict-changing lamp: a source-derived, uniform estimate controlling
prime overlap (or its exact reflection commutator deficit) with hypotheses
weaker and independently established from the desired all-even conclusion.
