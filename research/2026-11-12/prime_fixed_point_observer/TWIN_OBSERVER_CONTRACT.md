## User proposal and faithful reading

Embed the current carrier in a larger system, prepare correlated twin
observers at opposite boundaries, transport them to a common interface,
and compare their internal models. Preserve all branch information during
transport; perform the declared comparison at the interface. Determine
which hypotheses make agreement a theorem and which information the
comparison actually certifies. Literal quantum entanglement and physical
collapse require a separately specified physical model.

## Hole contract before the probes

- **Proposed statement:** a faithful larger-carrier realization with
  coherent local transports admits a two-sided, cut-independent comparison;
  sufficiently rich comparison data certify the declared representation
  relations. Lossless transport must retain the information discarded by
  the compressed arithmetic returns.
- **Current truth state:** UNVERIFIED as a combined construction. Standard
  duality, dilation, naturality and gluing results are candidate ingredients,
  not evidence that unconditional observer agreement follows.
- **Domains and quantifiers:** finite typed paths and finite-dimensional
  exact linear maps first; then bounded operators on the current countable
  Hilbert carrier, with every infinite-limit claim proved separately.
  A second observer is dual or conjugate only under an explicit transport.
- **Weakest sufficient form:** prove cut invariance for every finite path;
  exhibit an information-preserving extension of each current partial
  return; characterize what vanishing comparison defects determine.
- **Dependency edges:** earlier common root -> whole-map comparison;
  compressed returns -> reversible carrier with retained failure records;
  local comparisons -> globally compatible representation only under
  stated generator, relation and source-identification hypotheses.
- **Leverage:** distinguish real internal agreement from agreement caused
  by projection to the common fixed line, a coarse readout, or a shared
  incorrectly labeled source. Provide a rigorous meaning of postponing
  the final readout to the meeting.
- **Novelty tax:** the October twin-frame and dual-boundary dossiers already
  prove conditional semantic agreement and flux-matched gluing. This round
  must add an explicit lossless extension of the present arithmetic return
  operators and calibrated full-map comparison, not rename those results
  or infer an arithmetic estimate from observer symmetry.
- **Known boundaries:** noninjective maps cannot be inverted on their
  original space; agreement on one root does not imply operator agreement;
  the infinite cylinder has no finite-travel endpoint at infinity; ordinary
  infinite-dimensional Hilbert space has no normalized uniform diagonal
  twin vector. Quantum collapse is not identified with model reconciliation.
- **Cheapest falsifiers:** a rejected return basis input; two maps equal
  on the root and different elsewhere; a sign/holonomy defect; mismatched
  boundary preparations; a shared source relabeling; a finite-window twin
  that misses a defect outside its support; an environment that records
  which route before the observers meet.
- **Controls:** exact identity/isometry cases, rank-deficient rejection,
  a relative phase mutation, and a genuinely separating comparison on a
  complete finite basis. Check both path orientations and the distinction
  between adjoint and inverse. Include source-faithfulness controls from
  the preceding return-family round.
- **Fresh regime:** choose additional rational matrices and arithmetic
  indices after deriving the formulas; use literal path execution and a
  separate implementation where practical. Do not tune a proposed theorem
  on the same instances used to claim validation.
- **Candidate languages:** typed linear representations and natural
  transformations; finite chain duality; Hilbert-space dilation; exact
  vectorization; pullbacks of compatible models; finite-window limits.
- **Computational boundary:** exact finite probes can refute a universal
  statement and check implementations. They do not prove an unbounded
  arithmetic theorem, an infinite observer meeting, physical coherence,
  quantum advantage or a universal truth/hardness scale.
- **Next lamp if unresolved:** state the residual source-specific,
  quantitative observation or compatibility theorem explicitly. Do not
  promote common preparation or shared provenance to independent evidence.

## Later user refinement: present outputs and frame transport

The direct comparison is restricted to model outputs at the present meeting
event. Raw histories from separated patches must not be equated. A historical
record may influence a present output only through a declared interpretation
and transport from its event/frame to the meeting frame. This refinement
precedes the new probes; it is not an inference from a successful test.

Model patches as a base graph/category, fibers as typed observer state or
output spaces, and frame changes as invertible maps. Retain path-dependent
transport and loop holonomy. Do not assume a physical Lorentz metric or
path independence merely from the word spacetime. The comparator receives
only the two current, calibrated outputs in one declared output type.
Internal branch records may make the dynamics reversible without becoming
inputs to that comparator.

Add discriminating controls: numerically different local coordinates for
the same transported output; numerically equal coordinates for distinct
outputs; nonunitary coordinate change with the appropriately transported
metric; an alternate-route holonomy defect; and distinct internal histories
with exactly the same permitted present outputs. A full-map twin diagnostic
is valid only when that richer diagnostic is itself part of the declared
current output protocol.
