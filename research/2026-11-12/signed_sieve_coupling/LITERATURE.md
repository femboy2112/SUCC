# Provenance and priority boundary (checked 2026-11-12)

These are external sources for the **classical ingredients** and warnings. Current project algebra is proved separately in `THEOREMS.md`. This bibliography does **not** assert priority for the cube-root Liouville observation, which has independently circulated in recent Goldbach work.

1. **G. Bhowmik and L. Grimmelt**, *The exceptional set of the Goldbach problem*, **Analysis Mathematica** 52 (2026), 767–808, published September 23, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10476-026-00191-z . Peer-reviewed survey of rigorous almost-all results, major-arc formula, and remaining pointwise Goldbach gap. Its results do not supply this dossier's signed cube-root inequality.
2. **Terence Tao**, *Heuristic limitations of the circle method* (2012), https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2012/05/20/heuristic-limitations-of-the-circle-method/ . Relevant warning about upper bounds and binary minor-arc sign, source modifications, and parity barrier. Heuristic discussion is not itself an impossibility theorem for every analytic method.
3. **Matteo Bordignon**, *An explicit version of Chen's theorem*, *Bull. Aust. Math. Soc.* 105 (2022), 344–346, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-the-australian-mathematical-society/article/an-explicit-version-of-chens-theorem/2E6F5B87A198E00561CF2C21D147346A . Genuine prime + at most two prime factors for sufficiently large even numbers; does not force the signed majority in SFC-04.
4. **A. Mangerel**, *On a Goldbach-Type Problem for the Liouville Function*, *International Mathematics Research Notices* (2024), 11865–11877, https://academic.oup.com/imrn/article/2024/16/11865/7704606 . Liouville additive-sign research; unrestricted Liouville patterns cannot be substituted for prime support.
5. **B. Bahboui**, *Centered Prime Segments, Modular Covering, and the Remaining Goldbach Proof Barrier*, October 26, 2026 preprint, https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202610.2486 . Explicitly studies cube-root rough survivors and Liouville selectors, proving basic finite reductions but not uniform Goldbach positivity. **Not peer reviewed**; cited for antecedent/contemporaneous subject overlap, *not* as mathematical authority for our operator results.
6. **F. Anderson**, *K-CORRECTION 2026: Exact Multiplicity Identities, Moment Bounds, Rough Complements, and the Remaining Barrier to Strong Goldbach*, November 2026 preprint, https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202611.0975 . Another very recent unrefereed investigation of rough complements and parity. Independently flags an unresolved uniform estimate. Do not infer a breakthrough from preprint claim labels.
7. **Internal SUCC:** parent draft PR #600, `research/2026-11-12/prime_fixedpoint_cylinder/` and `research/2026-11-12/scott_relative_defect/`, plus the legacy `prime_interferometer_legacy/` CRT–Haar certificate. The new majority condition is closely related to that existing finite pigeonhole certificate; it is **not** claimed to be a previously unknown fundamental principle.

Classical prime number theorem and its prime-sum partial summation implications are imported in SFC-06 and SFC-07. We do not import RH, Weil positivity, unproved Goldbach asymptotics, an exceptional-zero exclusion, unverified formalizations, or independent quantum hardware dynamics.
