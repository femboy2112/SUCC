# Signed carrier-first Goldbach operator: exact theorems, finite audits, and remaining gap

**Date:** 2026-11-12. **Scope:** arithmetic sequences on integers >=2, finitely supported Hilbert vectors for unbounded additive operators, and bounded diagonal sieve projections. Status is paper/algebraic local proof, not Lean or external peer review. Classical imports are explicitly named.

## 0. Syntax and source

For integer `k>=2`, put `p(k)=1` iff k is a genuine rational prime. The convolution is ordered:

`(f*g)(N)=sum_{a=2}^{N-2} f(a)g(N-a), N>=4`.

Use `H=ell²({2,3,...})` with basis `|k>`, `Delta_+ |N>=sum_{a+b=N,a,b>=2}|a,b>` on the dense finite-support domain. Let `P` be the diagonal prime projection, recoverable from the kernel of the reduced multiplicative divisor coproduct of the parent SUCC dossier. For even N>=4, `D=(P⊗P)Delta_+-Delta_+P` obeys `D|N>=(P⊗P)Delta_+|N>` (N composite), and

`||D|N>||² = R(N)=(p*p)(N)`.

All statements below are independent of any π-normality/RH source. Never identify the prime source with the BBP four-pole source.

## SFC-01. Prime-inclusive z-rough majorant and exact signed polarization (PROVED LOCAL)

Fix real/integer `z>=1`. Define `q_z(k)=1` if k is prime or if k is composite and **no** prime `ell<=z` divides k; set 0 otherwise (and `q_z(0)=q_z(1)=0`). In executable form, initialize all k>=2 to 1 and delete **proper multiples** `2ell,3ell,...` of each prime `ell<=z`, retaining ell itself. Thus q_z>=p, and `c_z=q_z-p` is a 0/1 composite-only mask. Put `s_z=p-c_z=2p-q_z`, whose entries are +1 on primes, -1 on surviving composites, 0 otherwise.

The elementary polarization identities are

```
q_z*s_z = (p+c_z)*(p-c_z) = p*p-c_z*c_z,
          = 2(p*q_z)-q_z*q_z.
```

The mixed terms `p*c_z` and `c_z*p` cancel exactly by commutativity of additive convolution. Hence `L_z(N):=(q_z*s_z)(N)=R(N)-(c_z*c_z)(N)<=R(N)` for every N>=4.

**Sufficient condition:** `L_z(N)>0 => R(N)>=1` (integer ordered count); this does not claim the converse. Indeed `L_z(N)` may be nonpositive even when there are prime pairs.

**One-sided majority identity.** Let `A_z(N)=(p*q_z)(N)` and `B_z(N)=(q_z*q_z)(N)`. Then `L_z=2A_z-B_z`. Let `J_{N,z}={a in [2,N-2] : q_z(a)=q_z(N-a)=1}`. Because k->N-k permutes J, `B_z=|J|` and `A_z=|J intersect Primes|`. Thus `L_z>0` iff **more than half** of the endpoints in the two-sided z-rough corridor are prime. This is a precise carrier-first pigeonhole criterion, closely related to the existing CRT–Haar margin, not an existence theorem independent of prime distribution.

## SFC-02. Source-derived increasing lower operators (PROVED LOCAL)

Let `Q_z,C_z,S_z` be the bounded diagonal operators with entries q_z,c_z,s_z respectively. On `H⊗H` define

`K_z=(Q_z⊗S_z+S_z⊗Q_z)/2 = P⊗P-C_z⊗C_z`.

The second identity follows from `Q_z=P+C_z` and `S_z=P-C_z`. `K_z` is self-adjoint and has diagonal entries +1 (prime,prime), -1 (surviving composite,surviving composite), and 0 otherwise. It is **not positive** in general.

If `z' >= z`, surviving composites satisfy `0<=C_{z'}<=C_z` as nested diagonal projections; hence

`K_z <= K_{z'} <= P⊗P`

as quadratic forms (Löwner order). More exactly let `E=C_z-C_{z'}`, an orthogonal diagonal projection disjoint from C_{z'}. Then

`K_{z'}-K_z = E⊗C_{z'}+C_{z'}⊗E+E⊗E >=0`.

On the additive basis, this gives the exact *nonnegative sieve-increment formula*

`L_{z'}(N)-L_z(N)=2(e*c_{z'})(N)+(e*e)(N)>=0`.

The prime projection is fixed by the **multiplicative** source and the fiber is generated by the **additive** source. On the even finite-support core:

`D*D >= Delta_+* K_z Delta_+` as quadratic forms.

This is a valid lower operator even though K_z is indefinite. It does not give `D*D>=I` without a separately derived signed source estimate.

## SFC-03. Strong convergence is not norm convergence (PROVED LOCAL)

For every fixed k>=2, `c_z(k)=0` once z>=sqrt(k): every composite k has a proper prime divisor <=sqrt(k). Thus `C_z` and `C_z⊗C_z` converge **strongly** to zero on finite-support vectors; by uniform boundedness (`||C_z||<=1`), the convergence extends to H and H⊗H. So `K_z -> P⊗P` strongly.

But for every finite z, choose a prime r>z. Then r² is a surviving composite: `c_z(r²)=1`. Therefore `||C_z||=||C_z⊗C_z||=1` for every finite z, and

`||K_z-P⊗P||=1`.

A finite observer resolves each fixed finite arithmetic fiber eventually; no fixed finite cutoff resolves the infinite carrier uniformly. This is a rigorous operator-theoretic nonuniformity statement and does not imply any Goldbach coverage result.

## SFC-04. Cube-root cutoff and Liouville sign (PROVED LOCAL)

Let `N>=4`, `z=floor(N^(1/3))` (exact integer cube root), and restrict all candidate arguments to `2..N-2`. If `c_z(k)=1`, every prime factor of k exceeds z. Three such factors would multiply to at least `(z+1)^3>N>=k`, impossible. So `Omega(k)=2`, i.e. **every surviving composite is a semiprime** (squares included). Every surviving prime has Omega=1.

Let `lambda(k)=(-1)^Omega(k)` be the actual Liouville function. **Only on the restricted finite index range k<=N**, `s_z(k)=-lambda(k)q_z(k)`. Consequently

```
L_z(N) = - (q_z * (lambda q_z))(N)
       = R(N) - #{(a,b): a+b=N,
                        each a,b a surviving semiprime}.
```

The second term counts ordered pairs. This formula is a signed prime-versus-semiprime certificate; it deliberately cancels prime-semiprime interactions. It does **not** assert the global operator identity S_z=-Lambda Q_z at finite z on all H, since rough composites with Omega>=3 occur at unbounded k.

The open stronger-than-Goldbach conjecture is

`SCG: for every sufficiently large even N, L_floor_cuberoot(N)(N) >0`.

SCG => eventual binary Goldbach; no reverse implication. It is not established analytically. A variant with nonstrict >=0 is insufficient to conclude Goldbach (can be exactly 0).

## SFC-05. Exact Fourier and Haar signed amplitudes (PROVED LOCAL)

Fix even N, z, and write `e(t)=exp(2pi i t)`. For source polynomials `F_q(alpha)=sum_{2<=k<=N-2}q_z(k)e(k alpha)` and `F_s` similarly, character orthogonality gives

`L_z(N)=int_0^1 F_q(alpha)F_s(alpha)e(-N alpha) d alpha`.

This is a **signed** correlation; magnitudes of Fourier marginals alone cannot assert its sign. At a finite N, one can use a sufficiently long roots-of-unity transform with no coefficient alias; short phase grids can count different sums modulo M as if they were N.

To express the same quantity in dyadic Haar coordinates set `a_k=q_z(k)`, `b_k=s_z(N-k)` for `k=2..N-2`; pad both to length `M=2^r`. Then

`L_z(N)=(sum a)(sum b)/M + sum_{I dyadic,|I|>=2} (Delta_I a)(Delta_I b)/|I|`,

where `Delta_I a = sum_{k in left-half(I)}a_k-sum_{k in right-half(I)}a_k`. This is exact Gram/Parseval in the orthonormal Haar basis. Signed bands can be negative. At N=2048, z=12, the finest band contributes +23 but the width-4 band contributes -14; the full sum is 46. (All numbers are exact rationals from the Python implementation.) No arbitrary physical two-slit or nontrivial gauge holonomy is claimed.

## SFC-06. Fixed sieve depth provably fails forever (PROVED LOCAL using classical prime density)

Fix integer z>=2 and let `M_z=product_{prime ell<=z} ell` and `delta_z=phi(M_z)/M_z>0`. Choose even N tending to infinity through multiples of M_z. For each a coprime to M_z, the reflected N-a is also coprime, so both survive q_z. There are exactly `delta_z N` coprime residues a in `[1,N]`; removing boundary a=1,N-1 yields

`B_z(N)=(q_z*q_z)(N) >= delta_z N-2`.

Meanwhile `A_z(N)=(p*q_z)(N)<=pi(N)=o(N)` by the prime number theorem (indeed Chebyshev's weaker upper estimate suffices). Therefore

`L_z(N)=2 A_z(N)-B_z(N) <= 2pi(N)-delta_z N+2 <0`

for all sufficiently large multiples N of M_z. So **no fixed z** can certify all sufficiently large even N by this criterion. Sieve/observer resolution must increase with the arithmetic horizon. This is not a universal lower bound on *how quickly* z must grow for arbitrary N.

## SFC-07. Global average signed energy is unconditionally positive (PROVED LOCAL from classical PNT)

Fix a large X and choose the *one global cutoff* `z=floor(X^(1/3))`. Restrict sources to 2<=k<=X. Write `P_X=#{p<=X}` and `C_X` for the number of z-rough composites <=X. By SFC-04 these composites are exactly semiprimes p*q with `z<p<=q` and p*q<=X. Count by their smaller prime:

`C_X = sum_{z<p<=sqrt X}(pi(X/p)-pi(p-1))`.

Apply the prime number theorem uniformly for p between X^(1/3) and X^(1/2), then prime summation (or partial summation) to obtain

```
C_X ~ (X/log X)*integral_{1/3}^{1/2} du/[u(1-u)]
    = (log 2) X/log X,
P_X ~ X/log X.
```

The `sum pi(p-1)` correction is `O(X/log²X)` and does not change the leading constant. Summing the exact signed source over **all additive output N in [4,2X]** gives

`sum_{N=4}^{2X} L_{X,z}(N) = P_X²-C_X²
 ~(1-(log 2)^2) X²/log²X >0`.

For even N only, the leading term is unchanged: all z-rough semiprimes are odd once z>=2; the lone even prime 2 contributes only O(P_X) odd-sum pairs.

**Crucial boundary:** positive *global* signed energy only ensures some targets carry positive mass; it does not imply `L_z(N)>0` for every N. The toy arrays P={2,5}, C={3} give total `|P|²-|C|²=3>0` but signed coefficient at N=6 is -1. A localization/pointwise correlation theorem is still required.

## SFC-08. Finite tests and phase/alias countermodels (OBSERVED)

The production Python suite is `test_signed_sieve.py`; independent direct trial-divisor implementation is `independent_audit.py`. The C++17 exact-NTT implementation uses prime modulus 998244353 and primitive root 3, with NTT length below 2^23 and all relevant ordered count coefficients <=X<998244353, hence no modular wrap. It cross-checks selected coefficients against direct integer counting within each cutoff band.

Results: all even N<=500000 satisfy SCG (249999 exact integer cases). An independent implementation checks N<=8192 by a different primality and roughness decomposition; 100000 further previously unexamined even inputs above 300000 were an exact holdout. This is **finite certification only**, never a proof of an all-N statement. NTT and direct implementations share the mathematical criterion and are not blind theorem reviews.

Adversarial controls:

- `p'={genuine primes congruent 1 mod 4}` preserves a prime-only subset but lacks every sum divisible by 4; if S is constructed as `2p'-q`, the lower inequality correctly becomes nonpositive there. The Liouville cube-root formula is **not** preserved by replacing p with p': the removed genuine primes sit in the counterfeit composite complement.
- At N=8192,z=2, the true R=152 but L=-2042. A positive witness count does not force the coarse signed minorant positive.
- The apparently cheaper cutoff floor(N^(1/4)) yields **8 failures** <=500000: `(N,L)` = `(12,-1)`, `(14,-1)`, `(14318,-1)`, `(14348,0)`, `(80552,-2)`, `(81878,-1)`, `(82178,-1)`, `(82862,-2)`.
- At N=20, ignoring prime restoration at the sieve boundary invalidates `q>=p`, destroying the lower-bound proof.
- Extra arbitrary beta expansions, finite-angle aliases, and coarse matched Fourier magnitudes are not source-derived independent Goldbach witnesses.

## Explicit next theorem / proof debt

Show a prime-source-derived estimate, uniformly for every sufficiently large even N, of the form

`2 sum_{p prime, 2<=p<=N-2} q_floor_cuberoot(N)(N-p)
 -sum_{2<=a<=N-2}q_floor_cuberoot(N)(a)q_floor_cuberoot(N)(N-a)
 >0`.

A quantitative stronger target is `>=c N/log²N` for some c>0, all sufficiently large even N. The first sum is a genuine prime in a z-rough-complement counting problem; the second is a pure two-sided sieve count. The known sieve parity obstacle, exploding CRT modulus at z=N^(1/3), and the signed minor-arc source correlation are all unresolved. A successful proof would need fresh arguments beyond polarization, finite tests, or generic positivity. Hostile nonprime and low-resolution models must survive every proposed estimate.
