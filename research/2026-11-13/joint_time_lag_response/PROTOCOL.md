## Hole contract

The authenticated source is exactly `duck_bbp.forcing`, with U_0=0 and
Y_n=U_n+T_n=16^n*pi/16. The proposed statistical statement is decay of
the signed zero-padded Fejer response F_(B,H,h) on every sufficiently late
shell, for every fixed nonzero integer h. That statement is OPEN and,
across all modes, already equivalent to normality by IAD-02. Choosing H
does not pay its novelty tax. A uniform saving sufficient for R0 also
remains OPEN. The question is whether the joint field admits an additional
source estimate or a better source-preserving instrument.

Three independently checkable obligations precede any statistical claim:

1. Prove a finite inverse-stability inequality from adjacent-lag records
   plus an available source anchor. Test the claimed threshold and the
   fifteenfold ambiguity.
2. Resolve IAD-02's outstanding question about absolute lag averages using
   positive definiteness of the squared correlations. Do not confuse a
   single-mode statement with an all-mode normality equivalence.
3. Quantify rational-to-completed transport with its signed lag ordering
   retained; test exactly the original forcing, not a smooth surrogate.

These can pay for an inverse estimate, a resolved analytic distinction,
or a source transform. None alone is a source-selected cancellation bound.

## Predeclared finite probes

- Development source shells: B=16,32,64; H=2,4,8 (H<=B), h=1,2,15.
- Held-out source shells: B=97,193,389; H=3,11,31 (H<=B), h=1,2,15.
- Exact Gaussian controls: constant, alternating, period-four rotation,
  and deterministic irregular words on {1,i,-1,-i}; lengths 5,17,53;
  compare direct lag sums with independently accumulated padded windows.
- Inverse stability: N=1,2,7,19, epsilon=1/32; all fifteen aliases;
  sharp failure at epsilon=1/17, seed difference 1/255.
- Source certificate: exact rational recurrence from zero; independent
  four-fraction forcing comparison; rational Machin/Taylor character
  enclosures already in oily_signed_certificate. Completion uses proved
  Green-tail bounds, never floating pi digits as authority.
- Hostile controls: completed zero, fixed point 1/15, period-two point
  1/255, changed seeds with identical lag records, and a phase/conjugation
  mutation. A finite implementation cannot certify an infinite Bernoulli
  law; its counterexample is proved in the manuscript.

Pass means exact inequalities and enclosure comparisons hold. Fail means an
exact counterexample or a broken outward enclosure; record it before repair.
Ambiguous means a wide interval or merely finite statistical agreement.
No shell is used to choose a new universal constant or a distributional law.
The source finite results certify only their stated B,H,h. All infinite
claims require the displayed proofs and retain their imported premises.

The constructor, translator, and adversary share a model family. Distinct
derivations and implementations are useful audits, not external refereeing.
