## B. The precise mathematical wall

The sole authenticated source is

\[
r(n)=\frac4{8n-7}-\frac2{8n-4}-\frac1{8n-3}-\frac1{8n-2},
\quad U_0=0,\quad U_n=16U_{n-1}+r(n),
\quad U_n+T_n=16^n x,\quad x=\pi/16.
\]

The all-mode Weyl gate is
`N^-1 sum_(0<=n<N) e(h16^n x) -> 0` for every fixed nonzero integer h.
The first open rung is

\[
\exists\eta>0, B_0\quad\forall B\ge B_0:\qquad
\mathcal A_B:=\frac1B\sum_{B\le n<2B}[1-\cos(2\pi16^nx)]\ge\eta.
\]

Exact completion and summable tail transport are already proved. Positive
reconstruction and phase evaluation are not the missing arrows. The missing
arrow is a **source-selected signed inequality with a uniform statistical
margin**. Normality implies R0; R0 does not imply normality. Main XXV proves
that R0 forces a restricted radix exponent below one, but that exponent bound
is neither established for pi here nor sufficient by itself for R0. The
ordinary irrationality measure has different quantifiers.

The new balanced-carry dictionary is particularly direct. Put
`K_n=floor(16^n x+1/2)`, `e_n=16^n x-K_n`, and
`a_n=K_(n+1)-16K_n in {-8,...,8}`. If
`C_B=sum_(B<=n<2B-1) a_n^2`, then

\[
\frac8{289}C_B\le B\mathcal A_B
\le\frac{2\pi^2}{225}(C_B+64).
\]

Thus R0 failure requires a sequence of shells with only o(B) nonzero balanced
carries. On a set of density tending to one, it simultaneously forces very
small real errors and divisibility of K_n by growing powers of 16. The growth
rate need not exceed log B. This is why an ordinary long-run contradiction
does not settle the density problem.

The obstruction is principally a **missing proof pathway** for the selected

## F. The smoking gun, and its limitation

The strongest archival coincidence is the repeated loss of a **relative
orientation**, not lack of source identification. Prime-only averaging loses
the accumulating complementary phase. Ordinary Gamma products lose the
chronological weights. Equal-scale loops lose the seed. Neutral higher-arity
products retain only frequencies divisible by 15. Marginal Haar data lose the
joining. These failures have explicit maps and controls; they are not merely
similar metaphors.

The small new instrument preserves one needed orientation cheaply:
`e(a/16)(2y-1)^j` couples the leading digit to its own future, and only six
moments already give a sub-10^-7 truncation error. The phase-decorated Oily
Gram and the chronological Gamma product show that an object retaining the
source, order and phase was already present in the archive. The unresolved
question is a bound on its actual response.

The strongest reason this may still be a red herring is also exact: keeping
all information does not force cancellation. The coherent counterseed carries
the same forcing geometry, and the new null source has the same full isometry
law. A proof needs a use of the *literal original source and seed* that those
controls fail. No collection of relabelings can substitute for that inequality.
