## Documentation state and unresolved obligations

Architecture contains historical “staged” and “not yet merged” wording even
where the corresponding module is present in the inherited tree. These phrases
describe their source-era entries; they are not a current Git branch inventory.
Likewise, Roadmap's earlier VOYAGER “not formalized” wording is explicitly
superseded by its later kernel-checked update. Neither should silently lower or
raise the scope of a particular proof receipt.

The major missing arrow remains **mathematical sufficiency → lawful acquisition
on one resource-bounded, possibly disturbed carrier**. CG-10 addresses a fixed
linear schedule, and the earlier finite policy results address finite complete
tables. The general bridge remains open. Full quantum/semantic functoriality,
source-authenticated physical geometry, a computable completion modulus without
regularity, and original-source arithmetic cancellation are also not supplied by
this milestone. These are explicit limitations, not contradictions repaired by
terminology.

No test, Lean check, physical measurement or scholarly-priority validation is
claimed by this reading receipt. The campaign's separate validation artifact
records executed checks and their actual boundaries.
