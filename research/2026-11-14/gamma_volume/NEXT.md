The mathematical volume is now explicit. The next construction must identify what the observer actually receives and which target it wants to predict.

## The precise target

Specify a source carrier `X`, its actual step `K`, a report `P`, and a target observable `h`. Derive the Gamma propagation from that source/probe law, or label it as an observer-designed filter. Then prove either `P F_α(K)=Q_α P` on the realized source domain, or exhibit the pair of indistinguishable source states that obstruct it. Report the cost and error amplification of evaluating `Q_α`.

For the original Oily auxiliary field, the retained coordinate `x` already closes the depth semigroup. A realistic finite observer instead needs either a posterior over `x` or a sufficient statistic for its selected target. A single scalar is sufficient for the authenticated moment sequence in GV-08; it is not sufficient for arbitrary future reports, interventions, or an unknown source class.

## Highest-value discriminating probe

Use two source hypotheses that agree on the received finite reports but differ on the next requested Gamma-depth observable. Evaluate whether an additional source-labelled probe separates them, or whether a proved source restriction removes one rival. Keep the persistent-source and independent-redraw models as a joint-law control: their one-layer marginals are insufficient.

**Pass:** the proposed report carries a source-bound quotient with a valid domain, exact or certified-error evaluation, and a falsifiable residual prediction. **Fail:** an explicit collision survives. **Ambiguous:** the report or probe law is unspecified, or precision cannot separate the candidates. In the latter case the obstruction is access/probe specification, not a theorem that no observer can succeed.

## Keep the two time axes explicit

Depth `α` parametrizes a constructed Gamma semigroup. Arithmetic time `n` parametrizes the original source. A forward Gamma mixture is an average of future step observables. A retarded filter receives past signals. The old two-stage Oily filter has a compulsory `L²` delay. None of these clocks may be substituted for another to claim immediate receipt of an unobserved event.

The original zero-seeded BBP phase/carry problem remains separate: a positive packet or Gamma identity must be connected to that exact signed source by a proved observation/intertwining map before it can support a selected cancellation claim.
