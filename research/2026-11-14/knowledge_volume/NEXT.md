# Next dependency cut: a certified compiler for an observer's future questions

## What closed this round

The fixed-clock question has an exact arithmetic classification, and finite
source tables admit replayable all-horizon profiles. The nonlinear source
exhibits a strict separation between finite report determination, polynomial
construction, piecewise rational evaluation, and stable whole-future control.

The original blanket implication “successful extrapolation implies a new
analytic level” remains undefined until levels are specified. A precise
operational instance survives: a target outside one admitted expression
class becomes computable inside a justified larger class.

## Principal next target — source-bound predictor compilation

**State: OPEN.** Given a source presentation, a report map E, an expression
grammar G, and a requested family of future targets, synthesize either:

1. a decoder and a replayable source identity for a finite target;
2. a reusable descent/composition certificate for a whole family;
3. a source-compatible equal-report/different-target counterexample;
4. an honest unresolved result when neither is constructible in G.

The output must retain the actual source image, singular strata, chronological
indexing, allowed interventions, computational cost and precision requirements.
A certificate should state whether it verifies mathematical source bytes,
a model-relative identity, or actual empirical source adequacy.

### Discriminating ladder

- **Calibration:** recover the exact finite horizon profiles and the rational
  evaluator for T(x,y)=(xy,y). Reject (0,0,1) as a source completion.
- **Fresh challenge:** keep the expression grammar fixed and supply a source
  whose next pullback is outside it. Return an obstruction before extending
  the grammar. Do not let an unconstrained symbolic solver hide the extension.
- **Stability:** accept a source-bound error modulus or exhibit a sequence
  of indistinguishable finite-precision reports with separated target values.
- **Programmable walks:** distinguish a finite-state recognizer of admissible
  action words from a finite generating set. KV-H7 prevents a false universal
  finite-generator requirement.
- **Source authentication:** predeclare a future report whose prediction
  differs between competing source models, then obtain genuinely fresh
  lawful evidence. A model cannot validate itself by replaying its own rules.

### Useful failure products

A certificate of nonmembership in the current grammar is useful even if the
desired extension cannot yet be synthesized. A singular phantom identifies
an erased source constraint. A stability counterexample identifies the bound
or instrument precision missing from a prospective application. An exact
language counterexample prevents overgeneralizing the one-clock theorem.

### Stop and acceptance conditions

Stop a proposed compiler claim when it requires already knowing the answer,
switches source, treats a sampled plateau as a global law, conflates a finite
coordinate with an all-horizon predicate, or hides output length in a unit-cost
operation. Accept only a finite replayable certificate or a separately proved
uniform theorem, with its domain and grammar explicit.

## Arithmetic transfer remains a separate task

For the authentic BBP source, retain U_0=0 and the exact four-pole forcing.
The candidate report must be a function of that source or a justified source-
preserving representation. A future coordinate already has a finite evaluator;
the missing normality bridge is a signed all-scale estimate. A useful next
arithmetic lemma must exploit the source to control that statistic, not merely
repackage finite computability or demand cancellation for nonpointed mutants.

No π normality, R0, RH or universal proof-compiler claim follows from this
round. Those require their own exact hole contract and falsifier.
