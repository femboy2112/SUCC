# From a plane of observations to a volume of understanding

**Leah's SUCC automata proposal — research continuation, 2026-11-14.**

An observer at contextual zero sends programmable SUCC automata to explore
an unknown carrier. Reports enlarge the explored map. Constructions of
constructions then sometimes determine unvisited future points. What exactly
becomes detectable at that next level, and when can those points be known
without walking all the way to them?

**The surviving answer:** a new level can be a certified rule for transforming
and composing what the observer knows. Its detectability is relative to a
source, report, target, and available operations. The distinction between
merely storing more points and acquiring a reusable transformation law is
mathematically real. A successful prediction alone does not establish an
extra physical dimension or a formal jump in the analytic hierarchy.

This round supplies two concrete advances in the SUCC interface, with proofs
and executable checks:

1. **An exact classification of globally predictable times.** They form an
   additive submonoid of nonnegative integers. Every such pattern has a
   finite arithmetic description and a finite deterministic realization.
2. **A strict example of higher operational closure.** Two source reports
   determine every future output even though the polynomial construction
   hierarchy grows forever. Division plus a source-valid singular branch
   supplies a reusable predictor; explicit bounds separate exact prediction
   from stable prediction and from cheap computation.

These are elementary paper proofs and classical synthesis, not claims of
new-to-literature priority. Earlier target factorization, finite observer
algebra, Nerode plateau and predictive-span closure are recovered foundations.

## 1. The first sharp result: foresight can have an arithmetic pattern

Let T advance a fixed source by one SUCC, and let h report what the observer
currently distinguishes. A time n is globally predictable exactly when

\[
h(T^n x)=g_n(h(x))\quad\text{for every admitted source state }x.
\]

The decoder g_n is a construction the observer can reuse. It need not
recover the whole hidden state. A collision pair with equal current reports
and unequal future reports certifies failure.

If times m and n are predictable, their decoders compose to predict m+n.
The complete set H therefore has arithmetic structure. Either H={0}, or
it is generated by finitely many positive strides and eventually consists
of exactly the multiples of a fixed d. This existence theorem works even
on an infinite carrier. Finding the generators still requires justified
source knowledge; finite samples do not authenticate them.

| Declared source/report situation | Predictable horizons H | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| One-step report dynamics already close | 0,1,2,3,… | A single descent law covers all times |
| Four-state reversible cycle, reports 0,0,1,1 | 0,2,4,6,… | A later point can be visible while intermediate ones remain hidden |
| Three-state chain a→b→c→c, reports 0,0,1 | 0,2,3,4,… | Merging can make later prediction easier |
| Finite two-track realization of strides 3 and 5 | 0,3,5,6,8,9,10,… | Two composable certificates cover every time from 8 onward |
| Two tracks permanently split by one step | {0} | No positive horizon is determined by the coarse report |

The classification is sharp: every additive submonoid is realized by a
finite reported source. On a finite reversible source there are no transient
holes: H is exactly dN_0. Full proofs, finite construction and scope controls
are in [HORIZON_THEOREMS.md](HORIZON_THEOREMS.md).

If the automata choose arbitrary labelled action paths, a further distinction
appears: even a finite source can have a regularly describable language of
predictable programs that needs infinitely many generators under concatenation.
KV-H7 gives an exact four-state example. The finite arithmetic generator theorem
belongs to one successor clock; it is not silently extended to every program.

**Load-bearing scope:** this arithmetic law concerns the same full report,
uniformly over its whole declared source domain. A different target or one
currently realized fiber need not have additive predictable times. The tests
include counterexamples to both extensions.

## 2. The second sharp result: a new tool can open an entire future

Consider the supplied source

\[
T(x,y)=(xy,y),\qquad \text{report }x.
\]

The reports are x,xy,xy²,xy³,… . At polynomial depth m the generated
algebra is K[x,xy,…,xy^m]. Every next report lies outside the preceding
algebra, so this construction ladder never closes at any finite stage.

But the first two reports (a,b)=(x,xy) determine everything:

\[
G_n(a,b)=a(b/a)^n\quad(a\ne0),\qquad G_n(0,0)=0.
\]

Two source contacts can therefore be sufficient informationally while the
current language of construction is insufficient indefinitely. Once division
and the correct zero case are admitted, one exact law supplies every horizon.
This is a rigorous instance of moving upward through available constructions.

For already universal SUCC programs, the division routine is programmable
from the original primitives. The result concerns a verified expression
language and its cost, not a mysterious increase in absolute computability.
The new object available for reuse is the certified law and its evaluator.

There is a useful hidden trap. The first three values obey ac=b², but
(0,0,1) also obeys that equation and is not source-realizable. The original
source forces all future values to be zero when x=0. Retaining the actual
source image is essential; a compact equation can forget a decisive distinction.

The source also separates precision from determination. At any fixed n≥2,
(a,b)=(ε^n,ε^(n−1)) approaches the zero report while G_n stays 1.
If the source instead guarantees |y|≤M, then

\[
|\Delta G_n|\le(n-1)M^n|\Delta a|+nM^{n-1}|\Delta b|.
\]

For M<1 there is a uniform bound for the whole future. At M=1 every fixed
future coordinate remains stable, while the entire future in the supremum
metric need not be. The passage from many finite points to one completed
object requires its own uniformity theorem.

The evaluator uses O(log n) exact field operations, compared with n source
steps. Its expanded output can still have O(n) or larger bit length. It is
a genuine declared-cost shortcut, not instantaneous free computation.

All source-image, strict-hierarchy, stability and cost proofs are in
[TOOLKIT_THEOREMS.md](TOOLKIT_THEOREMS.md).

## 3. What the observer has at zero

“No data yet” leaves the observer with rules, a syntax of programs, a source
access contract, and perhaps a class of admissible carriers. These premises
are already structure. The observer must keep separate:

| Component | What expands it | What certifies the expansion |
|---|---|---|
| Actual source record | An executed lawful probe and returned report | Source-labelled evidence |
| Determined targets | Evidence plus valid source constraints | Constancy on the nonempty compatible fiber |
| Expressible consequences | A stronger justified construction toolkit | A new evaluator or proof term |
| Reusable future laws | A source-preserving composition rule | An identity such as ET=\overline T E |
| Stable prediction reach | Quantitative control of errors and singularities | A horizon-dependent or uniform modulus |
| Efficient access | A compiled algorithm and output format | Work/communication/bit-cost analysis |

These components are related but not interchangeable. They are not combined
into a numerical truth score or asserted to be Euclidean dimensions.

If E is the record and F(E) is any deterministic construction of it, then
(E,F(E)) has exactly the same source fibers as E. Iterate this statement for
models of models. The observer can expose consequences that were previously
computationally inaccessible, but it has not received an additional independent
measurement. A sound global source law can be powerful new justified knowledge;
the source of its justification must be recorded separately from a fitted pattern.

On a completely fixed deterministic pointed source with known initial state,
semantic uniqueness can be trivial while finding a useful proof or efficient
evaluator is difficult. This is why the original BBP source is never replaced
by arbitrary seeds in this package. Contextual zero of the observer's record
and the mathematical basepoint of an authenticated source have different roles.

## 4. The infinite stack and the proposed analytic step

A countable supply of finite programs is a reserve of possible constructions.
Its formal union or completion is not itself an executed infinite computation,
nor does a stack of discrete objects become a continuum without a specified
topology, limit and access rule. If the analogy grants a stronger oracle,
its answer and communication powers must be declared as additional assumptions.

The faithful operational version of the analogy is:

> At some finite stage, a source-faithful certificate lets the observer replace
> repeated exploration of a class of targets by a reusable law. That law can
> become an object for the next layer of constructions.

For one particular current record, the exact criterion remains that its
requested target is constant on the nonempty compatible-source fiber. The
global horizon theorem adds a stronger reusable law across all such fibers;
it does not assume every local target has that extra arithmetic structure.

The horizon theorem describes how those laws compose in time. The nonlinear
example shows how a strictly stronger operation set changes what is accessible.
The stability theorem identifies when a whole future is controlled as one
object. Together they give three precise meanings to the emerging volume.

An absolute statement that every correct prediction detects “one analytic
step above” has no defined level relation yet. Interpreted as a strict increase
for every prediction it is false: an already-known constant source or a
previously certified transition can answer further queries without any new
construction level. The repaired claim requires a target proved outside the
old toolkit and a valid construction inside the enlarged one, as in KV-T2.

## 5. Relationship to the existing Principia program

This round extends the observer/source distinction already developed in
[Carrier-relative targets](../../../docs/CARRIER_RELATIVE_TARGETS.md),
[recursive observer closure](../recursive_observer_fixed_point/THEOREMS.md),
and [pointed-zero source rigidity](../seed_source_rank/POINTED_ZERO_THEOREM.md).
It does not relabel their established results as discoveries here.

For the original zero-seeded BBP source, exact finite construction of U_n
already exists. The missing π-normality estimate is still an all-scale signed
distribution statement. A faster finite evaluator, an exact quotient, or an
observer's all-future formula does not provide that estimate. A transfer would
need an explicit source-preserving report and a genuinely useful inequality
on the authentic sequence. No π-normality, R0 or RH claim is promoted.
