## A. Source-conditioned Bayesian/Markov experiments

Replace the deterministic readout with an authenticated likelihood `p(o|x,a)` and source transition `P_X(x'|x,a)`; keep actual input context and update timing. Compute (i) minimum guaranteed-target budget, (ii) minimum expected posterior-entropy budget under a specified prior, and (iii) minimum cost of a source-law-intertwining observation refinement. Show counterexamples where (ii) improves while (i) does not, and where the full possibility supports coincide but the probability laws differ (SUCC EODCS #47). Preserve exact rational probabilities.

## B. Probe cost with destructive measurement and resource accounting

Introduce a finite resource counter: permitted resets, duplicate preparations, source-clock ticks, and readout precision. Compare a destructive single-copy no-go against an explicitly paid second copy. A probe that observes a hidden label must come from the source manifest with a lawful causal port, not be synthesized from the proof target.

## C. Preserving the actual history

Retain a source time/epoch and event log. A state-only Markov model cannot reconstruct two histories that merged into the same present state; add a genuine history-sensitive readout and prove what finite archive capacity or retained source bits make historical identification possible. Avoid assuming unrecorded pre-activation information is available.
