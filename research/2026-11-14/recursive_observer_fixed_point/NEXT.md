# Next executable research frontier — The Self-Model Source Authentication Gate

**Objective:** Find a source where a causal intervention, observation likelihood, and resource cost all arise from an authenticated source-mapped experiment, without inserting a free oracle that names hidden state or history.

1. **Source contact:** Specify admissible state preparations, physical source-transition operations, observed outcome instruments and their measurement times. Recover a complete *joint* law `J_a(x,x',o)`, not just independent transition and likelihood marginal guesses. Include a negative source-map control with identical post-state marginals but different pre-transition emission correlations.
2. **Cost:** Measure a calibrated action cost in declared units (time/energy/source resource), and decide whether realized cost is also a readout. If it is, enlarge the observable result alphabet and update on it instead of quietly learning for free. Identify expensive destructive/reset/copy interventions separately from read-only probes.
3. **Self-model:** Make observer memory, source-model parameter, policy and model revision explicit source-labelled states. Predeclare whether the feedback map is continuous/contractive or only set-valued; test for multiple fixed points and cycles, not just one fitted attractive fixed point. Keep posterior belief distinct from source truth/identity.
4. **Finite predictive closure:** Generate invariant predictive-span basis from source-coupled operators, compare a counter-model through a common action/output/cost contract, and record the shortest distinguishing lawful future word. Require a **fresh holdout** rather than validating on a word already used to choose the model.
5. **Causal reach:** For a future event and same future policy, prove an explicit total variation forecast error using a source-grounded filter contraction or independent exact calculation. Mark every extrapolation with its initial history, cost budget, action availability and horizon; an analytic prediction is not actual future observation.
6. **Formal proof:** Mechanically formalize RFO-07 finite predictive-span stabilization and RFO-02 joint Bayes update. Check source/report dimension hypotheses, zero likelihood, normalization, and the n−1 depth bound. For RFO-05, formalize a basic Dobrushin contraction bound with no implicit adaptive-policy comparison.
7. **SUCC/TLICA transport:** Map only independently warranted observer evidence to κ/contact, fixed-target retrospective labels to ρ/identity coupling, tool-relative behavior distinctions to φ, source-map adequacy to σ, and actual lawful probe availability to μ. No scalar posterior is TLICA's truth score.

**Verdict-changing adversaries:** a wrong model with the same internal fixed belief, two models with identical future transcript laws but different hidden histories, a rare posterior branch with increased entropy, a time-varying outcome two-cycle, a destructive probe that reads its old source, and a source-dependent meter that leaks hidden information only if included as a real observed output.

**Non-goals:** no full psychological self-consciousness theorem, quantum nondemolition beyond a declared state class, set-theoretic AoC proof, physical inflation/GR derivation, π normality or RH proof. They remain separate open obligations.
