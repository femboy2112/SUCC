# The pointed-zero construction principle — source contract, not counterfactual seed

**Author correction, 2026-11-14.** SUCC is a construction from a source-typed zero and an authenticated successor, one finite step at a time. A challenger cannot change that initial datum and still call the result the same original object. This is about mathematical definitions and admissible source maps, not an empirical claim that all physical reality begins at numerical zero.

## PZ-01 — Pointed reachable-system rigidity (CLASSICAL; PROVED)

Let \((S,z,\tau)\) be a pointed set with a total endomorphism \(\tau:S\to S\). Define the **generated reachable orbit**
\[
R_z=\{\tau^n(z):n\in\mathbb N_0\}.
\]
If \(F:S\to S\) obeys \(F(z)=z\) and \(F\tau=\tau F\), then
\[
\boxed{\forall n\ge0,\quad F(\tau^n z)=\tau^n z.}
\]

**Proof:** at \(n=0\), \(F(z)=z\). If true at \(n\), then
\(F(\tau^{n+1}z)=\tau F(\tau^n z)=\tau^{n+1}z\). QED.
Thus a basepoint-preserving equivariant endomorphism is the identity **on the generated orbit**, but may act nontrivially on inaccessible or unreachable states. It is not a universal assertion of trivial gauge or absence of representation changes.

**Time-dependent BBP specialization.** Define the source-state carrier \(S=\mathbb N_0\times\mathbb R\) with
\[
\tau(n,u)=(n+1,16u+r(n+1)),\qquad z=(0,0).
\]
By construction
\[
\tau^n z=(n,U_n(0)),\qquad U_n(0)=\sum_{j=1}^n16^{n-j}r(j)\in\mathbb Q.
\]
The theorem says any origin-preserving arrow commuting with this *same fixed* source successor is the identity on all selected states. One cannot introduce \(s=-\pi/16\) by an endomorphism of this pointed generated orbit that keeps its origin fixed.

## PZ-02 — Seed mutants have different mathematical types (PROVED)

For \(\tau^n(0,s)\), the same recurrence's orbit is
\[
U_n(s)=16^n(s+P_n),\qquad
16^{-n}U_n(s)-P_n=s.
\]
The authentic source contract includes \(s=0\), not an arbitrary real. In the original rational finite-state carrier, \(s=-\pi/16\) is also not rational. As a completed symbolic expression it remains a mathematically legitimate solution of the **enlarged** recurrence, but not the selected source realization.

**Counterfactual utility:** it defeats any seed-blind inference quantified over **all** real seeds. It is not a counterexample to a theorem whose actual hypotheses include the pointed original source \((r,U_0=0)\). Likewise a different source polynomial is an adversarial model for a *broader* source class, not for the exact four-pole passport.

**Source-representation caveat:** SUCC ZSC-06 has a true paired source gauge on \((g,s)\): changing the source polynomial together with its rational seed can preserve the completed number. This is a transformation **between source representations**, not a fixed-source seed translation commuting with the pointed successor. Gauge arrows must specify which data they preserve; one must not identify a nontrivial normality-preserving rational-affine transformation with a gauge of the entire authenticated source.

## PZ-03 — Constructibility and the remaining π-normality obligation

The original source yields a finite algorithm for \(U_n(0)\) and for arbitrarily tight rational enclosures of \(x=\pi/16\). This makes requested **finite coordinates** constructible; it does not supply the asymptotic distribution of those coordinates.

The still-open target is, for all nonzero \(h\in\mathbb Z\),
\[
\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^{N}e^{2\pi i h U_n(0)}\longrightarrow0.
\]
A source-faithful proof may legitimately use any theorem about \(r\), the selected zero, its exact source identity, its canonical carries, or the Green tail. It does **not** have to prove the same cancellation for mutant seeds. To obtain normality, however, one must still produce an actual all-large-\(N\) signed estimate.
