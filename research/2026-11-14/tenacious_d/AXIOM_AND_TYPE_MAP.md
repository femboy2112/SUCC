## Algebraic contracts

The Fable geometer paper assumes a **full unital quantale**. Its poset $V$ has arbitrary joins, associative product $\otimes$ with two-sided unit $k$, and product preserves every join separately in each variable. Infima exist as joins of lower bounds. Bottom annihilates on both sides by preservation of the empty join. These axioms type matrix composition, saturation and path-weight valuation. The order on costs is reversed, so a join is a numeric infimum. Joins are idempotent. Products such as addition of costs, multiplication of reliabilities, or concatenation of words can be nonidempotent. Finiteness and commutativity are unnecessary for the paper path arguments.

The standalone Lean file accepts a strictly wider `LaxQuantale`. It requires a preorder with arbitrary least upper bounds, monotone multiplication, both upper distributivity inequalities, both associativity inequalities, both right-unit inequalities, and only $a\le k\otimes a$ on the left. The omitted reverse distributivity inequalities follow from monotonicity and the upper-bound property. Multiplication, joins, and antisymmetry are distinct requirements. Equality in the preorder results means mutual comparison. `PathW.refl` has weight $k$, and `PathW.tail` appends the edge weight on the right.

**Witness of the missing left-unit law.** Take $V=\mathcal P(\{u,v\})$ with inclusion, union, $k=\{u,v\}$, and $A\otimes B=A$ when $B\ne\varnothing$, otherwise $\varnothing$. A product of three elements is $A$ iff both later factors are nonempty, and is empty otherwise. This proves associativity. Unions commute with this test in each slot, $A\otimes k=A$, and $A\subseteq k\otimes A$. Yet $k\otimes\{u\}=k$. The encoded `PathW` gives this larger weight to a one-edge path with raw edge $\{u\}$. The paper's usual singleton-edge weight formula and the free-language valuation below use the full two-sided unit. The inherited Lean declarations continue to hold under their actual fields. A preorder in which all elements compare also satisfies the comparison axioms, even on a nontrivial underlying set with arbitrary multiplication and chosen suprema; every preorder exactness statement there is vacuous. Both witnesses answer the first attack question of the #620 handoff.

| Form of exactness | Type of comparison | Appropriate test |
|---|---|---|
| Source-specific action descent | Unique quotient operation and output on $B$ for every requested action | Compare one-step projected results on each fiber, including blocked status, labels and cost. |
| Rooted word exactness | A specified $x\in X$, terminal observation $b$, possibly all $x$ | Compare the language starting **at that $x$** with that from its image in the may-quotient. |
| Pooled endpoint exactness | Source states range over the starting fiber | Compare the join of source path languages from the whole fiber with the quotient language. A different representative may realize each word. |
| Valued path exactness | A specified $V$ and valuation of the public labels | Apply the unique arbitrary-join and monoid extension of that valuation to each language. One valuation can erase word distinctions. |
| Edge-specific valuation | A separate generator for each individual edge occurrence in the graph (repeated traversals use that edge's generator) | This changes the signature. Distinct hidden edges are distinguishable even when they share all public labels. D3 characterizes the resulting stronger demand. |
| Quantale must-bound | Meet over all representatives of the join of next-edge weights | This gives an order bound. A single route attaining that bound requires an attainment hypothesis. |

The interference and signed-response models in both papers require ordinary linear addition. A positive path cost, a signed response, a target value, and a recorded event are separate mathematical types. The detailed downward maps, strict examples, and provenance of the geometric equalities are in [Descent proofs](DESCENT_PROOFS.md).
