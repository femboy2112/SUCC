# Descent from actions to weighted paths

**Question.** Which quotient-path equalities characterize the partial action quotient in #619? The comparison uses #620's CG-6 graded-language observation, QG-2–4, QG-6–10, and the previously established SUCC PAP-01–05 and `FutureSafeForgetting` results. D1–D4 below give paper proofs. The accompanying finite probe checks the strict examples and one declared finite class. Its output and the original formal certificates concern different statements.

## D1. The observable alphabet and the rooted equivalence

Let $q:X\twoheadrightarrow B$ be a proposed observation map. Give states a current report $r:X\to D$. A deterministic instrument $a\in A$ either blocks or produces $(x',o,c)$, where $o$ is the step receipt and $c$ is an exact charge. The case of a Moore-only interface uses constant $o$. For this section a blocked action has no successful edge and stops a requested run. At most one successful result exists at each $(x,a)$. Give a successful edge its atom $\lambda=(a,o,c)$ in a disjoint union $\Sigma$ of operation-indexed labels. Its endpoints retain their $q$-reports. An initial report is kept independently, even for idle states. If refusal is public, its marker follows at the first unavailable action. Decorations name the entire declared contract; erasing one changes the theorem.

The source language $L_x(b)\subseteq\Sigma^*$ consists of successful words from $x$ reaching some state over $b$. Write $L_Q(d,b)$ for the same language on the **may** quotient, with a $\lambda$-edge $d\to b$ iff some source $\lambda$-edge has those projected endpoints. The empty word belongs when the start report equals the terminal report. In the quotient, different witnesses for successive edges may live at different representatives.

**Theorem D1 (all-start equivalence).** Given $r=\bar r q$, the following conditions are equivalent.

1. Every partial action factors uniquely through $q$, preserving its receipt and charge. In particular, enabledness and the next report are constant on each current fiber for each action.
2. Every successful labelled quotient *path*, with its complete list of intermediate report vertices, lifts from **every** representative of its initial class. A failed action is reflected by absence of a successful edge in the source and quotient.
3. $L_x(b)=L_Q(qx,b)$ for every $x,b$.
4. Equality in (3) holds after intersection with the words of length one for every $x,b$.
5. For each label $\lambda$, value all edges with that label by the binary relation $\{(0,1)\}$ on $\{0,1\}$ and all other edges by $\varnothing$. For **every** $x,b$, the $(0,1)$ entry of the resulting source-to-fiber path value equals that of the may quotient. Matrix products compose relations in path order.

**Proof.** Under (1), the unique labelled quotient edge from a requested action at $qx$ has exactly the source's lifted result from $x$, or both sides block. Induct on the path to get (2), including all intermediate reports and the full charge sequence. Every source path projects, so (2) gives (3). Restriction gives (4). For a fixed class $d$ and action $a$, if a representative has an enabled result $(x',o,c)$, then the one-letter word $(a,o,c)$ reaches $qx'$ in the quotient. By (4) this same edge can be taken from every representative of $d$. Determinism forces their projected next report, receipt and charge to be identical. If no representative is enabled, put the blocked result at $d$. This gives (1) and surjectivity gives uniqueness. The test relation in (5) has square empty. The identity has no $(0,1)$ entry. Thus precisely the one-step paths with label $\lambda$ contribute at $(0,1)$, and (4) and (5) are equivalent. The empty source and empty alphabet give the same statements vacuously after the separate $r$ condition. ∎

With the original triples `State.output()` as $r$ and constant $o$, (1) is exactly the existing Astra PO-Q1/SUCC partial Moore contract. Readout-before-transition Mealy instruments put their output in $o$ instead. Existing Fable CG-6(ii) already identifies one-step lumpability via degree-one language extraction. The explicit recovery of public costs, source/task declarations and refusal, the finite relation test, and the two start quantifiers fix the previously open wording of that interface. Registering all letters does not make the whole counterfactual language a single performed record.

**D1b. Four values are sufficient and necessary for a single universal test algebra.** Take positions 0 and 1, their diagonal $I$, and $E=\{(0,1)\}$. The four relations $\varnothing,I,E,I\cup E$ form a unital subquantale under union and chronological composition: $E^2=\varnothing$ and $I$ is the unit. D1(5) uses only these values. Thus for any public alphabet, testing all its one-letter valuations in this **fixed four-element quantale** detects every failure of rooted decorated-action descent, assuming the state reports already factor. In particular, equality under *all* valuations into this one algebra characterizes descent.

Four is minimal among fixed unital quantales, even if the tester may use *every possible* public-letter valuation into its chosen algebra. Every lattice of at most three elements is a chain. In a nontrivial unital quantale the unit cannot be bottom by annihilation. If the unit is top, the empty path dominates every value in a one-report quotient, even when the source has only an $a$-loop at $u$ and merges it with an idle $v$. If the unit is intermediate, there are exactly three elements $0<k<T$. The products with $0$ or $k$ are fixed, and monotonicity gives $T=T\otimes k\le T\otimes T\le T$. Every element is therefore multiplicatively idempotent. Take the deterministic unary cycle $0\to1\to2\to0$ with blocks $\{0,1\}|\{2\}$. The action from the first block has two projected destinations, so descent fails. Both the source and quotient have a positive-length walk from each start to every report, including a positive return. For any letter value $v$, each positive walk has value $v^n=v$. The two values at report $b$ therefore both equal $v$ off the initial report and $k\vee v$ on it. The trivial quantale also separates nothing. This exhausts all smaller algebras and proves minimality. ∎

D1b is a comparison result with an extremal statement, beyond the inherited degree-one implication. Its testing privilege is explicit: valuations may vary by public symbol, and pure mathematical equality of the corresponding rows is available. Computing acquired data or minimizing the number of physical queries are separate problems.

If several successors for an action are allowed, (4) still says that each representative can match every labelled successor **class**. Induction still lifts paths but yields a relation-valued quotient. A single-valued quotient action requires the determinism premise. If $r$ is not declared separately, two idle states with different reports but identical $q$ give identical path languages and fail PO-Q1. If $c$ is erased from $\lambda$, two states in one block with the same action self-loop of charges 1 and 2 have the same naked language $a^*$ and fail the charge contract. A single absorbing refusal convention requires the terminal/absorbing hypothesis in [the type map](AXIOM_AND_TYPE_MAP.md).

## D2. The universal valuation theorem and two starting conventions

Use a full unital quantale $(V,\vee,\otimes,k)$. Matrices have source **rows** and target **columns**. Thus $(F;G)(i,j)=\bigvee_h F(i,h)\otimes G(h,j)$ and a chronological word $a_1\cdots a_n$ contributes $v(a_1)\otimes\cdots\otimes v(a_n)$. For a valuation $v:\Sigma\to V$ write

$$H_v(L)=\bigvee_{w\in L}v(w),\qquad v(\epsilon)=k,\qquad v(w\lambda)=v(w)\otimes v(\lambda).$$

This is the unique unital quantale map extending the assignments $\{\lambda\}\mapsto v(\lambda)$. Every language is a union of singleton words, multiplication distributes over these unions, and concatenation of singleton words fixes their products. Conversely these observations prove $H_v$ preserves joins, unit and product. In source or quotient matrices, the star of the weighted adjacency is entrywise $H_v$ of the language star: expand the finite products by distributivity and take the join over all path lengths. This requires a fixed value per public letter. Different hidden occurrences do not silently receive different weights.

**Theorem D2 (universal test).** For any two source/quotient path languages $L,L'$ compared with the **same** endpoints, the following are equivalent:

1. $L=L'$.
2. $H_v(L)=H_v(L')$ for every unital quantale and every valuation of the alphabet.
3. Equality holds for every valuation in a finite quantale of binary relations on a finite set.

The statement applies independently to a selected start, all starts, or a joined starting fiber $L=\bigcup_{qx=d} L_x(b)$. These are three different quantifications over starts.

**Proof.** (1) implies (2) by substitution, and (2) implies (3). For (3) to (1), take $w=w_1\dots w_n$ in a symmetric difference. On positions $0,\dots,n$, assign a letter $\lambda$ the relation $\{(i-1,i):w_i=\lambda\}$. A word sends 0 to $n$ in this representation iff it equals $w$, since a successful run must take exactly the listed forward transitions. When $n=0$, all letter relations are empty, and the identity alone contains $(0,0)$. Membership of $(0,n)$ distinguishes $H_v(L)$ and $H_v(L')$. Thus even an infinite alphabet and unbounded languages admit a finite witness for each failed equality. ∎

For all starts of a deterministic source, D1 reduces D2's universal family to the two-position test for single letters, on the fixed four-element subquantale of $\mathrm{Rel}(\{0,1\})$ in D1b. For pooled endpoints the one-letter comparison is true by definition, even on an inexact source. In that setting the least degree witnessing an invented word can exceed one.

**Equivalent pooled matrix criterion.** Let $F$ be the singleton-language adjacency on the source and let $\Gamma(x,d)=\{\epsilon\}$ when $qx=d$ and $\varnothing$ otherwise. Put $Q=\Gamma^{\mathsf T};F;\Gamma$ and $S=\Gamma^{\mathsf T};F^*;\Gamma$. Pooled universal exactness is exactly $Q^*=S$, equivalently $S;S\subseteq S$. Indeed $S\subseteq Q^*$ by projecting paths. Surjectivity makes $S$ reflexive and it contains $Q$. If $S$ is transitive the least-closure property gives $Q^*\subseteq S$; equality itself implies transitivity. In rooted notation the stronger equality is $F^*;\Gamma=\Gamma;Q^*$. Replacing the left start by $\Gamma^{\mathsf T}$ joins all representatives and weakens the comparison. Fable Geometer CG-12(3) already gives pooled transitivity for general quantales.

## D3. When weights refer to individual edges

There is a stronger literal reading of “for every weighting”. Give each concrete edge $e$ its own independent weight, with parallel edges distinguished, and let the quotient join them by endpoints. For this theorem use the edge set $E$ itself as alphabet. The stronger input declares access to individual edge names.

**Theorem D3 (edge-specific boundary).** Universal weighted exactness of **pooled** endpoints holds iff

$$q(t(e))=q(s(f))\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t(e)=s(f)\qquad(e,f\in E),$$

where $s,t$ are source and target of an edge. Universal **rooted** exactness at all starts holds iff every block of cardinality greater than one consists entirely of vertices with no outgoing edges.

**Proof.** For pooled necessity, a quotient word $ef$ exists whenever the displayed reported endpoints coincide. The only source walk with exactly those two edge names must concatenate $e,f$ physically. The three-position D2 test isolates $ef$, even for a repeated edge $e=f$. Sufficiency follows by concatenating every consecutive pair of a nonempty quotient word and choosing its first source as the pooled witness. Empty words use surjectivity. For rooted necessity, if a nonsingleton block contains the source of $e$, choose a different member of that block as root. It cannot take the uniquely named edge $e$. The one-letter test detects the failure. Conversely all active quotient starts are singletons, and after each lifted edge either the path ends or the next active class is again a singleton. Induction lifts every rooted word. ∎

As a concrete distinction, let $u$ and $v$ each carry its own public $a$-loop in one block. The public action descends and both rooted public languages equal $a^*$. Independent names for the two loops introduce a start-sensitive question. The second loop's name is impossible from $u$, and their alternating edge-name word is impossible even with pooled starts. Consequently an unrestricted choice of hidden edge weights does not follow from public-label action descent. The pooled classification also gives a finite complexity reduction: a violation already has a two-edge certificate, independent of the graph's size. This statement and the bounded relation tests are specific additions in this comparison; no external priority is asserted.

## D4. Quantale closure, repair and the selected-start obstruction

Here $R:X\times X\to V$ is an arbitrary weighted relation over a full unital quantale. Work first on $B=q(X)$. Define $R^*$ as the join of all finite path products, including empty paths. Let $p_x(b)=\bigvee_{qz=b} R^*(x,z)$, let $Q$ be the may quotient, and put $N(a,b)=\bigwedge_{qu=a}\bigvee_{qv=b}R(u,v)$. For any $R$-potential $f$ the inequality $f(u)\otimes R(u,v)\le f(v)$ iterates along every source path. The row $R^*(x,\cdot)$ is itself a potential and has value at least $k$ at $x$, so it is the least such potential. The following reconstruction records the exact mathematical authority of #620 rather than upgrading its paper appendices to Lean theorems.

**Sandwich (Fable QG-1,2,4 / Geometer CG-7).** Write $M(a,b)=\bigwedge_{qx=a}p_x(b)$, $S(a,b)=\bigvee_{qx=a}p_x(b)$. For $a=qx$,

$$N^*(a,b)\le M(a,b)\le p_x(b)\le S(a,b)\le Q^*(a,b).$$

Projection gives the rightmost inequality term by term. For each representative $u$ of $d$, the common one-step bound $N(d,b)$ lies below its join of edges into $b$. Multiplying by $R^*(x,u)$, distributing joins, and appending one edge gives $p_x(d)\otimes N(d,b)\le p_x(b)$. The least-potential property gives the leftmost bound for each $x$ over $a$, hence their meet. Middle inequalities are the lattice bounds. On a larger codomain, these comparisons apply to actual starting rows. A must-row at an empty fiber can be top; a corresponding may-row is bottom. Lower/upper collapse suffices for exactness; it is stronger than exactness. The unit-cost/Boolean PAP-04 chain above has Boolean rooted exactness but no must-edge from the fused class into the terminal class.

**Endpoint criterion and adjoints (Fable QG-3,6,7,10 / Geometer CG-8).** Define $q_!f(b)=\bigvee_{qx=b}f(x)$, $q^*g=gq$, $q_*f(b)=\bigwedge_{qx=b}f(x)$, $Uf=q^*q_!f$, and $Cf=f;R^*$. For all-start exactness, the following are equivalent:

* $p_x=Q^*(qx,\cdot)$ for all $x$;
* the functions $p_x$ are equal within each fiber;
* $Q(a,b)\le p_x(b)$ for every $qx=a$, equivalently $Q\le M$;
* $q_!$ sends source potentials to may-quotient potentials, equivalently $U$ sends source potentials to source potentials;
* $UC$ is idempotent.

For the implications, exactness gives fiber constancy. Fiber constancy gives $Q\le M$ since each edge is below its source closure. If $Q\le M$, for every $c$ expand $p_x(b)\otimes Q(b,c)$ over $z$ with $qz=b$. Bound $Q(b,c)$ by $p_z(c)$ in each term. Concatenation puts the result below $p_x(c)$, so $p_x$ is quotient-closed. Its unit at $qx$ gives $Q^*(qx,\cdot)\le p_x$, and projection gives the reverse. The same argument with a general source potential $f$ yields $q_!f(b)\otimes Q(b,c)\le q_!f(c)$ by the potential bound along every source path. Conversely apply direct-image preservation to $R^*(x,\cdot)$. A quotient predicate is closed iff its pullback is source-closed, by expanding the may-edges. This proves the saturation equivalence. For idempotence, if $U$ preserves $C$-closed values, then $UCf$ is fixed by both $U$ and $C$. Conversely if $(UC)^2=UC$ and $Cf=f$, extensivity gives $CUf\le UCUf=Uf$, hence $Uf$ is closed.

For a single start $x$, exactness iff $p_x$ is closed. Denote quotient closure by $C_Q$. For a closed $g$, $C_Q q_!f\le g$ iff $q_!f\le g$ iff $f\le q^*g$. The left adjoint therefore sends $R^*(x,\cdot)$ to the entire quotient row: $p_x\le Q^*(qx,\cdot)$ and $C_Qp_x$ is closed above the unit at $qx$, giving both bounds. The right adjoint on closed predicates is $I_Q q_*$, with

$$I_Qh(a)=\bigwedge_b h(b)/Q^*(a,b),\qquad c/d=\bigvee\{s:s\otimes d\le c\}.$$

Indeed $I_Qh(a)\otimes Q(a,b)\otimes Q^*(b,c)\le h(c)$ by transitivity, proving closedness, and the empty path implies $I_Qh\le h$. Every closed $g\le h$ satisfies $g(a)\otimes Q^*(a,b)\le h(b)$ and hence $g\le I_Qh$. Thus for closed $g$, $q^*g\le f$ iff $g\le q_* f$ iff $g\le I_Q q_*f$. The left comparison $q_! f\le C_Qq_!f$ is equality for all source potentials exactly under forward exactness. The right comparison $I_Q q_* f\le q_* f$ is equality exactly under backward exactness. For the latter, test $q_*$ on $f(z)=\bigvee_{qu=a}R^*(u,z)$, which is closed and at least $k$ on fiber $a$. Closedness of $q_*f$ forces $Q(a,b)\le f(z)$ for each $qz=b$. Conversely, this bound and source-closedness imply $(q_*g)(a)\otimes Q(a,b)\le g(z)$ for each such $z$ by distributing over the source start fiber. Reversing paths and the order of factors now gives the backward counterpart of $Q\le M$. Left and right cannot be interchanged for a noncommutative product.

**Refinements (Geometer CG-10).** For a partition $P$, write $p_x^P(C)=\bigvee_{z\in C}R^*(x,z)$. If $P'$ refines $P$, its profiles for blocks inside a $P$-block join to the $P$-profile. For any family of all-start exact partitions, two states linked by one pair from a member have equal profiles for the joined partition. Chaining these pairs proves equal profiles on each join block. The discrete partition is exact. Thus the join of all exact refinements of an initial partition is the unique coarsest such refinement. On finite $X$, split each current block by $x\mapsto p_x^P$ until stable. Every exact refinement remains below every stage by the same joining identity, and the stable partition is exact. The constrained coarsest **full-action** refinement analogously agrees with the established behavioral/action partition when the complete label contract is used (D1). For an additional initial partition, include its block as a persistent state report in the refinement. Scalar exactness can yield fewer blocks than the decorated refinement.

Three variants of “on $\operatorname{Reach}(x)$” give distinct answers.

1. **Restrict source first; demand exactness from every state of the restricted source.** Restrict edges to the reachable subgraph, form its own quotient, then apply the preceding all-start proof. This gives its unique coarsest all-start exact refinement. The restriction changes the quotient when formerly merged inaccessible states contributed edges.
2. **Keep the global quotient; demand exactness from every state reachable from $x$.** This can lack a coarsest admissible refinement. Fable CG-10's Boolean countermodel already suffices. Take edges $2\to0,2\to1,3\to0$ and $x=0$, so only 0 is reachable. The partitions $\{0,3\}|1|2$ and $0|1|\{2,3\}$ are both exact at 0. Their join $\{0,2,3\}|1$, chosen as the initial partition, invents block $1$ from $0$. A greatest exact refinement would have to equal that join.
3. **Restrict source first; demand exactness only at the selected root.** For Boolean reachability every block in the restricted quotient contains a state reachable from $x$, so every observation is already exact from $x$. For nonnegative unit costs, a coarsest selected-exact refinement can fail even when all states are reachable. Use the chain $0\to1\to3\to2$ with start 0. Partitions $P=\{0,2\}|1|3$ and $T=0|\{1,2\}|3$ have true and quotient block distances $0,1,2$ in the natural chain order. Their join $J=\{0,1,2\}|3$ invents distance 1 to $3$ while the source distance is 2. Taking $J$ as initial, any greatest exact refinement would coarsen both $P,T$ and refine $J$, and so equal the inexact $J$. This refutes the proposed selected-start canon on the reachable graph. Four states are minimal for this issue with nonnegative costs. On at most three points incomparable candidate partitions join to the single-block partition, which is always selected-exact because its rooted distance is zero. A finite family closed under these joins has a greatest member. See the independent construction in [the countermodel exchange](COUNTERMODEL_EXCHANGE.md).

**Bounds and change of base.** A join-, product- and unit-preserving map $h:V\to W$ commutes with $R^*$ and $Q$ by expansion, transporting exactness at the same start. A join-preserving oplax $h$, with $h(u\otimes v)\le h(u)\otimes h(v)$ and $h(k)\le k_W$, gives $hR^*\le(hR)^*$. The one-step $Q\le M$ test then transports all-start exactness; it need not transport bare selected-start exactness. #620 CG-5 supplies its four-state counterexample, and a strict-unit lax counterexample. A join-preserving map need not preserve meets, so it need not commute with $N$. For any proposed observer geometry $G$, $R^*(x,z)\le G^*(qx,qz)$ for all pairs iff each source edge satisfies $R(u,v)\le G^*(qu,qv)$ iff $Q\le G^*$. The forward direction restricts to edges, and the reverse concatenates bounds using transitivity. Thus $Q^*$ is the least **closed** sound report geometry. A specific uncovered reachability pair may be due to an uncovered edge elsewhere along its path.

**Two surviving limitations of prose.** S-7 of the Fable geometer errata suggests grading a truncated free monoid with overflow $\#$ by $\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\}$ under addition. For a word $u$ of length $L\ge1$ and a letter $a$, $ua=\#$ but $L+1\ne\infty$. The suggested grading is not a homomorphism. Work instead in the grade monoid $\{0,\dots,L,\infty\}$ whose sum becomes $\infty$ above $L$, or simply require that a product of zero or at least two edge labels never equals a single edge letter. This weaker condition proves the degree-one extraction. Second, tropical bounds in QG-4 use infima. On an infinite graph take edges from $x$ to distinct $z_n$ over the same terminal report with costs $1+1/n$ ($n\ge1$). Both coarse and true infimum distances are 1, while every actual route costs more. Positive slack, or an attainment premise (e.g. a finite graph with finite nonnegative edge costs and a reachable target), repairs any deadline claim at the endpoint.

**Relation to open maps.** In the Boolean case use the two reachability *preorders*, with arrows representing existence of a path. Exactness is precisely extension lifting for every finite weakly increasing chain of report classes from a specified representative: for one step $qx\preceq b$ it gives $x\preceq z$ over $b$, and successive lifts give an entire chain. Conversely the one-extension case gives exactness. This proves the assertion for this explicit path convention. Lifting actual labelled graph edges requires D1's stronger conditions. Under general quantale weights an order inequality need not have an attaining edge or path. A claim for an arbitrary category of enriched open maps requires a choice of path objects and morphisms. For the general open-map framework see [Joyal–Nielsen–Winskel](https://doi.org/10.7146/brics.v1i7.21663). For the algebraic language antecedent and completeness terminology see [Kozen](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~kozen/Papers/ka.pdf) and [Giacobazzi–Ranzato–Scozzari](https://www.sci.unich.it/~scozzari/paper/JACM00.pdf).

**Counit and unused reports.** For quotient-closed $g$, $C_Qq_!q^*g=g$ on the image and is bottom off the image: no may-edge enters or leaves an unused vertex. If $V$ is nontrivial this counit is invertible for every $g$ iff $q$ is onto (take a constant top potential to witness necessity). In the one-element quantale every counit is invertible even for a non-surjective $q$. This qualifies Geometer CG-8(4)'s global “iff”.
