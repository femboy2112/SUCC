# Finite-precision prediction with constrained action and no central cell

The previous milestone asked for an executable policy or a checkable obstruction on a single disturbed source. TD6 supplies an exact schedule search for centered sensors and scalar linear prediction. TD3-P supplies a policy tree or finite certificate for bounded rational polyhedral interception, including quantized readouts and a continuous last command. The remaining question is how large a certificate an observer must actually acquire or transmit.

**Proposed next theorem.** Fix a dimension \(n\), a finite rational action/sensor alphabet, a compact rational initial polytope, finitely many closed rational quantizer cells, a rational convex command domain and a rational terminal scalar tolerance. Let the binary-encoded resource budget \(B\) vary. Characterize when there is an exact obstruction to every winning adaptive policy whose size is polynomial in the *input length and \(B\)*, or give a matching family requiring an exponentially large certificate in a specified certificate language. Counting in \(B\) itself already allows a pseudopolynomial dependence on its encoding. Allow physical updates to erase earlier coordinates. Specify whether certificates are trees, shared decision DAGs, or linear inequalities over reachable information sets.

The conditions to compare are protection of a distinguished central cell, an authenticated common low-dimensional invariant statistic, and a bound on the number of exposed directions after composing feasible actions. Induction over a full tree always terminates with the finite budget. That gives existence without the proposed size bound. A local Helly witness with \(m+1\) hidden cases prices one failed terminal command intersection. It leaves the number of distinct earlier observation histories open.

**First discriminating experiment.** Use two-dimensional rational shears and a three-cell quantizer. Enumerate finite budgets, canonicalize each reachable joined-state polygon by irredundant rational inequalities, and compare (i) different records, (ii) distinct polygons, (iii) distinct optimal safe-action answers. Include an aligned central-cell control from TD6, shifted thresholds, and an intervention that destroys one coordinate. A family with exponentially many polygons alone cannot establish an exponential proof lower bound: many polygons may share an answer or a common certificate. A candidate lower bound must exhibit inequivalent continuations, or prove a size bound in a specifically declared proof system. Tests can refute a proposed structural criterion. A universal bound requires proof.

**Independent arithmetic obligation.** The exact longer-window weighted minimax problem is open beyond the first \(2d+1\) forcing values and the strict next-readout gain established here. It is a precision problem. The changed BBP seed has exactly the same forcing at every index. A theorem contributing to R0 would need a signed estimate that distinguishes the authenticated zero-seeded orbit from this control for all sufficiently large shells.
