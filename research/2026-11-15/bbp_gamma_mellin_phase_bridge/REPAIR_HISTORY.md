# Failed calibrations retained, not counted as passes

This research bundle was developed in isolated container Python. Raw first-run diagnostics are summarized here rather than deleted from the audit history. The final stored `TEST_OUTPUT.txt` is the **final corrected exact-code run**; earlier failures are explicitly not misreported as passes.

1. **Initial targeted suite: 28 tests, four false-negative assertions.** `green_source_rational_bounds` converted rational endpoints to binary `float`, collapsing an extremely narrow exact tail interval to an equal rounded number. Three other tests compared mpmath values at the default global precision after computations performed inside a higher-precision local context; subtraction then rounded to approximately 15-digit precision. **Repair:** set global mpmath display/comparison precision to 70 digits for the test file, keep `mp.mpf(numerator)/denominator` rather than `float`, and preserve high-precision equality thresholds. No source formula or theorem was changed. Re-run: 28/28 PASS, then 30/30 PASS after new successor tests.
2. **Expanded source-time suite: 34 tests, one false-negative enclosure comparison.** An exact rational 45-term source tail enclosure had width below the 55-digit quadrature's effective error, so the quadrature approximation lay slightly beyond a mathematically valid interval. **Repair:** use a 30-term rational enclosure for that finite comparison and evaluate the independent quadrature with 60 digits, keeping the bound wide enough relative to numerical accuracy. The independent, exact `Fraction` enclosure itself was not changed. Re-run: 34/34 PASS.
3. **Signed-source phase certification improvement:** Use the exact rational `7h(upper-lower)/2` halfwidth from `2π<7` as the reported phase-error bound, rather than treating an mpmath evaluation of `2π` as a rigorous constant. The complex phase remains a high-precision diagnostic, while the source interval and conservative phase-error budget are exact rationals.

The errors above were test-instrumentation/precision issues, **not** a proof that the new infinite results are true. External mathematical review remains needed.
