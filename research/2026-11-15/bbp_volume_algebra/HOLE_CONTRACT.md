# Hole contract: a source-preserving BBP volume algebra

**Date:** 2026-11-15. **Parent:** draft #635, `e14d46c17a65e1e5f231c619657b9332caa181c0`. **Research branch:** `research/bbp-volume-algebra-2026-11-15`.

## Object and question

Fix the original four-pole forcing `r(n)=4/(8n-7)-2/(8n-4)-1/(8n-3)-1/(8n-2)`, `U_0=0`, and `U_n=16 U_{n-1}+r(n)`. Can expansion into causal-volume, Gamma, and source-labelled operators yield a reusable sound algebra that retains actual signed phases and integer carries?

## Acceptance criteria

1. Exact typed rules, domains, hypotheses and boundary/seed terms; at least one useful source-specific consequence beyond a verbal analogy.
2. A normal-form or executable certificate for an explicitly bounded fragment. No claim of a complete universal decision procedure.
3. A lawful account of the nonlinear phase and floor/carry observations, including any defect or information retained by a richer representation.
4. Independent derivation or implementation and hostile mutations that break the naive substitutions.
5. Explicit division between inherited identities, proved new packaging/consequences, finite observations, and unresolved asymptotic estimates.

## Controls frozen before implementation

- Keep the four signed poles and zero seed exact.
- Compare a changed seed only to test seed-blind claims, never to refute the fixed zero-seed problem.
- Test dropped endpoint terms, swapped noncommuting operations, exponentiation through integration, omitted carry, and projected/averaged source composition.
- Test a second rational forcing to decide which rules are generic and which authenticate the source.
- Any cancellation claim must state whether it is exact algebraic cancellation or a bound on the selected signed temporal sum.

## Live frontier

The inherited Green identity, Gamma/Beta convolution, conditional Oily semigroup and projection memory defect are available. The missing selected signed estimate is not supplied by positive marginal geometry. The current task is a pathway/probe gap in the intermediate algebra, with access to the complete repository and exact finite arithmetic.

## Verdict-changing result

A source-faithful identity that actually lowers the complexity of the complete signed/carry observable, with a certified uniform residual, would advance the frontier. Merely rewriting the original Weyl sum in a larger space does not pay that debt.
