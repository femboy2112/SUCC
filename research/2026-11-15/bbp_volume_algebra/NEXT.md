## A probe that changes the verdict

Before fitting a reduction, freeze its source-sensitive statistic, truncation budget and predicted error. Run the actual four-pole source, the same-forcing changed-seed diagnostic, a removed-pole mutant, a same-endpoint-jet source mutant, and the coherent fresh-prime-root control. A genuine new reduction must reproduce the authentic signed observable within its certified error and identify why the controls do not satisfy its source hypotheses. A small positive marginal error alone is insufficient.

**Pass:** a proof of a uniform signed saving for the original sequence, with a finite independent replay of its premises and explicit convergence/carry bounds.

**Fail:** the reduction deletes a source label, changes the seed, uses the rate-one positive Gamma series on raw U, averages a phase after discarding its integer lift, or predicts cancellation for a coherent control under the same stated hypotheses.

**Ambiguous:** an exact reformulation or finite observed saving with no uniform estimate. Preserve it as such; do not promote it to an asymptotic theorem.
