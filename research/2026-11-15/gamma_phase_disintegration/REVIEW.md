# Differential review and repaired claims

## Proof review scope

The constructor derived the forcing disk and the rational boundary argument.
The adversarial/modal bearing derived orthogonal phase coefficients and
reconstruction bounds. The source bearing derived the uniform detuned residual.
The root rechecked the coefficient normalization, finite product bounds,

## Corrections made before publication

1. **Expectation direction and alias scope.** The earlier informal quotation
   reversed the nonlinear move. An integer-valued coin is blind at the selected
   integer-circle harmonics; its full characteristic function still contains
   its probability parameter. `THEOREMS.md` GP-01 and exact controls repair both.
2. **Novelty tax.** The exact Gamma source and Green mixtures were rediscovered
   during exploration, then located in the October 26 main-branch dossier.
   They were reclassified as inherited rather than claimed as new.
3. **Normalization remainder.** An initial general-frequency extension of the
   Green bound omitted `|(1-D_a)F(c)|`. The root caught this. Away from a lattice
   zero the constant is 14, not 7, in `RESONANCE_THEOREMS.md` (S2). The inherited
   constant 7 remains correct when F(c)=0. The detuning theorem (S3) already
   includes its normalization cost and is unaffected.
4. **Seed typing.** The source-label law is determined by the forcing. The
   zero seed is separate arithmetic data. An early modal note suggested it
   could be inserted into B's law; the root and cross-review both rejected
   that wording and the final proofs distinguish these objects.
5. **No universal frequency identification.** No theorem forces the arithmetic
   target to be encoded with source log-frequency `omega=16^n`. Scalar inverse
   costs are explicitly restricted to the declared representation.
6. **No global Green nonvanishing.** The forcing sandwich does not pass through
   an arbitrary coherent Green mixture. The Green theorem is local near a
   fixed limiting resonance, uniformly over bounded detuning and sufficiently
   large n, with a computable cutoff.
7. **Numerical instrument failures retained.** A generic hypergeometric
   Laguerre evaluator failed at some quadrature nodes; finite polynomial
   evaluation replaced it. An exploratory demand for monotone small-n
   resonance convergence failed and was removed from the test contract. Neither
   failure was counted as support for the universal theorems; the receipts
   use the actual proven identities and explicit remainder bounds.
8. **Full-suite environment prerequisite.** The first full run omitted the
   documented `PYTHONPATH` setup. One inherited subprocess could not import
   `observational_succ`; the full run recorded 13,050 tests with one error.
   The exact failing probe was reproduced, then the documented path setting
   was applied to its focused module/probe rerun. No inherited code was edited
   to suppress the error. The validation report keeps these run scopes separate.

9. **Residues do not uniquely authenticate a source.** The root proposed a
   general observer-tilt hierarchy and its second-order variance/skewness term.
   The source bearing independently derived both, checked the globally
   integrable remainder, and found a positive three-cell source matching
   the first three authentic moments. Its endpoint is the rational 35/264.
   The result is retained as a source-response theorem with this decisive
   control against an unsupported source-selection or normality inference.

## Surviving boundary

The complete orthogonal expansion is an isometry for a specified phase family.
A single scalar projection discards coordinates. Keeping more coordinates is
constructive, but a proof about the auxiliary log-character is not a theorem
about ordered real BBP phases. Conditional source laws varying with time also
require an explicit joint coupling before temporal correlations may be claimed.

The same-forcing counterseed and the source-changing rational control test
different hypotheses. Neither is represented as a counterexample to a theorem
that explicitly fixes both the four poles and the literal zero seed. The
missing signed source-selected estimate remains OPEN.
