## Negative result that must remain visible

The existence of a nonnegative full-support weighted energy whose vanishing is equivalent to normality is **not** an arithmetic saving: any such energy can be constructed from the Weyl coefficients. The real source-specific content of this round is that the Oily critical transfer function supplies a compact rational kernel and that its unbounded-frequency energy can be transported from the rational original source with a uniform `O(B^-2)` bound. The missing theorem is that this actual source energy decreases to zero.
