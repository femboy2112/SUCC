## Weakest useful forms and novelty tax

Finite rational kernels, two hidden binary variables, dyadic Haar projections,
integer Gamma depths, and two-qubit real density matrices suffice for exact
instruments. Bayesian design, martingale evidence, Haar decomposition,
subordination, and entanglement witnesses are classical foundations; the work
must earn a specific interoperable SUCC interface and explicit controls, not
claim these general results as new discoveries.

No implication to pi normality, RH, a gravitational dual, physical entanglement
of abstract mathematical truth, or an unbounded probe hierarchy is presumed.

## Preregistered discriminators

- Positive: a hidden structural link raises a still-unresolved deeper probe's
  predictive contrast, information gain, and specified selection odds.
- Negative: repeatedly copying one dependent datum adds no conditional evidence;
  learning the target itself can lower information gain; an excluded probe
  cannot be selected by reweighting its zero prior probability.
- Classical hidden-state control: a reversible finite Markov chain has nonzero
  Haar/Gamma coarse composition defect without quantum entanglement.
- Quantum controls: product, classically correlated dephased, coherent Bell,
  opposite-phase, and partially dephased states; no-signalling must hold when
  outcomes are ignored. A same-basis conditional update alone must fail to
  certify entanglement.
- Mutation controls: reverse a difference sign, drop a hidden projection,
  replace the coherent state by its diagonal, postselect without normalization,
  or conflate predictable action probabilities with source evidence.
