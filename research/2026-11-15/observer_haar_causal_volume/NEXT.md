# Next experiments and the TLICA interface

## The smallest next physical/source claim

The proved structural-to-deep-probe bridge needs an actual instrument with
known joint law, not merely a pattern score. Supply a source contract for:

1. the target that remains unknown;
2. a hidden connection hypothesis;
3. a structural intervention and its conditional outcome law;
4. the deeper intervention and its disturbance of the carrier;
5. the record available to the adaptive policy, including any side channels;
6. the costs, fresh observations, and repeated-record identities.

Then predeclare rivals that agree on the current report but disagree on at
least one admissible higher-resolution intervention. A frozen holdout protocol
must distinguish them. Confidence cannot be promoted beyond this source class.

## Three records that must stay separate

- **Original target:** the mathematical/source property being investigated.
- **Observer state:** posterior, memory, confidence and probe-selection policy.
- **Current carrier state:** changed, possibly, by the intervention.

The existing `JointInstrument` already supports these distinctions, including
destructive measurements. This round's `ConnectionBelief` uses a persistent
four-state source and fresh conditional noise; its simplicity is a test model,
not a claim that repeated real observations are independent.

## TLICA mapping without a collapsed truth score

| TLICA coordinate | Operational object in this construction | Remaining obligation |
|---|---|---|
| kappa/contact | Actual source interactions, conditional likelihood increments and receipt identity | A replayed observation is not a fresh increment |
| rho/commitment | Declared priors, probe-reference weights and utility preferences | Strong commitment changes selection; it does not validate the source map |
| sigma/source-map adequacy | Candidate source models and instrument calibration | Posterior certainty inside a wrong model class can still be wrong |
| phi/toolkit-relative indistinguishability | Rival targets still identical under all supported probes | It is not identified with the scalar structural posterior p |
| mu/probe availability | Supported legal instrument/program set and resource limits | A missing action cannot acquire support by Gibbs reweighting alone |
| Independence/coherence/discrimination | Conditional freshness, signed detail and rival-separating responses | Squared energy alone loses orientation and does not imply entanglement |

The scalar p in the toy model means Pr(S=1); the density matrix rho_AB means a
specified quantum state. Neither is a replacement for the TLICA diagnostic
vector. Their notation should not be conflated with the user's rho coordinate.

## Native BBP target

The new Haar decomposition retains the original positive Oily source measure.
It does not identify its dyadic x-coordinate with completed BBP phase. The next
source-preserving map must specify how a source-coordinate detail is transported
to a signed phase/carry observable of U_0=0, and must discriminate the genuine
source from counterseeds and pole mutations. Retain orientation, all four
channels, the joint coupling, and the report map. A common Gamma endpoint or
positive covariance is insufficient for that map.

## Quantum hypothesis and ER=EPR

Before claiming a hidden quantum state, name the subsystem factorization,
preparation, local instruments and measurement contexts. Compare dephased,
classically correlated, product and coherent controls. Conditional remote
updates must be checked against ordinary classical conditioning. Discarded
outcomes must satisfy no-signalling; a joint back-action effect must survive a
classical disturbance model.

The two trusted Haar/XZ axes are enough for the stated qubit witness, but their
restricted statistics also have a classical shared-sign model if device trust
is removed. The rational rotated CHSH settings provide the next discriminator,
with setting-independence, communication and selection assumptions exposed.
These tests are specifications until run on a source that implements them.

An ER=EPR claim further needs a gravitational or holographic dictionary and
matching dynamics/observables. Causal adjacency, mutual information, shared
randomness and a tensor product alone do not provide one. No intervention on
an observer's computer is evidence that an abstract mathematical truth changed.
