## Faithful target and interpretation

The proposal is to use deliberately designed, strongly source-coupled
probabilistic interventions as mathematical detectors: amplify a hidden
distinction relevant to pi normality, retain the authentic source, and prove
that the resulting observations identify a source property. "High energy"
must denote a declared parameter, sensitivity, frequency, or resource cost.
The target's truth is distinct from probe-selection or detection probability.

Canonical source: U_0=0, U_n=16 U_(n-1)+r(n), with the original four BBP channels.
Target: for every nonzero integer h, N^(-1) sum_(n<N) exp(2 pi i h U_n) -> 0.
The first strictly weaker rung is a uniform positive dyadic-shell escape
A_B=B^(-1) sum_(B<=n<2B) (1-cos(2 pi U_n)) >= eta for all B>=B0.

## Initial hole (OPEN)

Construct a source-retaining randomized detector and a quantitative recovery
law which turns its controlled output into an inequality for the unmodified,
zero-seeded orbit. Expose the dependence of recovery on probe intensity,
retained randomization labels, time horizon, and source uncertainty.

Candidate theorem domains:

1. Integer finite-tap coherent probes of the exact affine BBP recurrence,
   including growing tap budgets and a source-independent seed coordinate.
2. Gamma-averaged phase rotations with a recorded versus discarded clock,
   and unbiased or inverse-weighted recovery of the original phase statistic.
3. Randomized collision/escape detectors with certified nonzero event rate;
   amplification of detection probability must expose the lower bound it uses.

## Dependency cut and novelty tax

The desired edge is source-specific signed control, not positivity of a
covariance, the existence of an information representation, or uniformity of a
deliberately randomized output. Every new reduction must name its remaining
arithmetic premise and test whether that premise is equivalent to normality.
An exact classification of which probes erase, amplify, or faithfully retain
the seed/source phase is a useful result even if the uniform escape rung stays
open. A theorem about a toy quantum source is not transported to BBP without a
declared intertwiner.

## Preregistered rival explanations and controls

- Source detection: a faithful probe distinguishes the original orbit from
  the counterseed -pi/16 and rational/periodic aliases with a bounded recovery
  cost, and the proof names the signed arithmetic quantity responsible.
- Detector-induced smoothing: apparent cancellation also occurs for a locked
  orbit; the randomization rather than the source caused the response.
- Seed erasure: a recurrence-annihilating echo is identical for all seeds.
- Adaptive-selection bias: a favorable sampling policy changes reported
  frequencies; propensity correction must recover the original statistic.
- High-intensity instability: the detector loses phase or amplifies numerical
  and source errors faster than its claimed signal gain.

Cheap falsifiers include the locked sequence, a rational periodic sequence,
the exact counterseed, zero intensity, a source-channel deletion, forgetting
the clock, and reusing one observation as independent repetitions. Fresh
numerical regions are held out from choosing a probe or scale.

## Truth and computational boundary

Finite exact computations check algebra and countermodels; numerical phase
tables do not establish all-scale escape or normality. The first intended
deliverable is a proof-backed detector law or classification with reproducible
controls. A successful normality claim requires a separate end-to-end proof
of every uniform source-specific estimate. The next lamp is the smallest
arithmetic inequality the surviving detector still needs.
