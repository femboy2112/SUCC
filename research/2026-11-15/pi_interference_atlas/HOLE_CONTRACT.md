## Faithful target

Find and construct the source-attached interference responses corresponding
to the open pi obligations already present in the pinned repository. Go
beyond one R0 detector: give a reusable response algebra, an indexed atlas
of the actual targets, and adversarial distinctions between them.

"All" has two declared mathematical meanings here:

1. Every named pi proof-obligation family in the audited canonical frontier,
   route contract, relevant archived routes and newest detector dossiers is
   represented, or explicitly marked unsupported/separate/underspecified.
2. Every finite response in a specified source-labelled interference grammar
   has a compilation/reconstruction theorem. No claim is made to list all
   possible experiments in mathematics or all future proof techniques.

The literal source is U_0=0 and U_(n+1)=16U_n+r(n+1), with the original four

## Principal proposed statement (OPEN at start)

A finite source-labelled response can be synthesized from declared linear
phase channels, phase references and pair cross terms; Hermitian quadratic
responses have an explicit signed interference decomposition. Their clock
and coefficient normalizations give an exact expectation identity and an
explicit resource/noise cost. Tensor lifting extends the declared grammar
to finite higher interactions. Conversely the permitted finite operations
produce only the stated response class.

The atlas maps each target to its observable, exact response, limit and
quantifiers, implication strength, source authentication, calibrated rival,
and unpaid arithmetic estimate. Equivalences are called equivalences;
sufficient gates retain all extra assumptions and transfers.

## Novelty tax and leverage

Classical polarization, finite Fourier/Walsh inversion, quadratic forms and
coherent reference measurements are inherited mathematics. The desired new
object is a source-typed, scope-complete compiler/atlas that exposes which
proof obligations share information and which need genuinely different
couplings. Compilation is not a bound on the compiled response. A new
source-specific estimate would be additional progress and must be proved
separately.

## Rivals and decisive controls

- A faithful apparatus distinguishes orientation from phase spread;
  pairwise energy alone is blind to global phase and cannot detect R0.
- Correct marginal prime or bit statistics can coexist with a locked
  complement or highest-order interaction.
- Finite modes, finite horizons and finite word lengths can be passed by
  nonnormal sources; the all-mode/all-large-length quantifiers matter.
- Generic positive energy, forgotten Gamma clocks, copied rows and adaptive
  selection can imitate the desired signal without the arithmetic property.
- A selected signed channel and a Galois/ensemble average are distinct.
- Completed Weil/RH, cross-base normality and prime/Goldbach predicates do
  not acquire a pi implication merely by entering the same response algebra.

Predeclared finite regions: exact rational/Gaussian-rational phase fixtures
at sizes 1..8 for development and fresh sizes 11,13,17 for holdout where
feasible; independent source clocks 19..41; independent finite enumeration
of signed kernels and branch cells. Every universal conclusion needs a
paper proof; numerical evaluations remain diagnostics.

## Dependency cut and next lamp

The original R0 residual bound and all-mode Weyl cancellation remain OPEN.
The compiler can be proved without either. Its success closes a measurement
and bookkeeping gap, not the signed arithmetic gap. The next lamp is a
source-specific response inequality that survives the matched controls,
or a proof that a proposed response cannot distinguish its stated rivals.

All work is additive research on a non-main branch. Publication follows
