## 4. Prescribed conditional energies need the correct direction

Shared-label sampling implements sum_b p_b|m_b|². Independent labels
implement |sum_b p_bm_b|². Conditional energy dominates the coherent
decoder square, but decoder cancellation need not make every conditional
mean small. Finer conditioning can raise the energy all the way to 1.

For the carry/arrival or golden spatial/time route, specify the partition,
coverage, horizon schedule and source-to-completion error before claiming
a theorem. A schedule chosen arbitrarily slowly after normality is assumed
does not prove a prescribed growing-resolution criterion.
