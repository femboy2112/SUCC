## Exact object and desired result

Keep `U_0=0`, `U_n=16 U_(n-1)+r(n)` with the four literal BBP poles.
Let `C_n=floor(U_n)`, `y_n=U_n-C_n`, `J_n=n mod4`, and `K_n=[y_n]_4`,
where brackets mean reduction of the odd-denominator rational in Z/4Z.
The inherited clock gives `C_n=J_n-K_n mod4`.

For every integer shell `B<=n<2B`, define its empirical law `P_B` and
`L_B=P_B(J=K=0)+P_B(K=1)-P_B(J<2,K<2)`.
The exact sixteen-cell inequality proves selected density `d_B>=L_B`.

Target: prove `exists delta>0,B0 forall integers B>=B0: L_B>=delta`, or
prove a genuinely weaker source-specific statement sufficient for `d_B>=delta`.
This would close the current fourth-root anti-lock rung, not by itself all
normality modes. Any proposed lift to normality must prove its extra edges.

## Leverage, novelty tax, and existing obstruction

The budget uses only three coarse joint masses and does not require full
joint independence or carry-Haar. Its sharp extremizer is strongly non-Haar.
However these masses retain the real floor through K; dyadic isometry and
positive Gamma volumes alone do not establish them. Same-forcing changed-seed
controls, matched finite-precision changed-source controls, and maximum-law
controls missing the cofactor marginal must remain visible.

An attack earns progress only through a proved source estimate, an exact new
bound with useful dependence, a narrower equivalent obstruction, or a decisive
counterexample to a proposed proof step. Rewriting the normality target with
new names does not pay the novelty tax.
