## 2. Full-scale Gamma–Mellin/Fourier identity (CLASSICAL)

Fix \(\sigma>0\), real \(\omega,y\), and \(\lambda_y=1-2\pi i y\), with \(\Re\lambda_y=1\). Then
\[
\boxed{
\frac1{\Gamma(\sigma)}
\int_0^\infty u^{\sigma+i\omega-1}e^{-u}e^{2\pi iyu}\,du
=
\frac{\Gamma(\sigma+i\omega)}{\Gamma(\sigma)}
\lambda_y^{-(\sigma+i\omega)}.
}\tag{GDB2}
\]

**Proof:** combine the two exponentials into \(e^{-\lambda_y u}\), then apply the Gamma/Laplace integral for \(\Re\lambda_y>0\), using the principal logarithm of \(\lambda_y\). The integral converges absolutely because \(|e^{2\pi iyu}u^{i\omega}|=1\) and the positive Gamma density is integrable. \(\square\)

In computational-time coordinates \(u=16^t\), with \(t\in\mathbb R\), this reads
\[
\frac{\log16}{\Gamma(\sigma)}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}16^{(\sigma+i\omega)t}
e^{-16^t}e^{2\pi iy16^t}\,dt
=\frac{\Gamma(\sigma+i\omega)}{\Gamma(\sigma)}
\lambda_y^{-(\sigma+i\omega)}.\tag{GDB3}
\]

**GDB-01 (CLASSICAL):** the log-Gamma clock gives an exact joint Mellin/Fourier realization of the chosen dilation path. The \(2\pi iy\) phase comes from the character, \(\omega\) from the Mellin character, and \(\log16\) from the Jacobian/time generator.
