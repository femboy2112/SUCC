- For R0, the lemma must imply one fixed delta>0 and one onset B0 valid for

  finite shell, or density-one exceptions do not meet that obligation.
- For full normality, it must give vanishing authentic swept discrepancy

  Positivity of a Gram form is insufficient.

  and exact rational-clock membership from H-01--03. A source identification
  lemma alone still owes the statistical estimate.
- It must retain the literal seed and the original small positive tail.
  GG-01 proves that the same completed orbit and all fractional source

- Before any claim promotion, test the proposed hypothesis on both fixed
  periodic orbits, the two-form locked source, matched finite prefixes,

The live obstruction remains a **pathway gap**, not an access gap: the
source, its full history and the quantitative observers are available.

Nor has any survivor earned a declaration of contextual saturation: the
needed arithmetic discriminator is still identifiable but unproved.
