# Research contract and verdict

## A. Construction target closed in this dossier

**Object.** A five-coordinate polynomial observer of the Fibonacci successor,
with actual two-/three-period projections and compatible lifts over powers of
2 and 3, connected by an explicit map to the authenticated BBP local clock.

**Statement.** For A=[[1,1],[1,0]], use T=Sym4(A^2). For all a,b,c>=1 prove the
exact periods3*2^max(a-3,0),2*3^(b-1),5^c; characterize the shared-clock image;
construct the compatible BBP-label factor and quantify its information loss.

**Weakest sufficient form.** Periods3 modulo2 and2 modulo3 already realize a
precise two-/three-cycle five-coordinate construction. The all-depth formulas
add local dynamics characterization; the source-clock factor supplies the
actual map connecting to SUCC's prior BBP work.

**Truth state.** PROVED LOCAL and PROVED COMPOSITE, with finite exact controls.
The source-clock inverse imports the existing original-zero Phi theorem.

**Dependency edges closed.** Two-coordinate Fibonacci -> three quadratic
coordinates -> five quartic coordinates; shared forward clock -> compatible
prime-power reports; actual BBP local labels -> this declared observer.

**Novelty tax.** This is an explicit checked construction, not a relabeling of
the statement that pi is normal. It applies to an auxiliary Fibonacci carrier
without assuming any pi statistic. The map through the BBP clock adds no new
phase information. Classical symmetric powers, matrix lifting and valuations
retain their attribution; academic originality is not established.

**Falsifiers and outcomes.** Failed monomial intertwiners, wrong exact order,
noncompatible reductions, or a failure at CRT wraparound would refute the
construction. A one-entry T mutation and H+t^4 mask mutation are calibrated
failures. A stride divisible by5 collapses the pentagon norm, as the theorem
requires. A purported all-frame integral splitting is rejected by a shear,
while the correct A-specific integral splitting survives.

**Finite boundary.** The all-depth proof is algebraic. Tests inspect finite
matrix powers, actual source states and independently reconstructed Fibonacci
pairs. They are implementation evidence, not a universal lift.

**Dominance over the earlier auxiliary A5 example.** The present construction
uses the actual Fibonacci polynomial successors and original BBP clock maps.
It preserves the valid distinction between projected cycles and forward causal
history, while removing the unsupported leap from an unrelated triangle group
to the requested Fibonacci construction. The older input note is preserved.

## B. The source-selected pi target remains open

**Exact statement.** With the unchanged source, x=pi/16 and
W_B(1)=B^(-1)sum_(B<=n<2B)e(16^n x), prove there exist eta>0,B0 such that
Re W_B(1)<=1-eta for every integer B>=B0. Full normality additionally requires
vanishing for every fixed nonzero harmonic.

**Gap classification.** A pathway gap for a quantitative, original-zero,
Archimedean signed estimate. Local observer construction and probe availability
are no longer the missing parts for the present finite objects.

**What is proved on the actual source.** At every fixed joint local precision,
the real phase repeatedly separates across equal-label times, with chord gap
at least2sin(pi/17) in limsup. A fixed finite-state observer cannot track it
below sin(pi/17) limsup error. This is a joint-place extension of inherited
HBR04, and it is weaker than positive lower shell density.

**Known obstruction.** Identical source-only filters and arbitrarily accurate
finite two-/three-adic histories can coexist with every finite carry word after
a rational seed change. A candidate bound uniform over that class must pay
boundary debt at least proportional to the bad-word length. The exact zero
predicate and height-qualified reconstruction remain lawful discriminators.

**Candidate proof language.** An original-zero, source/height-sensitive signed
potential or a quantitative conditional real-phase estimate retaining winding
and the changing denominator/cofactor state. Merely renaming the existing
terminal carry count is a reformulation; no gain is credited for it.

**Next lamp.** State an actual inequality, freeze its constants and source
variables, expose its boundary cost on the explicit shadows, and only then
test a genuinely untouched authentic source interval beyond131072. Failure
rejects the proposed finite inequality. Passing requires a separate all-shell
derivation before promotion. No such future run or success is promised here.

**Other conjectures.** The affine precision dictionary is a local Collatz
comparison; conditional ternary mixing is a literature template, not an
application already proved for BBP. RH still requires original prime-power
source, completed Gamma/pole terms, a valid test domain and the missing sign
bound. Neither conjecture is substituted for the pi target.
