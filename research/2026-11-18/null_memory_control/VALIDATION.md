## Evidence boundary

- The conditional information theorem and counterexamples are elementary classical probability identities, not new information theory.
- Memory-conservative and continuation-conservative equivalences are declared **design gates** on a specified observer state, not universal physical laws.
- A currently source-independent mask that changes a later available sensor is an **option-value intervention**, NOT an operationally inert null; no simultaneous claim of both is made.
- π's original null seed means zero **extra homogeneous input**, not stochastic uncertainty. R0 and all-mode normality remain OPEN.
