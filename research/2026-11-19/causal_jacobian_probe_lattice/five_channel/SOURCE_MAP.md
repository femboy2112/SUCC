## Executed algorithms and dependency overlap

The production finite signed runner imports the unchanged
`observational_succ.duck_bbp.forcing` and
`observational_succ.bbp_spatial_haar_lift.bulk_pi_hex_digits`. Its fixed prefix is
constructed from the exact four signed poles using outward integer rounding.

The observer module independently writes the four-pole coefficient family with
`Fraction` arithmetic. Its audit compares with a separately combined rational
forcing formula. The independent signed-table checker uses a BBP-derived pi
interval and full-angle Taylor bounds, while the production table uses Machin
arctangent intervals and quarter-wave symmetries. Minor-GCD and adjugate methods
audit the Gaussian inverse implementation. These are different algorithms with
shared source hypotheses, not statistically independent evidence streams.

The mathematical constructor, frame analysis, adversarial audit and root
synthesis share the same model family. The paper proofs were checked across
separate decompositions and exact arithmetic implementations. They are not
external academic refereeing, a formal proof-assistant verification, or a
claim of worldwide mathematical priority.

A specific Claude artifact for the conversational Cauchy-determinant claim was
not found in the inspected branch or retrieved references. The determinant and
schedule results were derived explicitly rather than attributed to that missing
artifact. The characteristic polynomial of the quartic Fibonacci operator was
already in #667 and is not claimed as new here.

  [On the Random Character of Fundamental Constant Expansions](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/bcrandom.pdf),
