# The next verdict-changing probe: source-selected objective agreement

**Target.** For each fixed h≠0, let the original zero-seeded rational BBP source produce the shell phases e(hU_n), and partition the shell into labelled source-rational phase cells of mesh δ_B→0 (e.g. Fibonacci-count equal cells, or the authenticated golden-circle mesh with retained time labels). Denote the *between-family* signed squared separation by D_B^U(h).

A proof that `D_B^U(h)→1` for every h would, by JOG-03 and the authenticated O(B^-2) completion transfer, prove base-16 normality. This is presently an equivalent reformulation, **not an independently established estimate**. For the first weaker rung, prove a uniform positive all-shell margin for the anchored signed phase or the fixed rational `(ell,h)=(1,1)` holonomy; no such margin is known.

**Candidate path, no theorem assumed:**

1. Source labels: retain exact zero seed, four pole classes, rational numerator/denominator, integer carries and future-tail interval. Only lawful chronological advancement counts as new contact.
2. Local geometry: compute the Cauchy/five-channel Jacobian and all p-normalized row ranks for p=3,5,7,11,29. The six-time schedule is a calibration, not an asymptotic proof. For growing p or deep p^k, choose next accessible probe by a preregistered rank/precision criterion, not by the sign of the would-be Weyl result.
3. Common objective basis: retain every per-cell complex centroid with its population and complete label/provenance, then form the signed global sum. Never infer global cancellation from local rank or absolute moment data.
4. Prime/cofactor joining: formulate the *full* CRT principal-part and residual Archimedean cofactor product. Seek a new arithmetic bound on inverse-tracking of source prime waves across many clocks. Check both resonant and off-resonant Fibonacci shifts.
5. Decisive controls: original source with wrong seed s*=-π/16 (perfectly coherent late-phase), rational fixed point x=1/15, extremely sparse-clumping irrational, p=29 out-of-source eigenrow, high-power pole depths, adapted-vs-unadapted p-adic rank repair, all original four-pole positions.
6. Quantifiers: freeze probe policy and holdout intervals before numerical evaluation; report positive/negative/ambiguous, raw exact residues, and certified error. Finite successes are OBSERVED, never PROVED all-shell.

**What would change the verdict:** A genuinely source-derived all-large-B signed bound, either `liminf_B H_{B;1,1}>0` (R0 only) or a fixed-h all-shell cross-family/Gram decay estimate with objective completion errors paid (normality after verifying all h). A fixed-Jacobian rank theorem, even at every p, cannot do that: the same forcing under the wrong seed leaves all innovation Jacobians unchanged.
