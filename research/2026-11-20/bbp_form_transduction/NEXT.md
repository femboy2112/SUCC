# Next discriminating offensive: long-range p-primary cofactor coupling

This is a preregistered research contract, **not** a completed theorem or a commitment to run monitoring. The goal is to prove a genuinely source-derived asymptotic inequality below full pi normality without changing the authenticated BBP program.

## Fixed target and source

Use the literal signed `r(n)` and zero seed, exact unwrapped `U_n`, original positive tail `T_n`, `theta_n=(U_n+T_n) mod1`, and `A_B=(1/B)sum(1-cos(2pi theta_n))` over every integer shell `[B,2B)`. **R0:** prove `liminf A_B>0`. Fix frequency `h=1`, Gaussian `lambda=1` for calibration; do not optimize h or lambda posthoc to turn a failed shell into a pass. For full normality, separately require every nonzero h.

## Candidate instrument

For each finite rational state `U_n`, decompose into canonical p-primary fractions `tau_p(U_n)` using ACT-06. Let `S_B` be a *predeclared* finite prime selection from the actual pole/clock source journal (for example, a declared p-square/multiple-p scheme), with all cofactor information retained. Define

`a_{S_B,n} = e(sum_{p in S_B} tau_p(U_n))`,

`b_{S_B,n} = e(U_n - sum_{p in S_B} tau_p(U_n) + T_n)`.

Their product is exactly `e(theta_n)`. ACT-07 gives the discrepancy with inverse tracking, but that identity alone is no gain. **Seek an arithmetic mechanism proving that the actual cofactor cannot follow `conj(a)` too closely on all late shells.** A legitimate mechanism could use original pole residues, CRT carry alignment, and temporal path constraints, with explicit bounds not themselves equal to the target Weyl sum. Full p-primary recombination, missing cofactor, and a selected finite Jacobian must be distinguished.

A second allowed formulation is the inherited **GYC-11** signed Gaussian witness `q_n`, where the actual source constructs q independently of the completed target, `(1/B)sum||q_n||²<=M²`, and the signed correlation `|(1/B)sum< K_{theta_n}-K_0,q_n>|>=c`. Then R0 follows. The witness cannot be `K_{theta_n}-K_0`, a cumulative Weyl sum, or one obtained by fitting a target shell.

## The newly proved sparsity gate

The event set `n_p=(p²-1)/8` contains at most O(sqrt B) event clocks up to 2B. If a bounded candidate witness is supported only for `L_B=O(log B)` ticks after those clocks, its normalized shell contribution tends to zero (ACT-08). **Reject short-lived event-local candidates before expensive runs.** A successful p-square mechanism must prove sustained memory/coupling beyond logarithmic periods, gain an additional dense class of genuinely sourced events, or infer a separate global constraint that does not rely on local pulse density.

## Proposed proof ladder

1. **Exact independent symbolic step:** derive full source-typed phase decomposition with all integer carries and tail; verify it against the untouched `observational_succ/duck_bbp.py` and #672 finite terminal.
2. **Signed cofactor transport:** build forward-time cofactor recurrence using actual pole labels; no assumption of independence between prime characters. Preserve the original zero anchor.
3. **Cross-channel rank:** condition on 11-adic source-selected filter classes, compare generic p=29 and matched-jet mutant; prove a signed difference in a predeclared target statistic, not just positive energies.
4. **Beyond-event-density mechanism:** give a rigorous lower bound that operates on positive-density chronological indices, or that forces a persistent phase displacement over enough clocks; ACT-08 blocks O(log B) event-only support.
5. **Uniform shell estimate:** fix constants and quantifiers, demonstrate the estimate for **every** integer B beyond B0; independent source bounds must exclude fixed locked counterseed. Only then claim R0.
6. **Hostile holdout and formalization:** verify a fresh shell with outward rational intervals, independent symbolic derivation, exact provenance and source mutation; consider a Lean proof for the finite algebra, not for an unproved asymptotic.

## Pass/fail/ambiguous criteria

- **Pass as theorem:** an authenticated real-phase-to-prime-local/cofactor transport plus a *new* uniform signed lower bound, with zero anchor, all-shell constants, and the locked control excluded. The resulting R0 certificate is genuinely earned.
- **Fail:** the proof would also certify `s=-pi/16`, derives positivity only from norm of original source forcing, suppresses compensators/carries, assumes false independence, or uses O(log B) pulses alone to claim positive shell density.
- **Ambiguous:** finite signed data, local p valuations, Gaussian width defects, exact but equivalent anti-tracking identity, or a candidate that needs an unproved global closure/bound. Mark the unresolved premise instead of promoting it.

The best immediate mathematical payoff is to classify **which source-typed cofactor correlations can have long memory** despite the O(sqrt B) count of p-square events. Even a rigorous impossibility theorem for a proposed finite-window family would narrow the frontier.
