## 3. A candidate falsifiable inequality (not a theorem)

`Re [(1/B)Σ_(n=B)^(2B−1)⟨K_λ(·−θ_n)−K_λ, q_n⟩] ≥ c >0`.

Then Cauchy–Schwarz gives `(1/B)Σ ||K_λ(·−θ_n)−K_λ||² ≥(c/C)²`; the inherited Gaussian R0 equivalence implies R0. The **missing** ingredient is the independently proved positive signed correlation.
