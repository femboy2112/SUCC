# Source and prior-art map

- **Authenticated BBP source** (unchanged): four signed poles and U0=0 from SUCC Main XXVI `observational_succ/duck_bbp.py`; exact rational tail `r(M+1)/(15*16^M)` is used for the pi/16 prefix. The classical BBP identity is taken as an imported known theorem; we did not independently prove pi's BBP evaluation here.
- **SUCC #680**: real/2-adic hexadecimal digit shadow, 3/5-adic clock, naive raw mirror no-go, the `5,3,15` fixed-word precision identities. This branch *does not overwrite or falsely contradict* its raw-state mirror no-go; the transported parity autoconjugacy is a different map.
- **SUCC #679**: digit/remainder bijection and unitary 16-channel splitter; #678: original source-filter root `1/4,3/4` chart and 5/11 auxiliary factor ports; #675: causal Fourier clock; #671: source parameter acquisition; #667: finite Collatz parity cylinders.
- **Daniel J. Bernstein and Jeffrey C. Lagarias (1996)**, *The 3x+1 Conjugacy Map*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 48(6):1154-1169. Original primary: https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1996-060-x. Provides the Q/Phi shift conjugacy, including its 2-adic scope.
- **Kenneth G. Monks and Jonathan Yazinski (2004)**, *The autoconjugacy of the 3x+1 function*, Discrete Mathematics 275:219-236. Primary: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0012-365X(03)00125-0. The only nontrivial Collatz autoconjugacy is Omega=Phi∘V∘Phi^-1, V(y)=-1-y. The present symbolic hexadecimal complement is another representation of that already-known symmetry.
- **General prior art:** arithmetic symbolic dynamics, categorical action-graph isomorphism and ordinary Yoneda; the source-specific BBP 48-bit computation is a new finite ledger in this project, not a universal theorem.

No imports or historical files were modified, and no physical energy law, integer-Collatz convergence, prime randomness or pi normality statement is asserted.
