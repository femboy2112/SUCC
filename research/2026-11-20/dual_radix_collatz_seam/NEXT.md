# NEXT: a source-selected signed 2↔3↔real transmitted-remainder gate

## What is proven and what isn't

A plain mirror of the 16-way digit split is the **exact involution** `(d,r)↦(15−d,1−r)` and commutes with the existing Hilbert unitary. The same recorded word produces *two different completions*: real high-to-low fractional digits and 2-adic low-to-high digit shadow. For constant 3 and 5 digits we proved exact qD+1 powers-of-16, 3/5-adic clock valuation, Collatz suffix peeling, first mismatch, and a 2/3/5 GPS checksum. None is an all-B source-specific π estimate. The 2-adic π shadow is a **digit re-encoding** of the original four-pole source, not an independent arithmetic route.

## Attack priorities

1. **Source-attested head alignment:** fix `U0=0`, original `r(n)`, complete unwrapped carries, `T_n`, `M_n=floor(16^nπ/16)`, and exact digit-reversal prefixes. Generate 2-adic shadow digits from an exact rational BBP tail certificate, never from a floating π value. Retain `M_n mod3`, `mod5`, last-digit parity and prime/cofactor labels. Check that 3/5/2 heads are genuinely separate computational routes rather than duplicates of the same extracted digit.
2. **Intertwine real/2-adic phase transport:** the diagram is exact on the full *symbolic* digit category, not a continuous field map `R→Q2` or `Q2→Q3`. Test whether an additional signed original-source invariant survives the digit reversal and mirror, beyond the universal shift equivalence. The source's original complex root factor `H(t)=(t²+2)(t²+2t+2)` and auxiliary `11/5` determinants are different typed maps—never identify them with the universal arithmetic q=5 merely by shared numeral.
3. **Conditional Collatz valuation gate:** preregister `V_(q,n)=v₂(qM_n+1)` for q=3,5,15. The events `V_(q,n+k)≥4k` represent exactly all-5, all-3, all-1 word cylinders. Prove (or fail to prove) a **source-derived lower density of at least one such cylinder** in every late dyadic integer shell, without conditioning on the answer or invoking normality. This would imply R0, but is as yet an OPEN arithmetic task.
4. **Source/head error correction and precision:** construct independent prime-cofactor/tail/carry probes supplying a quantitative witness with bounded inverse condition number; use [#676](https://github.com/femboy2112/SUCC/pull/676) code-distance controls. Head consistency and source identification alone do not give Weyl saving.
5. **Countermodel matrix:** the source-universal constant real fixed point 1/3 has all 5 digits and the most extreme `v₂(3M+1)` events, yet is nonnormal. Mirror to 2/3 gives all A digits, and ordinary Collatz does *not* commute with that mirror. The authentic four-pole source with `s=−π/16` has identical forcing but completed phase zero; the root head rejects it. The mutant `Q+(1−t)²` keeps leading source jet but changes the p² 4→5 mark; do not confuse this p² mark with the digit 5. Preserve real versus 2-adic branch conventions and π rational tail certification.
6. **Independent source-specific π target:** derive bounded source-generated `q_n` in the real-phase Gaussian Hilbert space, as laid out in [#672](https://github.com/femboy2112/SUCC/pull/672) and [#679](https://github.com/femboy2112/SUCC/pull/679), with a **uniform positive signed all-B correlation** and verified frame transport. A mere 2-adic word cylinder restatement of the digit event is not new source information and cannot be used circularly.

## Pass/fail

**PASS (structure):** both completion maps and mirror/shift squares commute; any chosen source has the correct conditional valuation receipts and calibrated output corrections. **PASS (source):** source-specific root/prime/cofactor probes reject matched-jet mutations and source aliases and produce an independent arithmetic constraint. **PASS R0 (not yet):** signed all-late-shell bound for original-zero BBP from independent authentic source estimates. **FAIL (shortcut):** transporting constant-digit Collatz identities into the π source without an actual source-dependent factor map, assuming an isomorphism between incompatible p-adic topologies, treating uniform 16-channel norm as digit-frequency Haar, or claiming 2-adic counterpart of real π arises without digit coding.
