# Next actual proof obligations, ordered by discriminatory power

## Definition of victory

A **legitimate complete human-readable proof of RH** must supply conventional and source-pinned derivations of *every theorem* on the path to the one-sided G(x) bound, including the deep inequality (M), with exact hypotheses and no unproved theta/lattice imports hidden as "observer conservation." A *complete FUCC proof of the released result* is an even stronger goal: derive (M) in a FUCC-source-authenticated way instead of translating the Lean archive verbatim.

## Priority 1 — arithmetic integrality *for the same theta map*

Reconstruct Rubinstein §III.5 integrality hypothesis (B) from the exact external Lean declarations, including CM elliptic model, choice of invariant derivation, height-two formal-group coordinates, coefficient field, local uniformizers, and normalization. **Discriminator:** show that the actual local theta kernel's derivative has the height-two valuation gain, rather than merely showing our formal-model f∘U does. Reject a nonintegral mutant. This is first because the entire global argument is powerless without a source-authenticated integral object.

## Priority 2 — local/global single-object capacity

For the same explicit normalized period map/minor, produce a lower bound on each finite place's valuation and an upper bound on the product of Archimedean norms. Pay denominator losses and conductor/index terms with explicit constants; no infinite source place can be omitted. Check a nonzero rank condition and the effect of changing the phase/prime source. **Falsifier:** a p^a-labelled shift of a unit vector that does not gain p-divisibility.

## Priority 3 — trace and rank

Translate Rubinstein §III.8, the three actual Lean trace/rank/pairing statements, into a stand-alone large-span lemma. Positive Gram energies are not sufficient. Rank-one repeated map heads are mandatory hostile controls. Demand independent reimplementation of finite rank examples and a universal paper proof for the stated lattice situation.

## Priority 4 — glue (M), B/C and sparse-window criterion

Reconstruct all of Rubinstein §II.4's weighted P_(u,t,j), M_(u,t,j) terms and their averaging normalization. Reproduce the full E/B error with `x/m + xm/k + m + k/n²`, check m~√x, k~x^(9/2), n~x², and the exact field/conductor constants.
