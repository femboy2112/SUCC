## 5. Exact next arithmetic contract

Let B index the original zero-seeded BBP shell. A proposed application of BM-04 must supply, from the authenticated source/carry history:

1. A field F_B with coefficient vector c_B of declared degree at most D_B.
2. A proof that c_B is nonzero under the relevant counterhypothesis.
3. Explicit positive integers Q_B,M_B with Q_B c_B in M_B Z^(D_B+1).
4. A proof that the actual counterhypothesis forces

\[
 \max_{j\le D_B}|\langle u^j,F_B\rangle_\mu|
   < \frac{M_B}{Q_B\|H_{D_B+1}^{-1}\|_\infty}.
\]

BM-04 then supplies an immediate contradiction. This is a **conditional application contract**, not a claim that R0 failure already implies item 4. In particular, a generic moment polynomial or a field that can be identically zero cannot substitute for items 1–2.

The degree cost is quantitative: the chosen threshold is at most

\[
 (4m_0 M_B/Q_B)16^{-D_B}
\]

when D_B>=1. A proposed divisibility gain must be compared with this actual conditioning cost. If it remains bounded while D_B grows, this instrument has no uniform positive floor.
