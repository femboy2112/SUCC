# Principia Intermedia — research contract

Date: 2026-11-26. Author's proposal: Leah's same-carrier observers at opposite or distant locations, a continuous family relative to carrier granularity, transported agreement, and a necessary relative-null or seam crossing.

## Exact target and acceptance criteria

Given one carrier C, authenticated endpoint observers a,b, a declared comparison target, lawful transitions and a specified seam Z:

1. Characterize when a finite or continuous family of agreeing observers joins a to b.
2. Prove when every such family meets Z, with an exact or resolution-dependent conclusion.
3. Construct an actual arithmetic example with simultaneous null readouts, retaining the underlying source.
4. Construct a nontrivial example from the original signed BBP source, starting from null constraints.
5. Use the published Alpöge–Claude RH argument to audit which additional arithmetic/analytic estimates a proof needs.
6. Deliver paper proofs, exact code, hostile controls, source provenance and a draft research PR.

The initial state of the proposed general assertion was CONJECTURED. The precise finite criterion, selected continuum results, arithmetic-place seam and BBP moment results below are proved within their stated domains. No unrestricted universal observer-continuation assertion is made.

## Hypotheses and quantifiers

Finite models quantify over all paths in an explicitly supplied, finite, history-retaining state graph. Continuum results quantify over continuous sections in a declared topology, under compactness, closed constraints and one uniform modulus, or under a constructive compact-convex selection hypothesis. Arithmetic-place results concern pointwise-continuous absolute values on Q, including the trivial absolute value. BBP results concern its original positive Mellin representation and exact rational moments; temporal orbit statistics are separate.

Agreement means equality after valid comparison on a declared source/target. It does not mean that incompatible local magnitudes must have identical numerical values. Full information-nullness, zero readout, identity of source, absence of a carrier and neutral-memory operations are distinct predicates.

## Weakest sufficient forms and dependency cut

Finite extension needs a common forward/backward reachable state at one cut. Forced crossing of an arbitrary seam needs non-vacuous endpoint connectivity before deleting the seam and loss of connectivity afterwards. A scalar certificate needs transported signs and control of jumps. A common infinite null requires simultaneous finite-block feasibility plus compactness, not a separate scalar root for each coordinate.

Dependency chain:

    authenticated source and causal memory
        -> correct feasible observer states and lawful maps
        -> extension / obstruction certificate
        -> forced seam or simultaneous null
        -> retained arithmetic witness and quantitative capacity
        -> application theorem

This round proves reusable edges through forced seams and finite source-capacity inequalities. The final source-specific implication from BBP late-shell R0 failure remains UNVERIFIED.

## Novelty tax

The framework composes classical reachability, compactness, holonomy, convex projection, IVT, Ostrowski classification and orthogonal-polynomial theory. It claims no historical novelty for those ingredients. Its added SUCC value is an exact source/history contract, an executable finite decision interface, a mandatory arithmetic null-seam realization, the jet/regularity distinction on that same carrier, and an original-source BBP null-to-crossing construction with a rational residual threshold.

Neither the existence of an observer path nor the new BBP moment threshold is equivalent to pi normality. They hold in many models without normal radix orbits. This pays the novelty tax as a framework proof step, not as an asymptotic pi proof.

## Finite/computational boundary

Compactness is mathematical existence, not an algorithm guaranteed to compute a compatible infinite branch. No Lean verification or external peer review is claimed for this dossier. External RH verification reports are cited as reports, and the RH proof is not rebuilt here.
