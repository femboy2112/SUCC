## PRF-16. Addition/multiplication coupling and authentic BBP joint head

Define successor S e_n=e_(n+1) on H, and V_m,D as PRF-10. On basis vectors

\[
 V_m S=S^m V_m,\qquad
 [D,S]e_n=\log(1+1/n)e_{n+1},\qquad
 [D,V_m]e_n=\log(m)e_{mn}.
\]

These exact identities retain the common integer labels and link additive chronological and multiplicative analytic clocks; the two different commutator coefficients are source-selected. They do not establish statistical independence of digits or mix the corresponding actions by fiat.

Retain the original four-pole forcing

\[
 r(n)=4/(8n-7)-2/(8n-4)-1/(8n-3)-1/(8n-2),\quad U_0=0,\quad
 U_n=16U_{n-1}+r(n).
\]

Let a_n=16^-n r(n). Then x=sum a_n=pi/16 under the original BBP identity, and U_n+T_n=16^n x with original tail T_n. Define the two-coordinate observer

\[
 H(z,s)=\sum_{n\ge1}a_n z^n n^{-s}.
\]

It is jointly holomorphic on |z|<16 and s in C: on compact subsets bound |z|/16 by rho<1, n^-Re(s) by n^M, and r(n) uniformly bounded; locally uniform absolute convergence follows. The z-Taylor coefficients recover a_n at s=0. The s derivatives multiply by (-log n)^j, and the z-Euler derivative multiplies by n; transport remains tied to chronological labels. H(1,0)=x. This is an exact common representation of the *forcing*, not of signed carry histories. It is unchanged by a counterseed, and therefore cannot distinguish normality from a same-forcing locked orbit by itself.

## The proof rule, applied without circularity

A useful SUCC first-cause rule is:

1. In a hypothesized hostile source regime, define the authentic causal model and an independently derived finite certificate of a contradiction or of a target theorem.
2. Prove the required certificate class nonempty by a source-specific argument (a rank/multiplicity/height witness, a verified finite construction or a robust finite readout margin).
3. Construct a fair lawful observer policy: finite reachable graph, recurrent replay, enumerated scripts, or the PRF-13/15 always-forward analytic clock for robust cylinders.
4. Use the nucleation theorem to infer finite acceptance almost surely, without constructing the particular search path. Soundness yields a deterministic mathematical existence/consequence statement. Track its quantifiers.

A random observer can choose a probe; it cannot create a source identity. In particular, treating pi's fixed orbit as random or assuming random digits inside the exploration measure would assume the desired conclusion. The BBP source-only dual observer is insensitive to an altered homogeneous seed, so a selected-seed and signed chronological bridge is essential.

The actual remaining gate for R0 is a uniform positive lower bound on late-shell average 1-cos(2pi 16^n x) for the original zero seed, not just recoverability of the source. Full normality requires signed Weyl cancellation at every nonzero fixed harmonic. A proposed proof by nucleation must show that failure of such a gate produces a nonzero, finitely certifiable, source-authenticated contradictory field or minor with paid degree, denominators, rank and normalization costs. Existing #689/#692 null constraints show capacity once the field is supplied; they do not derive it from arbitrary failure of R0. This is the next verdict-changing mathematical probe. No finite test of digit prefixes proves the infinite assertion.

For RH, the corresponding task is actual source-marked CM theta integrality, common normalizer, and simultaneous Archimedean/finite-place rank and volume bounds under the authentic proof's hypotheses. A stochastic search might discover a representation or finite certificate but does not replace those obligations. No zero positions as construction input.

## Recursive completion contract

A candidate general theorem at stage k must supply:
- old marked carrier C_k and lawful toolkit/access budget, with exact scope of saturation;
- duality/completion functors or explicit source-preserving maps, topology, and reconstruction theorem;
- a type-changing or cost-changing lift, with strict novelty proved via a previously unresolved pair, new row rank, new realizable morphism, or a stated resource threshold;
- finite recognizer/certificate domain and a recurrence/fairness proof on the actual search process;
- projection that recovers the old source, with ordered path/phase history retained, and a discriminator proving that the new structure is not just a relabelling;
- proof that these obligations recur if claiming infinitely many levels.

PRF-07–15 give one explicit family of arrows and an always-forward finite-resolution search realization. They do not show that arbitrary carriers force an infinite tower, nor that a non-symmetric braid is canonical. TLICA diagnostics (source-map adequacy, contact, toolkit-relative indistinguishability, accessible closure, discrimination, probe availability, commitment) remain separate. A recurrence proof is not a truth score.
