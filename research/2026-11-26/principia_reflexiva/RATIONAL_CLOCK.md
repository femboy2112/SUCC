# PRF-15. Exact finite-bit causal clock, with the same infinite exploration guarantee

The exponential law in PRF-13 is mathematically useful but sampling an exact continuously distributed real is not a finite digital operation. This is not required. There is a directly constructive countable-support version.

Independently sample A,B in N_+ with P(A=a)=2^-a and P(B=b)=2^-b (fair coins until the first head, counting the successful toss). Use the positive rational duration E=A/B. Both draws finish almost surely, with expected four total coin tosses. Every positive rational duration has positive probability. The law has full support on (0,infinity) in the ordinary topology because positive rationals are dense, and E[E]<=E[A]=2. Each causal clock increment is thus an exactly representable rational with a finite sampling certificate, and the cumulative rational clock strictly increases; it tends to infinity a.s. (e.g. the independent event A=B=1 has probability 1/4 on each trial).

By PRF-13, on the same compact prime-character carrier every recognizable robust open-cylinder target is reached almost surely in finitely many samples, with expected elapsed analytic time <=2/eps_C. Expected coin toss count is <=4/eps_C by the same stopped-sum argument: the next pair of toss sequences is independent of whether its trial is needed. Actual digital runtime also includes arithmetic on growing numerators and denominators, phase evaluation and certificate verification; it has not been given a uniform finite mean bound here.

For any nonzero character frequency omega, phi=E exp(-i omega E) has |phi|<1. If |phi|=1, equality in the triangle inequality (or E|Y-EY|^2=1-|EY|^2 for Y of modulus 1) forces exp(-i omega E) to be constant almost surely. Full support and continuity would make exp(-i omega t) constant on (0,infinity), impossible when omega!=0. The second-moment and square-subsequence proof of PRF-14 now applies without a closed formula for phi. Thus this finite-bit policy also gives almost-sure Haar empirical mapping of K and countable finite-cylinder coverage.

At finite precision, use computable log p, sin, cos and certified interval bounds to evaluate any finite number of prime phases at rational clock t. If a sound acceptance region contains a nonempty open cylinder with positive margin, a finite interval certificate suffices inside a smaller cylinder. For example, a declared target open rectangle of phase coordinates with rational endpoints has a smaller open rectangle with rational margin; evaluating all listed coordinates at a fixed tolerance suffices for a sound in-rectangle test on the smaller region. More general candidate recognizers need their own margin and finite verification proof. If finite computations are dovetailed rather than run at every visit, ensure that the retained exact clock/readout can be replayed and each required test receives finite service. Do not infer an exact isolated equality from finite intervals.

The law is blind to the target and independent of old-frame novelty, but the verifier is not replaced by a lucky random outcome. The prime labels, analytic flow, phase access and error discipline remain declared tools. This is an existence construction for a finite-bit always-forward observer, not a claim that a finite observer stores the whole infinite torus or that mathematical truth is random.

**Verdict:** PROVED LOCAL, countable-support constructive policy under the same compact-flow and recognizer hypotheses. Next falsifier: exhibit an intended SUCC bridge whose acceptance is not open and has no finite discrete construction certificate; the open-cylinder rule does not decide it.
