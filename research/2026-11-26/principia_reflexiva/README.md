# Principia Reflexiva — De Completione et Generatione Observatorum

**Authorial seed:** Leah, 2026-11-26; recursive finite/analytic dual completion, orthogonal grokking nucleation, and a perpetually moving observer that discovers a required differential from its retained causal history. **Research-only.** No change to SUCC kernel, BBP source, seed, formal proofs, or main.

## The proposed architecture

1. Master an explicitly typed finite/discrete frame, with saturation relative to accessible tools, domains, memory and observation budget.
2. Construct its coherent mathematical completion; distinguish a formal limit from elapsed time.
3. At a plateau, continue a lawful causal search with a retained journal. A verified finite differential can unlock a new representation or a cheaper route. Almost-sure finite discovery requires a fairness/recurrence and finite-recognition theorem, not just constructibility.
4. Explore a dual analytic frame and reconstruct the original marked carrier by double duality.
5. Retain the newly available transition operators, paths, cocycles or higher morphisms. Forgetting the extra structure recovers the original marked root, while the richer observer distinguishes additional action histories.
6. Repeat only where a next finite certificate and sound verifier have independently been supplied.

The essential distinction: exact reconstruction is not strict enrichment, and strict enrichment is not automatic. Null in a given display is not null in all retained histories. At infinite semantic closure there is no additional word of those same actions outside it.
