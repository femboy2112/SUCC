## Independent modular derivation

Fix N>=1 and X=Z/N with uniform probability measure. For m|N, let A_m be
the algebra of complex functions constant on the fibers of x -> x mod m.
Let E_m be its orthogonal conditional-expectation projector. Direct averaging
over the partition gives

`E_m[x,y] = (m/N) 1_(x=y mod m)`.

For m,n|N, a simultaneous fiber is a congruence class modulo lcm(m,n).
Compatible classes have N/lcm(m,n) elements. Counting intermediate points in
the product of the two averaging kernels gives

`E_m E_n = E_n E_m = E_gcd(m,n)`.

Products of indicator functions for the two partitions are precisely the
nonempty joint atoms. Therefore the *unital algebra* generated by A_m and A_n
is A_lcm(m,n). The linear sum A_m+A_n need not be an algebra. This distinction
is mathematical, not a numerical tolerance or a source-authentication claim.

Independent Fourier route: chi_k(x)=exp(2*pi*i*k*x/N), 0<=k<N, is an
orthonormal basis. Its conductor is d=N/gcd(k,N), with d=1 for k=0.
E_m chi_k=chi_k iff d|m, and zero otherwise. Hence P_d, the projector onto
characters of exact conductor d, satisfies

`P_d = sum_(e|d) mu(d/e) E_e`, `rank(P_d)=phi(d)`,
`P_d P_f = 0 (d!=f)`, `sum_(d|N) P_d=I`.

For a finite menu m_i, the linear pool projector is the sum of P_d over
conductors dividing at least one m_i. The joint algebra includes all d dividing
L=lcm_i(m_i). Its missing linear projector sums the remaining P_d. The minimum
number of menu algebras whose products can produce the d-sector is the least
subset size with d dividing that subset's lcm; constants have order zero.
The unitality, product availability and common carrier hypotheses are required.

## Exact adaptive no-go, including legality

Let q:S->Q be the declared source quotient. An observer network may contain
arbitrarily many agents, relay nodes, memory registers and adaptive choices.
At a reachable public transcript h, write z for all internal state that can
affect future behavior. The sufficient hypothesis is a quotient of the
*operational state*, not just the displayed scalar response:

1. Equal q(s) induces equal initial law of public and policy-relevant state.
   A private register initialized with a distinguishing source bit violates
   this hypothesis even if the probe catalogue is constant.
2. Action selection uses h and permitted internal state, with a source-independent
   random tape or a joint conditional law factoring through the quotient.
3. For every action and reachable h, legality, failure/rejection, payload,
   observable timing, cost, termination and next projected internal/source
   state have the same **joint conditional law** on each quotient fiber.
4. Updates preserve the operational quotient. Source-changing actions require
   a commuting quotient transition; merely returning a constant payload is
   insufficient. Any externally visible menu or scheduling decision is an output.

Then the law of every finite network transcript is constant on q-fibers.
Proof: couple equal initial projected states; at each common history use the
same action randomization and the same conditional output/update kernel.
Induction preserves equal transcript and projected-state laws, including
rejecting or stopping histories. Pure relays and postprocessing are functions
or common randomized kernels of those histories, so they preserve the claim.
For countable streams with the ordinary cylinder-generated transcript sigma
algebra, equal finite-dimensional laws give equal full transcript laws. No
unmodeled transfinite observation or nonmeasurable event is licensed.

Consequently, a target that differs inside a quotient fiber cannot be recovered
with zero error on all its members by such a network. This is a source-relative
and toolkit-relative boundary. It is not a statement that no larger apparatus
could refine q.

Exact counterexamples to omitted premises (two source worlds 0 and 1, q constant):

* **Legality:** the sole probe returns 0 wherever legal, but is legal only in
  world 0. A visible success/rejection distinguishes immediately.
* **Cost/time:** both payloads are 0; observed costs 1 and 2 distinguish.
* **Initial memory:** a hidden source bit controls which named action is sent.
  Action identity distinguishes despite constant response tables.
* **Transition:** a move outputs 0 but transfers the hidden bit into a later
  readable state; the subsequent read separates the worlds.
* **History:** announcing distinct prior histories or path menus is a source
  channel unless included in the quotient contract.
* **Marginals:** in world 0 two fair outputs are `(B,B)`, and in world 1 they
  are `(B,1-B)`. Every one-probe marginal is fair and identical, while the pair
  determines the world exactly. Marginal factorization is not joint or
  conditional factorization.

Finite exhaustive check: all 1,296 two-action deterministic operational tables
on two quotient states, including two payloads and visible rejection, and all

post-rejection branch). Hidden bits evolve without affecting the quotient.

## Spectral projection: independent conditional derivation

This section independently derives the claim requested during review. Fix
I=(-1/2,1/2), l_j(x)=P_j(2x), low space span(l_1,...,l_4), high
space QH=polynomials-of-degree-at-most-4 perpendicular, and
M=polynomials-of-degree-at-most-3 perpendicular. Let Vu(x)=integral_-1/2^x u.
For u in QH, Vu has both endpoint traces zero, and integration by parts shows
Vu is orthogonal to every polynomial of degree at most three. Thus Vu in M.

The inherited source premise is `D >= kappa V*V` on QH,
`kappa=20699/1000000`; this audit does not independently prove that global
coercivity inequality. For low x, derivative T x=(Gx)' and high u,

`<Gx,u> = -<Tx,Vu> = -<Pi_M Tx,Vu>`.

The boundary term vanishes because Vu, not x, has zero endpoint traces.
Cauchy–Schwarz and coercivity bound this functional in the D-energy norm by
`kappa^(-1/2)||Pi_M Tx||`. The variational inverse-energy formula then gives

`<R0 x,x> <= kappa^(-1)||Pi_M Tx||^2`.

Equivalently, the finite spectral inverse energies increase to a bounded limit,
which supplies the required square-root range. No bounded zero-shift inverse,
dense-range approximation rate, or negative-direction assumption is introduced.
Mean-zero compression of G subtracts a constant and hence does not alter Tx.

Set h_(j,J)=(G_J l_j)' for **one fixed finite J**. Since
`||l_k||^2=1/(2k+1)`, orthogonal projection gives exactly

`||Pi_M h_(j,J)||^2 = ||h_(j,J)||^2
                      - sum_(k=0)^3 (2k+1)|<h_(j,J),l_k>|^2`.

If E_j bounds the total norm squared from above and [a_jk,b_jk] encloses
that same finite-J real moment, put d_jk=max(0,a_jk,-b_jk). Then

`E_j^projected = E_j - sum_(k=0)^3 (2k+1)d_jk^2`

is a valid upper bound on the projected norm squared. A negative resulting
upper signals incompatible premises or arithmetic failure; it is not a
negative physical squared norm. Intersecting two independently justified
intervals for the **same** moment is sound even if their sources are shared.
The intersection creates no independence and cannot repair a false premise.

For normalized inputs e_j=sqrt(2j+1)l_j, the Hilbert–Schmidt bound yields
`t_p=sum_(j in sector p)(2j+1) E_j^projected` and
`||Pi_M T_J|p|| <= sqrt(t_p)`. The entire omitted derivative Gamma ladder
has a Young operator bound eta_J. Projection is contractive, so

`||Pi_M T_full|p|| <= sqrt(t_p)+eta_J`,
`R0|p <= c_p I`, `c_p=(s_p+eta_J)^2/kappa`, `s_p^2>=t_p`.

This adds the tail **once in operator norm after finite-J projection**.
There is no sector-rank multiplier for this operator tail. Omitting the
cross-term is unsound: a unit finite residual and a unit tail in the same
direction have squared norm 4, not 2. Using full-source moments against a
finite-J total is also unsound: h_J=l_4 and tail=l_0 have finite energy 1/9

The coefficient metric is W=diag(1/3,1/5,1/7,1/9). An operator bound

`A^- - diag(c_odd/3,c_even/5,c_odd/7,c_even/9) <= S0`.
