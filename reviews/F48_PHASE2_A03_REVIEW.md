## 1. T1 proof reconstructed at the actual tree and restriction maps

Fix one policy and a complete original/source/current/history contract. Let \(T_n\) be **all** its locally legal prepared private prefixes of depth \(n\), with actual truncations \(r_{m,n}\). Its roots retain the immutable original source. Assume finitely many prepared private roots and finite branching at every prefix; changing types by depth are allowed. Here \(\Omega\) is exactly **all infinite locally legal branches of this tree**, without another global admission condition.

For \(p\in T_n\), membership in \(E_n\) means that the same prefix \(p\), not a newly chosen source, has an extension at every later finite depth. Finite preparation plus finite branching makes every \(T_n\) finite. Truncating an extendable prefix preserves extendability. Conversely, if every one of \(p\)'s finitely many children had a finite upper bound on its extension depth, their maximum would bound extensions of \(p\). Thus an extendable child exists and \(E_{n+1}\to E_n\) is onto. This includes the empty case: an empty target forces an empty domain by restriction closure.

A subsystem here means actual subsets \(Y_n\subseteq T_n\) closed under those truncations, with their inherited maps. If its adjacent maps are onto, successively lifting \(p\in Y_n\) through any **finite** number of stages supplies extensions at every requested depth. Therefore \(p\in E_n\), proving greatestness without presupposing a complete branch.

For an individual \(p\in E_n\), its finitely branching descendant tree has arbitrarily deep nodes. Classical König realization supplies an infinite descendant branch. Conversely a complete branch extends every prefix, so evaluation \(\Omega\to T_n\) has image exactly \(E_n\). A coherent inverse family retains one original prepared root and determines all subsequent configurations and branch tokens; assembling those coordinates is inverse to taking prefixes. Thus \(\lim E_n\cong\Omega\), including when both are empty. A depth-preserving lawful tree map commuting with truncation sends each finite extension to an extension, hence sends \(E\) to \(E'\). It need not be injective, reflect enabledness, or lift policies. **The four algebraic assertions survive.** The classical finite inverse-system existence context was independently checked at [Stacks 086J](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/086J).

**Topology interpretation.** Give each finite prefix stage its declared discrete topology and \(\Omega\) the induced finite-prefix cylinder topology. Adjacent equality constraints define a closed subset of \(\prod_n T_n\); finite discrete stages make that product compact. Evaluation and prefix assembly are continuous because their finite coordinates are projections, giving the asserted homeomorphism. This is a declared branch topology, not an assertion about an arbitrary preexisting physical-carrier/source topology. Two permanent roots with an indiscrete ambient carrier topology already defeat any unqualified identification with the discrete two-point prefix topology. No such ambient topology is a T1 premise.

**Important variance/surjectivity interpretation.** The stages aggregate every policy-consistent **public outcome** at that depth. The theorem does not make each chosen public-prefix arrow of \(H:T^{op}\to\mathrm{Set}\) onto. Minimal counterexample: two allowed prepared sources \(\sigma=0,1\), a deterministic first report equal to \(\sigma\), then permanent continuation. Both private roots extend indefinitely. The aggregate depth-1 restriction covers both roots; the particular report-0 restriction covers only source 0. Conditioning refines compatibility. This is a type clarification, not a counterexample to the stated aggregated-stage theorem. The fresh probe verifies both images explicitly.

## 3. T2 reconstructed: CP channels do not determine labelled Kraus atoms

Interpret a Kraus representation as a finite **indexed, labelled family**, not a duplicate-collapsing mathematical set. On the one-dimensional operator space with normalized nonzero input \(\rho=1\), the family \((I)\) gives the identity CP channel and one positive labelled atom. The family \((I/\sqrt2,I/\sqrt2)\), indexed by two distinct labels, gives the same channel, since its squared coefficients sum to one, but two positive labelled atoms. A channel-invariant fiber cannot be bijective to both cardinalities. At depth \(n\) the corresponding positive labelled word counts are 1 and \(2^n\). This proves precisely the two-property no-go in the dossier; **T2 survives**. Indexed-family terminology is the needed interpretation of the brace notation. The fresh probe checks exact squared coefficients, not approximate square roots. [Watrous, Kraus representations and Corollary 2.23](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/TQI.2.pdf).

The conclusion forbids an invariant fiber that also reproduces **every representation's atoms**. It does not forbid coarse-grained fibers, declared unravelings, or classical public outcome words. If the auxiliary labels are physically measured, they define a different report-labelled instrument. For a fixed declared public report and its CP map, positive-cone updates and pulled-back effects are representation-independent operator constructions. They neither authenticate an apparatus nor identify unobserved private physical quantum paths. Retaining the original density as a mathematical law parameter does not store a physical copy.

The finite-height gate must concern **one complete original cone with common branch maps**, including any apparatus/source parameter needed by those maps. Fiberwise finite dimension over infinitely many unknown parameters does not suffice. Let source \(n\in\mathbb N\) carry a one-dimensional public-survival branch that is identity through depth \(n\) and zero at \(n+1\). Every finite depth has a source witness; no fixed \(n\) survives all depths. Encoding the parameter in a common original direct sum produces unbounded face height, so the finite-height premise is absent. A finite complete parameter family admits a finite direct sum and is killed after its maximum survival depth. The probe checks the four-parameter cap; the unbounded countermodel is established by the formula, not by enumerating infinity.

Even finite height only bounds the number of strict face changes, not a universal word-time deadline: a stationary one-dimensional instrument can have wait=identity and drop=zero, with a supplied word `wait^n drop` delaying the first change arbitrarily. A closed no-input finite generator is an additional X11 premise; that tentative synthesis statement is not recertified here. ## 4. Original/history laws, variance, policy lifting and resources

**Complete hidden/public types.** Source-indexed \(R_{a,\sigma,h}\) correctly handles original-source dependence. **Actual all-prior square.** On the supplied discrete state domains, apply the displayed decoded joint-kernel equation to a Dirac joint prior \(\delta_{(\sigma,c)}\): its two sides are exactly the fine decoded selected-report mass at \((\sigma,d)\) and the coarse selected-report mass. This proves necessity for all permitted Dirac priors. Conversely multiplying each row by \(\nu(\sigma,c)\) and summing proves equality for every nonnegative joint prior by linearity/Tonelli. The same row square at every corresponding prefix gives the adaptive law induction. Preparation, public decoder, source/target factorization and policy maps must also commute. For uncountable spaces this requires an actual measurable-kernel pushforward formulation; singleton or marginal equalities are not a replacement.

Marginals lose load-bearing original/current dependence: fair joint laws supported on \(\sigma=c\) and \(\sigma\ne c\) have identical two marginals, but an instrument reporting \(\mathbf1_{\sigma=c}\) gives probabilities 1 and 0. The probe verifies this smallest binary joint countermodel. The square must therefore retain the original/current joint law, not two unrelated fair marginals. Finite stochastic composition context was independently checked at [Fritz, Example 2.5](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.07021v8).

**Variance.** Selected-report nonnegative measures travel forward and compose as positive linear maps. Keeping zero gives a total map even for impossible report words; normalization is defined only at positive mass. The corresponding covariant category-of-elements construction has its unique lifted arrow with prescribed **source**, hence is an opfibration. Private histories truncate backward; past predicates pull back to future histories, with existential/universal fiber adjoints. These are distinct types. CP effects likewise pull back by the dual pairing, while states update forward. A mathematical zero or total cone arrow is not an acquired event. Nonnegative discrete support composition has no cancellation, but support contains no probabilities or independence assumption.

**Safe lifting.** It is sufficient that every coarse action safe on its whole compatible set has a safe fine implementation on its lifted compatible set, together with the prepared joint-law, history, target and accounting squares. Extra fine-only safe actions do not impair the lifted coarse policy: coarse menu `{read}` and fine menu `{read,wait}` is a minimal witness against requiring menu equality. The implementation follows the coarse public policy after source-blind decoding; it does not use a private source to choose a branch. This is a law/policy simulation theorem, not a claim that fine and coarse physical work are equal.

Resource state and accumulated private charges must participate in safety, the complete middle configuration, or a separately proved cost-domination map. Otherwise an omitted spent sensor or fine cost above the coarse budget defeats the lift. A marginal kernel square alone says nothing about costs. Infimal path composition cannot existentially switch fresh/spent representatives at the same displayed carrier endpoint, and robust private cost is not merely expected cost or a scalar zero Bellman value. The architecture preserves these accounting gates rather than deriving them from its new pruning theorem.
