## D29 — Full flows, finite maps, and Conley information

**Exact hypotheses.** Continuous full flows \(\Phi_t:X\to X\), \(\Psi_t:Y\to Y\), \(t\in\mathbb R\), on locally compact metric spaces; continuous proper surjection \(\pi:X\to Y\); and \(\pi\Phi_t=\Psi_t\pi\) for every real \(t\). A compact target neighborhood \(N\) satisfies \(\operatorname{Inv}_\Psi N\subset\operatorname{int}N\). The finite theorem instead has finite deterministic endomaps, a surjective semiconjugacy, and **bi-infinite** orbit invariance. Those are different types, not interchangeable definitions.

**D29-A reconstruction.** For any \(x\), all \(\Phi_t x\) lie in \(\pi^{-1}N\) iff all \(\Psi_t\pi x\) lie in \(N\). Hence
\[
\operatorname{Inv}_\Phi(\pi^{-1}N)=\pi^{-1}(\operatorname{Inv}_\Psi N).
\]
Properness is not needed for this set identity; it supplies compactness of \(\pi^{-1}N\). Continuity gives \(\pi^{-1}(\operatorname{int}N)\subset\operatorname{int}(\pi^{-1}N)\), so the preimage is isolating. Full time supplies the stronger equality
\[
\Phi_t(\pi^{-1}N)=\pi^{-1}(\Psi_tN):
\]
if \(\pi x=\Psi_t y\) with \(y\in N\), then \(\pi\Phi_{-t}x=y\), establishing the otherwise missing reverse inclusion. Pullback therefore preserves forward invariance and \(\Psi_TN\subset\operatorname{int}N\), and commutes with the nested intersection defining the attractor. Surjectivity ensures the lifted trapping neighborhood is nonempty. Compact nested images approach their intersection: a contrary sequence outside an open neighborhood has a convergent subnet/subsequence whose limit belongs to every earlier image and hence to the intersection. **Verdict: ACCEPT.**

**D29-B reconstruction.** In a finite deterministic map, every point supporting a bi-infinite orbit is on a directed cycle. An arbitrarily long past repeats a state; once repeated, determinism prevents leaving that cycle. Conversely every cycle supports a bi-infinite orbit. A source cycle projects onto a target cycle. Given a target cycle contained in \(N\), choose any lift of one of its states; its forward orbit remains over that cycle and eventually enters a source cycle whose image is the full target cycle. Thus the **image** of the source invariant set equals \(\operatorname{Inv}_g N\). If \(f\) is bijective, its two-sided orbit of any lift stays above the target cycle, giving the stronger preimage identity. Forward eventual cycles project as stated, but their periods can shrink. **Verdict: ACCEPT.**

**Minimal mutation.** \(f=(1,1)\), \(g=(0)\), \(\pi=(0,0)\) is a lawful finite semiconjugacy. Its source bi-infinite states are \(\{1\}\), target bi-infinite states \(\{0\}\), and full target preimage \(\{0,1\}\). This refutes the stronger inverse-image identity when invertibility is removed. A six-cycle projected by reduction mod 3 to a three-cycle witnesses period loss. Both were reconstructed in the review probe. They support finite-map statements, not a numerical certificate for continuous full flows.

**Conley counterexample reconstructed at actual maps.** The source cylinder flow is \((x,\theta)\mapsto(e^{-t}x,\theta)\), target flow \(y\mapsto e^{-t}y\), with proper coordinate projection. Their blocks \(N_X=[-1,1]\times S^1\), \(N_Y=[-1,1]\) have invariant sets \(\{0\}\times S^1\) and \(\{0\}\). No forward orbit exits either block, so \((N_X,\varnothing)\) and \((N_Y,\varnothing)\) satisfy the exit-set conditions. Adding the disjoint basepoint gives pointed quotients of types \(S^1_+\) and \(S^0\). The projection induces a pointed map, but reduced first homology is \(\mathbb Z\to0\), ruling out homotopy equivalence. This is an actual index failure under a proper semiconjugacy. It is not a claim that the two underlying blocks are homeomorphic to these reduced representatives. For a **discrete** Conley index, the index map/shift-equivalence data must additionally be retained; a pointed-space calculation alone does not define that index. [Khandhawit–Lin–Sasahira §4.2, Definitions 4.4–4.5](https://msp.org/gt/2018/22-4/gt-v22-n4-p03-s.pdf), [Franks–Richeson §§2–4](https://arxiv.org/html/math/9910171).

**Other hostile boundaries.** On identity flows, \(\mathbb R\sqcup\{p\}\to\mathbb R\), identity on the first component and \(p\mapsto0\), is proper and onto. The isolated open-component attractor \(\{p\}\) maps to \(\{0\}\), which is neither isolated nor attracting in the target identity flow. This correctly separates pullbacks from images. For \((x,z)\mapsto(e^{-t}x,e^tz)\), coordinate projection pulls \([-1,1]\) back to an unbounded strip; its maximal invariant subset is the noncompact vertical line. Removing properness therefore defeats the compact-neighborhood conclusion while retaining the orbit set identity. The three-state refinement example is also valid: states 0 and 2 have the same old report but different successor reports, so no autonomous old report map exists.

**Repair / next gate.** Properness and semiconjugacy preserve the stated preimage structure; they do not supply a pointed quotient homotopy equivalence. Index preservation needs an actual suitable equivalence of index data, or a legitimate continuation family with a common isolating neighborhood. A sudden source/reconstruction change is not such a family. The original isolation/block literature supports the type boundary, not a universal self-update law. [Conley–Easton §1](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/sites/default/files/attached-files/isolated_invariant_sets_and_isolating_blocks.pdf).

## E37 — Mellin approximation, continuation, and test-space control

**Exact hypotheses.** \(N\geq1\) integral; \(N^z=e^{z\log N}\) with real logarithm; the initial Mellin domain \(H=\{\Re z>0\}\); compact \(K\subset H\); fixed derivative order \(r\geq0\). Continuation uses compact sets avoiding every nonpositive integer. The vertical test-space statement fixes a particular \(\sigma>0\), with constants allowed to depend on \(\sigma\). It does not assert a bound uniform as \(\sigma\downarrow0\).

**Mellin/Beta identity.** Substitution \(t=Nx\) yields \(N^z\int_0^1x^{z-1}(1-x)^Ndx\). Integrating by parts repeatedly is legitimate for \(\Re z>0\): the zero endpoint is killed by \(x^z\), and the other by the remaining positive power of \(1-x\), until the final elementary integral. It yields exactly \(N!N^z/[z(z+1)\cdots(z+N)]\). This reconstructs the identity without using the desired limiting Gamma quotient. The review probe checks 16 rational positive-integer instances by independently expanding the polynomial and integrating monomials. Those finite checks are not a convergence proof. [DLMF Beta integral and domains](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.12.E1).

**Uniform compact convergence and derivatives.** For every fixed \(t>0\), \(f_N(t)\to e^{-t}\), including eventual membership in \([0,N]\), and \(0\leq f_N(t)\leq e^{-t}\). Put \(a=\min_K\Re z>0\), \(b=\max_K\Re z\). For all \(z\in K\),
\[
|t^{z-1}(\log t)^r|\leq(t^{a-1}+t^{b-1})|\log t|^r.
\]
Multiplying by \(|f_N-e^{-t}|\) gives the author's supremum estimate. Its dominant is integrable at zero because \(a>0\), and at infinity because of the exponential. Local versions also justify holomorphy and differentiation under the integral. Dominated convergence therefore establishes all stated fixed derivative orders. **Verdict: ACCEPT**, with genuine domination rather than finitely sampled agreement. Gamma's Mellin definition is classical. [DLMF §5.2(i)](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.2).

**Finite recurrence and lawful continuation.** Direct finite product cancellation gives
\[
G_N(z+1)/G_N(z)=Nz/(N+z+1)
\]
as a meromorphic identity after cancellation. For a compact pole-free \(K\), choose one integer \(k\) with \(K+k\subset H\). The exact product correction
\[
G_N(z)=G_N(z+k)\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}\frac{N+z+j+1}{N(z+j)}
\]
converges uniformly on \(K\); its denominators stay bounded away from zero there. Together with the half-plane result this gives the meromorphic continuation and local uniformity. Recurrence identifies overlapping continuation charts. A fixed small contour around \(-m\), with \(N\geq m\), yields residue convergence; direct cancellation gives \((-1)^m\binom NmN^{-m}\to(-1)^m/m!\). This is analytic continuation from an exact identity, not extrapolation from recorded finite values. **Verdict: ACCEPT.** [DLMF Gamma recurrence](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.5.E1).

**Uniform logarithmic derivative bound, including the missing quotient premise.** Define \(z=\sigma+it\), \(R=|z|\). The exact identity is
\[
\psi_N(z)=\log N-H_N-1/z+\sum_{j=1}^N\frac{z}{j(z+j)}.
\]
For \(\sigma>0\), \(|z+j|\geq|z|\) and \(|z+j|\geq j\). The head \(j\leq R\) is bounded by a harmonic sum, at most \(1+\log(\max(1,R))\); the tail is bounded by \(R\sum_{j>R}j^{-2}\leq2\) for \(R\geq1\), and by \(\sum j^{-2}<2\) for \(R<1\). Also \(|1/z|\leq1/\sigma\), and \(|\log N-H_N|\leq1\). Since \(R\leq\sigma+|t|\), the explicit safe bound
\[
|\psi_N(\sigma+it)|\leq5+1/\sigma+\log(1+\sigma)+\log(2+|t|)
\]
holds simultaneously for every \(N\geq1\) and real \(t\).

Passing from \(G_N,G'_N\) convergence to \(G'_N/G_N\) additionally uses \(\Gamma(z)\ne0\). This is a classical fact explicitly stated in DLMF §5.2, and can also be derived here using Hurwitz: all \(G_N\) are zero-free on \(H\), the compact-uniform limit is holomorphic, and \(\Gamma(1)=1\) excludes an identically zero limit. The dossier should state this short premise rather than leave quotient convergence implicit. It is a **minor proof completion**, not a falsifier. Pointwise quotient convergence and the displayed bound imply, for every fixed Schwartz \(h\), absolute integrable domination of \(\psi_N(\sigma+it)h(t)\). The result is weak convergence in \(\mathcal S'(\mathbb R)\) on that fixed vertical line. It does not assert strong distribution topology, moving-line uniformity, or convergence of the full completed Weil form. **Verdict: ACCEPT with this explicit clarification.**

**Trivial-zero cancellation and fake-source guard.** At \(s=-2m\), \(m\geq1\), the zeta reflection formula has one simple sine zero and all other factors nonzero at the center; \(\zeta(2m+1)>0\) follows already from its convergent positive Dirichlet series. Thus \(\zeta\) has a simple zero. Gamma has leading term \(2(-1)^m/[m!(s+2m)]\), so the product's finite value is \(2(-1)^m\zeta'(-2m)/m!\). The sign, order, and factor two are correct. For \(D=\zeta+6^{-s}\), the added entire term is nonzero at \(-2m\), and \(\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)D(s)\) has the extra residue \(2(-1)^m6^{2m}\pi^m/m!\). The coefficient test \(a(6)-a(2)a(3)=1\) explicitly rejects that non-Euler source. Gamma alone does not cause the arithmetic cancellation. **Verdict: ACCEPT.** [DLMF zeta reflection formula](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.4.E2).

**Identification adversaries.** The periodic gauge \(\Gamma e^{\epsilon\sin(2\pi z)}\) keeps recurrence and positive-integer values but changes the quarter-point value; it is a valid countermodel absent an additional selector. For finite samples, the displayed symmetric polynomial has at least a double zero at each sampled/reflected point, so first jets are preserved. To make the zero-change aspect decisive, independently use the symmetric entire base \(1\), samples \(0,1,1/2\), and the same polynomial form \(P\); the rational coefficient \(\epsilon=-1/P(1/4)\) makes \(1+\epsilon P\) vanish at \(1/4,3/4\) while retaining all sampled values and first derivatives. The review probe verifies the exact values. This is an explicit fake entire-source example, not an arithmetic zeta function or an input from its nontrivial zeros. If reality/conjugation is also required for arbitrary complex samples, close the finite sample set under conjugation before forming \(P\). **Repair / next gate:** a source-derived arithmetic/test-core identification, with the pole, conductor, phase, and half-density obligations, remains outside the accepted Gamma result.

## Initial F48 — Incidence covers, history variance, and compression

**Exact initial domain.** A fixed public typed run \(h\), one preparation relation, declared epoch/outcome/branch-token transition relations, retained original source tag, and a finite path incidence complex. Future-dependent legality is represented in the state if that legality is to be checked locally. The initial declared \(R_a\) is source-independent apart from the preparation/source-equality labels. The theorem does not already supply a source-indexed quantum instrument model or arbitrary history-dependent policy semantics.

**T1 proof reconstruction.** Objects are incidence subcomplexes, and a cover means union of **all cells**, including every transition edge. Restriction forgets labels and branch tokens, so identities and composition are literal. A matching cover family assigns the same labels to any repeated cell; its union is therefore a unique assignment. Every preparation constraint resides on its root vertex, and every transition/source-equality constraint resides on its edge plus endpoints; a covering member contains that constraint and establishes it. The union thus satisfies all constraints. Conversely global restriction is matching. For the complete run, the connected edges propagate one original source tag and collect precisely the prepared carrier and branch-token history, giving \(F_h(K_h)\cong H(h)\). **Verdict: ACCEPT, PROVED LOCAL**, a genuine sheaf theorem for this declared site. [Stacks sheaf equalizer definition](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00VL).

**Smallest topology mutation.** A one-edge run with an empty transition relation has no global history, yet its two isolated vertex objects can each have assignments. Their union omits the edge, so this family is **not a cover** of the run complex. Calling vertex coverage alone a cover would incorrectly demand a glue despite the missing transition constraint. Root preparation may be satisfied at the initial vertex and does not repair the absent edge. T1 correctly avoids this mutation.

**Private-prefix variance and actual fibration.** For \(h\leq h'\), truncation \(r_{h,h'}:H(h')\to H(h)\) composes exactly. In the category of elements, the unique arrow over \(h\to h'\) **with prescribed target** \(p'\in H(h')\) has source \(r(p')\). This is the discrete-fibration lifting property. No unique future from a prescribed past is asserted: a past can have zero or multiple extensions. Two histories \(P\to L\to Z\) and \(P\to R\to Z\) have the same source/endpoint belief but different preceding private states; an endpoint object alone cannot define that private truncation. **Verdict: ACCEPT**, including the direction of variance. The Stacks Grothendieck construction supplies the general categorical context; the set-valued discrete lift here follows directly from truncation. [Stacks tag 02XU](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02XU).

**T2 quantifiers and minimal join witness.** Let \(I\) be the **whole equality interface**—in this application it includes original source, carrier state, and any contract/memory data needed by the declared local relations. For all arbitrary typed relations \(L\subset A\times I\), \(R\subset I\times B\), matching reports under \(q:I\to O\) admit no spurious joins iff \(q\) is injective. Sufficiency is immediate from \(q(x)=q(y)\Rightarrow x=y\). Necessity uses \(x\ne y\) with the same report, \(L=\{(a,x)\}\), \(R=\{(y,b)\}\): the reported join is nonempty and the true join empty. The review probe uses the two-element interface, a constant report, and these singleton relations. **Verdict: ACCEPT, PROVED LOCAL.** Injectivity of a map on the current carrier alone is not injectivity on a larger original-source/memory interface.

**Existential public projection.** On the legitimate two-edge cover, the two private singleton relations above each have a local public image and agree on their reported middle interface. The full private fiber product is empty, so its public image is empty. The pointwise-image presheaf therefore fails the sheaf matching-family axiom. Sheafifying it introduces an abstract public glue, not a private source history. This is a strict refutation receipt, unlike mere lack of uniqueness. A report can be noninjective yet safe for a **restricted** relation class; proving that restriction/congruence is a separate obligation and does not contradict T2's universal quantifier.

**Initial-phase acceptance boundary.** The fixed-run result does not mean that endpoint beliefs alone retain every model menu or public-history policy. A Markov interpretation is valid only after all future-relevant memory/contract data are included, and does not encode an arbitrary policy depending on the full public transcript. For example, two past histories ending at the same carrier can trigger different next actions under a history-only policy. Natural history maps that preserve preparation/transitions do commute with private truncation; transporting policy reachability additionally requires specified compatible policies, enabled-domain and model-menu conditions, costs/budgets, and public observation labels. One-way forward simulation alone does not establish that package.

For a source-indexed instrument one must declare \(R_{a,\sigma}\), or include the relevant original-source/latent parameter in the interface and demonstrate the corresponding transition square. If the same current carrier has outcome 0 for source \(\sigma_0\) and outcome 1 for source \(\sigma_1\), the initial source-independent relation is insufficient to establish that distinction. This is an extension gate, not a counterexample to the initial source-independent theorem.

Quantum CP maps and probabilities also require extra interpretation. Even the identity channel has Kraus presentations \(\{I\}\) and \(\{I/\sqrt2,I/\sqrt2\}\), since both give \(\rho\mapsto\rho\). Treating Kraus labels as intrinsic hidden branch tokens would yield one versus two private branches from the same CP map. Thus the initial set-history sheaf does not canonically identify physical private paths, their probabilities, or an unraveling. An explicitly chosen instrument/environment record and source-conditioned law can be attached later and audited; CP-map composition by itself does not supply those labels. This follows directly from the two algebraic presentations, using the usual Kraus-map definition. [Watrous, *The Theory of Quantum Information*, §2.2, author-hosted text](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/TQI.pdf).

Normalized stochastic kernels and support relations are distinct objects: support forgets weights, and compatible marginals need not determine a unique joint law. The author's positive full-support bit laws and three fair anticorrelations are valid elementary adversaries. Their existence does not turn the classical private-path sheaf into a probability sheaf or quantum reconstruction theorem. Likewise, the initial claims about inverse limits are mathematical limits, not elapsed physical infinite histories. **Verdict for quantum/policy/source-indexed whole-swarm synthesis: NOT ACCEPTED BY THIS INITIAL REVIEW; requires the later phase-2 reconciliation.** This is a stated scope boundary, not a refutation of a theorem the initial dossier did not assert.

## D32 — Short delay/reconstruction audit

**Targets and universal domain.** Fiber constancy is precisely the necessary/sufficient condition for a decoder on the observed delay image. For linear delays on **all** \(K^n\), equal outputs differ by \(\ker O_k\); hence an original linear target decodes iff \(\ker O_k\subseteq\ker T\), equivalently \(\operatorname{row}T\subseteq\operatorname{row}O_k\). The induced map on the image is linear and can be extended to an ambient decoder. A current target after the last sampled update instead uses \(TF^{k-1}\). A restricted nonlinear carrier can admit a decoder without the full ambient row criterion. These hypotheses and distinctions are correct.

**Plateau and sharp bound.** For \(W_j=\operatorname{span}\{\operatorname{row}C,\ldots,\operatorname{row}CF^{j-1}\}\), equality \(W_{j+1}=W_j\) places \(\operatorname{row}CF^j\) in \(W_j\). Multiplying any earlier generator by \(F\) yields either an already listed row or that last new row, proving \(W_jF\subseteq W_j\). Thus no later delay row escapes. For \(j=0\), equality means \(C=0\), and the same conclusion is immediate. If the first \(n\) inclusions are strict, their dimensions reach \(n\); otherwise the earlier plateau settles the whole future. The scalar cyclic-shift sensor has \(CF^j=e_j^T\), so \(0\) and \(e_{n-1}\) agree for \(n-1\) reports but separate at report \(n\). **Finite n-report stabilization and sharpness: ACCEPT.** This uses known dimension/stationarity; it is not a conclusion from a finite trace alone.

**Backaction and actual conjugacy.** Under the stated read-then-backact-then-update order, the inter-read dynamics is exactly \(f\circ B\). For the swapping \(F\) and \(B=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)\), hidden initial \((0,1)\) is killed by \(FB\); passive predictions cannot be counted as its acquired delays. When \(O_k\) has full column rank, \(LO_k=I\), so \(L\) on the delay image is the genuine inverse and \(\widetilde F=O_kFL\) conjugates the source endomap to its image endomap. The stated shear matrices obey these identities directly. Invertibility of \(F\) is needed only to supply inverse dynamics. For deficient rank, a well-defined dynamic quotient requires \(F\ker O_k\subseteq\ker O_k\). **ACCEPT.**

**Precision and smooth theorem boundary.** For \(O=\left[\begin{smallmatrix}1&0\\1&\epsilon\end{smallmatrix}\right]\), positive \(\epsilon\), its infinity norm is \(1+\epsilon\), its inverse norm \(2/\epsilon\), hence condition number \(2/\epsilon+2\). At \(10^{-6}\) this is exactly \(2{,}000{,}002\), despite full exact rank. The closed noise balls of radius \(\epsilon/2\) around reports of \(0\) and \(e_1\) meet at \((0,\epsilon/2)\). Exact identifiability therefore does not establish robust reconstruction. Takens' generic-pair, compact smooth manifold, diffeomorphism and regularity hypotheses do not cover arbitrary fixed sensors, identity dynamics, logistic critical/noninvertible dynamics, or a backacting sampled clock. **ACCEPT the stated limits**, with no empirical reconstruction promotion. [Takens, Theorem 1, pp.369–371](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~saito/data/synchrosqueezing/takens_detect-strange-attractors-turbulence.pdf).
